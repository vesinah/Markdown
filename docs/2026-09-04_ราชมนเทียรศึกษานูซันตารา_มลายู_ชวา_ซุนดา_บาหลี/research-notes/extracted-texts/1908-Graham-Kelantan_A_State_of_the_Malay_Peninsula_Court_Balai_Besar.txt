The climate ofKelantan ismild and equable.
Intheplains itisstrongly affected bythe sea,
themorning land breeze and theafternoon sea
breeze blowing with peculiar regularity during
thegreater partoftheyear. Here thetempera-
ture, while rarely falling below 69"^ F.,never rises,
even atthehottest time ofthe year, beyond
93'' F.,andtheaverage daily range oftemperature
isabout 14F.Among the hills, fiftyandmore

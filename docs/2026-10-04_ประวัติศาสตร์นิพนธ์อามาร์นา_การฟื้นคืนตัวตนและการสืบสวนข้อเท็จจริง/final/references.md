### 1. จารึก ปัพพาชนีย์ และเอกสารปฐมภูมิอียิปต์โบราณและอักษรลิ่ม (Primary Epigraphic Sources)

- **Abydos King List.** *The King List of Seti I at Abydos*. Temple of Seti I, Abydos. Published in Auguste Mariette, *Abydos: Description des fouilles exécutées sur l'emplacement de cette ville*, vol. 1. Paris: Imprimerie Nationale, 1869.
- **Amarna Boundary Stelae.** *The Boundary Stelae of Neferkheperure Waenre Akhenaten* (Stelae A, B, F, J, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, S, U, V, X). Published in Norman de Garis Davies, *The Rock Tombs of El Amarna*, vol. 5: *Smaller Tombs and Boundary Stelae*. London: Egypt Exploration Fund, 1908; and William J. Murnane and Charles C. Van Siclen III, *The Boundary Stelae of Akhenaten*. London: Kegan Paul International, 1993.
- **Amarna Diplomatic Letters (EA 1–382).** *The Cuneiform Archives of Tell el-Amarna*. Preserved in the British Museum, Egyptian Museum Cairo, Vorderasiatisches Museum Berlin, and Ashmolean Museum Oxford. Edited and translated by J. A. Knudtzon, *Die El-Amarna-Tafeln, mit Einleitung und Erläuterungen*, 2 vols. Leipzig: J. C. Hinrichs, 1915; and William L. Moran, *The Amarna Letters*. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1992.
- **Dayr Abū Ḥinnis Year 16 Graffito.** *The Year 16 Quarry Inscription of Akhenaten and Nefertiti*. Dayr Abū Ḥinnis limestone quarry. Edited by Athena Van der Perre, "The Year 16 Graffito of Akhenaten in Dayr Abū Ḥinnis: A Contribution to the Study of the Later Years of Nefertiti." *Journal of Egyptian History* 7 (2014): 67–108.
- **Great Hymn to the Aten.** *The Solar Liturgy in the Tomb of Ay (South Tomb 25, Tell el-Amarna)*. Edited by Norman de Garis Davies, *The Rock Tombs of El Amarna*, vol. 6: *Tombs of Parennefer, Tutu, and Ay*. London: Egypt Exploration Fund, 1908; and Jan Assmann, *Ägyptische Hymnen und Gebete*. Zürich: Artemis Verlag, 1975.
- **Inscription of Mes.** *The Legal Dispute of the Land of the Mes Family*. Saqqara, Dynasty 19. Edited by Alan H. Gardiner, *The Inscription of Mes: A Contribution to the Study of Egyptian Judicial Procedure*. Untersuchungen zur Geschichte und Altertumskunde Aegyptens 4. Leipzig: J. C. Hinrichs, 1905.
- **Pairi Tomb Graffito (TT139).** *The Year 3 Inscription of King Ankhkheperure Neferneferuaten*. Theban Tomb 139, Sheikh Abd el-Qurna. Edited by Alan H. Gardiner, "The Graffito from the Tomb of Pere." *Journal of Egyptian Archaeology* 14 (1928): 10–12.
- **Saqqara Tablet.** *The Royal Canon of Saqqara*. Tomb of Tjuneroy, Saqqara, Dynasty 19. Cairo Museum JE 11335. Edited by Auguste Mariette, "La table de Saqqarah." *Revue Archéologique* 10 (1864): 169–186.
- **Tutankhamun Restoration Stela (Cairo CG 34183).** *The Restoration Inscription of King Tutankhamun from the Temple of Karnak*. Preserved in the Egyptian Museum, Cairo. Edited by Wolfgang Helck, *Urkunden der 18. Dynastie: Inschriften der Könige von Amenophis III. bis Haremhab* (Heft 22). Berlin: Akademie-Verlag, 1958; and John Bennett, "The Restoration Inscription of Tut'ankhamun." *Journal of Egyptian Archaeology* 25, no. 1 (1939): 8–15.

- **Quotes (verbatim):**
  1. "In contemporary Myanmar, the Zinpanzar (Jinapañjara Gāthā) is revered by Weikza devotees not merely as an ordinary blessing chant, but as the supreme esoteric armor that transforms the vulnerable mortal flesh into an inviolable Buddhist citadel."
  2. "The fourteen verses of the Burmese Zinpanzar are systematically mapped onto the practitioner's body, invoking the twenty-eight Buddhas upon the crown and the great disciples at the sensory gates, which wizard-masters like Bo Bo Aung utilized to attain invulnerability and spiritual longevity."
  3. "Cabalistic diagrams known as 'In' (အင်း) frequently incorporate the verses of the Jinapañjara to establish consecrated boundaries (sīmā) around pagodas and homes against demonic interference and destructive worldly influences."

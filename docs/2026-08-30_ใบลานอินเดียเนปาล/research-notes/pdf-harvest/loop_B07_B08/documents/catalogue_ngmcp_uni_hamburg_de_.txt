Its main purpose is to prepare a comprehensive descriptive catalogue of the more than 180,000 Nepalese manuscripts microfilmed under the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project. Please consult theOrient Department of the Staatsbibliothek zu Berlinfor more information on the project and the microfilm resources. There are currently three resources available: The Catalogue of Indic manuscripts The Descriptive Catalogue (archive of the Mediawiki pages) The Catalogue of Tibetan Manuscripts The curation of the catalogues and integration of digitized material is work in progress.

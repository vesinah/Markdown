  1. (Preface, จาก S-274 scan) "There are in the Library 93 bundles of palm leaf MSS., which, with eight bundles acquired by the late Mahārāja Sir Vira Sumsher Jung Bahadur Rana, make up the total of 101 bundles. These bundles contain altogether 448 MSS., though many of them are mere fragments. The number of MSS. in the Library, paper and palm leaf together, come up to nearly 5,000."
  2. (Preface p. xi) "It speaks volumes of the climate of Nepal, that palm-leaves of the 12th and 13th centuries have not yet decayed. Works like Dāsakarma and Vivāhakarmasamuccaya, liturgical works, which are, as a rule, most carelessly kept, have lasted in Nepal for more than seven centuries." (Dāsakarmapaddhati copied 1176 AD; Vivāhakarma-samuccaya copied N.S. 233 = 1118 AD)
  3. (Preface p. xiv) "This shows that the Buddhist monks who excelled in Caligraphy were extensively employed in copying Hindu MSS. In the Durbar Library, too, the Buddhist Vajracāryas are still the best scribes." (เรื่อง MS Nārada-smṛti 1380 AD, commentary เป็นภาษา Newari)
  4. (Preface p. xiii) "There are 91 leaves of a work on Grammar, p. 70, No. 1595 … The matter given in the Prarambha Vākya … is what may fit in exactly on one side of a narrow palm-leaf. The first leaf is invariably written on one side only."
- **ประเด็นที่ map:** สถิติ Durbar Library จริง (101 bundles / 448 palm-leaf MSS / ~5,000 รวม); ความยาว/รูปแบบ pothi (leaf เขียนข้างเดียวหน้าแรก); บทบาท Vajrācārya scribes; การประเมิน Vedic corpus ในเนปาล ("Brahmaṇas and Upaniṣads are unknown… Works on Vedic sacrifices too are conspicuous by their absence")
- **Citation chaining:** Śāstrī อ้าง Bendall's Cam. Cat. (S-272), Cat. Cat. (Aufrecht, S-271), Raj. Mitra, Eggeling — ปิดวงจร 3 แคตตาล็อกใหญ่เชื่อมกัน

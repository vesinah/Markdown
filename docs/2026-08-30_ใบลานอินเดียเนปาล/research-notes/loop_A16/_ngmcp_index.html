About NGMCP

The Nepalese-German Manuscript Cataloguing Project was launched in April 2002. Its main purpose is to prepare a comprehensive descriptive catalogue of the more than 180,000 Nepalese manuscripts microfilmed under the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project.

Please consult theOrient Department of the Staatsbibliothek zu Berlinfor more information on the project and the microfilm resources.

There are currently three resources available:

  • The Catalogue of Indic manuscripts
  • The Descriptive Catalogue (archive of the Mediawiki pages)
  • The Catalogue of Tibetan Manuscripts

The curation of the catalogues and integration of digitized material is work in progress. Please contact theFDMZ-Teamif you have of any questions. If you use the catalogues, we would appreciate your feedback.

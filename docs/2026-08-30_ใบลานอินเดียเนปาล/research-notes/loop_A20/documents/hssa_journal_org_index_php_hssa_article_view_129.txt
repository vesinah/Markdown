Extant Manuscripts of Śrīdhara's Works
Supplementing David Pingree's Census of the Exact Sciences in Sanskrit
Authors
Taro Tokutake
CNRS-SPHERE Laboratory
https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8118-626X
DOI:
https://doi.org/10.18732/hssa129
Keywords:
Manuscripts,
Śrīdhara,
Triśatī,
David Pingree,
Census of the Exact Sciences in Sanskrit
Abstract
This paper aims to supplement David Pingree's Census of the Exact Sciences in Sanskrit by adding an entry for ”Śrīdhara,” providing detailed information on the extant manuscripts of Śrīdhara's works, based on manuscript surveys conducted in India in 2024. The investigation further seeks to elucidate aspects such as the distribution, dates, scripts, commentaries, and marginal notes associated with these manuscripts. While the primary focus is on Śrīdhara's Triśatī (”[A Book] of Three Hundred [Verses]”), some attention is also given to other treatises attributed to the same author.

Manuscripts Conservation and Digitisation
Mythic Society’s project to conserve and digitise palm leaf manuscripts at the ORI repository using scientific methods.
Conservation and Digitization of Palm Leaf Manuscripts at Oriental Research Institute in Mysore University.
Mythic Society has embarked upon as ambitious project to the conservation of Palm Leaf Manuscripts in the repository of the Oriental Research Institute (ORI), established in the year 1885.
The repository has over 12,000 manuscripts, with over 1.2 million folios about the Indian Knowledge System.
Some of the manuscript’s dates back 1000 years.
Manuscripts are being scientifically conserved as per prescribed norms of conservation.
The process of digitation of the manuscripts is also being done.
Some of the rare manuscript’s collections in the repository
Arthasastra of Kautilya (Treatise on Polity and Economics).
Lilavathi by Bhaskaracharya (Mathematical treaties).
Surya-Siddhanta - anonymous (Astronomy).
Kashyapa-samhita by Kashyapa (Treatment for bite of Poisonous reptiles).
Graha-ganita-bhaskara by Tammayya (Jyotisha/ Astronomy, Astrology).
Bija-ganita by Bhaskara (treaties Algebra).

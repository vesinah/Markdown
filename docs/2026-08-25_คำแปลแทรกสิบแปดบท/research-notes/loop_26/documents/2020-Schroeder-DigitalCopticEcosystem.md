## Content notes (verified from full text)

1. Coptic Scriptorium is a DH project bringing Coptic resources online in uniform, machine-readable, open formats; led by Schroeder and Zeldes; focus on classical Sahidic.
2. Toolchain described in the paper: collaborative annotation ("virtual departments"), ABBA/ALICE transcription workflow under GitHub revision control; ANNIS as the multilayer treebank/search database; NLP pipeline for tokenizing, segmenting, normalizing, part-of-speech tagging Coptic (Racine tools); WebAnno-based named entity and coreference annotation; automatic entity annotation to scale up person/place annotations.
3. Outputs: annotated corpora aligned with translations (bitexts), a part-of-speech tagged corpus with syntax trees, entity annotations, all openly licensed.
4. Rationale stated by authors: Coptic grammar is fusional with incorporational morphology; Greek influence complicates analysis; manuscripts are fragmented and dispersed due to colonial-era dispersal; digital standards historically idiosyncratic.
5. Related outputs cited in the paper's bibliography: Feder, Kupreyev, Manning, Schroeder, Zeldes, "A Linked Coptic Dictionary Online" (SIGHUM 2018, aclweb.org/anthology/W18-4502) - developed with German partners in the KELLIA project; Zeldes & Schroeder, "Computational Methods for Coptic" (DSH 30, 2015, DOI 10.1093/llc/fqv043).
6. Relevance: Coptic Scriptorium provides the modern analogue of interlinear presentation - Coptic text browsed with aligned English translations - and its ANNIS layers are the digital continuation of glossing practice.

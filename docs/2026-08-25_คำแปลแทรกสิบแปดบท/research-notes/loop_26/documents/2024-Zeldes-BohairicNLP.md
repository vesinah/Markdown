## Content notes (verified from full text)

1. Reports a decade of Coptic Scriptorium work on Sahidic and new extension to Bohairic: guidelines, gold-standard annotated core corpus, and NLP tools trained on it.
2. Deliverable announced: first fully segmented, POS-tagged, dependency-parsed treebank of Bohairic Coptic, to be released through Universal Dependencies.
3. Linguistic contrasts documented as NLP challenges: Bohairic distinguishes hore /h/ from khei /x'/ (Sahidic has only hore); aspirated allophones before sonorants in Bohairic (phnouti vs pnoute "the god"; ethbe vs etbe "on account of"); identical spelling of interrogative and negative particle an in Bohairic vs distinct ene/an in Sahidic; Bohairic-only lemmata needed.
4. Hyper-lemmatization groups related words across dialects under common identifiers to link to the multi-dialect Coptic Dictionary Online (Feder et al. 2018).
5. Significance for the interlinear-gloss book: documents the modern digital continuation of glossing - aligned translations, morphological glossing layers, dialect-linking lemmas; also confirms Hany Takla's participation in Bohairic digitization (relevant to Coptic liturgical text transmission).
6. Bibliography of the paper confirms key references: Schroeder & Zeldes 2020 JDMDH DOI 10.46298/jdmdh.5969; Zeldes & Schroeder 2015 DSH DOI 10.1093/llc/fqv043; Feder et al. 2018 ACL W18-4502.

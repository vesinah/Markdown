## Content notes (verified from the fetched page)

1. The paper is based on Trismegistos (TM) data: about 50,000 documentary texts from Egypt, all languages and writing supports, dated between 3rd c. BC and 3rd c. AD; only texts dated within a maximum span of 200 years are counted.
2. Bilingual Greek-Demotic documents constitute about 5 percent of the total: 2,626 texts (count accessed August 2016).
3. Chronological table published on the page (absolute figures):
   - BC3: Greek 5397, Demotic 1728, Bilingual 362
   - BC2: Greek 4819, Demotic 2506, Bilingual 318
   - BC1: Greek 2602, Demotic 1392, Bilingual 160
   - AD1: Greek 6467, Demotic 1244, Bilingual 258
   - AD2: Greek 19698, Demotic 469, Bilingual 603
   - AD3: Greek 12598, Demotic 177, Bilingual 744
4. Interpretation: bilingual documents never exceed roughly 5 percent; their share rises in the third century AD because hundreds of bilingual mummy labels survive from the late second and early third century; before disappearing almost completely during the third century AD, Demotic is increasingly accompanied by Greek.
5. Greek predominates overall partly because a larger percentage of Greek texts has been published and included in TM; a slump of Greek documentation occurs in the first century BC.

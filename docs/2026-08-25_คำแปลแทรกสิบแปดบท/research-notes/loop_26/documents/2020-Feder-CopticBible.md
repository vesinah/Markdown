## Content notes (verified from full text)

1. Since 2015 a long-term academy project at the Goettingen Academy of Sciences and Humanities works on "The Complete Digital Edition and Translation of the Coptic-Sahidic Old Testament"; project website is the Goettingen Virtual Manuscript Room http://coptot.manuscriptroom.com.
2. The Institut fuer Neutestamentliche Textforschung (INTF, Muenster) runs the Schmitz-Mink-Richter (SMR) database of Coptic New Testament manuscripts (online since 2010) and the Virtual Manuscript Room (VMR) with an XML-based Online Transcription Editor.
3. In January 2016 INTF and the Goettingen project agreed that coptot.manuscriptroom.com is the common repository for Coptic biblical manuscripts of both Testaments; ntvmr.uni-muenster.de displays only NT manuscripts.
4. A new unified List of Coptic Biblical Manuscripts (LCBM) numbering was developed by Siegfried Richter and Frank Feder: sa 1 - sa 1999 reserved for NT and liturgical/miscellany manuscripts, OT numbers start at sa 2000; dialect ranges bo (Bohairic), fa (Fayyumic), mae (Mesokemic), ac (Akhmimic), ly (Lyco-Diospolitan), with subdialect sigla pbo (Palaeo-Bohairic), v, w (subdialects attested by pMich 3520, 3521, 6868a).
5. The Goettingen archive absorbed the image/material archives of the Koptische Septuaginta project (Halle-Wittenberg 1994-2000), of Karlheinz Schuessler's catalogue Biblia Coptica, and of Tito Orlandi's CMCL (Corpus dei Manoscritti Copti Letterari, cmcl.it). Together INTF + Goettingen form the largest archive of reproductions of Coptic biblical and literary manuscripts worldwide.
6. Example of manuscript consolidation: Biblia Coptica sigla sa 12 and sa 139 turned out to belong to one manuscript containing Leviticus and Numbers, now numbered sa 2047; this discovery is credited to Mother Antonia St. Demiana, who prepares an edition of the Sahidic Leviticus (the same edition contains her chapter "Explanatory Glosses in Sahidic Leviticus", Brill 2024, DOI 10.1163/9789004545892_019).
7. Workflow: digital diplomatic edition = manuscript surrogate (image) plus Unicode XML transcription plus metadata; a collation engine creates a preliminary edition text that editors revise.
8. Relevance to interlinear-gloss topic: the infrastructure documents dispersed White Monastery manuscript fragments and supports reconstruction of bitextual transmission contexts (Greek Vorlage vs Coptic version), but the article does not itself treat glosses.

## Content notes (from abstract and verified apparatus)

- Primary source: Luận ngữ ước giải 論語約解 ("Concise Interpretation of the Analects", LNUG), PRINTED IN 1839, held by the Institute of Sino-Nôm Studies (Hán-Nôm institute), call number AB.270/3. It is a Literary Sinitic (LS)/Nôm BILINGUAL text: the LS original is presented passage by passage in full-size characters, followed by a vernacular Vietnamese translation in Chữ Nôm ("Nôm translation"), with LS commentary added INTRA-LINEARLY in gezhu 割注 double-column annotation format.
- LNUG belongs to the genre giải âm 解音 (lit. "sound interpretation"), "a type of LS/Nôm bilingual text" - the Vietnamese analogue space to furigana/gloss traditions.
- Findings: three types of NAME MARKS (proper-noun marks) are used in the LS portion; marks added to personal names show differential treatment that CORRESPONDS WITH THE USE OF CLASSIFIERS in the Nôm translation - i.e., mark placement is calibrated to vernacular grammar. Comparison across other Vietnamese commentaries shows commonalities in name-mark practice; comparison with name marks in JAPANESE-glossed LS texts reveals MAJOR DIFFERENCES between the two cultures' glossing practice.
- Comparison sources named: Luận ngữ tiết yếu 論語節要 ("Digest of the Analects"), vol. 3, printed 1850, Vietnam National University Hanoi collection; Tự Đức thánh chế Luận ngữ thích nghĩa ca 嗣德聖製論語釈義歌 (Emperor Tu Duc, r. 1847-1883) - another LS/Nôm bilingual Analects, excluded from analysis because verse form with Hue dialect elements.

## Honest negative / unverified leads
- No evidence found this loop for tone-corner marks (shōten-style) or inversion marks (kaeriten-style) as an established system in Vietnamese manuscripts. One lead seen only in a Wikipedia image caption (excluded as source per project rules): the manuscript Sơ học vấn tân 初學問津 reportedly uses reversal marks including a check-mark similar to the Japanese reten レ point while its printed edition pre-switches word order - flagged here as UNVERIFIED lead (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน); the place to chase it is Nguyen 2021/2023 full texts.

## Vietnam entries (gap 4)

- Vol. 133 (2014): 小助川貞次「ベトナムの加点資料について」[On Vietnamese glossed materials] - foundational Japanese study opening Vietnamese glossed materials as kunten-adjacent evidence; same issue carries the lecture record 「ベトナムの漢文訓読について」[On kanbun vernacular reading in Vietnam] by グエン・テイ・オワイン.
- Vol. 145 (2020): グエン・ティー・トゥー・フエン「ベトナム加点資料の句読点から見た訓読の可能性」[Possibility of vernacular reading seen from punctuation in Vietnamese glossed texts].
- Vol. 151 (2023): same author, 「ベトナム語による漢文読解について―漢喃研究院蔵『論語約解』衛霊公篇を中心に―」[Reading LS texts through Vietnamese: the Weilinggong chapter of Rongo yakukai 論語約解 held by the Institute of Hán-Nôm].

## Kakuhitsu entries (gap 2)

- Vol. 112 (2004): 原卓志「近世地方寺院における僧侶の修学と角筆使用」[Monk education and stylus use at early-modern provincial temples] - direct evidence that stylus glossing continued into the early modern period outside the great manuscript repositories.

## Print culture / early-modern entries (gap 3)

- Vol. 110 (2003): 白井純「『落葉集』と活字印刷」[Rakuyoshu and movable-type printing].
- Vol. 112 (2003): 鈴木功眞「古活字版倭玉篇諸本の系譜に就いて」[Genealogy of old movable-type Wakuhen copies].
- Vol. 125 (2010): 鈴木功眞「寛永八年版和刻本大広益会玉篇の和訓―『夢梅本』および『寛永五年版倭玉篇』との関係に就いて―」[Wakun glosses in the Kan'ei 8 (1631) printed Daikōekikai Gohen: relations with the Yumebai copy and the Kan'ei 5 Wakuhen] - shows commercial Edo prints carrying wakun (kana reading glosses), not full point apparatuses.
- Vol. 149 (2022): 中野直樹「琉球における漢文訓読の実態―琉球版『論語集註』による―」[The actual practice of kanbun kundoku in Ryukyu: from the Ryukyu-printed Rongo shusho] - peripheral printing tradition with vernacular-reading evidence.
- Vol. 156 (2026): small special collection on Kirishitan materials incl. interlinear insertion lines in Kirishitan-ban and Valignano manuscripts (黒川茉莉).

## Tone-mark and accent-history entries (gap 1 extension)

- Vol. 124 (2010): 沼本克明「仮名の位置による四声表示法の源流について―陀羅尼訓点の一形態―」.
- Vol. 129 (2012): 佐々木勇「親鸞使用の声点加点形式について―坂東本『教行信証』声点の位置づけ―」.
- Vol. 115 (2005): 佐々木勇「古辞書における反切・同字音注への声点加点について」; vol. 114 (2004): 原 裕 resource note on erased vermilion shōten (Mahamayuri ms., Univ. of Tokyo).
- Vol. 151 (2023): 加藤大鶴 on departing-tone characters in Kamakura-period annotated Wakan roeishu copies vs a cross-source kanji-sound database.

## School-history entries (gap 5 context)

- Sustained series by 宇都宮啓吾 on individual schools: Nishihaka-ten origins/multi-layering (vol. 150, 2023); Eizan-ten formation (vol. 140, 2021); Hōdōin-ten formation via Genshin/Jakushō/En'in/Kōkei (vol. 123, 2009); Otsuten-zu origins and kanbun reading (vol. 130, 2010); Kita'in-ten propagation at Kofukuji (vol. 154, 2025); Ninnaji wokototen history (vol. 153, 2024); kunten tools/implements (vol. 126, 2011).
- Korea-Japan comparison continues: 張景俊 on signs in seokdok gugyeol and kunten materials (vol. 131, 2013) and Korean Yogacarabhumi jeomto seokdok gugyeol (vol. 143, 2019); 尹幸舜 on Korean jeomto vs Japanese wokototen in kanbun reading (vol. 127, 2011).
- Dunhuang connection: 小助川貞次「敦煌本漢籍における加点の問題について」(vol. 143, 2019); 「敦煌本春秋経伝集解（S.85）について」(vol. 142, 2019).
- Journal facts: founded publication 1954 (Bensei publishing page); vol. 156 dated 31 March 2026 (Reiwa 8); society's 70th anniversary in vol. 154 with congratulatory address by Kosukegawa Teiji.

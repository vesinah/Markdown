## Critical scope correction for the book

This paper is NOT about nissaya as an interlinear translation/glossing genre. It concerns nissaya in the Vinaya sense: the five-year dependence of a newly ordained bhikkhu (สัทธิวิหาริก) on his preceptor (อุปัชฌาย์), the duties of upajjhaya-vatta and saddhiviharika-vatta. Its reference list is entirely Vinaya/preceptor literature (Phra Dhammapitaka, Sangha administration theses).

Consequences for the draft:

1. Do not cite it for kham phi/nissaya-glossing continuity claims (B11 Gap 1). It cannot support any claim that modern Pali translation training derives from the nissaya genre.
2. It IS usable in the terminology section: Thai monastic usage keeps two distinct senses of นิสสัย/นิสสยะ live today — (a) Vinaya dependence, documented by this 2017 article; (b) word-for-word Pali rendering tradition, documented by Herngseng 2023, McDaniel 2002/2008 and the Lao vohara corpus. A short terminological disambiguation paragraph would be well sourced.
3. The machine-translated English title circulating in S2 should never be quoted as the official title; cite the Thai original above.

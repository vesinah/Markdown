# Harvest: NGMCP catalogue witnesses - commentary and annotation arrangement

All entries fetched this loop from the static web-archive of the former NGMCP MediaWiki
(http://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/ngmcp/ - the live wiki was retired; only edited pages kept,
see the companion access-status document). Entries document HOW commentarial text travels with root
texts on Nepalese pothis.

## 1. Root text + ṭīkā copied continuously in ONE pothi ("saṭīka")

Entry "A 1078-2 Sūryaśataka with ṭīkā" (= Mayūracitra, NAK 3/689):
- Title as catalogued: "Sūryaśataka saṭīka"; Remarks: "the Sūryaśataka together with the Ṭīkā of
  Vibhākara"; author of root text Mayūra Bhaṭṭa.
- Palm-leaf, Newari script, complete but damaged, 24.3 x 5.3 cm, 70 folios, 7 lines per folio,
  one binding hole in centre, foliation figures right margin of verso.
- Scribe: Jayakīrttirāja; dated NS 665 āśvinaśukla 1, somavāra (= 1545 CE).
- Two folios of table of contents at the beginning.
- Colophon verbatim: "iti śrīsūryaśatakaṃ saṭīkaṃ samāptam iti || ... daivajñajayakīrttirājena likhitam iti ||"
- Interpretation: root text and commentary interwoven by one scribe in one continuous pothi production.

## 2. Commentary-only pothi

Entry "A 155-13 Tripurāsāraṭīkā on the Tripurāsārasamuccaya" (NAK 5/514):
- Govindācārya's Tripurāsāraṭīkā ("saṃpradādadīpikā vyākhyāyate"), a commentary-only manuscript;
  paper, Devanagari, incomplete, 23.5 x 10 cm, 47 folios, 8-11 lines per folio; foliation under the
  abbreviation "tri. sā." upper left and under the word rāma lower right on versos; filmed 11-10-1971;
  categories include "Digitized manuscripts".

## 3. Root + commentary bundled in same reel ("sahita")

Entry "A 322-3(1) (Amarakoṣavyākhyāsudhāsahita)" (NAK 4/993):
- Authors Amarasiṃha and Bhānujidākṣita; paper, Devanagari, incomplete fragment (1 folio), 35.5 x 9.5 cm,
  5-6 lines per folio; title form shows Amarakośa transmitted WITH its vyākhyāsudhā.
(Compare also multi-item reel A 1111-19: Śivamahimnastotra + Śivamahimnastotravyākhyā +
Sundarīmahimnastotra + Sundarīmahimnastotraṭīkā + Śivamānasapūjā, VS 1917.)

## 4. Ayurvedic witnesses (Gap 2)

### 4.1 Suśrutasaṃhitā with a documented INTERLINEAR insertion - B 29-19 (NAK 5/333)
- Catalogued title: "Suśrutasaṃhitā + Sauśrutanighaṇṭu" (medical composite).
- Palm-leaf, Newari script, COMPLETE, 34.0 x 5.0 cm, 435 folios, 6 lines per page; scribe Amarasiṃhaka,
  son of Kamaladatta; dated Sunday, 29 July 1465.
- Foliation system (verbatim structure): figure numerals in the RIGHT-hand margin giving the folio number
  of the whole MS; LETTER numerals in the left-hand margin restarting at every section (sthāna), from
  Kalpasthāna fol. 21 prefixed kalpa-, later uttara-; the nighaṇṭu portion foliated afresh from 1 in both margins.
- Manuscript features: some folios microfilmed twice; among the Uttaratantra folios a single-folio
  alchemical text Gandhakakalpa attributed to Nāgārjuna, transcribed in the entry.
- KEY ANNOTATION EVIDENCE: the entry's critical transcription encodes a scribal insertion -
  "[[bhavet]]" with the catalogue note "| bhavet | is added UNDER | bhavati | and the latter is not
  deleted" (the note is wrapped in the wiki's ref markup in the original) - i.e. an interlinear
  correction written beneath the line-word in this dated medical pothi, with the original reading
  left undeleted. Direct documentary proof of interlinear correction practice in Nepalese Ayurvedic
  manuscripts.

### 4.2 Carakasaṃhitā (jvaracikitsita) - A 213-10 (NAK 2/189)
- Devanagari, paper, 36 x 16 cm, 26 folios, 9 lines per folio; state: incomplete, marginal damage;
  foliation figures upper-right and lower-left margin of verso; marginal title carak; stamp Candraśaṃśara;
  filmed 19-12-1971, 28 exposures (4th exposure double); catalogued MS/SG, 21-07-2003; category
  "Digitized manuscripts". Content: explanation of the cause of fever and its treatment (jvaracikitsita),
  opening with the Ātreya-Agniveśa dialogue frame.

### 4.3 Suśrutasaṃhitā - A 45-5 (NAK 1-1079)
- Palm-leaf, Newari script, incomplete and damaged, 56 x 5 cm, two binding holes, 98 folios,
  7 lines per folio, foliation figures in both margins of the verso; field "Used for Edition: no/yes".

### 4.4 NGMCP "Manuscript of the Week", 3 June 2009 (official project news page)
- "There is a very old (dated 9th century) manuscript of the Suśrutasaṃhitā [in the NAK]. Since its text
  appears to offer significant variation from printed editions, it is a very important manuscript. Not
  only that, it turns out that the scribe was a Buddhist. He starts the manuscript with 'oṃ namaḥ
  sarvvajñāyānuttaravaidyarājāya.'"

## 5. Prajñāpāramitā witnesses beyond Add.1643 (Gap 3 support)

- B 86-8 Abhisamayālokānyāṣṭāsahasrikāprajñāpāramitāvyākhyā (Haribhadra): NAK 3/242; paper, Devanagari,
  incomplete, 49 x 20 cm, 167 folios, 13 lines per page; scribe Jyotimānada Vajrācārya; dated
  Ś 1816 / VS 1952 / NS 1015 = 1895 CE; marginal abbreviations abhisamayā. (upper left) and laṅkāra
  (lower right) on versos. A Nepalese copy of the Prajñāpāramitā-commentarial apparatus itself.
- A 107-1 Aṣṭasāhasrikā prajñāpāramitā: NAK 3/276; Newari script; 48.0 x 13.0 cm; 311 folios;
  7 lines per folio; scribe Vidyādatta Vajrācārya; dated NS 963 (= 1843 CE); abbreviation prajñāpāra.
  upper-left verso margin; fols 18, 157, 134 missing. Reference BSP 7.1 p. 47 no. 57 (3/276).
- Further reels exist in the title lists (A 35-5/8, A 37-6/8, A 39-9/12, A 107-1ff., B 22-15ff., B 23-11).

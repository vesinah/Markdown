## Resolution for Loop B13 / Phase-A gap

1. The project "In the Margins of the Text: Annotated Manuscripts from Northern India and Nepal"
   EXISTED, at the AAI Hamburg, running within the interdisciplinary research group
   "Manuskriptkulturen in Asien, Afrika und Europa", 2008-2011. Formigatti's dissertation states the
   project was DFG-sponsored (see companion document).
2. The group title is IDENTICAL to the later Hamburg SFB 950 ("Manuskriptkulturen in Asien, Afrika und
   Europa", funded 2011/2012-2020). The 2008-2011 entity documented here is the pre-SFB research group
   phase hosted at the same institute with a partly overlapping disciplinary spread.
3. NO grant number appears anywhere on the page. The Phase-A label "DFG FOR 963" remains WITHOUT any
   documentary basis in accessible sources; GEPRIS could not be queried (JavaScript-only application;
   no Wayback captures of relevant GEPRIS search pages exist). Honest status: the correct citation form
   is "research group Manuskriptkulturen in Asien, Afrika und Europa, Univ. Hamburg, 2008-2011
   (DFG-sponsored per Formigatti 2015)"; the FOR number "963" should be dropped as unverified

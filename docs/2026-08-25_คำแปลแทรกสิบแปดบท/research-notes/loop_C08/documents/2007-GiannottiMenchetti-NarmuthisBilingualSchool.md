# Narmouthis (Medinet Madi) "House of the Ostraca": a documented Greek-Demotic bilingual school-exercise corpus with proper designations

Loop: C08. Date: 2026-08-26. Closes the loop_B18 residual on finding ONE well-documented bilingual Greek-Demotic school text beyond the disproven P.Bingen/PUG II 53 candidate.

## Primary harvest source (fetched in full this session)

Katelijn Vandorpe and Herbert Verreth, "Temple of Narmouthis: House of the ostraca", Trismegistos Archives, ArchID 534, Version 1 (2012), https://www.trismegistos.org/arch/archives/pdf/534.pdf (5 pp.; direct download succeeded this session even though the main TM site was CAPTCHA-blocked in earlier loops).

Verbatim archive profile:
- Place: Arsinoites (Fayum), meris of Polemon, Narmouthis. Date: about AD 150-225.
- Languages: "Greek, Demotic and bilingual Greek-Demotic". Material: ostraca.
- Number of texts: "About 1481, only 507 of them published or described". Type: temple archive. Collections: Cairo, Egyptian Museum (part formerly Kom Aushim/Karanis Museum).
- Find: Italian excavations 1938 (and 2006) at Medinet Madi; room III yielded c. 1300 ostraca, partly kept in two large vessels (Vogliano 1938); after joins 1471 pieces.
- Language counts of published texts (verbatim): "Most of the published texts are written in Demotic (77), Greek (186) or in a remarkable bilingual mixture of Demotic and Greek (233)."
- School context (verbatim): "Some ostraca seem to have been gathered by a priest responsible for school education and for the instruction of new temple personnel ... It is possible that Phatres was also the keeper of the school ostraca (MESSERI / PINTAUDI 2002, p. 210 & n. 4)."
- Script mixing (verbatim): "In some cases a kind of cryptography is involved where Greek words are written in Demotic with every letter rendered by its numerical value (e.g. EVO 32 (2009), p. 43-44 no. 2, l. 2 & 9: p3 1 60 10 800 40 1 = p3 axioma, 'the decree') (MENCHETTI, EVO 28 (2005), p. 237-243)."
- Lexical trilingual material: "A few ostraca contain Hieratic words (with Hieroglyphic signs) sometimes with Old Coptic transcriptions (O. Narm. Dem. I 17; II 34-41, 43)."

## Why this satisfies the B18 residual

Unlike PUG II 53 (Greek-only alphabetical syllable list), the Narmuthis corpus contains explicitly bilingual Greek-Demotic school/list ostraca with standard sigla (OMM Cairo inventory numbers; ODN/OGN edition numbers) and a peer-reviewed publication apparatus spanning ZPE, CdE and EVO 22-33 plus four Pisa volumes. For any draft needing ONE named item, cite: an ODN-numbered exercise from Giannotti/Gorini EVO 29 (2006) (e.g., ODN 208-216 dossier) or one of the 12 bilingual librarian-instruction ostraca in Giannotti EVO 30 (2007).

## Adjacent verified leads (context)

- Clarysse, "Egyptian Scribes Writing Greek", CdE 68 (1993) 186-201, DOI 10.1484/j.cde.2.308932 - existence verified via Brepols record; establishes Egyptian scribes writing Greek (brush vs pen habit), cited by later scholarship as "already Clarysse 1993".
- Prada, "Egyptian Education in Hellenistic and Roman Egypt: A Take from the Fayum - School Textbook and P.Schuluebung Revisited" (in Le Fayoum colloquium vol.), BMCR 2020.02.19 summary: P.Schuluebung = Demotic school text on the recto of P.Berlin P 13639, reinterpreted as Fayum textbook, with new join P.Michigan Dem. 6445a - Demotic-only, useful as control, not bilingual.
- Dosoo/Nodar Dominguez/Sarischouli, "Bilingual Scribes in Magical Formularies: The Cases of PGM/PDM XII (= GEMF 15) and XIV (= GEMF 16)", Tyche 2024, DOI 10.1080/00397679.2025.2454757 - abstract documents a Demotic-Greek bilingual scribe integrating short Greek sections "either in-line or as glosses"; adjacent evidence for mixed-script glossing practice, magical register.

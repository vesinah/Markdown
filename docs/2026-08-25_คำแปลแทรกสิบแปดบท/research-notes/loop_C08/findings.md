Verbatim scale statement (Introduction p.2): "The present corpus consists of marginal and interlinear notes from 293 ancient manuscripts found certainly or possibly in Egypt and dating from the third century B.C.E. to the seventh century C.E." Breakdown: 201 rolls (+parts), 69 papyrus codices, 19 parchment/vellum codices, four wooden tablets; equals "roughly 5% of the 5,431 classical literary papyri listed in LDAB as of August 2005". Bonus quantifications harvested: c.75 supralinear/sublinear notes survive corpus-wide ("About half the seventy-five notes written above or below the main text are three or more lines in length"); long notes (>8 words) in only about forty rolls; ~100 texts annotated by text hand; multiple annotators in ~35 books; lemma-formatted marginalia in ~30 papyri; Oxyrhynchus has produced about 160 annotated texts.

# Document — St Gall Priscian Glosses official counts re-verified (resolves site-internal 3,000-vs-4,000 symbol variation)

Fetched: 2026-08-26, http://www.stgallpriscian.ie/ (homepage, full text).

## Verified facts (site v2.1, accessed 2026-08-26)

- Version footer: "Rijcklof Hofman, Padraic Moran, Bernhard Bauer, St Gall Priscian Glosses, version 2.1 (2023)".
- Manuscript dating: "a copy of Priscian written by Irish scribes in AD 850–1, probably in Ireland. It was brought to the Continent between 855 and 863, reaching St Gall sometime after 888."
- Counts (homepage wording): "a commentary of over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses, as well as c. 3,000 symbol glosses. About one-third of the verbal glosses are written in Old Irish."
- Structure note: "The manuscript breaks off in book 17. Book 18 is missing."
- Credits: text transcribed by Rijcklof Hofman in the early 1990s; digital edition created by Padraic Moran in 2010; Bernhard Bauer supplied Old Irish linguistic information 2017.
- Browse interface exposes an internal 59-category gloss typology (prosody, lexical, morphology, syntax-with-symbols, explanatory, etymological, encyclopedic, variant-reading glosses etc.).

## Fix applied

Loop B16 documented a site-internal variation (homepage "c. 3,000" vs /glosses page "c. 4,000" symbol/construe marks). As of this fetch the homepage states c. 3,000. Recommended print formulation stands: "over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses plus roughly 3,000-4,000 symbol/construe marks, about one-third Old Irish," citing v2.1 (2023). The AD 850-1 dating and the book-17 break-off are reconfirmed verbatim for the master table.

# Document — Qara Khitai era name Tianxi 天禧: range fixed 1178-1211 (sharpens Ch 3716 colophon date caveat)

Fetched: 2026-08-26. Sources:

1. Encyclopaedia Iranica, "QARĀ ḴEṬĀY", https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/qara-ketay/ (search-rendered excerpt, page dated 2024-10-20): "The last Qarā Ḵeṭāy Gurkhan, Yelü Zhilugu (r. 1178-1211), was confronted by dual threats..." — reign range 1178-1211 confirmed.
2. Chinese Text Project datawiki entry 天禧 Tianxi (Liao Shi-based), https://ctext.org/datawiki.pl?if=en&res=689921 : "It was an era name used during the reign of Emperor Yelü Zhilugu of the Western Liao. This era name is the last one of the Western Liao... used this era name until 1211." Reconstruction history quoted there: Qian Daxin argued the change to Tianxi fell in wuzi (1168); Wang Yuansun argued wuxu (1178) to xinwei (1211), 34 years; modern scholar Ji Anzong supports 1178 as the first year.

## Application to the Old Uighur Qianziwen colophon (Berlin Ch 3716 = T II Y 62)

Colophon wording (Yakup PIAC 2022 abstract, verified loop C04): written by the Uyghur Šingsön Tutong of Yarkhoto "in the 13th year of Tianxi 天禧, year Xinwei 辛未."

- With Tianxi year 1 = 1178 (Wang Yuansun/Ji Anzong), ordinal year 13 = 1190 CE (gengxu 庚戌).
- The cyclical sign xinwei 辛未 corresponds to 1211 CE, which is the FINAL year of Tianxi (ordinal 34), not year 13.
- The two internal signals therefore disagree under any single reckoning; Qian Daxin's 1168 start would give year 13 = 1180 (gengzi), still not xinwei.

## Fix applied / status

The era RANGE is now citable (Tianxi = final Western Liao era, 1178-1211; Iranica + Liao Shi reconstructions). The CE conversion of the colophon remains genuinely ambiguous between c. 1190 (ordinal count) and 1211 (cyclical sign); resolution requires Yakup 2023 full text (Acta Orientalia Hung. 76.4, paywalled). Print guidance: date as "Tianxi 13 (Qara Khitai era running 1178-1211; either c. 1190 or 1211 depending on whether the ordinal or the cyclical sign controls)" and keep "(ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน)" on a single CE year.

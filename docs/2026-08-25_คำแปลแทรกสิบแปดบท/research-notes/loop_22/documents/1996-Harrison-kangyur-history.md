## Content notes (from fetched material)

- Harrison specializes in Buddhist literature and history, especially Mahayana, and in Sanskrit, Chinese and Tibetan manuscripts; author of The Samadhi of Direct Encounter with the Buddhas of the Present (1990); co-editor of "Buddhist Manuscripts in the Schøyen Collection"; with Cristina Scherrer-Schaub published Tabo Studies III: A Catalogue of the Manuscript Collection of Tabo Monastery vol. 1 (2009) — directly linking him to Tabo manuscript studies.
- From the chapter: firm evidence that Bu ston worked on substantial portions of the bKa' 'gyur, with editorial work continued by his successors at Zha lu and rGyal rtse; a hypothetical *Zha lu ma Kangyur of the first half of the 14th century may have been closer to the Old sNar thang than its Tshal pa counterpart in organization. Both Tshal pa and Zha lu editions may rest on the same raw materials, but different decisions on deleting duplicates account for much that followed.
- Harrison 1992c "Meritorious Activity or Waste of Time? Some Remarks on the Editing of Texts in the Tibetan Kanjur" (Narita IATS proceedings, pp. 77-93); Harrison 1994 "In Search of the Source of the Tibetan Kanjur".
- The Phug brag manuscript Kangyur is independent but preserves the same recension as Them spangs ma line Kangyurs (London, sTog, Tokyo) for the Druma-kinnara-raja-pariprccha; Jeffrey Schoening's Salistamba-sutra work suggests Phug brag is closer to the Tshal pa line.
- sNar thang blockprint bsTan 'gyur completed in 225 volumes in 1742 under bSod nams stobs rgyal.
- Related canon scholarship verified in fetched bibliographies: Peter Skilling 1997 "From bKa' bstan bcos to bKa' 'gyur and bsTan 'gyur" (in Eimer ed., Transmission of the Tibetan Canon, OAW Wien, 87-111); Skilling "Kanjur Titles and Colophons" cited as Tibetan Studies vol. 2, Oslo 1994, pp. 768-780 (citation found in https://dakinitranslations.com/2020/10/24/the-kangyur-and-the-karmapas-important-role-in-their-publication-and-preservation/); Hackett, Catalogue of the Comparative Kangyur (2012); RKTS Resources for Kanjur and Tanjur Studies (www.istb.univie.ac.at/kanjur).
- Kangyur recension lines: Tshal pa line (Tsal Gungtang, 1347-1351, myriarch Tshal pa Kun dga' rdo rje) vs Them spangs ma line; assumption that both stem from Old Narthang abandoned; first woodblock Kangyur = Jang/Litang (1615, catalogue by 6th Zhwa dmar Chos kyi dbang phyug, TBRC W1CZ881); Derge Kangyur (1729-1733) based mostly on Litang.

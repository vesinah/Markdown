## Why this document matters (Loop C02 Target 1)

This is a published transcription of actual Nanatsudera-issaikyo colophons recording PROOFREADING completion with named individuals - exactly the collator-colophon evidence Loop B02 lacked.

## Transcribed colophons (okugaki 奥書)

Nanatsudera copy of the Jingtu lun 浄土論 (attributed inner title: 浄土論巻上　帝京弘法寺釈迦才撰), 3 scrolls, handscrolls, 16-20 chars per column, copyist unnamed (筆師未詳):

- juan 1 (巻上) okugaki: 「一校了　栄俊」 ("one round of collation completed - Eijun")
- juan 2 (巻中) okugaki: 「一校了　栄俊」
- juan 3 (巻下) okugaki: 「一校了　永俊」

Reading: the standard late-Heian canon-copying proof formula was 「一校了」 + collator's personal name. Two different named collators appear across the set (Eijun 栄俊 on juan 1-2, Eishun 永俊 on juan 3). This documents distributed collation labor among Owari-region monks rather than a single reviser.

## Further codicological facts

- The three scrolls are in ONE hand (一筆) though stored in different chests; middle scroll also has a later folded-book (折本) duplicate.
- ca. 5,000 scrolls of the canon were copied in only five years, concentrated in the first three years; consequently clear miscopyings exist, yet the copyist showed excellent command of Chinese characters (漢字に対する知識はすぐれたものがあった).
- Date: late Heian (平安時代末, 1175-1180), matching Hubbard 1991.
- Descent from Nara-period transcription-line recensions; oldest known complete manuscript of the Jingtu lun in the world; collation/critical edition ongoing by the Nanatsudera koitsu kyoten kenkyukai (七寺古逸経典研究会).

## Honest limits

The colophons here name proofreaders but do NOT carry formal office-titles (校経者/点経大德 etc.). For fuller role-rosters the primary series 七寺古逸經典研究叢書 remains necessary.

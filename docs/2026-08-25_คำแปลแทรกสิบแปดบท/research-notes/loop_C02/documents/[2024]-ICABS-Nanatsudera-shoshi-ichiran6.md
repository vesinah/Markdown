## What this source is

An annual per-item census of one box of the Nanatsudera issaikyo: for each scroll it gives 貞元番号 (order per Zhenyuan lu), old/new chest numbers, transcribed 外題/内題/首題/尾題/譯者號/奥書, ruled-line and format data, seals, condition. Transcription conventions are stated explicitly: [ ] for missing characters restored by inference from残画, □…□ for illegible passages, ／ for line breaks in okugaki; separate-hand okugaki added through collation (校合等による別筆の奥書) are flagged. This is a continuing published TRANSCRIPTION series for canon colophons.

## Key facts on the copying project (from this report's summary section)

- The whole set was written between 1175 (承安五年) and 1180 (治承四年), i.e. about five and a half years, AS STATED IN ITS OWN OKUGAKI (その書写、校合...の五年半の間に書写されたことが奥書から知られ).
- Copying was carried out by monks from various places across Owari province (尾張地方各地の僧侶によって行われた); some scrolls bear transcribed printing-colophons (刊記) of the Kaibao canon 開宝蔵 (Northern Song imperially sponsored canon) inside their own okugaki - evidence that Song printed exemplars stood behind the project.
- Okugaki also record that texts which could not be copied locally were copied at Kiyomizu-dera from the golden-letter sutras of Hossho-ji (清水寺で法勝寺の金字経を書写したことも奥書に記されている).
- ca. 30% of the scrolls have been rebound as folded books (折本) since 1696 (元禄九年改装 recorded in the mokuroku); repairs 1717, 1887.
- Chests: originally catalogued as 27 chests, now 26 at the temple; single chests deposited at Kyoto National Museum, Nara National Museum, Tokyo National Museum, Osaka City Museum of Art, Nagoya City Museum.
- Surveys by the institute have run regularly since 2005; this is the sixth report in the current series.

## Relevance for interlinear-glossing / proofreading-culture chapters

Documents that late-Heian provincial canon-copying embedded named proofreading statements (一校了-type) and provenance notes inside colophons, and that the modern transcription apparatus exists openly. Complements Sakagami 1993 (loop_C02 documents) and Hubbard 1991 (loop_B02 documents).

## Key statements (verbatim from indexed OA text)

1. On the Mohe zhiguan as reportatio + redaction:
   - The work "is the result of a series of lectures given by Zhiyi" and "the text of the Mohe zhiguan proper is the edited result of a series of lectures by Zhiyi recorded at the Yuquansi over the course of the summer retreat of 594, just three years prior to the death of Zhiyi."
   - REDACTION HISTORY: "Beginning shortly before Zhiyi's death in 597, and on two separate occasions thereafter, Guanding edited these lecture notes and reworked his introduction, the result being three editions of the text, of which only the last survives."
   - Guanding's surviving introduction opens with (a) location/date of the 594 lectures plus a lineage statement, (b) summation of the three kinds of calming-and-contemplation, (c) scriptural verification section.
2. Parallel pipeline for the second sandaibu member: the Shi chan boluomi cidi famen (Cidi chanmen) "derives from a set of lectures delivered in 571, early in Zhiyi's career, taken down by Fashen, and later edited by Guanding."
3. Method framing: Penkower situates Guanding's lineage statement inside "the construction process of the Mohe zhiguan," i.e., treats the text as an artifact of post-lecture editorial stages rather than a stenographic transcript.

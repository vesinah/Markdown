- DOI: 10.15017/4782113 | OA PDF: https://catalog.lib.kyushu-u.ac.jp/opac_download_md/4782113/130-131_p454.pdf (Kyushu University repository; downloaded and text-extracted 2026-08-26, 15 pp.)

## Core facts (Loop C02 Target 2: authorship of the Huayan sound-gloss private notes)

1. The work: 『新訳華厳経音義私記』 (Shin'yaku Kegonkyo ongi shiki), copied late Nara (奈良朝末期書写), is the OLDEST extant manuscript of a Buddhist ongi composed by a Japanese monk; sole witness = Ogawa-bon 小川家藏本 (National Treasure 1931).
2. AUTHOR IS UNKNOWN - no attribution survives anywhere; dictionary-level confirmation: Britannica International Encyclopedia concise (via kotobank, fetched 2026-08-26): 「著者未詳。2巻。奈良時代末の成立とみられる」; man'yo-gana wakun preserve jōdai tokushu kanazukai; includes Wu Zetian's zetiantoku graphs.
3. Sources used by the compiler: primarily Huiyuan's Tang 新訳華厳経音義 (慧苑音義) plus a Japan-made 『新華厳経音義』 (大治本新音義, itself author unknown "撰者不詳，當為奈良時期華嚴學僧所著" per Liang Xiaohong/Miao Yu 2014 monograph metadata), further 玄応一切経音義, Gu Yewang's Yu pian, ziyang-type graph books.
4. Kobayashi Yoshinori 1978 hypothesis, quoted verbatim in Jia 2021: 「ともあれ、新譯花嚴經音義私記が、奈良時代の天平勝寶頃かそれより半世紀の間に、華嚴經學の東大寺、又はその系統の寺院で撰述されたと見ることは無理ではないであろう。」 - i.e., composed around Tenpyō-shōhō era or within half a century, at Tōdaiji or temples in its Kegon-studies lineage.
5. Tōdaiji evidence chain (Jia 2021 summarizing Kobayashi 1978):
   - The Ogawa copy's pastedown carries an erased colophon mentioning 馬道手箱; Kobayashi links 馬道 to 石作馬道, who signs (with 安宿廣成, a scribe of the Tōdaiji scriptorium 東大寺寫經所經師) at the end of Tōdaiji-held 華嚴略疏刊定記 juan 13.
   - That same 刊定記 scroll ends: 「延曆二年（七八三）十一月廿三日於東大寺與新羅正本自挍勘畢」 ("collated by me against the Silla correct copy, finished at Tōdaiji, 783/11/23") - a named-practice collation formula contemporary with the Shiki's milieu.
   - Graph forms/kunten conventions match Tōdaiji practice.
6. Jia's own two-stage conclusion: first half of the glossing was most likely performed DURING Huayan sutra lecture courses (contrastive notes old/new translation; wakun type c from annotated texts); after the lecture course ended the compiler added later entries drawing on Chinese dictionaries; rare type-b wakun possibly the compiler's own additions. Result agrees with Kobayashi 1978 and Miho 1973.
7. Lecture context: Tōdaiji Kanshin-dera record (東大寺要録): first Huayan lecture by Shinshō 審祥 from Tenpyō 12/10/8 (740), three-year course over the 60-fascicle translation with three fukushi 複師 (respondents) 慈訓・鏡忍・圓證; Shōmu-era copying of the 80-fascicle translation attested from Tenpyō 11 (739) in Shōsōin documents (高屋連赤麻呂写経請本注文: 新譯花嚴音義二卷); Zennyū-in catalog ca. 753 lists 一切経音義廿四卷（沙門釋玄應撰）and 新譯花嚴音義二卷 - Huiyuan's ongi had reached Nara Japan by mid-8th c.
8. Earlier scholarship: Okada Kikuo 1939 解説: the Ogawa copy itself is a transcript made spring of Enryaku 13 (794) per its erased colophon (天下の孤本).

## Verdict for the book

Authorship: anonymous Nara-period Huayan-studies monk, most plausibly within the Tōdaiji orbit (Kobayashi/Jia); no name recoverable (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน for any personal name). Composition tied to live lecture practice - directly parallels the Guanding reportatio model of loop_B02.

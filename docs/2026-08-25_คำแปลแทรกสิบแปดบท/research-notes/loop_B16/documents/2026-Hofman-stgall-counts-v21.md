# Document: St Gall Priscian gloss counts - official v2.1 figures and reconciliation

Harvested: 2026-08-26 (Loop B16). Purpose: close loop_24 remaining gap 2 (9,400 vs other counts).

## Official numbers, verbatim from stgallpriscian.ie version 2.1

Home page (fetched 25 Aug 2026):
- "The manuscript also contains a commentary of over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses, as well as c. 3,000 symbol glosses. About one-third of the verbal glosses are written in Old Irish..."

About-the-glosses page (/glosses, fetched 25 Aug 2026):
- "...contains over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses, in addition to c. 4,000 construe marks (symbols to aid reading). More than one-third of these glosses were written in Old Irish..."
- Old Irish glosses printed in Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus vol. 2, pp. 49-224.
- Hofman's Sankt Gall Priscian Commentary Part 1 (2 vols, Muenster 1996) published "roughly the first half of all of the glosses (Irish, Latin and symbols)".

Note the site's own internal variation: symbol/construe marks are given as c. 3,000 on the home page but c. 4,000 on the /glosses page. Both are official v2.1 statements; the safe formulation is "roughly three to four thousand symbol/construe marks".

## Version history (from /resource)

- v1.0 (2010): Hofman transcription + Moran digital edition; IRC-funded postdoc 2009-2011.
- v2.0 (2018): incorporation of Bernhard Bauer's FWF P22859 database of Old Irish Priscian glosses; beta Nov 2017; replaced original 13 May 2018.
- v2.1: released 28 June 2023; front-end adapted to Bootstrap 5.2 - i.e., a technical update only; the COUNTS were not restated as changed.

Conclusion: no genuine contradiction - 9,400+ refers to word-glosses only; adding roughly 3,000-4,000 symbols gives a total in the 12,500-13,500 range, matching Gloss Corpus' 13,395 item count. The current OFFICIAL number to print: "over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses plus c. 3,000-4,000 symbol/construe marks, about one third in Old Irish" with citation Hofman/Moran/Bauer, St Gall Priscian Glosses, version 2.1 (2023), http://stgallpriscian.ie/ [accessed 25 August 2026].

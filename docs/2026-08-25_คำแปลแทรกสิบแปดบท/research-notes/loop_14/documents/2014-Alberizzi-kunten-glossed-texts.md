## Verified content

Opening sentence of the article (from PDF first-page text visible in search index): "Japan has the best established tradition among all the East Asian cultures which developed strategies to interpret written Chinese based on their non-Sinitic vernaculars."

Author bio as printed with the article: Valerio Luigi Alberizzi holds a Ph.D. in Japanese Language and Linguistics from Ca' Foscari University Venice (2004); at publication time Associate Professor at the Open Education Center, Waseda University, Tokyo, coordinating the Italian language program; previously taught modern/classical Japanese at Ca' Foscari and the University of Bologna.

Illustration caption in the article: "The precious key to the esoteric treasure Hizōhōyaku 秘藏寶鑰 (University of Tōkyō, 1168, p. 1)" - i.e., the article uses Kūkai's Sangō shiiki companion work Hizō hōyaku in a 1168 manuscript with glosses as a visual example.

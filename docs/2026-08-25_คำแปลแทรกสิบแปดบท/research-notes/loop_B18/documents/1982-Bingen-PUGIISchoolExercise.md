## 3. Interpretation for the project (negative result, honestly documented)

- PUG II 53 is NOT a Greek-Demotic bilingual text: the official catalog records the language as Greek only. It is an alphabetic word-list exercise of the type known from Greco-Roman scribal education.
- Therefore Bingen's 1982 article cannot be the "famous Greek-Demotic bilingual school text" that Phase A half-remembered.
- Searches performed this loop found NO papyrus series "P.Bingen" with an item numbered 123; the abbreviation "P. Bingen" appears in Trismegistos citations only in connection with Bingen's own editions (e.g., TM 37353 cites "(P. Bingen Fr. 2)"; TM 397887 cites Bingen's Pages d'epigraphie grecque II, Epigraphica Bruxellensia 3). The candidate "P. Sorbonne" designation likewise could not be verified for any bilingual school text.
- Conclusion for the chapter: the specific "famous Greek-Demotic bilingual school text published in Bingen's volume" remains UNVERIFIED (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน). What is now verified instead: (a) Bingen's actual school-text contribution = PUG II 53 (Greek); (b) the real PUG series = Papiri dell'Universita di Genova (TM collection 138, ca. 400 fragments, mostly Greek documentary; site https://pug.unige.net/).

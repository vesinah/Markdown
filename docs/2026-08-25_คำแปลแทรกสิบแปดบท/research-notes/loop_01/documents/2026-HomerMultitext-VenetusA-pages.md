## From https://www.homermultitext.org/texts.html (verbatim)

"The Venetus A manuscript (Marciana 822 = Graecus Z. 454)

Complete texts of the HMT project edition of the Iliad and scholia (pages 12 recto - 326 verso).

Scholia grouped by page zone: For each page, scholia are grouped by the zone they belong to: main, intermarginal, interior, exterior or interlinear scholia.

Scholia grouped by Iliad line: For each page, scholia are grouped by the line of the Iliad they comment on."

## Relevance note (harvester's summary)

The Venetus A = Marciana Graecus Z. 454 (822), a 10th-century Byzantine codex whose margins preserve the main, intermarginal, interior, exterior, AND INTERLINEAR scholia — i.e., Alexandrian-derived scholarship physically transmitted onto manuscript margins and even between the lines, which is the core evidence chain for ancient annotation culture reaching medieval books. The HMT names the manuscript explicitly as Marciana 822 = Graecus Z. 454 and publishes the complete scholia corpus machine-readably.

## Verified headline statistics (home page, v1.0)
"Gloss Corpus currently holds **66** collections of glosses on **4** primary texts, comprising **24,872** glosses in total."
Primary texts and collections visible:
- Isidore of Seville, Etymologiae book 1: 54 gloss collections, 4,279 glosses total (editions by E. Stein and others; MSS include BL Arundel 129; Bern Burgerbibliothek 101, 611; Vat. Barb. lat. 447; Bodleian Auct T 2.20 etc.)
- Paul, Epistles: Würzburg, Universitätsbibliothek, M.p.th.f. 12 ("Wb."), ed. Stokes & Strachan rev. A. Doyle — the Old Irish glosses, 3,642 glosses
- Priscian, Ars grammatica: St Gall, Stiftsbibliothek, 904 ("G"), ed. R. Hofman rev. P. Moran — 13,395 glosses
- Óengus mac Óengobann, Félire Óenguso: Oxford, Bodleian Library, Rawlinson B505 ("R1"), ed. N. Stam — 1,519 glosses

Staging server (staging.glossing.org) shows a newer state not yet in v1.0 release: 73 collections on 7 primary texts, 33,648 glosses, adding Milan Ambrosiana C 301 inf. commentary on the Psalms (8,443 glosses), Ælfric's Glossary (BL Cotton Faustina A X, 239), further Priscian collections incl. BnF lat. 10290/7503/7504/7497.

## Editorial principles (verbatim)
- "Contributors receive full credit for their own content."
- "Editions are citable with unique DOIs and persistent URL."
- "Editions may be optionally peer-reviewed by an editorial committee."

## Which traditions included
As of v1.0 the corpus covers the early medieval Latin West and Insular Latin/Old Irish glossing only (Isidore glosses across many Carolingian manuscripts; Würzburg Pauline glosses; St Gall Priscian; Félire glosses). No non-Latin-script traditions are yet represented; the comparative ambition is carried instead by the Network for the Study of Glossing and the Handbook of Glossing (see separate harvest).

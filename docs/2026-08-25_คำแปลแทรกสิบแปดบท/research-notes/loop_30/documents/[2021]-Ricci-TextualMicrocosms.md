## Scope (verbatim from CORDIS objective)
"Interlinear translation – a bilingual genre that includes an original text and its word-for-word 'equivalents' in translation written in alternating lines on a single page – has been practiced in diverse societies over many centuries, yet has been little studied." Focus: interlinear translations produced between the late 16th and 20th centuries in the Indonesian-Malay world (Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei, Singapore, southern Thailand, southern Philippines). Five themes: local terminologies; religio-cultural aspects; script choice and use; standardization, untranslatability and mistranslation; visual elements.

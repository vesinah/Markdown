## Scope (verbatim substance from project site/CORDIS)
- Glosses as "fingerprints of the society in which texts were composed, copied, and read"; first-hand testimonies of contacts between Insular Celtic (Old Breton, Old Irish, Old Welsh) and Latin speakers.
- Core sources: early medieval copies of the computistical works of Bede and of Priscian's Latin grammar with multiple manuscript witnesses transmitting Insular Celtic and Latin glosses (9th-10th centuries).
- Methods: comparative philology and historical linguistics + digital humanities (handwritten text recognition, network analysis, natural language processing) + cultural history + biological computation ("applying DNA-sequence alignment methods to glosses", described as first-of-its-kind).
- Motivation: "A striking lack of editions has so far been a strong obstacle for in-depth investigations" — GlossIT produces digital editions.

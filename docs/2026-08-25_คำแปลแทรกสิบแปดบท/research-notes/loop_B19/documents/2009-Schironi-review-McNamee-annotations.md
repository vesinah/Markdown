# McNamee 2007 "Annotations in Greek and Latin Texts from Egypt" - verification cluster

Harvested: loop B19, 2026-08-26. Purpose: verify exact title/venue of the work often miscited as "Marginalia and Glosses in Bookrolls of Canonical Literary Authors".

## Title correction (precision pass result)

No publication titled "Marginalia and Glosses in Bookrolls of Canonical Literary Authors" by Kathleen McNamee was found in any queried database this session (Crossref bibliographic queries, Open Library author search, Internet Archive search, BMCR search). Open Library's three records under author "Kathleen McNamee" are:
1. Abbreviations in Greek Literary Papyri and Ostraca (Scholars Press, 1981; ISBN 9780891305309; 122 pp.; IA scan abbreviationsing0000mcna).
2. Sigla and select marginalia in Greek literary papyri (Fondation Egyptologique Reine Elisabeth, 1992; IA scan siglaselectmargi00mcna).
3. Annotations in Greek and Latin texts from Egypt (American Society of Papyrologists, 2007; ISBN 9780970059178; 577 pp.).

Conclusion: the canonical-literary-authors marginalia corpus is her 2007 volume "Annotations in Greek and Latin Texts from Egypt"; the title in the gap list appears to be a conflation with the 1992 volume's subject matter. Any citation in the book draft should use the verified titles above.

## Verified record of the 2007 volume

- Author (ed.): Kathleen McNamee.
- Title: Annotations in Greek and Latin Texts from Egypt.
- Series: American Studies in Papyrology 45 (per Crossref review record below).
- Publication: Oxford: Oxbow Books, for the American Society of Papyrologists, 2007.
- Extent: Pp. xviii + 577, figs, pls. ISBN 978-0-9700591-7-8.
- DOI (University of Michigan Press digital record): https://doi.org/10.3998/mpub.9749749

## Structure evidence from partial IA scan

Internet Archive identifier `annotations-in-greek-and-latin-texts-from-egypt` (collection: patron-library-collection / biblioteca-del-cen) holds a partial image-based scan. Its OCR layer contains only scattered pages but shows section headings including:
- "ADESPOTA: GREEK POETRY"
- "LATIN LITERATURE"
- "COMPREHENSIVE LIST OF ANNOTATED PAPYRI" (with a concordance table apparatus)
This confirms the book's organization as an annotated census/catalog of annotated Greek and Latin papyri. Full introduction text not accessible (epub pages are image placeholders; djvu.txt is only ~8 KB).

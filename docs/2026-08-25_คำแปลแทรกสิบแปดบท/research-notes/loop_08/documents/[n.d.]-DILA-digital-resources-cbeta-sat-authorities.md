# Digital resources for Buddhist gloss research: CBETA, SAT Daizokyo, DILA Authority databases

## Metadata
- Compiled from official project pages fetched 2026-08-25 (access date); institutional self-descriptions, not third-party commentary.

## 1. CBETA (Chinese Buddhist Electronic Text Association / 財團法人佛教電子佛典基金會)
- Official site: https://cbeta.org/ (fetched)
- Online reader: https://cbetaonline.dila.edu.tw/ — "CBETA Online" developed and maintained by Dharma Drum Institute of Liberal Arts (法鼓文理學院 DILA); features per cbeta.org: full-text search, dictionary lookup, person/place authority data, word-frequency statistics; export to TXT/PDF/EPUB/DOCX/ODT; punctuation options.
- Corpus: 電子佛典集成 includes 大正新脩大藏經 (Taisho), 卍新纂續藏經 (Xuzangjing), 嘉興藏 (Jiaxing), 國家圖書館善本佛典, 歷代藏經補輯, plus modern collections; current release 2026.R1 announced 2026-04-30.
- Open data on GitHub: https://github.com/cbeta-org/xml-p5 ; revision/collation database: https://revisiondb.cbeta.org/ ; canon catalogue database 佛教藏經目錄數位資料庫: http://jinglu.cbeta.org/
- Verification performed this loop: the official Taisho list page http://www.cbeta.org/cbreader/help/list_Taisho.htm was downloaded; its rows show T54n2128 一切經音義 with 100 juan, and T54n2123 (Xuanying's Yiqiejing yinyi) in Taisho vol. 54 (row shows juan count 20 there; dictionaries uniformly give 25 juan for Xuanying's work — discrepancy noted, unresolved).
- Relevance: canonical digital text of T2128 (Huilin) and T2123 (Xuanying) for citation as digital canon.

## 2. SAT Daizojo Text Database (SAT大正新脩大藏經テキストデータベース)
- Home (Japanese, fetched): https://21dzk.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/SAT/ ; established March 17, 1998; University of Tokyo Graduate School of Humanities and Sociology.
- Scope: complete digitization of the Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō vols. 1-85 ("智慧の宝庫、『大正新脩大藏經』の第1巻から第85巻までの全テキストをデータベース化").
- Current versions: SAT DB 2018 (https://21dzk.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/SAT2018/master30.php) under KAKENHI JP15H05725; earlier 2015/2008 versions still linked.
- Extras relevant to manuscript/gloss studies: IIIF image server (https://dzkimgs.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/omekas/s/sat-iiif/page/welcome), OCR alignment of Zengidara/nanjio-era printed canons held at Zōjōji etc. using NDL Classical Texts OCR (2023 announcements), TEI/XML Shōman gyō gisho 『勝鬘経義疏』 digital edition (2020) — a Japanese gisho (commentary) in TEI; Digital Hōbōgirin (法寶義林) vocabulary index DB (2021, with Académie des inscriptions et belles-lettres): https://tripitaka.l.u-tokyo.ac.jp/hbgrn/ ; Unicode encoding work for Taishō non-standard hanzi (>2800 chars proposed into Unicode 10.0; 329 more in Unicode 13.0).
- From April 2025 a new KAKENHI Special Priority Research project builds the "Reiwa Daizōkyō" (令和大蔵経) on top of SAT data: https://reiwadzk.dhii.asia/

## 3. DILA Buddhist Studies Authority Database Project (佛學規範資料庫)
- Portal (fetched): https://authority.dila.edu.tw/ ; project director Huimin Bhikṣu 釋惠敏; co-directors Aming Tu 杜正民 and Marcus Bingenheimer 馬德偉; produced by Dharma Drum Institute of Liberal Arts Library & Information Center, Digital Archives Section.
- Four authority databases: Time (CJK calendar conversion, China from 220 BCE, Korea 56 BCE-1910, Japan from 593 CE): https://authority.dila.edu.tw/time ; Person: https://authority.dila.edu.tw/person ; Place (with geo-coordinates/KML): https://authority.dila.edu.tw/place ; Tripitaka Catalogues: https://authority.dila.edu.tw/catalog
- Open content/download section and Web Services API documented at https://authority.dila.edu.tw/docs/
- Relevance: disambiguation of glossators/translators named in Yinyi entries (e.g., Xuanying, Huilin, Xilin) and of catalogue persons; integrated into CBETA Online reader.

## Notes for the book chapter
- These three platforms are the standard digital infrastructure any modern chapter on Chinese Buddhist glosses must cite when referring readers to primary texts (T2123/T2128 etc.) and to prosopographical control of names.

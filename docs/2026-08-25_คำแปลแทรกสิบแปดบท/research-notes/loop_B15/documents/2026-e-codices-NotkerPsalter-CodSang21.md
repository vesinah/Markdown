## Record facts (gap 6)

Title: "Translatio barbarica psalterii Notkeri tertii (Old High German Psalter by Notker the German)" / German title "Althochdeutscher Psalter Notkers des Deutschen".
Support/extent/format: parchment, 578 pages, 2deg (31 x 24 cm). Origin of THIS copy: Einsiedeln, twelfth century. Summary: "Old High German translation and commentary on the Psalms by the monk Notker the German of St. Gall, dating from around the year 1000. This 12th century copy from Einsiedeln is the only extant complete copy."

## Layout evidence (Scherrer description, verbatim German)

"Der lateinische Text in rother, die altdeutsche Uebersetzung in schwarzer Schrift." — In this psalter copy the LATIN TEXT IS WRITTEN IN RED, THE OLD HIGH GERMAN TRANSLATION IN BLACK. This documents a continuous two-language running format rather than scattered interlinear glossing.

Contents per Scherrer:
- pp. 8-540: Incipit translatio barbarica psalterii Notkeri tertii. Beatus vir ... (the 150 psalms)
- pp. 540-574: Cantica prophetarum; p. 563 Pater noster; p. 565ff Symbola (altdeutsche Vater unser and confession of faith)
- p. 575: copyist distichon: "Notker Teutonicus domino finitur amicus. Gaudeat ille locis in paradysiacis." (possibly by Ekkehart IV)

Provenance highlights:
- Copy formerly at Einsiedeln (fourteenth-century possession inscriptions); still seen there by Metzler (d. 1639).
- The LOST ORIGINAL: Empress Gisela, present at St Gall in 1027, had it copied for herself — "Psalterium et Job sibi exemplari fecit" reported by Ekkehart IV in his own hand in Liber benedictionum (Cod. 393 p. 155, interlinear gloss to verse 16). Metzler's chronicle adds that a father copied it within fourteen days while the brothers were reluctant ("una cum Jobo, satis quidem invitis patribus, asportavit").
- Title wording "translatio barbarica" echoes Jerome's preface vocabulary; St Gall paper ms No. 1286 copied from Cod. 21.

## Negative identifications for psalter questions

- Cod. Sang. 22 = Golden Psalter (Psalterium aureum), Psalterium Gallicanum, around 883-888 and around 890-900, illuminated with 17 miniatures, West Franconian involvement — LATIN ONLY, not an OHG monument. https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/csg/0022
- Cod. Sang. 23 = Folchart Psalter, Psalterium Gallicanum with Cantica, St. Gall 872-883, masterpiece of late Carolingian illumination — LATIN ONLY. https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/csg/0023 , DOI 10.5076/e-codices-csg-0023

## Other verified OHG interlinear anchors found during searches

- Cod. Sang. 916: Regula S. Benedicti, beginning of ninth century — "The Latin-Old High German Rule of St Benedict, one of the oldest monuments of the Old High German language"; incipit "Regula [S.] Benedicti] cum versione interlineari germanica"; sample interlinear OHG: "keuuisso zekarauenne sint herzun kearneem". https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/csg/0916
- VadSlg Ms. 312 (Vadiana, St Gallen): Horace Opera, tenth century, laid out with space for marginal + interlinear glosses by various hands in different ink; exactly ONE Old High German gloss over 'sicarius': "mxchfrk" (= mucheri) per Siewert; localization uncertain (Franconia?). https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/vad/0312
- Bern Burgerbibliothek Cod. 803 (Rotulus von Muehlinen, Murbach, end eleventh/beginning twelfth century): 460+ recipes with selective OHG interlinear glosses plus medical glossary — comparative material. https://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/bbb/0803
- Cod. Sang. 162 / 166 (Augustine Enarrationes volumes, c. 850, Grimald era): OHG glosses by Ekkehart IV after 1000; descriptions cite Bergmann/Stricker, Katalog der althochdeutschen und altsächsischen Glossenhandschriften (Bd. I-II, Berlin 2005) as the counting authority; some descriptions by the UCLA Reichenau-St. Gall Virtual Library (Hendrix 2012).
- Zürich Zentralbibliothek Ms. C 98 and Ms. C 121 (on long-term loan in St Gall Abbey Library): LATIN works of Notker Labeo (Distributio, De dialectica, De rhetorica; Quid sit syllogismus etc.), eleventh century, St Gall — show ZB "C" series holds Notker Latin school texts; no Rh.-series OHG psalter surfaced anywhere on e-codices.

## Search-method note

e-codices full-text search "Interlinearversion" returns exactly three manuscripts: Basel UB A V 26 (fifteenth century composite), Einsiedeln Codex 199(638), and Cod. Sang. 916. Full-text search "Psalterium germanice" produced no Reichenau or Zurich-Rh. psalter. Language facet counts on the platform list 10 "Old High German" items total among Swiss digitized mss.

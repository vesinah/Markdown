## Object and tradition numbers

- Semi-diplomatic edition of Leiden, Universitätsbibliothek, Vossianus Latinus Folio 48 (ninth century), fol. 2r-91v — "one of the four most important witnesses" of the earliest extensive commentary on De nuptiis that began with Carolingian educational reforms.
- Commentary transmitted in sixteen manuscripts, twelve from the ninth century; partial print edition: Lutz, Dunchad Glossae in Martianum (1944), based on incomplete Paris BnF lat. 12960.
- First complete publication of gloss and INTERLINEAR commentary material ("Glossen- und Interlinearkommentare erstmals vollständig") by international specialist team under Mariken Teeuwen.

## Attribution result (editors' conclusion, quoted by Fischer)

"Ein wichtiges Ergebnis ... besteht darin, dass sich die hier edierten frühesten Glossenkommentare nicht ... auf einen einzelnen Autor zurückführen lassen, sondern in der Zeit zwischen 820 und 840 in regem Austausch der an den karolingischen Klöstern und Höfen diskutierenden und kommentierenden Gelehrten entstanden sind." (Earliest gloss commentaries arose 820-840 in lively exchange among scholars discussing and commenting at Carolingian monasteries AND courts.) This is why the edition follows one manuscript's version rather than a reconstructed authorial text.

## Platform and funding facts

- Published on eLaborate, developed at Huygens Instituut Den Haag (KNAW).
- NWO-funded May 2003 - May 2007; cooperation with Utrecht University research group.
- Online since November 2008, unrestricted access; last update indicated August 2011; self-described work-in-progress but without visible change log.

## How gloss layers are represented (encoding detail — gap 5 core)

- Facsimile: black-and-white photographs of moderate quality; high-resolution colour photos exist on Leiden University's Socrates portal but were not integrated.
- Manuscript description by Teeuwen is very detailed; assigns the manuscript a 'great personality', including annotations "u.a. von der Hand des Bischofs Ratherius von Verona aus dem 10. Jh."
- Text split into Haupttext (base text) and Glossentext (gloss commentary): "Der Editionstext ist in Haupttext und Glossentext aufgeteilt. Beide lassen sich auf unterschiedlichen Texttafeln ('Panels') wahlweise synoptisch oder einzeln aufrufen."
- Cross-referencing: "Die kommentierte Textpassage und der Kommentar verweisen einerseits über diakritische Nummerierungen im Haupttext auf einander, die bei On-mouse-over den Kommentartext zusätzlich als Pop-up erscheinen lassen; andererseits verweist im Editionstext des Glossenkommentars die entsprechende Nummerierung sowie das Lemma auf die kommentierte Passage im Haupttext."
- Facsimile panel: zoomable, scrolls synchronously while reading; main/gloss panels are fixed so related passages drift apart when scrolling.
- Transcription conventions: line-faithful ("zeilengetreu"); abbreviations silently resolved; modern punctuation and capitalization; u/v handling inconsistent across the edition (criticized); e-caudata normally rendered ae where diphthong; expunctions and erasures rendered as strikethroughs; superscripts preserved as superscripts. Raw data not delivered; underlying format not stated.
- Checking basis: transcript corrected against three further witnesses (Besançon BM 594, Leiden BPL 88, Vatican Reg.lat. 1987) and against printed commentaries of 'Dunchad'/Lutz, Johannes Scotus, Remigius; no systematic critical annotation of variants throughout.
- Search: site-wide or current page; results grouped into 'Texten' (accompanying prose), 'Transkriptionen' (main text), 'Annotationen' (glosses); fuzzy search tolerates orthographic variance; no word registers; direct addressing via folder URLs down to folio and gloss number, e.g., martianus.huygensinstituut.knaw.nl/path/facsimile/leiden_vossianus_48/book_2_de_nuptiis/folio_11r#a-84 (paragraph numbers not directly addressable).

## Status of the CCCM continuation (gap 2, as of 2014)

"Fazit" paragraph: the critical exploitation of the whole transmission proceeds in two Corpus Christianorum PRINT volumes — "Ein erster Band, der die beiden ersten Bücher umfasst, ist bereits erschienen (O'Sullivan 2010).

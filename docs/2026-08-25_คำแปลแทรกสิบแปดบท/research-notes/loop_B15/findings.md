## Key findings (with URLs)

1. GAP 2 CLOSED - CCCM books III-IX never published. Live Brepols CCCM series listing fetched 2026-08-26 contains only CCCM 237 (books I-II, O'Sullivan 2010); no second Glossae aeui Carolini volume is listed. Baseline statements: Teeuwen's project page (captured 2014) announced a two-volume plan ("the second volume will be a semi-diplomatic edition of books 3-5 (trivial arts) and 6-9 (quadrivial arts)"); Fischer's RIDE review (2014) recorded it as "in Planung". Status after 2026 check: announced-only, not published. Draft guidance: cite CCCM 237 as the sole published volume; describe III-IX as available only via martianus.huygens.knaw.nl (semi-diplomatic VLF 48) and via Lutz 1944 partial editions.

2. GAP 3 CLOSED SUBSTANTIALLY - palace-court attribution now sourced at three levels. (a) Teeuwen verbatim (project page): the oldest De nuptiis commentary was "perhaps produced by intellectual circles at the courts of Louis the Pious or Charles the Bald", fitting Alcuin's educational ideal; but her final formulation keeps both spheres: such manuscripts are "not school texts, but encyclopedic collections of secular learning, created by and for the intellectual elites of the great Carolingian monasteries and courts". (b) Herren summary of Teeuwen's results: OGT = work of several scholars active under LOUIS THE PIOUS (not Charles the Bald); transmission geography Auxerre-Tours-Fleury / Corbie-Rheims / Lorsch-Cologne (O'Sullivan) plus Loire valley and north/northeastern France, Cologne, Lorsch (Contreni). (c) The only firm SCHOLA PALATINA link is Eriugena himself (Ramelli: De nuptiis as teaching text used by Eriugena at the schola palatina) - not the anonymous OGT layer. (d) Counterweight: Herren doubts the committee model ("We may need more evidence for glossing by committees"); single-editor alternative (Bern 363 model). Draft guidance: attribute the oldest gloss LAYER to collective scholarly networks spanning court and monastery c. 820-840; reserve "palace school" strictly for Eriugena's context.

3. GAP 4 CLOSED - CCCM 237 introduction content documented from Contreni's full TMR review (plus Brepols summary): clxxxii-page introduction titled around "the oldest gloss tradition"; refusal of an Ur-text; presentation of ALL glosses from FOURTEEN principal mss (twenty 9th/10th-c. witnesses transmit the layer); editorial principles at cxxxi-cxlii; lemma-and-category organization with second/third hand additions flagged; dual apparatuses (variants; sources incl. Festus, Fulgentius, Vatican Mythographers); thematic payoff = obscurity masking hidden truths, fiction as ornament of truth (O'Sullivan chapter "Obscurity, Pagan Lore, and Secrecy", CELAMA 12, pp. 99-121).

4. GAP 5 CLOSED - VLF 48 digital edition layers and encoding: base manuscript Leiden Voss. Lat. Fol. 48 fol. 2r-91v, semi-diplomatic, checked against Besancon BM 594, Leiden BPL 88, Vatican Reg.lat. 1987 plus Lutz/Eriugena/Remigius prints; edition separates HAUPTTEXT and GLOSSENTEXT onto panels viewable synoptically; diacritical numbers cross-reference lemma and gloss with mouse-over popups; synchronized zoomable facsimile; line-faithful transcription conventions (silent abbreviation expansion, modern punctuation, strikethrough for expunction/rasure, superscripts preserved); search classes literally named Texte / Transkriptionen / Annotationen; NWO 2003-2007, online since November 2008, last update August 2011. Original-format claim (Herren/Teeuwen): text with marginal and interlinear scholia, NOT a continuous commentary; ms also carries Ratherius of Verona annotations.

5. GAP 6 CLOSED - Notker III's move beyond glossing, verified: e-codices/Scherrer show his Psalter (Cod. Sang. 21, unique complete Einsiedeln copy s. XII of a c.-1000 original, now lost) as TRANSLATION + COMMENTARY with a continuous bilingual layout - "Der lateinische Text in rother, die altdeutsche Uebersetzung in schwarzer Schrift" (Latin red, OHG black) - i.e., parallel running rendering rather than scattered interlinear glosses; Mayer/BLB states purpose ("dem besseren Verständnis im Schulunterricht dienen") and period significance ("Mit Notkers Werken endet die Periode des Althochdeutschen"); Ekkehart IV records Empress Gisela ordering a copy in 1027; Crossref confirms the De Gruyter 'Notker latinus' zum Martianus Capella showing Notker also rendered Martianus into OHG with Latin-source apparatus. Note: the classic three-layer description (Latin lemma / OHG translation / commentary per verse) is consistent with these fetched facts but its precise verse-by-verse mechanics should still be checked against Grotans 2006 in a later loop before print.

6. GAP 1 RESOLVED AS CORRECTION - no verifiable "Reichenau Psalter" standard exists. (a) The Phase A citation is refuted at source: the BLB Mayer PDF, re-fetched and full-text-searched, contains NO mention of Zurich Rh. 34, no Zurich shelfmark, and no OHG psalter. (b) handschriftencensus.de exact search "Rh. 34": zero hits across Handschriften/Werke/Personen/Forschungsliteratur - fatal for any claimed German-language monument under that shelfmark. (c) e-codices searches surface NO Reichenau OHG interlinear psalter among Swiss digitized holdings; the only early full OHG INTERLINEAR monument there is Cod. Sang. 916 (Latin-OHG Rule of St Benedict, early 9th c.), while St Gall's famous ninth-century psalters (Cod. Sang. 22 Golden Psalter; Cod. Sang. 23 Folchart Psalter) are illuminated Latin copies without OHG. (d) Verified anchors to use instead of the phantom: Abrogans copy BLB Aug. perg. 111 (per Mayer PDF footnote, Holder Bd.1 S. 286-289); Reginbert's catalogue entry "...et carmina diversa ad docendum Theodiscam linguam" (Holder Bd.3 S. 94); Cod. Sang. 916; Notker's Psalter (Cod. Sang. 21). Any residual claim of a classic "Reichenau glosses/Psalter" reference manuscript must carry (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน). Additional caution flag: Zurich ZB's "Ms. Rh." prefix conventionally denotes Rheinau abbey holdings rather than Reichenau - plausible source of the Phase A confusion - but this expansion could not itself be confirmed from fetched sources this loop and needs the same marker if used.

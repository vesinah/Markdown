## Key content notes

### What the text is

- The Hundred Surnames (Baijiaxing) is an elementary Song-dynasty list of common surnames in quadrisyllabic rhyming couplets (~503 surnames; 71 lines).
- A version "with Phags-pa spellings for each surname was produced during the Yuan dynasty, probably as an aid to Chinese officials who were required to learn the new script."
- It is "the only surviving example of a Chinese literary text (albeit a very simple one) written in the Phags-pa script."
- Entitled Baijiaxing Mengguwen 百家姓蒙古文 [bay gya sing mung gu wun].

### Where it survives - concrete Yuan blockprint context

Survives only inside editions of the popular encyclopaedia Shilin Guangji 事林廣記 (Chen Yuanjing):
- Yuan Taiding 3 [1325] edition, known through a Japanese Genroku 12 [1699] recarved reprint (facsimile: Hekeben Leishu Jicheng vol. 1, Shanghai Guji Chubanshe 1990);
- Yuan Zhishun era (1330-1333) Chunzhuang Shuyuan edition (facsimile: Shilin Guangji, Zhonghua Shuju 1963);
- Yuan Zhiyuan 6 [1340] Jicheng Tang edition;
- Ming Yongle 16 [1418] Cuiyan Jingshan/Cuiyan Jingshe edition.
- 427 surnames given across editions (407 single-character + 20 double-character); textual corruption history analyzed (e.g. 1699 editor repaired unclear Phags-pa spellings using a dictionary such as Menggu Ziyun, sometimes picking invalid readings).
- Preface to the 1330-1333/1340 editions frames the text as exam-oriented: "The study of Mongolian [writing] has been established as a special subject, and if you can become proficient in this text at an early stage you will find it to be a shortcut to a career as an official."
- The 1699 preface says the compiler engraved it immediately after the section on ancient seal script within the juan devoted to writing/calligraphy (vol. 4).
- Page economy inference: "each half-folio ... would only have listed twelve surnames ... quite possible if the Phags-pa words and Chinese ideographs were written very large, as is often the case with elementary texts."
- Facsimile sources cited by BabelStone: Junast, Basibazi Baijiaxing jiaokan 八思巴字百家姓校勘 (p. 267) for the 1330-1333 ed.; Luo Changpei and Cai Meibiao, Basibazi yu Yuandai Hanyu 八思巴字與元代漢語 (pp. 59-60) for the 1340 ed.; Ligeti, Le Po Kia Sing en Ecriture 'Phags-pa (1956, p. 49) for the 1418 ed.

### Companion object: Menggu Ziyun 蒙古字韻

From https://www.babelstone.co.uk/Phags-pa/MengguZiyun.html :
- A rhyming dictionary of over 9,000 Chinese ideographs arranged under roughly 800+ (extant: 813) Phags-pa syllables in fifteen rhyme categories; only extant version is Zhu Zongwen's (Mongolian name Bayan) revision of 1308.
- Purpose per Zhu's own 1308 preface: "Even if you recognise a particular ideograph, but do not know how to pronounce it, then it is as if you are unable to speak. The Mongolian Rhyming Dictionary unites the written and the spoken forms of ideographs."
- Only extant witness: British Library manuscript Or. 6972 (bought 1909 from Mrs Bushell), copied during the Qianlong period (1736-1795) per imperial taboo-graph analysis; read vertically left-to-right following Mongolian layout.
- Entry structure: each Phags-pa syllable head followed by lists of homophonous ideographs ordered by the four tones (level, rising, departing, entering).
- Facsimiles: Luo Changpei and Cai Meibiao 1959 (BL manuscript facsimile pp. 95-127); Junast and Yang Naisi, Menggu Ziyun jiaoben 蒙古字韻校本 (reprint of Kansai University 1957 facsimile; also reprinted in Xuxiu Siku Quanshu).

## Relevance to project

Concrete, verifiable Yuan-era BLOCKPRINT examples of script-pairing pedagogy: the Baijiaxing Mengguwen embedded in Yuan (1325-1340) woodblock editions of the Shilin guangji pairs every Chinese character with its square-script spelling, i.e. a printed bilingual phonetic primer; the Menggu Ziyun (1308) is its dictionary counterpart. These document Yuan 'Phags-pa usage as phonetic annotation of Chinese characters in print. Note honestly: in these objects the pairing is columnar/large-character rather than glosses squeezed between lines of running text; no verified case of Phags-pa inserted BETWEEN the lines of a continuous Chinese text was found this loop "(ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน)".

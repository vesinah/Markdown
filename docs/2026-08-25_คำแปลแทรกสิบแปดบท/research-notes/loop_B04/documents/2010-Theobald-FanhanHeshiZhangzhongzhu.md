## Key content notes

### Identity and history

- Fan-Han heshi zhangzhongzhu 番漢合時掌中珠 "Simultaneous Tangut-Chinese pearls-in-the-hand"; compiled by a Tangut scholar transliterated in Chinese as Gule Maocai 骨勒茂才 at the court of emperor Renzong 西夏仁宗 (r. 1140-1193).
- Finished in 1190; discovered 1909 by Pyotr K. Kozlov in the ruins of Khara-Khoto (Heishuicheng, Ejen Banner, Inner Mongolia).
- The Kozlov copy is a woodblock print of 37 pages in butterfly binding.
- Preface in two languages (Tangut and Chinese) stressing the need to learn both languages to administer an empire ruled by a Tangut dynasty with Chinese subjects.

### Page structure and entry structure (the core deliverable for Loop B04)

- "Each word in the dictionary is, if in Tangutan language, transcribed with Chinese characters, and, on the Chinese side, transcribed with Tangutan script." - i.e., every entry pairs a word with a REVERSE-SCRIPT phonetic transcription: Tangut headword + Chinese-character transcription of its sound; Chinese equivalent + Tangut-script transcription of its sound.
- Arrangement: encyclopedic semantic spheres rather than alphabetical/radical order - heaven; stars and planets; nature; earth; rivers and mountains; minerals, plants, animals; humans; human body; human activities. The last chapter covers almost half the book (kinship, Buddhism, architecture, tools, clothing, agricultural implements, administration/law, musical instruments, food, equestrian gear, marriage terms etc.).
- Figure caption on the same page: "Each page is divided into three panels with two columns (the latter without dividing line)" - describing the illustrated sample page showing meteorological terms with their Chinese correspondences (image: ../Science/Images/fanhanheshizhangzhongzhu.jpg, from Gule Maocai and Huang Zhenhua ed. 1989).

### Editions

- First facsimile printed in the Soviet Union; first Chinese edition by Luo Fucheng 羅福成 1924; modern facsimile 1989 Ningxia Renmin Press (Gule Maocai / Huang Zhenhua et al.).
- Cited scholarship: Nie Hongyin 1987 on the phonetic notation symbols (注音符號) of the Pearl; Jing Yongshi 2013 on reconstructing the content/order of the Russian-held copies and version criticism; Shi Jinbo encyclopedia entries; Ivanov 1909 first report.

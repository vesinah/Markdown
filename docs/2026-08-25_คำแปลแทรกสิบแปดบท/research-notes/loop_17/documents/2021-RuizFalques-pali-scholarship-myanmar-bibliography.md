SELECTED ENTRIES FROM THE "BASIC BIBLIOGRAPHY" (verbatim, diacritics simplified where the PDF
encoding was inconsistent):

- BODE, Mabel H. The Pali Literature of Burma. London: Royal Asiatic Society, 1909.

- CHARNEY, Michael W. Powerful Learning. Buddhist Literati and the Throne in Burma's Last Dynasty,
  1752-1885. Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press, 2006.

- DHAMMASAMI, Ven. Khammai. "Between Idealism and Pragmatism: A Study of Monastic Education in
  Burma and Thailand from the Seventeenth Century to the Present", unpublished PhD dissertation,
  University of Oxford, 2004.

- HANDLIN, Lilian. 2018. "King Saw Lu and the World Around Him" in Was Bagan "the metropolis of
  Buddhism in Indo-China?" (Duroiselle 1924), edited by Elizabeth Moore. Yangon, Pyinsagon Press:
  237-264.

- KIRICHENKO, Alexey. "Dynamics of Monastic Mobility and Networking in the Seventeenth- and
  Eighteenth-Century Upper Burma", Buddhist Dynamics in Premodern and Early Modern Southeast Asia,
  edited by D. Christian Lammerts. Singapore: Institute of Southeast Asian Studies, 2015: 333-372.

- KYAW, Pyi Phyo. Pattthana (Conditional Relations) in Burmese Buddhism, unpublished PhD
  dissertation, King's College London, University of London, 2014.

- LAMMERTS, D. Christian. Buddhism and Written Law: Dhammasattha Manuscripts and Texts in Premodern
  Burma, unpublished PhD dissertation, Cornell University, 2010.
  Also: LAMMERTS. Buddhist Law in Burma. A History of Dhammasattha Texts and Jurisprudence,
  1250-1850. Honolulu: University of Hawai'i Press, 2018.

- LUCE, G.H., and TIN HTWAY. "A 15th Century Inscription and Library at Pagan, Burma." In
  Malalasekera Commemoration Volume, ed. O.H. de A. Wijesekera, 203-256. Colombo: The Malalasekera
  Commemoration Volume Editorial Committee, 1976.
  [NOTE: a JPTS XXX (2009) PDF on palitextsociety.org renders the title as "A Fifth Century
  Inscription..." in one citation instance; the Luce papers finding aid at the National Library of
  Australia (sealang.net/archives/luce/UThawKaung.pdf, fetched) lists the manuscript folder as
  "'A 15th Century inscription and library at Pagan, Burma' (co-author Tin Htway) for Malasakera
  volume. MS, typescript, correspondence 1973-76" - supporting "15th Century" as correct.]

- NYUNT, Peter (trans.). Catalogue of the Pitaka and Other Texts in Pali, Pali-Burmese, and Burmese
  (Pitakat-to-samuing:) by Mang-kri Mahasirijeyasu. Bristol: Pali Text Society, 2012.
  [English translation of the classical Pitakataik catalogue of the Sangharaja Mahasirijeyasura -
  key source on monastic library holdings including nissaya categories.]

- OKELL, John. "Nissaya Burmese: A Case of Systematic Adaptation to a Foreign Grammar and Syntax",
  Lingua 15 (1965): 186-227.

- OKELL, John. "'Translation' and 'Embellishment' in an Early Burmese 'Jataka' Poem", Journal of
  the Royal Asiatic Society, N. 3/4 (Oct. 1967): 133-148.

- PRUITT, William. Etude linguistique de nissaya birmans. Traduction commentee de textes
  bouddhiques. Paris: Ecole Francaise d'Extreme-Orient, 1994.
  [VERIFICATION NOTE: the Loop brief said "Pruitt 1991 monograph"; this authoritative bibliography
  dates the EFEO monograph to 1994. Crossref additionally confirms Pruitt's article "The Study of
  Burmese by Westerners With Special Reference to Burmese Nissayas," International Journal of
  Lexicography 5.4 (1992), DOI 10.1093/ijl/5.4.278. Okell reviewed the monograph in Buddhist
  Studies Review 14.2 (1996), DOI 10.1558/bsrv.v14i2.14875.]

- RUIZ-FALQUES, Aleix. "A Firefly in the Bamboo Reed," unpublished PhD dissertation, University of
  Cambridge, 2015.

- RUIZ-FALQUES, Aleix. "The Role of Pal Grammar in Burmese Buddhism." Journal of Burma Studies 21,
  no. 1 (2017): 1-96.

- TIN LWIN. 1991. "The Saddaniti," in: Studies in Honour of Mingun Sayadaw. Yangon: Tipitaka
  Nikaya Ministrative Organization: 117-126.

Contextual note from handout framing: it is the standard scholarly bibliography for Pali studies in
Myanmar, covering grammar (Kaccayana, Moggalana, Padarupasiddhi, Saddaniti of Aggavamsa),
monastic education, inscriptions, and manuscript libraries.

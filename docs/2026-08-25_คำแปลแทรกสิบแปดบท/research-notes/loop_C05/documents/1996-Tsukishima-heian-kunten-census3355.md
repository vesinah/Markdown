## The census (verbatim Japanese passage from the Kosukegawa chapter)

「日本語訓点資料（以下、訓点資料）はどのくらい現存しているのか。築島裕『平安時代訓点本論考（研究篇）』（1996年、汲古書院）によれば、平安時代の訓点資料の数は平安初期（783～900）が180点、平安中期（901～1000）が189点、平安後期（1001～1100）が582点、院政期（1101～1200）が2404点、合計3355点が挙げられている。しかもこの数値は「現在までに筆者が何等かの形で調査を為し得た点数」（同書27頁）であって、12世紀の仏書訓点資料は数万点は現存するともいわれている」

## Structured summary

| Period | Count |
|---|---|
| Heian I 平安初期 783-900 | 180 |
| Heian II 平安中期 901-1000 | 189 |
| Heian III 平安後期 1001-1100 | 582 |
| Insei 院政期 1101-1200 | 2,404 |
| Total 合計 | 3,355 |

Qualifier: the count is "the number [Tsukishima] was able to survey in some form to date" (平安時代訓点本論考 研究篇, p. 27) - a floor established by one scholar's surveys, NOT an exhaustive inventory. Twelfth-century Buddhist-book kunten materials alone are reported as possibly numbering tens of thousands of surviving items.

Sub-census for kanbun (漢籍) kunten materials quoted in the same section: 平安中期 7点; 平安後期・院政期 15点; roughly 200点 even including Kamakura-period items - kanji-text kunten are a small minority versus Buddhist-book kunten.

## Key points for the manuscript

1. CITE AS: "3,355 kunten-bearing sources from the 8th through 12th centuries surveyed by Tsukishima Hiroshi as of 1996 (平安初期180・中期189・後期582・院政期2,404)" - never as "all extant kunten materials".
2. The 8th-13th-century window used elsewhere in the draft maps onto this census only up to 1200; Kamakura material is outside the 3,355 (except the ~200 kanji-kunten note).
3. The Buddhist-vs-kanji skew (tens of thousands vs ~200) is the sharpest citable fact for any claim about the social distribution of reading-mark practice in Heian Japan.

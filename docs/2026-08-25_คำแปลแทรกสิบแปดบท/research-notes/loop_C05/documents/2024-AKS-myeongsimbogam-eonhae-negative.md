## What each authoritative source actually says

1. 한국민족문화대백과사전 「명심보감」 (E0018312; written 1995 by 이원호, Busan Natl. Univ. education; last revised 2024-02-23): https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0018312
   - 서지적 사항: "2권 1책. 목판본과 석판본 등 10여 종이 있다." - ten-odd editions, all woodblock/lithograph classes.
   - Korean transmission line: 고려 충렬왕 때 추적(秋適)이 재편집한 것으로 전해지는 『명심보감초(明心寶鑑抄)』 -> many variant recensions; used after 『천자문』 with 『동몽선습』 as a primer in schools.
   - Holdings: 국립중앙도서관과 규장각 등.
   - NO mention of 언해/諺解, no 15th-century translation bureau involvement, no gugyeol.

2. 동양고전종합DB 동양고전해제집 「명심보감(明心寶鑑)」 해제: https://db.cyberseodang.or.kr/front/sabuList/BookMain.do?bnCode=jti_5a1001&titleId=C250
   - Editions enumerated worldwide: 중국 명간본·청간본 (1368 교정산보본 at National Library of China; 1393 신간교정대자명심보감; 1553 중간본; 1585 어제중집본); 조선 청주본 (1454, printed at Cheongju, 유득화 발문, held at 청주 고인쇄박물관 and Tsukuba University Library) and 초략본; 일본 화각본 (reprinted repeatedly); 베트남판 Mih-Tam Buu-Giam; 스페인어판 Beng Sim po Can (Juan Cobo 1592, dedicated to Philip III; Madrid BN + Sophia Univ. Cristan bunko; Navarrete's 1676 retranslation read by Leibniz/Quesnay/Voltaire).
   - Joseon use stated as schoolroom primer ("조선에서는 서당 및 글방의 교재로 사용"); the 1454 Cheongju copy was taken to Japan in the Imjin War.
   - NO 언해본 anywhere in the world-edition survey.

## Interpretation guardrails

- The plausible origin of the phantom "Myeongsim bogam eonhae (1465)" remains unidentified; do not speculate in print. The verified mid-15th-century eonhae-with-gugyeol anchors stay 능엄경언해 (1462), 목우자수심결언해 (1467), 두시언해 (1481) per loop_12/B06.
- 명심보감 itself remains fully citable as a Literary-Sinitic moral primer transmitted across East Asia (AKS + cyberseodang 해제 give exact edition genealogy incl. the 1454 Cheongju woodblock) - just never as a translated/glossed Korean text.
- If a manuscript eonhae copy surfaces in future library cataloguing, this record should be reopened; until then the negative is final for this project.

## Content

The scan is of a lontar copy of the Arjunawiwaha supplied with maarti translation, kept at the Documentation Centre of the Bali Provincial Cultural Office (Pusdok Dinas Kebudayaan Provinsi Bali) and digitized into the archive.org "Balinese Digital Library" collection. The title leaf reads: Judul: Kekawin Ari Wangsa Maarti; Ukuran: P.54,5em L.3,5em Jik. 246; wilik: Gst.Pt. Djelantik, Singaraja.

Andrea Acri (Inside Indonesia 121, 2015) reports that the Centre for Documentation of Balinese Culture in Denpasar digitally photographed most of its lontar collection and uploaded it as the "Balinese Digital Library" on archive.org, giving free access to images of nearly 3,000 lontar. The present item is one instance of that corpus.

OCR quality is low (Latin-script OCR over Balinese-script photographs), so the file serves as image evidence rather than readable text.

## Notes for the book chapter
- Confirms a second named maarti manuscript of a canonical OJ epic (Ariwangsa = Arjunawiwaha) publicly viewable online, held in Denpasar with Singaraja provenance.
- Together with the Bharatayuda Maarti and the Balai Bahasa Arjuna Wiwaha Maarti, shows the two most-copied classics (Bharatayuddha, Arjunawiwaha) both attested in maarti form - consistent with Creese's popularity ranking.

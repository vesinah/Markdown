## Key content from retrieved passages

- WikiLontar 2021: program by Komunitas Wikimedia Denpasar (Jan-Apr 2021), metadata + catalogue + conservation for community-owned lontar in Klungkung, Gianyar, Buleleng, Denpasar; 806 cakep documented, 606 rescued from deterioration; funded by Wikimedia Foundation; run by 13 volunteers.
- Penyuluh Bahasa Bali (Balinese language instructors) recorded 25,106 cakep lontar across Bali's 9 districts/cities in 2016-2018 (Ardiyasa 2021); most community lontar damaged; owners often cannot read archaic Balinese/Javanese script.
- Images uploaded to Wikimedia Commons; re-typing on ban.wikisource (WikiPustaka, collaboration Panlex.org + Wikimedia Foundation) provides Latin transliteration options; Wikidata mapping of lontar locations (example: collector Sugi Lanus, 161 lontar plotted).
- Restriction culture around reading: formula haywa wera / aywa wera ("don't spread it") in e.g. Tegesing Wisik Warah; owners fear misfortune from careless reading - explains why many lontar are kept unread.
- Named example files include a Maarti manuscript image: https://ban.wikisource.org/wiki/Kaca:Bali-lontar-Sawan-Bharata_Yudha_Maarti.jpg (Sawan village Bharata Yudha Maarti), plus Tojan Kakawin Bharatayudha and Sukawati Kakawin Bharata Yuda images; Wikidata item Q104529414 cited for a related lontar.
- History note quoted from Hooykaas 1979 p. 348: government established the foundation Kirtya in 1892 to protect lontar from enthusiastic buyers (note: Gedong Kirtya institution itself was founded 1928 at Singaraja per multiple sources; the 1892 date refers to an earlier protective initiative and needs care in citation).

## Content

Catalogue record created under WikiLontar 2021, a digital cataloguing program run by Komunitas Wikimedia Denpasar, January-April 2021. The same campaign catalogued 142 cakep lontar held at Balai Bahasa Provinsi Bali.

Indonesian description on the page: the manuscript contains 23 sargah (pupuh), beginning with meter Jagaddhita and ending at sargah 23 in meter Tebusol, 14th line of the first stanza; the narrative runs to the episode where Yudistira with Bima, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadewa and Kresna prepares for battle against Karna, first paying respect to Bisma lying on Arjuna's arrow-bed. Themes: loyalty, respect for teachers, moral-spiritual preparation before battle.

Transcription of leaf 01 shows the working arrangement of the maarti format: the page is split [Depan] front side and [Belakang] back side; the Old Javanese stanza text (opening awighnam astu invocation, saŋ śūrām wiha yajñāriŋ samara...) is followed by running Balinese gloss material, and the leaf ends with a dated colophon: iśakā 1814 ... puput hikā nurat sastramamadhu (finished writing), naming mpu Sedah as composer (mpusḍaḥ).

A related village-copy image exists on Wikisource: Bali-lontar-Sawan-Bharata_Yudha_Maarti.jpg (Sawan, Buleleng), referenced in Citrawati/Putra 2024.

## Key content

### Abstract points
Kakawin Arjuna Wiwaha is popular in Bali as a functional text accompanying Panca Yadnya ceremonies; copying has long been ongoing so manuscripts are widely distributed. To help Balinese who cannot read Balinese script and have limited Old Javanese knowledge, lontar kakawin were later published as books equipped with meanings and interpretations in Balinese. This is tied to the mabebasan tradition in which OJ kakawin is interpreted orally by the paneges in Balinese. The kakawin also entered digital media (website lontar kakawin digital and online content).

### Sekaa pasantian definition (footnote 1)
Pasantian derives from santi meaning peace/tranquility; prefix pa- and suffix -an yield Pasantian, a vessel or place for obtaining peace or tranquility (citing Ida Bagus Putu Eka Suadnyana 2020, after Sugriwa). Sekaa pasantian or sekaa mabebasan are literary appreciation groups that diligently perform (menembangkan) and interpret OJ kakawin texts in Panca Yadnya ceremonies; members translate and interpret into Balinese.

### Maarti manuscript described (survey of Pusdok holdings)
At Pusat Dokumentasi (Pusdok) Dinas Kebudayaan Provinsi Bali the author found 11 text variants of Arjuna Wiwaha: 7 in kakawin form, 2 peparikan, 1 illustrated lontar (naskah perasi). Among the 7 kakawin-form ones, one is a "kakawin Arjuna Wiwaha Maarti", i.e. a kakawin complete with its Balinese translation. Quote (translated): maarti kakawin manuscripts usually make it easier for a penembang (kakawin singer) to gloss each stanza of an OJ text, since not all Balinese have good command of Old Javanese. Such a maarti text greatly helps the peneges when performing the kakawin in mabebasan sessions; the author considers the lontar maarti the precursor of printed book editions of the kakawin supplied with Balinese arti/interpretation.

## Part 2: The Wissembourg Priscian Glosses digital edition (OEAW ACDH / IMAFO)

### OEAW resource page (https://www.oeaw.ac.at/acdh/research/dh-research-infrastructure/resources/editions/priscian-glosses, fetched 2026-08-26)

Verbatim scope statement: "The Wissembourg Priscian Glosses provides a digital edition and an interpretation of the roughly 2.000 Latin and 150 Old High German glosses on the first sixteen books of Priscian's Ars Grammatica contained in the manuscript Wolfenbüttel, Herzog August Bibliothek, Cod. Guelf. 50 Weissenburg."

### Edition site state (https://priscian-glosses.acdh-dev.oeaw.ac.at/index.html, fetched 2026-08-26)

- Self-declared status: "Beta-Version".
- How-to-cite string published on site: Grifoni, Cinzia, The Wissembourg Priscian Glosses. The Study of Latin at ninth-century Wissembourg. Digital Edition of MS Wolfenbüttel, Herzog August Bibliothek, Cod. Guelf. 50 Weiss, Vienna 2024.
- Site sections live: Home; The Project (About, Team); Digital Edition (Introduction, Edition, Interactive Edition, Simplified Edition, Statistics, Technical Documentation); Old High German Glosses; Output & Events; Contact.
- Hosting caveat: edition still served from an acdh-DEV subdomain (priscian-glosses.acdh-dev.oeaw.ac.at), i.e. pre-production infrastructure, although publicly reachable and citable per its own citation string.
- Data repository: https://github.com/acdh-oeaw/matc ("Critical Edition of the Wissembourg Priscian Glosses"), default branch master, repo actively pushed (last push 2026-08-26T02:46:09Z, size 4701 KB).
- Project context: FWF-funded project "Margins at the Centre" (OEAW IMAFO, Historical Identity Research unit); ACDH-CH technical partner.

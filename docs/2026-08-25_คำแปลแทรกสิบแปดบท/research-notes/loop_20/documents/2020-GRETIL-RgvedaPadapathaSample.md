## Notation system observed (annotation-on-page before any commentary)

- Vertical bar `|` = pada boundary (word-by-word segmentation).
- Double hyphen `--` = explicit sandhi split marker showing the fused saṃhitā form inside the pada stream (e.g. dive--dive).
- Single hyphen `-` = internal morphological/compound split retained in the pada entry (puraḥ-hitam, ratna-dhātamam, vīravat-tamam, citraśravaḥ-tamaḥ).
- Particle `iti` used as a quotation/recitation cue inside the pada text.
- Addressing scheme in legacy header: RV_aśṭaka:adhyāya/varga and RV_maṇḍala,sūkta.verse double referencing.

## Context from Peacock 2025 (JAIS 25.3, open access, full PDF harvested this loop)

- The Rylands codex is trilingual: complete Arabic Qur'an plus complete Persian rendering plus the Turkic interlinear/glossed translation; together with the Tashkent manuscript it is one of two trilingual witnesses among the eight Eastern Turkish interlinear Qur'an manuscripts Peacock censuses.
- The set entered the Crawford collection; per loop_29 records it bears the waqf statement of amir Aytamish (Cairo, c. 1335 or late 14th c.).
- Eckmann's monograph is the foundational linguistic treatment of its Turkic gloss layer ("Middle Turkic"); later scholarship (e.g., the Rylands blog above) keeps alive the classification debate (Khorezmian vs Chagatai vs Qarakhanid).

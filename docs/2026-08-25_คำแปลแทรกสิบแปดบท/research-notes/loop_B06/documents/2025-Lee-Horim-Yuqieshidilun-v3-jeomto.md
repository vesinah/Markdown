## Abstract (English, verbatim)

This study serves as a supplement to the foundational research on the point-attached seokdokkugyol found in volume 3 of the Yuqieshidilun(瑜伽師地論) preserved at Horim Museum. Its primary aim is to provide a more precise description of the existing readings and interpretations. In addition to the 2006 photographic edition, this research makes use of the 2022, 2022(reverse), and 2023 photographs, forming the basis of a three times re-examination of previous readings and interpretations presented in the main discussion. Chapter 2 not only reconsiders existing readings and interpretations but also classifies cases such as newly deciphered symbols, the reading and interpretation of uncertain "?" passages, and positional interpretation analyses, thereby identifying features of the point-attached seokdokkugyol in volume 3 of the Yuqieshidilun previously overlooked. Section 2.1 focuses on the characteristics of point-attached kugyol notation. Based on the observation that kugyol points are inscribed overlapping with character strokes, the study suggests the possibility that the glossators of Horim Museum's Volume 3 and Sungam Museum's Volumes 5 and 8 of the Yuqieshidilun may be different individuals. The Horim manuscript's kugyol points appear in two types: thick and deeply inscribed (typical form) and thin and shallowly inscribed, with the latter tending to serve a supplementary role to the former. Section 2.2 addresses issues related to paper damage. Kugyol points that were previously unidentifiable due to creases and deterioration in earlier photographs have been deciphered using the newly obtained images. Section 2.3 presents the possibility of reading additional symbols, including new boundary lines, "pull-out" lines, and hook points. The coexistence of ink dots(墨點) and carved dots(角筆點) is noted, as well as a fine-brush symbol in the shape of the character 不, likely indicating erroneously written kugyol points. The study also identifies a square marking of unknown function and newly deciphered carved kugyol characters. Chapter 3 provides a summary and proposed refinements. Finally, among the total 166 instances previously marked with "?" in existing readings and interpretations, 139 deciphered cases and 27 undecipherable cases are presented in the Appendix.

## Key points for the manuscript

1. Holding institutions verified: 호림박물관 (권3), 성암박물관 (권5, 권8) - two named museum collections within the 유가사지론 jeomto set.
2. Evidence that multiple glossators/scribes were involved across copies (different point-placement habits) - bears on how annotation practice circulated.
3. Mark-system richness: dual point types with supplementary function; mixed ink-dot and stylus-dot layers; an error-marking symbol shaped like 不; boundary lines, hook points, pull-out lines; one symbol type of unknown function - i.e., the system even encodes scribal self-correction.
4. Progress metric: re-photography resolved 139 of 166 previously unreadable marks (83 percent); 27 remain unreadable - honest state-of-the-art figure for decipherment completeness in this copy.
5. Photographic documentation timeline: 2006, 2022, 2022(reverse), 2023 imaging campaigns show active re-examination of the corpus into the mid-2020s.

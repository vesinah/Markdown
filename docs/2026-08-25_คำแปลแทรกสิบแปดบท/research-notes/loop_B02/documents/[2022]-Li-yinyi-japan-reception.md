# Document: Japanese reception of Xuanying's Yinyi — 729 earliest copying record; Ruiju myōgi shō absorption; Japanese-made yinyi works

- Compiled from sources fetched 2026-08-26 (URLs inline). Verification status per item.

## A. Earliest Japanese copy of Xuanying's Yiqiejing yinyi: 729 CE — VERIFIED

LI Naiqi, 「一切経音義における小字双行について」 (About the small characters in two lines form of Yiqiejingyinyi), Journal of Indian and Buddhist Studies 70-3, pp. 1228-1233 (2022). DOI 10.4259/ibk.70.3_1228 (OA). Author's official publication page fetched today, abstract states verbatim:

「一切経音義は...唐代の僧侶である玄応が660年頃に編纂したものである。...日本で書写された最古の記録は729年であり、現在10種類以上の日本古写本が残されている。」

= "the oldest record of copying in Japan is 729"; 10+ old Japanese manuscripts survive. Same page documents the manuscript format study: one-line large-character writing vs. two-line small-character format (「一行の大文字」/「二行に分けて小文字」), including hybrid entries — the East Asian small-note convention inside a sound-gloss dictionary.

## B. Ruiju myōgi shō as a Japanese-made dictionary absorbing Yinyi material — VERIFIED

- LI Naiqi, "Regarding Yiqiejingyinyi classical Japanese manuscripts," Hokkaido University Journal of the Faculty of Humanities and Human Sciences 15, pp. 59-65 (2020). https://hdl.handle.net/2115/77527 (OA; abstract fetched): "In the early Inseii period, around 1100 CE, the Ruiju myōgi shō 類聚名義抄 (Zushoryō-bon 図書寮本 manuscript) was compiled by a monk of the Hossō 法相 sect. The Ruiju myōgi shō quotes a total of around 1,300 Chinese character radicals [entries] from Yiqiejingyinyi..., which is the largest such collection." Ten manuscript versions listed by holding institution.
- LI Naiqi 2016, 図書寮本『類聚名義抄』における玄応撰『一切経音義』の依拠テキスト : 『一切経音義』巻第四を中心に, 訓点語と訓点資料 137, pp. 115-132 (listed on the same profile page; body not fetched) — identifies which textual lineage of the Yinyi the Zushoryō-bon depended on for juan 4.
- Kotobank aggregate entry 類聚名義抄 (fetched 2026-08-26, https://kotobank.jp/word/類聚名義抄 ): late-Heian kanji dictionary in 佛/法/僧 three sections, 120 radicals, recording 字音・字義・和訓 with 声点 accent marks on wakun; original compilation by a Hossō monk (11th c., only Zushoryō fragmentary copy survives); revised/expanded late 12th c. by Shingon monks (Kanchiin-bon complete codex: ~32,000 head-graphs, >40,000 wakun); called 集大成 ("grand synthesis") of 音義・辞書・訓点本.

## C. Japanese-made imitations beyond Ruiju myōgi shō — VERIFIED CLUSTER

1) 新訳八十巻華厳経音義私記 (Shin'yaku hachijūkan Kegon-kyō ongi shiki) — private record glossing the new (80-fascicle) Huayan translation:
   - Listed among early yinyi works appearing "奈良時代末期から平安初期" in kotobank's 【訓】 essay excerpt (fetched via the 類聚名義抄 page).
   - Scholarship cluster verified via NDL Search (fetched): Jia Zhi 賈智 2013 PhD dissertation 『新訳華厳経音義私記』の研究：私記所引の字様をめぐって (Univ. of Tokyo); Miao Yu 苗昱 & Liang Xiaohong 梁曉虹 《新譯華嚴經音義私記》俗字研究 (2014) and 「新譯大方廣佛華嚴經音義私記」整理與研究 (Fenghuang, 2014); Ikeda Shōju 池田証寿, 日本辞書史研究：草創と形成 (汲古書院 2024) chapter 『新訳華厳経音義私記』―先行資料の利用法; 古辞書音義集成 第1巻 (汲古書院 1978 facsimile). Related items in same NDL results: 高山寺本『新訳華厳経音義』; 山寺本/石山寺本『大般若経音義』; 本孔雀経音義 (Ishizuka Harumichi article); 倶舎論音義.
2) 四分律音義 — named together with the above in the same kotobank 【訓】 passage as an early Japanese sound-and-meaning work.
3) 浄土三部経音義集 (4 juan) — compiled by the Kamakura Jōdo monk Keiseibō Shinrui 敬西房信瑞 信瑞纂:
   - Compiler hanzi VERIFIED via NDL Search records (fetched): 「浄土三部経音義集：4巻　信瑞纂 [出版者不明] 1900」; Taishō zokuzōkyō placement 「淨土三部經音義集（四卷）〔cf. Nos. 360,365,366〕日本 信瑞」 (大正新脩大蔵経 第57巻 contents); 浄土宗全書 続 vol.4 listing 「浄土三部経音義集(信瑞撰)」.
   - Modern study: Maeshima Shinya 前島信也, 敬西房信瑞の研究：鎌倉浄土教典籍論 (法藏館 2021) — TOC includes 『浄土三部経音義集』―信瑞の宋代仏教文化の受容 ("Shinrui's reception of Song Buddhist culture").
   - Comparative study (loop_08 cross-ref): MAEJIMA 2019, "Jōdosanbukyō-ongi-shū compiled by Shinzui and Yiqiejing yinyi compiled by Xuanying," doi 10.5845/bukkyobunka.2018.27_111 — Crossref romanizes the compiler "Shinzui"; NDL hanzi 信瑞 now fixes the graph form.
4) 金光明最勝王経音義 — formation studied by LI Naiqi (talk title 『金光明最勝王経音義』成立過程の再検討, 日本語学会 2025 fall meeting, listed on the Nagoya profile page) — evidence of an ongoing research program on Japan-composed butten yinyi.
5) Programmatic confirmation: Li Naiqi's invited lecture 日本撰述仏典音義研究 ("Studies of Buddhist yinyi composed in Japan," Fudan University, Sept 2025) confirms 日本撰述仏典音義 as an established category.

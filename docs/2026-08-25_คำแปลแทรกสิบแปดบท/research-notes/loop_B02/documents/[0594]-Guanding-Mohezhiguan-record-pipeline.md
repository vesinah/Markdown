## 1. Standard attribution formula

Every juan-head of the Mohe zhiguan carries the formula (x20 occurrences of 「門人灌頂記」):

「隋天台智者大師說　門人灌頂記」

= "Spoken by the Sui Tiantai Zhizhe Dashi (Zhiyi); recorded by his disciple Guanding." Same pattern in Xuanyi (T33n1716): head metadata 「隋 智顗說」, juan heads 「天台智者大師說」 x20, opening preface signed 「沙門灌頂述」; Wenju (T34n1718): 「天台智者大師說」 x35.

## 2. Guanding's own preface to the Mohe zhiguan = a reportatio report

「止觀明靜，前代未聞。智者，大隋開皇十四年四月二十六日，於荊州玉泉寺，一夏敷揚、二時慈霔，雖樂說不窮，纔至『見境』，法輪停轉，後分弗宣。」

Facts verifiable from this sentence: the lectures were delivered at Yuquan Temple 玉泉寺 (Jingzhou) beginning Kaihuang 14/4/26 (= 594 CE); expounded through one summer in two daily sessions ("一夏敷揚、二時慈霔"); the oral exposition broke off mid-course at the chapter "Contemplating the Realm" (見境), so "the latter part was never proclaimed" (法輪停轉，後分弗宣). This is explicit evidence that the text preserves a lecture series, fixed in writing by the attending disciple — structurally identical to Latin reportationes of scholastic lectures.

## 3. Explicit "private record" statement inside the Wenju tradition (T34n1718)

A Tang-era colophon passage embedded in the fetched text states:

「自智者弘法三十餘年，獨有《維摩疏》，隋朝奉煬帝勅，撰此之玄文，迺是灌頂法師私記，合二十卷，非智不彈，斯言允矣。及其滅後一百餘載，至唐天寶中歲在戊子...」

— i.e., the Xuanyi was "Guanding's private record" (灌頂法師私記) in twenty juan. Term 私記 is the East Asian exact analogue of Latin reportatio/private lecture-notes.

## 4. Guanding as author of Zhiyi's biography

T50n2050 head: 「No. 2050 隋天台智者大師別傳　門人灌頂撰」 — the same disciple both recorded the lectures and composed the hagiography.

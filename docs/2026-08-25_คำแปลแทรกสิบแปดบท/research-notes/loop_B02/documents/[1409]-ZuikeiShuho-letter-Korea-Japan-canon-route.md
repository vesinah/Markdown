## A. Zuikei Shūhō 瑞渓周鳳 — person and diplomatic compendium VERIFIED

Kotobank aggregate entry 瑞渓周鳳 (fetched 2026-08-26, https://kotobank.jp/word/瑞渓周鳳 ):

- Dates 1391/1392-1473 (Nipponica: 1391-1473; 日本人名大辞典: 明徳2=元中8 生まれ, d. 文明5 = 1473).
- Mid-Muromachi Rinzai monk (Gōun Sanjin 臥雲山人); served three terms as sōroku shi 僧録司 (Rokuon-in tally master); trusted by shoguns Ashikaga Yoshinori/Yoshimasa; "幕府の外交に参与し" and "明(中国)への国書の起草もおこなった" — drafted diplomatic correspondence.
- Editor of the Zenrin kokuhōki 善隣国宝記 (diplomatic document collection, 3 juan); diary Goun nichenroku; sekō anthology etc.

This corroborates the Sin 2024 claim that the Ōei 16 (=1409) letter requesting the canon from Chosŏn is preserved in Zenrin kokuhōki compiled by Zuikei Shūhō. The letter text itself was not separately fetched this loop (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน beyond Sin 2024 + the biographical corroboration above).

## B. Route chain as verified across fetched sources (summary)

1. Huilin 慧琳 Yinyi completed 807 (T54n2128, 100 juan); lost in China after Northern Song (kotobank 一切経音義 entry, loop_08; Sin 2024).
2. Preserved via Liao/Qidan canon into Koryŏ; sought by Koryŏ already in Zhou Xiande era 954-960 when no print existed (Sin 2024, De Gruyter OA, doi 10.1515/9783111322599-003).
3. Included in the re-carved Koryŏ Chaejo Taejanggyŏng 高麗再雕大藏經 (Sin 2024).
4. Canon sent to Japan on repeated request — documented by Zuikei Shūhō's 1409 letter in Zenrin kokuhōki (Sin 2024; person/work verified via kotobank as above).
5. Copies kept at Kenninji 建仁寺 (Kyoto) and Zōjōji 増上寺 (Tokyo); print from Kenninji copy acquired by Yang Shoujing 楊守敬, reintroduced to China late Qing (Sin 2024).
6. Companion volume Xilin 希麟 Xu Yiqiejing yinyi (987, Liao): Korea -> Japan, first printed in Japan 1737, returned to China via 1912 Pinjia Jingshe printing (Theobald, chinaknowledge.de, loop_14).

## C. Additional Korean-route datapoint fetched today (Li Naiqi 2023 IBK abstract)

Li 2023 (思渓蔵『一切経音義』について, doi 10.4259/ibk.71.3_1128, OA abstract fetched from Nagoya profile page) states the Sixi zang 思渓蔵 print is valuable 「高麗再雕本を底本とする大正蔵の対校本として」 — i.e., modern editors treat the Koryo re-carving as a base text against which Song prints are collated: concrete evidence that the Koryo canon remained the reference line for yinyi texts reaching East Asian philology.

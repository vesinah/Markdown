## Pressmark systems (verbatim from CEToM Edition guidelines)

"Many fragments are known by more than one pressmark. In general, we refer to fragments using the most recent, unified systems. The most important are: IOL and Or for the London collection; PK for the Paris Pelliot collection; SI for the St. Petersburg collection; THT for the Berlin Turfan collection."

### THT 527 record (fetched in full)

- Find spot: Kocho; expedition code T III D 6, T III D 1; Berlin Turfan Collection.
- Language: Skt.; TB; classical stage, classical script. Form: pothi, ink on paper, 5 lines per side (a/b).
- Title per CEToM: "Bilingual list of doctrinal terms"; genre Literary / Doctrine / prose.
- Sample transliteration showing interwoven Sanskrit lemmata and Tocharian equivalents (verbatim extract):
  a2 /// ca ksu ra ni [t]o /// /// ya ta - e ka ntse ma -e ka t sa nn-e s( )p ///
  a3 /// su ... bhiksy[o]r yoni so manasi kurva /// /// lsko ne ya maske manentse - ca ksu ra [n]o ///
  b4 kna - i rya[p]e /// /// [ka] lpa ya ti - rai tta te - ca n[k]ra ma
  b5 sa m- pa dya te - te /// /// no - ne -ya ma sate - vi vi dh- nya rcis(s)i
  Identifiable Sanskrit items include caksur-anita-, avadhatsva, srotra-, bhiksor..., yatha samahite, kalpayati, sankram(a)-, sampadyate, vividhany arcis(i)-; TB forms such as ekantse mā ekatsaññe, yamaskemanentse, raittate alternate with them within the lines.
- Commentary reference: Couvreur 1968: 277 — Walter Couvreur, "Zu einigen Sanskrit-Kutschischen Listen von Stichwortern aus dem Catusparisatsutra, Dasottarasutra und Nidanasamyukta," in Pratidanam (The Hague/Paris: Mouton, 1968), 275-282.
- Edition: Sieg and Siegling 1953, Tocharische Sprachreste, Sprache B, Heft 2, Fragmente Nr. 71-633 (ed. Werner Thomas). Gottingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht.
- Translations cited: Schmidt 1974 (K.T. Schmidt PhD thesis, Die Gebrauchsweisen des Mediums im Tocharischen, Gottingen) at a1 (25), b4 (345-346n4).
- Online access listed by CEToM: IDP (THT 527) and TITUS (titus.fkidg1.uni-frankfurt.de/texte/tocharic/thtframe.htm).

### A 385 record (fetched in full)

- Find spot: Bezeklik; expedition code T III M 170.2; object = manuscript A 384-386; pothi, late script.
- Title: "Abhidharmakosa, Sanskrit terms with Tocharian explanation."
- CEToM's own English translation shows the glossary/commentary character — Sanskrit lemmata embedded in Tocharian A explanatory sentences (verbatim samples):
  a3 "The dharma appropriate for the activity (lit. rising) of the mental consciousness is vicara."
  a3+ "What is negligence?" — a4 "The not practicing of good dharmas." [...]
  b2 "What is non-negligence? The practicing of good dharmas. The dharma opposite of negligence."

### Three gloss records fetched in full

1. SHT 412.32 (Skt.; TA; Shorchuk, Handschriftenhohle, T III So 33,34):
   - TA "nom" glosses Skt. adhivaca(na?) 'appellation, name' (per Sander/Waldschmidt 1980: 64).
   - TA "ma cas" glosses Skt. nemam 'not him'; commentary: "The gloss obviously clarifies the sandhi form nemam for na imam."
   - Reference: Peyrot 2014, "Notes on Tocharian glosses and colophons in Sanskrit manuscripts I," Tocharian and Indo-European Studies 15: 131-79.

2. THT 4083.d (Skt.; TA; Murtuk; T III M 132/143/145):
   - TA verbal glosses on a Sanskrit leaf: klawastra "(s)he calls"; sarka pat "or he burnt" (discussion of tsark- 'torment'); commentary notes the Tocharian glossator "mistakenly glosses the 3pl. of the Sanskrit text" and misunderstood a Sanskrit compound tense (pratiktam read as isolated participle). Underlying Sanskrit edition: Waldschmidt 1968 (SHT Teil 2; Maghandika episode).

3. THT 4081 (TB; Kizil Ming-Oy, Rothkuppelraum):
   - "The recto side contains a Sanskrit Pratimoksa text, the verso side contains two lines in TB on top of the leaf, which is written by a different hand than the recto side in a clumsy, uneven ductus; on the bottom of the otherwise blank side one finds another TB clause written again by a different hand in normal book-style ductus." Text: nesaa p(p)anktee "The Buddhas exist."
   - Reading credited to K.T. Schmidt apud Waldschmidt 1971 (SHT Teil 3: 97-98).
   - Key survey cited: Malzahn 2007b, "A preliminary survey of the Tocharian glosses in the Berlin Turfan Collection," in Instrumenta Tocharica (Heidelberg: Winter), 301-19.

4. SHT 395 (Skt.; TB; Shorchuk, T III S 64): subgenre "Gloss; Writing exercise": verso carries Karmavacana in Sanskrit (ed. Hartel 1956: no. 47); the TB lines edited by Ogihara 2014 (TIES 15: 115-116) include a line with aksharas written upside down; three further lines with random aksharas by a different hand.

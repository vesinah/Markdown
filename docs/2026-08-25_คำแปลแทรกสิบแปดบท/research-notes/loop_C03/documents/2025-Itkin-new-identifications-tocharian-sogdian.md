# Document harvest: new Tocharian and Sogdian fragment identifications and announcements, 2020-2026

Loop: C03 (Phase C), harvested 2026-08-26. Target 4 of this loop: any NEW gloss-related discovery announcements from Turfanforschung and adjacent projects since 2020.

## Headline result

No 2020-2026 announcement found that reports a NEWLY DISCOVERED interlinear-gloss fragment as such. What the period does deliver: (a) systematic re-identification of unedited Tocharian A material in Berlin (Itkin et al. 2025); (b) new Sanskrit-Tocharian bilingual texts (TIES 19 items; THT 1846); (c) first editions and recoveries of lost Sogdian fragments, including Buddhist ones (Morano 2024; Benkato 2024; Colditz 2024; Reck 2020); (d) a dedicated study of the one famous mixed TA/TB gloss leaf SHT 4438 (Pan 2021 - see companion documents). The nearest thing to a gloss-specific advance is therefore interpretive (Pan on Peyrot), not a new find.

## Sogdian side

1. Enrico Morano, "Where the Demons Fell. A Manichaean Sogdian Manuscript in Sogdian Script from Mani's Book of the Giants", Journal of Iranian Linguistics 1.1 (2024): 8-20; DOI https://doi.org/10.46991/jil/2024.01.01 (published 2024-11-13). Abstract: TWO UNPUBLISHED Sogdian-script fragments of the Berlin Turfan collection, glassed together, same page, contain a cosmogonic text on the fall of the demons/archons to the four directions and part of the creation-of-protoplasts myth by Saqlun and Pesus; proposed as part of the Sogdian version of Mani's Book of Giants.
2. Adam Benkato, "Lost Turfan fragments from the Nachlass of W.B. Henning", Bulletin of the School of Oriental and African Studies; DOI https://doi.org/10.1017/S0041977X24000375 (2024). Recovered identifications include: Buddhist-Sogdian no. 10123 (find siglum T i alpha, King Prasenajit narrative) known only from Henning-era photographs; no. 13921 now = So 13924 (Mahaparinirvanasutra, T II D 63 confirmed from glass label); nos. 14094-96 now = So 14091(1)/14092(1)/So 14093(1) - the last being OLD TURKIC within a Sogdian-numbered plate; M501a = M252a. Companion volume: Iris Colditz 2024, A Sogdian Version of the Mahayana Mahaparinirvana-sutra in Pustaka Format: Fragments from the Berlin Turfan Collection and the Otani Collection Kyoto (Berliner Turfantexte 53).
3. Christiane Reck, "The Sogdians and Their Religions in Turfan...", Entangled Religions 11.6 (2020); DOI https://doi.org/10.46586/er.11.2020.9222 - FIRST PUBLICATION of fragments So 16102(2) (T III S 23/501) and So 16146, religious affiliation unresolved between Buddhist/Manichaean/Zoroastrian readings; also surveys multilingual colophon evidence (Old Uyghur names beside Sogdian) and Yoshida's conclusion that Turkicized Sogdophones wrote the Turfan materials.
4. Enrico Morano, "Fragments from a Sogdian Cosmogonical Manuscript in Manichaean Script", in M. Macuch and A. Zeini (eds.), Deciphering the Illegible. Festschrift in Honour of Dieter Weber (2024) - complete first edition of all fragments of a lost Sogdian Manichaean cosmogony book of the Berlin collection (announcement: https://sisrweb.it/2024/11/26/fragments-from-a-sogdian-cosmogonical-manuscript-in-manichaean-script/).

## Gloss-relevance assessment

For the book's Silk Road chapter these items update the B03 picture: the Berlin pipeline is still actively converting unknown fragments into edited text (TA overview 2025; So-series recoveries 2024), and the Sogdian Mahaparinirvanasutra corpus now extends to the Otani collection - relevant because Yoshida's non-Chinese-derived Sogdian signatures (B03 finding) sit in exactly this Buddhist-Sogdian stream. None adds a new WORD-LEVEL interlinear gloss case; the Old-Turkish-or-Tocharian uncertain glosses recorded by Malzahn 2007b (SHT 1121, 1155, 1157, 1159, 1181 - see companion document) remain the open file for future identification work.

Not opened this session (recorded honestly): individual BBAW Jahresbericht PDFs for 2021-2025 (the academy yearbook sections where Turfanforschung reports new identifications annually); the turfan.bbaw.de magazine PDF fetched via search rendering proved to be an older general brochure.

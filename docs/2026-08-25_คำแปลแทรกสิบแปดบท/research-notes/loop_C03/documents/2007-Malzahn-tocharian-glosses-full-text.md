## Chapter structure (observed)

1. Scope opening (p. 301, verbatim): "The following survey is based on volumes 1-9 of the edition of the Sanskrit manuscripts from the Berlin Turfan collection (= SHT). The descriptions of the Tocharian glosses and annotations generally follow those of the respective SHT entries (unless otherwise stated)."
2. Footnote crediting the Paris material: "Some Tocharian glosses contained in Sanskrit texts from the Paris collection were mentioned by Couvreur, 1970: Sanskrit manuscript 71.080 (Pravaranasutra) displays the TB gloss //// ndriske (ka)lpyare kes pyakle silne mi //// (p. 182); PK NS 14 a 2-4 and PK NA 17 + 900 b 2 (p. 179ff.)."
3. Main section "Berlin glosses": catalogue-style entries ordered by SHT volume and catalogue number, from SHT 1 (1965), 146 up to SHT 9 (2004), 3082, each entry recording find-spot siglum (MQR, MQ, M, S, or unknown), Sanskrit genre, plate references, the POSITION of the Tocharian writing (under line / over line / between lines / string-hole space / blank verso), transliteration where readable (many supplied by Klaus T. Schmidt apud SHT, others newly transliterated by Malzahn from facsimiles), plus her own linguistic commentary and translation.
4. Unedited addendum: "Texts with SHT numbers not yet edited" - SHT 4438 (= Mainz 651), with its gloss list and pointer to the Digital Turfan Archive photograph (URL given in 2007 form: bbaw.de/forschung/turfanforschung/dta/mainz/dta_mainz_index.htm); plus a list of Brahmi charts carrying Tocharian foreign signs (SHT 1,244; 1,445; 1,445,17 R; 1,446; 1,488; 1,794; cf. Couvreur 1965).
5. References (Bernhard 1965; Couvreur 1965, 1970; Hartmann 1988; Hu-von Hinuber 1994; Sander 1994; Schmidt 1974, 1983, 1990, 1994, 2004; Thomas 1985; Waldschmidt 1963; SHT vols.; SWTF; TochSprR(B)).
6. Closing word indexes: a TA Index and a TB Index, each mapping every published gloss form to its SHT catalogue number(s) (confirms the Preface claim "includes a word index").

## Position-and-function typology derivable from the entries (Phase-C synthesis; labels below are our analytical grouping, not chapter headings)

A. Continuous Sanskrit-Tocharian bilingual texts (stanza-by-stanza or line-by-line):
- SHT 1,170 (MQR, Mahaparinirvanasutra leaf): Sanskrit-TB bilingual of Udanavarga 1.18 from line 6 onward ("not in Bernhard 1965"; plate in Waldschmidt 1963 pl. CLVIII d).
- SHT 7,1656 (MQ, Posadha calendar): Sanskrit-TB bilingual calendar (transliteration Sander/Schmidt apud SHT).
- SHT 8,1815 = THT 3187 (maybe Qizil): Sanskrit-TB bilingual, probably magical (transliteration Else Lueders and Schmidt).
- SHT 9,2198 (?): small Sanskrit-TB bilingual; TB fragments translate lost Sanskrit words (kru+ for yada; tako+ for bhavet; teyknesa lek(e) for atha nityam; makte makte for yatha yatha).
- SHT 9,2348 (S): recto Sanskrit-TB bilingual ("... ts(a) kaklawats jambudvipne ... 'in the Jambudvipa mentioned by ...'"); VERSO carries a Sogdian text - a trilingual-object datum relevant to the book's Silk Road chapter.
- SHT 9,3082: Sanskrit-TA bilingual (nyani / sakyik / ma lyutar=nantsuts / wutatsu bhumim mudra ...).

B. Interlinear word/phrase glosses beneath or between lines (the dominant category):
- SHT 5,1030 (M, Vinaya): TA naken ka under verso(=recto) l.2; TA pla glosses Skt. mantrayanti; TA hanasonka 'pain of parting' glosses Skt. avapravraj.
- SHT 5,1033 (M, Posadhavastu): between recto ll.1-2 TA kunti la ('pot'); between verso ll.1-2 TA tsununtsi (unclear); TA ywarksa 'in the middle, in between' glosses Skt. (a)ntara; TA sukrane pe oki glosses Skt. kakapadaka; TA sul 'it has to be sewn in between like a crowfoot(suture)' glosses Skt. datavya (Schmidt 1994: 270).
- SHT 5,1109 (M, sutra?): "Tocharian glosses in neat writing between all lines"; rwatar 'he pulls out' glosses Skt. chid; a[s](p)awatte glosses anejya- 'immovable'.
- SHT 5,1098 (M, Vinayavibhangasarvastivada): short TA glosses on six fragments; katkasi (katk- 'rejoice') belongs to Skt. hasyapreksya; klawatra 'he proclaims' glosses pathanti; TA pats glosses irsyako 'jealous'; wasko glosses asudanda-.
- SHT 6,1281: TA yrka yamimatse glossing satkuryam; t(a)n(e-wka)yo karsorats glossing iti matva; lokik.
- SHT 6,1335: TA yrkamas or yrka sasts between recto ll.3-4; orto glossing ud- between verso ll.2-3.
- SHT 6,1432: TA ataskaces kakmu under recto l.2; TA pats rapluneyasala (corrected per SWTF kulapata to 'with the rooting out of the family') under verso l.3.
- SHT 6,1478: TA (pu)k yrasara pakeple ('fully around'); yamuratse glossing bahulikrtam.
- SHT 6,1536 (Vinayavibhangasarvastivada): several TA glosses incl. aluts maskatse glossing pravarayanti (with Malzahn's remark that Schmidt's rendering is strange because TA mask- 'to be' is always intransitive even in Pt III); tri under verso l.3.
- SHT 8,1818: TA msenuntse, sne-tirits, sprakalune under verso ll.3, 4, 6.
- SHT 8,1821: several TA glosses (trikarsalune akilune tstats; tisreyo; tsam al akontse markampallantwatse; wessitsuneyyatse).
- SHT 8,1836: Toch. gloss antarabhavats under recto l.3.
- SHT 8,1868: several TB glosses: menaka(nts) 'simile'; ma tarkananatre '(it) is not to be uttered'.
- SHT 8,1916: TA pakwartse 'bad' between recto ll.2-3 referring to Skt. radhi (sic, for artha- class words).
- SHT 8,1983: Skt. caravartan glossed by TA tiryan swartwaluneyo 'by behaving the right way'; rupatan(?) by TA luram 'semen'; vijnaptiya by TA prakseñc 'they ask for'; vidhuna by TA lo patwammam 'stretching away'; note on word-internal virama parallels in TB B 333-335.
- SHT 9,2053: TA ts=lot(re) under recto l.4; TA kapsasi watnot(?) atsa under verso l.1; TA kasits under verso l.3.
- SHT 9,2054 + 2250 (S, Asvaghosa, Buddhacarita): "several TB glosses read by Klaus T. Schmidt apud Hartmann, 1988, 58ff." with the interline assignments spelled out: SHT 2250 between recto ll.1-2 (sanma)nmamem tarkatsitse pe(lykits); between recto ll.3-4 wartse vajropants ompol[s]ko[ntse]; between verso ll.1-2 no teta spelke prerentsa; between verso ll.2-3 retke kakawatse saswetse rants; between verso ll.3-4 katkemane ipretse; SHT 2054 aimasu between recto ll.2-3; kawasesa between recto ll.3-4; [p]rants pkte rants between verso ll.1-2; pelkim walo taka kamate between verso ll.2-3; yamate between verso ll.3-4.
- SHT 9,2082 (Pañcalakasutra of Madhyamagama): ad tena Toch. [la]sa; ad (sa)mudacarati TB [so]ts.
- SHT 9,2195: TB ekanñentantse 'of the possessions' glosses Skt. vrttisamvidhanam under recto l.1.
- SHT 4438 (= Mainz 651, unedited in 2007): TA ma yal ad Skt. akaryan; TA karpisyo ad anaryain; TA tritakluneyo wlar ad trigartahatam; TA karsnal ad jneyam; kantantuyo ad satasam; TB sainñe (rendered sai11e) ad Skt. jagat.
- Kośabhāṣya cluster SHT 1704, 1708, 1709, 1738, 1743: extensive TB gloss material with readings/translations according to Schmidt apud SHT and Schmidt 1990: 474ff (long strings quoted in the chapter, e.g. upali pañikti kaññim preksa 'Upali asked the Buddha, the teacher'; yoktsi yatam 'they can be drunk'; commentary-style expansions such as 'they [sura and maireya] are not ready yet, or are already gone; there is no brandy').

C. Glosses written ABOVE the line (placement datum): SHT 5,1081 (M, Vinaya) - "Tocharian glosses are to be found over the recto lines 1 and 2, and under the verso lines 3 and 4"; restored reading TB (ka)rmapatha maskatar 'is the way of karma' over recto l.1; (?)spawam under recto l.1; TB maksu 'which' under verso l.3. Companion piece SHT 5,1109 may belong to the same manuscript.

D. Labels, subscriptions and colophon-like additions:
- SHT 1,146 (MQR, the only complete Sanskrit pustaka from Qizil): three lines of TB under the Sanskrit text on the verso, transliterated AND translated by Malzahn from facsimile pl. 26 - a water spell ending "...Thereby one who is evil-minded is becoming friendly" (namo prasannamanasa ... svaha; TB mamanto-palsko ... takarke masketra), with grammatical commentary (masketra as Class III middle for expected masketar; prasunna for prasanna; genitive instead of dative; takarke as the adjective that "often glosses or translates precisely Skt. prasanna").
- SHT 1,552 (M, Sanghabhedavastu): in the free space left for the STRING HOLE on the verso, in smaller differing ductus, TB vinaiññe kluttar 'Kludravastu belonging to the Vinaya' (according to Franz Bernhard apud SHT) - a title label.
- SHT 5,1028 (M, Vinayavibhanga): Tocharian dharmawina written in the string-hole space BY ANOTHER HAND.
- SHT 3,851 (MQR, Pratimoksa): three TB lines recto with unclear relation to the Sanskrit verso; line 3 nesam pankte 'The Buddha exists'.
- SHT 3,872 (MQ, glorification of a donor): TB subscription with unclear relationship to the preceding Sanskrit text (silabhatriske atatane Lakaki tutse lotri Lakatar paikka).
- SHT 7,1621 (MQR): TB colophon talattallonasa arare ...
- SHT 1,768 (Mahavadanasutra): line 17 is a Tocharian verse addressed to readers ('You (pl.) should know that we have written these Udanas for Indr(a)s...' per Schmidt 1974: 451, haplology indrasena for indrasenentse).
- SHT 9,1998: Tocharian subscription (according to Else Lueders) without transliteration.
- SHT 9,2011: fragment b has TB piyye trau / yva on the verso among Sanskrit lines.
- SHT 9,2030 (Mahamayurividyarajni): verso l.4 contains Toch. suklyika 'the Sogdian' (Schmidt apud SHT, referring to Skt. suluka/sulika) - an ethnic term embedded in a Sanskrit line.
- SHT 9,2550: single line TB ko# lake.
- SHT 9,2625: verso nmasa dirgh ams emended to '...Dirgha by name'.

E. Reused leaves and non-gloss writing (negative controls within the corpus itself):
- SHT 3,827 (MQ, medical): tiny fragment b with TB na tekamma tsa; Sanskrit recto medical, far older ductus (this is the entry CEToM cites at p. 303).
- SHT 3,833 (MQ, Pratityasamutpada): bottom of blank verso carries a brush-written line "probably completely in Tocharian ... to be read upside down, i.e., we clearly have to do with a reused manuscript", plus stray aksharas under it.
- SHT 3,902 (MQ) = TochSprR(B) 503-505: readings differing between SHT and TochSprR(B).

F. Language-uncertain glosses flagged as "Old Turkish or Tocharian" (directly relevant to the B03 open question about possible Old Turkic interlinear material):
- SHT 5,1121 (Cangavastu): gloss between recto ll.1-2 "in Old Turkish or Tocharian", judged readable as KLansa(n), which "may somehow belong to TA/TB klank- 'to doubt', and somehow gloss Skt. prativahatam 'of the rejected'".
- SHT 5,1155; SHT 5,1157; SHT 5,1159; SHT 7,1181: further glosses explicitly labelled "in Old Turkish or Tocharian", not transliterable from the facsimiles.
Malzahn thus recorded, as early as 2007, five Berlin Sanskrit fragments whose interlinear glosses could not be assigned securely to either language - the honest state of the Old Turkic question before Maue 2009/2010.

G. Non-Tocharian glossing inside the same population (via Pan 2021, see companion document): some glosses on SHT 4438 are IN SANSKRIT (e.g. Skt. aprameyam substituted for atulam on SHT 4438 Ve) - glossing activity on these leaves was multilingual.

H. Script-learning apparatus: Brahmi charts with Tocharian foreign signs listed (see above) - links the gloss corpus to scribal training sheets.

## Quantitative shape

Entries span SHT volumes 1-9 (catalogue numbers 146-3082) plus unedited SHT 4438; roughly forty numbered entries carry Tocharian material, most of it interlinear word glosses in TA or TB, with a minority of continuous bilingual texts and subscriptions. The closing TA and TB word indexes map approximately 60 TA and 100 TB index forms to catalogue numbers (index columns in the render).

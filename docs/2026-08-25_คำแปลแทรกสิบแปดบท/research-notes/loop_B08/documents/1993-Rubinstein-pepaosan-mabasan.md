## Notes for the book chapter

- Closes Loop 15 remaining-gap "exact reference data for Rubinstein 1993 on mabasan": venue is an ethnomusicology Festschrift volume (Forum Ethnomusicologicum 4, Winterthur: Amadeus), not a mainstream philology journal - explains why it never surfaced in Crossref.
- Terminology note: Rubinstein's own title uses "pepaosan" (her spelling Pepeosan), i.e., the oral dual-language performance practice; "mabasan" is the label Creese uses for the reading groups in English-language scholarship. Both refer to the same performative glossing complex; see Ganchrow 2025 document for a current definition of pepaosan.

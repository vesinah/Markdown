## Cross-cutting observations

- Recurring provenance "Gst. Pt. Djelantik, Singaraja" on at least five Pusdok maarti copies suggests one early-20th-century private library as a major feeder of the state collection.
- Leaf counts recorded on cards range 134-311; leaf widths consistently 3,5 cm; lengths 40-60 cm - consistent with the layout constraints argument for above/below glossing rather than interlinear insertion proper.
- Genre spread of attested maarti copies now covers: Ramayana, Arjunawiwaha (x3 incl. Ariwangsa), Bharatayuddha (x2), Arjunawijaya, Wirabadra/Bhomakawya, Smaradahana, Nitisara (x2), plus a kidung-Tantri item. Sutasoma maarti still not individually identified (ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน).

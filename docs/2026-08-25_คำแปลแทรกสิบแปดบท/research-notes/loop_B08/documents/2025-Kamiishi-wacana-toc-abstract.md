## Abstract (as published, key points)

- Object: British Library Add MS 12279 containing Modern Javanese word-for-word and line-for-line "translations" inserted between the lines of the Old Javanese Bharatayuddha.
- Frame: eighteenth-/nineteenth-century Kawi revival in south Central Javanese courts; rewriting Old Javanese works in Modern Javanese renewed access to literary heritage.
- Claim: the manuscript's unique structure reveals translation strategies balancing transmission of meaning with modernization of language; illuminates entwinement of Old Javanese/Modern Javanese, Hindu/Islam, ancient/modern.

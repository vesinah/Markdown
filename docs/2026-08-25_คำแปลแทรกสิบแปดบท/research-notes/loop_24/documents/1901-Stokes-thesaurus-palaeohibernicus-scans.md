## Content (compiled from fetched project pages that cite TPH)

TPH collects the three great bodies of Old Irish glosses together with scholia, prose and verse:
1. Würzburg glosses on the Pauline Epistles (Codex Paulinus Wirziburgensis, Würzburg UB M.p.th.f. 12) — TPH vol. 1 presents 3,501 glosses with Irish content; relatively diplomatic editing retains orthographic features of the manuscripts.
2. Milan glosses on a Latin commentary on the Psalms (Milan, Biblioteca Ambrosiana, C 301 inf.) — TPH i 7–483, the largest corpus within the glosses.
3. St Gall glosses on Priscian (St Gallen, Stiftsbibliothek 904).

The Vienna Milan Glosses Project states the glosses are "almost the only testimonies of Old Irish from contemporaneous manuscripts", so their language shows no interference from Middle or Modern Irish.

A Supplement to Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus appears in the 1975 reprint of vol. 2, pp. 423–506 (cited by wurzburg.ie as "TPH Supplement").

## Notes on use
- Modern digital editions (wurzburg.ie; stgallpriscian.ie; Vienna databases) take TPH as their base text but incorporate corrections (e.g., the Vienna project's ml_corrections files record errata to Thes. Pal.; where they diverge, the revised version should be followed).
- Doyle, McCrae & Downey (2019): "All three sets of glosses have been collected in the two-volume Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus (Stokes and Strachan, 1901, 1903), where the relatively diplomatic editing of the text has retained orthographic features and information from the original manuscript content" — including scriptio continua-style spacing anomalies.

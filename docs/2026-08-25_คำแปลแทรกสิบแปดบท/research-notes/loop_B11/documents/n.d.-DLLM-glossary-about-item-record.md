## Collection scale (About page)

- PLMP (Preservation of Lao Manuscripts Programme, Ministry of Information & Culture, German Foreign Office support 1992-2004): surveyed holdings of over 800 monasteries across all provinces; approx. 86,000 texts / 368,000 fascicles preserved; inventory datasheets in hard copy at the National Library of Laos.
- Microfilm corpus: ~12,000 selected texts, almost 500,000 frames (each frame = 6-8 palm-leaf pages, i.e. some 3-4 million recorded manuscript pages). Master copy: Lao National Film Archive and Video Centre; working copies: National Library of Laos and Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin.
- Selection criteria: historico-cultural importance, cultural diversity/regional representation, age (all mss over 150 years), quality; priority to extra-canonical literature.
- Pre-history documented on the same page: Finot's Recherches sur la litterature laotienne (BEFEO 17.5, 1917) with the Liste generale (Bibliotheque Royale de Luang Prabang, Meiller catalogue, 1,181 entries; EFEO library 338 entries); Lafont's Inventaire des Manuscrits des Pagodes du Laos (BEFEO 52.2, 1965; 83 monasteries: 13 LPQ, 25 Vientiane, 45 Campasak); Cœdès' 1966 Copenhagen Royal Library catalogue of 116 mss (23 in Pali + Lan Na script); Chanthabouly Buddhist Council survey 1934-36 under Chao Phetsarat.

## Bilingual/bitext character (About + Glossary)

- "The majority of Lao manuscripts are from the Theravada Buddhist tradition, most commonly in bi-lingual versions, i.e. Pali with more or less elaborated Lao translations or commentaries, which shed light on the local interpretation of the Pali texts."
- Glossary "Sutta": "A number of manuscripts ... in which sut forms part of the title are bi-lingual versions, often with elaborated Pali-vernacular translations." Same statement for canonical-title Vinaya texts. Category fields therefore mark bilingualism indirectly (Language field: e.g. "Pali, Lao"), not by a dedicated nissaya/vohara genre term — the Glossary contains NO entries for nissaya/nisai/vohara/namasadda (checked full text). This is itself a citable observation: the Lao catalogue tradition classifies bitexts under text categories + Language field, not as a named gloss genre.

## Legacy item-record schema (Wayback snapshot, texts/10455 = Vohan phothi / Phothipakkhinyatham)

Fields: Title; Alternative Title; Language (Pali, Lao); Script (Tham Lao); Location (monastery/museum with province); Date (incl. CS/BE); Category; Material (palm-leaf/khoi/sa paper); Complete; No. of Fascicles; No. of Folios; Condition; Image Legibility; Colophon (yes/no); Illustrated; PLMP Code (13-digit); Remarks (PLMP datasheet title). Bundle-level navigation ("Show Other Texts from this Bundle") keyed to first 11 digits of PLMP code. Whole-text PDF downloads offered per record (23 MB for this example).
Note: current CrossAsia hosting exposes /api/* endpoints that close connections to scripted clients, and the IIIF search UI sits behind an Anubis challenge — programmatic genre-level counting remains blocked.

## Usable quotes for the book

- On bilingual norm: see blockquote above (About page).
- Glossary "Fascicle": average religious fascicle = 6-12 folios, max 12 fascicles per bundle; secular texts may run several hundred folios.
- Glossary "Jataka": DLLM holds complete bilingual (Pali-Lao) Pancñasajataka / Ha sip sat sets plus c. 20 further bundles of these "Fifty Apocryphal Jatakas," believed Southeast Asian, perhaps Lan Na origin.

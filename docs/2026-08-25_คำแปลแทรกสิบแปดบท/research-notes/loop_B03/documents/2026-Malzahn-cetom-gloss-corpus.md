## Five worked examples (fetched full pages this loop unless noted)

1. SHT 2054.b (https://cetom.univie.ac.at/?m-sht2054b) - Aśvaghoṣa Buddhacarita 2.48a-54d, Sengim, expedition code T II S 50, pothi 5 x 3.7 cm. TB words aimasu 'wishing', kāwañesa 'by killing', pr(a)m-ñäkteram 'like Brahman', pelkiṃ walo tāka 'because [...] he was a king', kamäte 'brought', yamate 'he did (?)', ra each GLOSS a single Sanskrit pāda-final word (Bc.2.48d ārurukṣaḥ; 2.49d hiṃsrā°; 2.51d ka iv; 2.52d dadarśa understood as 'reigned'; 2.53a babhāra; 2.53d ācakāṃkṣe). Placement details recorded: glosses sit under/beside the Skt akṣaras; one gloss "written a little higher ... apparently does not belong"; paper patch over part of an akṣara; fainter additional glosses to the right. Refs: Peyrot 2014: 154-6; Malzahn 2007b: 312-3; Hartmann 1988a: 59-60.

2. SHT 2250 and 2254.a (https://cetom.univie.ac.at/?m-sht2250and2254a) - Buddhacarita 13.66d-14.1a, same purchased Sengim lot (T II S 50 + T III S 51?). Documents TWO-LAYER glossing ("written in blacker ink with a thicker line through an original gloss in fainter ink"), word-by-word alignment under compounds ((śanmā)nm(a)meṃ under °bandhana; tarkatsitse under °mokṣa°; pe〈lkiṃ〉 under °hetoḥ), variant preference (gloss katkemane based on sahāsā, not transmitted vipāpmā), sense-not-form rendering ("The Sanskrit compound is not followed literally in the Tocharian gloss"), and later-added caesura strokes. Refs: Peyrot 2014: 133, 156-9; Malzahn 2007b: 312.

3. SHT 4477 (https://cetom.univie.ac.at/?m-sht4477) - Vasubandhu, Abhidharmakośabhāṣya 349.8-22 (Pradhan p. 349). TB kramtsa santänäntse glosses Skt krameṇa hi santānasya using two CALQUES; editor's remark that "perhaps the glossator only wanted to indicate the case of the Sanskrit words". kos yämormta glosses kati karmaṇi 'how many deeds?'; mokṣabhāgi mäskeṃträ '[these] are mokṣabhāgīyas' EXPLAINS which deeds are meant - an interpretive expansion beyond literal translation. Refs: Peyrot 2014: 165; Wille 2012: 81-2 (SHT Teil 11).

4. PS Bl 13 (https://www.univie.ac.at/tocharian/?m-psbl13) - Paris BnF Pelliot Koutchéen, codes 635/616; end of Pravāraṇa-sūtra + start of Dhvajāgra-sūtra; images via gallica.bnf.fr. TB scribbling UNDER Skt hartāraṃ sarvaśalyānāṃ vande; edition states verbatim: "it can be excluded that it is a gloss translating the Sanskrit text directly above." Negative control proving placement alone does not equal gloss. Refs: Peyrot 2015b: 112-113; Couvreur 1970: 181-182; Hartmann/Wille 1997: 137.

5. SHT 827.b (https://cetom.univie.ac.at/?m-sht827) - Kizil Ming-Öy, T III MQ 49; recto Sanskrit MEDICAL text in Gupta-type script (Waldschmidt 1971: 47-49), verso two lines of classical TB (... na tekañma tsäṣ '... the ... diseases ...'); blank space below the TB lines. Edited inside the gloss survey itself: "Edition: Malzahn 2007b: 303". Shows Sanskrit scientific books physically annotated/reused by Tocharian writers outside the literary canon.

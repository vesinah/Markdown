## Verbatim quotes (Chinese)

### (a) Li Lin's own conclusion (quoted identically by hosts 1 and 2)
"明清读者普遍通过十行本系统版本认识义疏。经文大字、注文中字、疏文双行小字，成为义疏的常态。读者耳濡目染，自然觉得义疏仿佛天然是经注的注脚，鲜能深入探寻义疏自身的丰富内涵。"

### (b) Li Lin on Jianyang market dynamics (host 1)
"福建坊肆推出五花八门的十行本注疏，全面突出经注本的核心地位，使义疏成为经注的注脚，且多附入释音，更便于阅读经注。尽管十行本哗众取宠、粗制滥造，却大受市场欢迎。"

### (c) 乔秀岩 (book introduction, host 3)
"单疏本是纯粹的义疏版本，八行本是合编了经注的义疏版本，十行本给人的印象，则是经注、释音、义疏的合编。所谓《十三经注疏》，便是十行本的子孙。"

### (d) 陈侃理 (host 4) on the two Southern Song series and their bases
"南宋注疏本的两大丛刊——越刊八行本和建刊十行本。二者都是拼合经注本和单疏本而来，不同在于：前者的编校是以单疏本为基础，在适当位置补入经文注文；后者则大致是在经注释音本的基础上，散入义疏文句而成。" — plus: "随着十行本的流行，它对义疏的定位深入人心…"

### (e) Reviewer observation on manuscript-era precedent (host 1)
"注文双行小字，写本时代已十分常见。雕版印刷的出现，固化了这种空间秩序和权力结构。" — Northern Song guozijian 单疏本 described as "均质化" 行款: 半页十五行、行二十五字左右, no big/small character distinction.

## Interpretation for the book chapter
- The standardization claim now has a named scholarly statement: the interleaved form (main text large characters, commentary mid-size, shu/scholium in double-line small characters) became THE normal form ("常态") through the Jianyang 十行本 system of the late Southern Song, inherited by Ming-Qing readers down to 阮刻《十三经注疏》.
- Decade-scale anchor points assembled across sources: combined 注+疏 editions first appear at the Song-Jin transition / 12th century (越刊八行本, 黄唐本); Jianyang 十行本 follow slightly later and win the market; most extant 十行本 blocks are Yuan reprints of Song originals (尾崎康 result relayed via sbksc.zcxn.com essay: e.g., 论语注疏 版心 "泰定四年程瑞卿").
- Companion museum statement (NPM/teldap): "It was not until the 12th century that editions joining annotations and commentaries were published… carried out by the Tea and Salt Supervisorate… under the charge of Huang T'ang… known as the 'Eight-line editions'" (https://culture.teldap.tw/culture/index.php?id=1020&id2=1022 ; NPM Chinese version: https://theme.npm.edu.tw/selection/Article.aspx?sNo=04001046).
- Companion TOC evidence: 张丽娟《宋代經書注疏刊刻研究》(北京大學出版社, ISBN 9787301227350) structures the evolution as 經注本→單疏本→經注附釋文本→纂圖互注重言重意本→注疏合刻本 (TOC seen via neodb.social/book/6dKESBV3WfssIuqX8UbfTY and bookseller mirror).

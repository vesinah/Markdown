## Dating synthesis (honest)
- Compilation: by Lü Zuqian during his lifetime (1137–1181), i.e., mid-Southern-Song; multiple concordant sources.
- First printing(s): by Jianyang bookshops, Song era — asserted by ctext + NJU (surviving Song-print component) but no exact Song print year found in accessible records this loop: "(ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน)" for a precise Song print date.
- Extant dated witness used by catalogs: Ming Zhengde 11 (1516) Jianyang Liu-Hongyi Shendu zhai reprint with interleaved small-character commentary (註文小字雙行) — layout continuity of jiajie-style pages into Ming reprints of Song compilation formats.

## Key content (faithful digest of the hyangchal entry)

### Definition and corpus
- Hyangchal = transcription system capable of recording Korean sentences completely in borrowed characters; the term appears once in 균여전 (Gyuyeon-jon) in 최행귀's preface to the translated verses, opposed to 당문.
- Corpus: 14 hyangga in 삼국유사, 11 in 균여전, 고려 예종의 도이장가 - 26 songs total; plus 향약구급방 (13th c.) vernacular glosses including one full hyangchal sentence (이가 솟고 시다 / 齒所叱史如).
- 20th-century scholarship initially called all chaja notation idu; some recent scholars split Silla-era notation = hyangchal vs post-Goryeo = idu, but the entry rejects period-based splitting since both coexisted.

### Structure and relation to gugyeol
- Notation unit: 어절 structured as 독자(讀字) + 가자(假字): content words written by meaning; particles/endings and word-final syllables written phonographically. Same structural principle as idu and gugyeol.
- Differences from idu: hyangchal arranges everything in pure Korean order; to are almost completely marked; no dependence on Chinese-style expressions. Idu mixes hanmun and Korean order with set formulas.
- Development sequence proposed: proper-noun transcription -> idu -> gugyeol -> hyangchal; hyangchal arose c. 7th-8th century when to-marking gugyeol (developed for reading hanmun) fused with Korean-order idu sentences. Concrete early evidence: 신라화엄사경조성기 (755) shows full Korean order plus to plus 말음첨기 and abbreviated characters.
- KEY LINK FOR THIS PROJECT: arranging a seokdokgugyeol text (구역인왕경 example) in the order its to dictate yields exactly hyangchal-type Korean writing; hence seokdokgugyeol is the background discipline of hyangchal, and hyangchal's decline paralleled seokdokgugyeol's decline. The 헌화가 line demonstrates identical ideogram+phonogram structure.

## From the research-history article (새국어생활 1997)

- 이숭녕 (1955) first split chaja notation into lexical vs sentence notation systems.
- 오구라 신페이 deciphered all 25 hyangga (선구적 but error-laden); 양주동 『고가연구』 corrected much and established the framework; his 용자법 taxonomy (정借/전借/통借/약借/반切/괘서) and identification of 말음첨기법 as distinctive.
- 김완진 (1980) set four criteria for hyangga decipherment: 일자일음 원리, 원전 비판, 연락 일치의 기준, 율격적 기준 - raising decoding rigor via Middle Korean evidence.
- 구역인왕경 introduced to scholarship in 1975 (심재기; 남풍현·심재기 1976); thereafter five seokdokgugyeol texts (10th-13th c.) and late-Goryeo/early-Joseon eumdokgugyeol materials were excavated, transforming pre-hangeul language history.
- Society history: 구결연구회 founded 1988, later reorganized as 구결학회; monthly 강독회 and joint research meetings; journal 『구결연구』; 1996 international conference 아시아 제민족의 문자 planned by the society.
- Article's claims usable verbatim-in-substance: seokdokgugyeol has "완전한 번역문의 성격" (character of a complete translation); it supplies vocabulary/grammar data for Goryeo Korean; its gugyeolja taken as 원字 reproduce hyangga-like orthography, so seokdokgugyeol is decisive for hyangga decipherment.

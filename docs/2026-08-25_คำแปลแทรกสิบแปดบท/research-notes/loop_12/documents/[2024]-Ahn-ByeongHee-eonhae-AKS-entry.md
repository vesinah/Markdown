## Key content (faithful digest)

### Definition and formal requirements
- Eonhae = Joseon-era translation of Chinese or vernacular-Chinese (백화문) sources into hangul; also called 언역 or 언석.
- Two requirements: source is hanmun or baihua; rendering is in hangul. Idu-based translations such as 대명률직해 and 양잠경험촬요 are NOT eonhae because no hangul appears; 석보상절 lacks the paired source text and so is not eonhae proper.
- Defining layout: hangul translation set against the paragraphed source text (대문); the paired source normally carries hangul gugyeol. Hangul glossing alone on a source is distinguished as 언토/언두.

### Gugyeol-to-eonhae workflow
- Colophons of 능엄경언해 and 금강경언해 state that gugyeol was fixed on the source first and the translation followed that gugyeol; once the gugyeol is settled translation becomes easy. The glossed source was printed alongside the translation as a reading guide.

### Chronology
- Earliest eonhae-like item: 훈민정음언해, c. 1447 (세종 29), era of 석보상절서 in 월인석보.
- Full-scale eonhae under 세조: 간경도감 produced about ten Buddhist eonhae including 능엄경언해 and 법화경언해; 구급방언해 also issued; under 성종 expanded to 삼강행실도언해, 내훈, 두시언해 and other fields (medicine, moral instruction, poetry).
- 16th century: provincial printing begins; peak output late 18th century (영조-정조): 교화서, 역학서, over thirty 윤음언해, plus reprints.
- Historical significance: 경서언해 by 교정청 came about 100 years after the Buddhist eonhae - evidence for Confucianism's later development; differences between 교정청 classics eonhae, 이이's 사서언해, and 15th-16th-century gugyeol/translations serve as intellectual-history data.

### From the 법화경언해 entry
- 1463 (세조 9): Sejo himself supplied the gugyeol on 묘법연화경 and 간경도감 translated and printed it (per colophon dated 9/2 and 세조실록 same-date entry 간경도감진신간법화경). Layout identical to 능엄경언해 and 원각경언해. Linguistic notes: no ㅸ notation unlike 능엄경언해; useful for comparing with 석보상절/월인석보 parallel passages.

### Corroborating detail from KCI abstract (김무봉 2004, 한국사상과 문화 23, ART001138622)
- 간경도감 (1461-1472) printed about 30 hanmun Buddhist works and 9 vernacular translations: 능엄경언해 (1462), 법화경언해 (1463), 선종영가집언해 (1464), 불설아미타경언해 (1464), 금강경언해 (1464), 반야심경언해 (1464), 원각경언해 (1465), 목우자수심결언해 (1467), 사법어언해 (1467).
- Standard layout described: source cut into paragraphs, large-character source with gugyeol forming 구결문; translation in small double-line (쌍행) text below; 요해 sections get middle-size characters one line lower; circle mark separates 구결문 from 언해문; 흑어미 marks for collation notes - all reader-service conventions.

### Corroborating detail from 세종한글고전 해제 (db.sejongkorea.org, 목우자수심결언해·사법어언해 volume)
- Mokujaja susimgyeol eonhae colophon formula: 비현합诀(丕顯閤訣) 혜각존자역(慧覺尊者譯) - the crown prince's office 비현합 made the gugyeol; 혜각존자 신미 did the translation; printed by 간경도감 1467 (간기 成化三年丁亥歲朝鮮國刊經都監奉敎雕造); original at SNU Kyujanggak (보물 770호).
- Shows institutional division of labor: gugyeol composition as a distinct step performed by a separate body from the translator.

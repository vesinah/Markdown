## Key content (faithful digest)

### Definition
- Seokdokgugyeol = gugyeol inserted between phrases so that a Chinese text can be read out in Korean (풀어 읽기); alias 훈독구결. It shows not only Korean word order but grammatical forms and parts of lexical items - hence the oldest Korean "translation" corpus and the main source for 10th–13th-century Korean.

### Two types
1. 점토석독구결: to expressed by dot/line-shaped 구결점.
2. 자토석독구결: to expressed by gugyeolja simplified from Chinese character shapes.
- Worked example 不忘念初地: jato version reads 初地ᄅᆞᆯ 念홀ᄃᆞᆯ 니즐 안ᄃᆞᆯᄒᆞ다 with right-side to + reverse dots; jeomto version places four numbered dots on 地 plus 역독선 (long downward-curving lines marking later reading order) and 합부 (vertical connector marking one constituent). Text families: 유가사지론-lineage vs 화엄경-lineage conventions.

### Status of materials
- Jato seokdokgugyeol: after the 1973 discovery of 『구역인왕경』 seokdokgugyeol, five more texts known from the 1990s: 『유가사지론』 vol. 20, 『화엄경』 vol. 14, 『합부금광명경』 vol. 3, 『화엄경소』 vol. 35. All five fully computer-input and collated, with dictionaries prepared (per this entry).
- Jeomto seokdokgugyeol: first found July 2000 in 『유가사지론』 vol. 8; subsequently 14 sources total: 화엄경 7, 유가사지론 5, 합부금광명경 1, 법화경 1; joint-research facsimiles and decipherments published for 2 Yugasajiron and 7 Hwaeom items. Difficulty of reading without training keeps use to specialists.

### Significance
- Extended historical Korean study beyond idu/hyangchal for the 10th–13th centuries; new lexical and grammatical facts; renewed basis for hyangga decipherment.
- Jeomto point-sign system exploiting position on the character square is described as unique among world notation systems; left/right to placement plus reverse dots judged a simple but successful invention.
- Because it transcribes hanmun read as Korean, seokdokgugyeol counts as Korea's oldest translation product; frequently compared with Japanese kunten (訓點).

## 3. 書本 section — the 23 base texts collated

Criticism of the two most praised earlier editions:

> 前輩謂興國于氏本及建安余氏本為最善逮詳考之亦彼善於此爾又于本音義不列於本文下率隔數頁始一聚見不便尋索且經之與註遺脱滋多余本間不免誤舛要皆不足以言善也

("Former masters held that the Xingguo Yu [于氏] edition and the Jian'an Yu [余氏 = 余仁仲] edition were the best; on detailed examination each is merely better than the other. Moreover, in the Yu edition the phonetic definitions 音義 are NOT placed beneath the main text but gathered together only after several pages — inconvenient to consult; and classic text and commentary alike have many omissions...")

The collation base list:

> 今以家塾所藏　唐石刻本　晉天福銅版本　京師大字舊本　紹興初監本　監中見行本　蜀大字舊本　蜀學重刋大字本　中字本　又中字有句讀附音本　潭州舊本　撫州舊本　建大字本(俗謂無比九經)　俞韶卿家本　又中字凡四本　婺州舊本併興國于氏建安余仁仲凡二十本　又以越中舊本註疏　建本有音釋註疏　蜀註疏　合二十三本專屬本經名士反覆參訂始命良工入梓

Edition-type vocabulary attested here (hanzi verified directly from this fetch): 附音本 ("attached-sounds" edition, in combination 中字有句讀附音本 "mid-size-character edition with punctuation and attached sounds"); 建本有音釋註疏 ("Jianyang edition of the zhushu with sound-glosses" — i.e., a Jianyang 注疏 edition carrying 音釋); plus 監本 Directorate editions, Shu large-character editions, 潭州/撫州/婺州 old editions, 興國于氏本, 建安余仁仲本.

## 4. 音釋 section — placement policy of sound glosses (core evidence for 附釋音 layout)

> 唐石本晉銅版本舊新監本蜀諸本與他善本並刋古註若音釋則自為一書難檢尋而易差誤建本蜀中本則附音於註文之下甚便繙閲然龎雜重贅適増眩瞀今欲求其便之尤便則亦附音釋如建蜀本然亦粗有審訂音有平上去入之殊則隨音圈發或者不亮其意而以為病則但望如監本及他善本視之捨此而自觀釋文可也若大學中庸論孟四書則併附文公音於各章之末

Reading (my working translation, for research use only): "The Tang stone classics, the Jin Tianfu bronze-block edition, old and new Directorate (監本) editions, the various Shu editions and other good editions print only the ancient commentary (古註); if there are sound-glosses (音釋) they form a separate book, hard to look up and prone to error. The Jianyang editions and the Shu editions attach the sounds BELOW THE COMMENTARY TEXT (附音於註文之下) — very convenient for leafing through — though cluttered and redundant, adding confusion. Now, seeking the greater of these conveniences, we too attach the sound-glosses like the Jianyang and Shu editions, but with some editorial vetting. Where tones differ (level/rising/departing/entering) we mark them with tone-circles following the sound (隨音圈發)... As for the Great Learning, Doctrine of the Mean, Analects and Mencius (the Four Books), Master Zhu's sounds are likewise appended at the end of each chapter."

Editorial selection rules for which readings to gloss:

> 有字本易識初不假音者音釋為難字設也今凡正文之音皆存之其有音切雖多而只同前音者與别無他音而衆所共識者未免擇其甚贅者間削去惟註亦然釋文每有後可以意求及更不重出及後放此之説則不必贅出亦明矣

("Characters easily recognizable do not need glosses; sound-glosses exist for difficult characters. All sounds in the main text are kept; where multiple fanqie spellings merely repeat an earlier sound, or where there is no other reading and everyone knows the character, the most redundant are selectively pruned; the same applies under the commentary; where the Shiwen [= 釋文] says 'not repeated later' or 'hereafter follows this', it need not be repeated.")

Further examples discuss fanqie variants attributed to 徐 (Xu [= Xu Xian? unverified]) and 沈 (Shen) with dialect remarks (吳音), e.g.:

> 如堯典光被四表被皮寄反而徐又音扶義反以扶字切之則為音吠蓋徐以吳音為字母遂以蒲為扶以蒲切之無異於皮寄反法應刪

and tone-disambiguation examples:

> 有的然之音不待釋者在上之上(時亮反)在下之下(户雅反)此指高卑定體而言若自下而上(時掌反)自上而下(遐嫁反)此指升降而言

## 5. Why this matters for Loop B01

1. A Song-dynasty printer's own programmatic statement that (a) older authoritative editions carried commentary only, with shiwen-type glosses circulating as separate books; (b) commercial Jianyang/Shu editions had moved the sound-glosses into the page itself, attached beneath the commentary lines; (c) this interleaved placement had become the convenience standard that even a luxury collated edition adopted (with vetting).
2. Terminological set verified verbatim: 附音本 / 有句讀附音本 / 有音釋註疏 / 附音於註文之下 / 隨音圈發 / 音釋 / 釋文.
3. Confirms 余仁仲 (建安余氏) and 興國于氏 editions as the famous reference points of late-Sung classics printing, and confirms the existence of a distinct 註疏 stream (越中舊本註疏, 蜀註疏) versus 古註-only streams.
4. Note: the phrase 雙行小字 (double small-character columns) does NOT occur in this text; the physical double-column arrangement rests on Tsien 1985 p.222 (harvested separately).

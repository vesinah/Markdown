## คัมภีร์ปฐมภูมิและฉบับสอบชำระ (Primary Canonical Sources & Critical Editions)

- Aśvaghoṣa. *Gurupañcāśikā*. Sanskrit text edited by Sylvain Lévi. Paris: Journal Asiatique, 1929.
- Bapat, P. V., and Akira Hirakawa. *Shan-Chien-P'i-P'o-Sha: A Chinese Version by Sanghabhadra of Samantapāsādikā*. Poona: Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, 1970.
- Buddhaghosa. *Samantapāsādikā: Vinayaṭṭhakathā*. Edited by J. Takakusu and M. Nagai. 7 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1924–1947.
- Saṅgharakkhita. *Vuttodaya: A Study of Pāli Metre*. Edited and translated by R. Siddhartha. Calcutta: University of Calcutta, 1929.
- Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō [大正新脩大藏經]. Edited by Junjirō Takakusu and Kaikyoku Watanabe. Tokyo: Taishō Issaikyō Kankōkai, 1924–1934.

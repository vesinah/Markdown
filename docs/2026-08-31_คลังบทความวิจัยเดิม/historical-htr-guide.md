## 1. แหล่งปฐมภูมิ (Primary Sources)

*   *Annals of the Joseon Dynasty (Sillok)*. National Institute of Korean History (NIKH). Hanja (Hanmun) scripts (1392–1863).
*   *Cairo Genizah Fragments*. Synagogue Ben Ezra (Cairo, Egypt). Hebrew, Judeo-Arabic, and Aramaic manuscripts (9th–19th century).
*   *Cod. Sang. 562*. Abbey Library of Saint Gall. Latin manuscript (9th century). Carolingian minuscule script.
*   *Cod. Sang. 857 (Parzival)*. Abbey Library of Saint Gall. Middle High German manuscript (13th century). Gothic script.
*   *George Washington Papers*. Library of Congress (Washington, D.C.). English Cursive script (18th century).
*   *Jeremy Bentham Manuscripts*. University College London Library (London, UK). English Cursive script (18th–19th century).
*   *Llibres d'Esposalles*. Barcelona Cathedral Archives (Barcelona, Spain). Ancient Catalan and Spanish marriage records (15th–17th century).
*   *Lo Chia-Lun Manuscripts*. National Archives of Taiwan / National Chengchi University. Modern Chinese cursive script (Waisu) (20th century).
*   *Lontar Bali Collections*. Kirtya Library (Singaraja, Bali). Balinese palm-leaf manuscripts.
*   *Lontar Sunda Collections*. Ciburuy Archaeological Site (Situs Kabuyutan Ciburuy, West Java). Old Sundanese palm-leaf manuscripts (15th century).
*   *PHerc.Paris 4 (Herculaneum Scroll)*. Institut de France (Paris, France). Carbonized Greek papyrus scroll (1st century BCE).
*   *SleukRith Palm-Leaf Collections*. National Archives of Cambodia / Temple collections (Phnom Penh, Cambodia). Khmer palm-leaf manuscripts (Middle to Post-Angkor period).
*   *Sukhothai Stone Inscriptions*. National Museum Bangkok / Historical temples (Sukhothai, Thailand). Thai stone inscriptions (13th–14th century).
*   *Tibetan Cursive Manuscripts (Drutsa/Betsug)*. Austrian National Library / Tibetan Research Institutes. Tibetan cursive script on paper.

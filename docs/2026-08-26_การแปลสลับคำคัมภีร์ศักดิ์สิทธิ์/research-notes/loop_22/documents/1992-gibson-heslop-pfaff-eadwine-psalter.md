## Verified bibliographic record

- Title: The Eadwine Psalter: Text, Image, and Monastic Culture in Twelfth-Century Canterbury
- Editors: Margaret [T.] Gibson, T. A. Heslop, Richard W[illiam] Pfaff (eds)
- Series: Publications of the Modern Humanities Research Association 14 (ISSN 0581-0280)
- Co-publication: London: MHRA in conjunction with University Park, PA: Pennsylvania State University Press, 1992 (MHRA page: "1 January 1992")
- ISBN: 0-947623-46-9 / 978-0-947623-46-x (MHRA issue); 0-271-00837-7 / 978-0-271-00837-0 (Penn State issue)
- Extent: xvii + 228 pp. incl. frontispiece + 48 plates (per Barker-Benfield's JEH review heading); bookseller counts "xvii, 228 pp, 49 unnumbered pages of bw and color plates"; OCLC 24468570.
- Manuscript: Cambridge, Trinity College MS R.17.1; called "arguably the most ambitious manuscript produced in England in the twelfth century" (bookseller text); contains FIVE versions of the Psalms text: three Latin + Old English interlinear gloss + Anglo-Norman version, with prologue, commentary (parva glosatura), and concluding prayer per psalm; adaptations of Utrecht Psalter illustration cycle; four bible picture leaves; Eadwine portrait ("Prince of Scribes", f. 283v per historyofinformation.com citing Gibson et al. 178); comet drawing (Keynes ch.); Canterbury waterworks plans c. 1160 (Woodman ch.).

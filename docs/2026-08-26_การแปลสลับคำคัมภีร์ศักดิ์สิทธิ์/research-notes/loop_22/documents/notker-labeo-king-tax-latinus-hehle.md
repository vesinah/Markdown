## Die Werke Notkers des Deutschen. Neue Ausgabe (King & Tax) — structure verified

Serial page fetched: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/serial/notker-b/html — "Die Werke Notkers des Deutschen. Neue Ausgabe", ed. James C. King and Petrus W. Tax (series Altdeutsche Textbibliothek; begun by Edward H. Sehrt and Taylor Starck). Volumes seen on the serial page:
- Bd 1 Boethius 'De consolatione Philosophiae' Buch I/II, Petrus W. Tax, ATB 94 (1986), DOI 10.1515/9783110940091
- Bd 1A Notker latinus zu Boethius CPh I/II: Kommentar, Tax, ATB 120, DOI 10.1515/9783110975215
- Bd 2A / 3A Notker latinus zu Boethius III (Kommentar), Tax, ATB 122, DOI 10.1515/9783110770902
- Bd 4 Martianus Capella 'De nuptiis', King, ATB 87 (1979); Bd 4A Notker latinus zum Martianus Capella, KING, ATB 98 (1986)
- Bd 5 Boethius' Bearbeitung der 'Categoriae', King, ATB 73 (1972); Bd 6 'De Interpretatione', King, ATB 81 (1975)
- Bd 8 Der Psalter. Psalm 1-50, Tax, ATB 84 (1979) [Psalter volumes continue in subsequent ATB numbers]
- Bd 7 Die kleineren Schriften, King & Tax, ATB 109, Tübingen: Niemeyer 1996 (CXXIX+349 pp.) — adds three Latin texts to the canon (St. Galler Traktat, Distributio, De dialectica); authenticity argued in Tax's article "Die lateinischen Schriften Notkers des Deutschen. Altes und Neues", PBB 124 (2002), 411-441.
- Bd 7A Notker latinus zu den kleineren Schriften, King & Tax, ATB 117, Niemeyer 2003, 202 pp., ISBN 3-484-20217-3.

So "Notker Latinus by Petrus W. Tax" = CORRECT for the Boethius commentary volumes (1A, 2A, 3A are Tax's), but the Notker latinus to Martianus is by KING (4A) and that to the smaller writings by KING & TAX (7A). Any downstream citation should assign authorship per volume.

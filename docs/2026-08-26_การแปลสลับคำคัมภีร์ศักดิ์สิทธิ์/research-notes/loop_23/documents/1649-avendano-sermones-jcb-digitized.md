## Bibliographic description (from JCB metadata, verbatim facts)

- Title: "Sermones de los misterios de nuestra santa fe catolica, en lengua castellana, y la general del Inca. Impugnanse los errores particulares que los indios han tenido. Por el doctor don Fernando de Avendano, arcediano de la Santa Iglesia Metropolitana de Lima, calificador del Santo Oficio, catedratico de prima de teologia, y examinador sinodal."
- Author: Fernando de Avendano; dedicated to "Illvstrissimo Senor Don Pedro de Villagomez, Arcobispo de Lima"
- Imprint (from OCR title page): "Impresso en Lima, Por Iorge Lopez de Herrera, impressor de libros, en la Calle de la carcel de Corte"
- Collation: 2 v. ([5], 129 leaves; 110 [i.e., 101] leaves); 28 cm (folio); in Spanish and Quechua with Latin quotations
- Dating: IA date field = 1648 based on licencia date "27. de mayo de 1648" and aprobacion "29. de junio de 1648"; conventional citation date 1649
- Vol. 1 signatures: pi1 2pi [A]2 B-Q R (-R8); Vol. 2 signatures: A2 B-M N O-Q2 R2 (-R2)
- Vol. 2 has no separate title page; caption title "Segunda parte". Medina suggests there may have been a "Tercera parte". Vargas Ugarte believed Sermones was published together with Villagomez's Carta pastoral de exortacion e instruccion contra las idolatrias... (Lima 1649)
- References cited by JCB: JCB Lib. cat., pre-1675, II:367-68; Medina, J.T. Lima, 288; Palau y Dulcet (2. ed.) 68866; Vargas Ugarte, R. Impresos peruanos, 410
- JCB copy condition: vols bound together out of order (leaves 95-110 [i.e., 101] of Segunda parte bound between leaves [5] and 1 of Parte primera); worm holes with text loss in vol. 1; leaf 125 torn; leaf 94 of vol. 2 torn and mended, text affected
- Licencia per OCR front matter: printing authorized by Viceroy Marques de Mancera, licencia dated 27 May 1648; aprobacion by Padre Francisco Conde of the Compania de Jesus
- Front matter also includes errata list correcting Quechua passages with folio references ("Erratas")

## Scholarly description secured

- Sabine Dedenbach-Salazar Saenz, "'... luego no puedes negar que ay Dios Criador del mundo, pues tus Incas con no ser Christianos lo alcansaron a sauer, y lo llamaron Pachacamacc' - La lengua de la cristianizacion en los Sermones de los misterios de nuestra santa fe catolica de Fernando de Avendano (1649)", in S. Dedenbach-Salazar Saenz & L. Crickmay (eds.), La lengua de la cristianizacion en Latinoamerica: Catequizacion e instruccion en lenguas amerindias, Bonn Americanist Studies (BAS) 32, Munich: Verlag Anton Sauerwein, 1999, pp. 223-248. STORRE record: http://hdl.handle.net/1893/28409
- Abstract states (verbatim points): first Quechua/Aymara sermon collections published at initiative of the Tercer Concilio Limense in 1585 (Tercero Cathecismo); c. 60 years later Francisco de Avila and Fernando de Avendano published new sermon collections; Avila presented New Testament themes in Quechua and Castilian; Avendano gave a different form to the part of his sermon book he wrote himself - his SEGUNDA PARTE IS THE REPRODUCTION OF SOME SERMONS OF THE TERCERO CATHECISMO; Avendano adopts a fictitious dialogue between an indigenous audience and himself as cleric, addressing indigenous people paternally with asymmetric Quechua treatment term "churi" ('son/daughter of father'); content covers basic Christian concepts (e.g., one God), refers more explicitly to the Andean world, often even in titles written to combat indigenous beliefs.

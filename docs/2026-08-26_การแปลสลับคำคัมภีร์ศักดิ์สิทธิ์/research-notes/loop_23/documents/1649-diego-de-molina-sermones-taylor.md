## Contextual corroboration located this session

- Revista Identidad 4(4) (UNHEVAL, 2019), article on kichwa del Chinchaysuyu (revistas.unheval.edu.pe/index.php/rifce/article/download/585/465/1442): quotes Alfredo Torero (2005) describing the mid-17th-century norm dispute between Cuzco-oriented and regional Quechua; states that "el cura de Huánuco Diego de Molina subraya, ese mismo año [1649], que si él fuera examinador en el Obispado del Chinchaysuyu, no examinaría en los términos del Cuzco, sino en su lengua materna, pues esta es la lengua que hablan y entienden los naturales" - i.e., Molina's sermons defend the Chinchaysuyu (Lima/Huanuco) Quechua variety against the Cuzco norm, exactly parallel to Avendano's stated choice (quoted in same passage from Avendaño 1649).

## Relationship to Avendaño (for loop context)

- Same year 1649: Avendaño prints his Lima-Quechua sermonario in Lima (Jorge Lopez de Herrera press); Diego de Molina writes/publishes sermons in Huanuco Quechua defending the regional lingua franca. Both belong to the post-Tercer-Catecismo vernacular sermon production wave documented by Dedenbach-Salazar Saenz 1999.

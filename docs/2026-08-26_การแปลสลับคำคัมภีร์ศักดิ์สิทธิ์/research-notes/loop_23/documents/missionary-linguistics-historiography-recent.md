## Assessment

The field's self-historiography now spans: founding manifesto (Hovdhaugen 1996) -> encyclopedia entries (Nowak 2006; Brill ECLL) -> decennial mega-survey (Zwartjes 2012) -> ORE state-of-field (Zwartjes 2018) -> critical reframing toward colonial linguistics (Stolz/Warnke 2015; HL special issue 2015; Van Hal 2016) -> regionalized 21st-century reference articles (De Gruyter 2024; Elsevier IELL 2026; Brill Taiwan 2025). For the loop's purposes, citable one-stop surveys are Zwartjes 2012 (open abstract, huge bibliography) and Zwartjes 2018 ORE.

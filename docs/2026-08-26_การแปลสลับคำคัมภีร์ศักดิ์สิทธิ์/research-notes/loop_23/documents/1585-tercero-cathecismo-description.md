## Title and imprint (verbatim from JCB metadata)

"Tercero cathecismo y exposicion de la doctrina christiana, por sermones. Para que los curas y otros ministros prediquen y ensenen a los yndios y a las demas personas. Conforme a lo que en el sancto Concilio Prouincial de Lima se proueyo."
- Imprint: "Impresso ... en la Ciudad delos Reyes, por Antonio Ricardo primero impressor en estos reynos del Piru", 1585
- Creators recorded: Catholic Church Province of Lima Concilio Provincial (1583); Jose de Acosta 1540-1600; Juan de Atienza 1542-1592
- Colophon in OCR: "Impresso en la ciudad de los Reyes, por Antonio Ricardo, primeiro Impressor en estos Reynos del Piru, Ano de M.D.L.X.X.X.V."

## Physical description (JCB)

- [8], 215 leaves; 22 cm (4to); text in Spanish, Quechua, and Aymara
- Signatures: [par.] 2A-3C 3D (-3D8), with missignings noted (2B3 as 2A3 etc.)
- Numerous errors in foliation; in some copies leaf 2N6 is a cancel
- Title page vignette (Jesuit trigram); initials, printer's devices; errata statement leaf [1] verso; index on leaves [4-7]

## Layout evidence (from downloaded OCR, file 1585_tercero_cathecismo_jcb_ocr_djvu.txt)

- Trilingual arrangement with language-block headers: Spanish text followed by sections headed "QVICHVA." and "AY MARA:" (visible in OCR sample page); i.e., each unit given in Castilian then Quechua then Aymara blocks, not interlinear
- Front matter includes approval/licensing apparatus and erratas correcting vernacular passages

## Relationship to the 1584 Doctrina Christiana

- Per BNP description (fuenteshistoricasdelperu.com Doctrina page): the 1584 volume's table of contents announced an "Exposicion de la Doctrina Christiana, por sermones" that was NOT published with it - it appeared separately as this 1585 Tercero Cathecismo
- Dedenbach-Salazar Saenz 1999 (STORRE abstract): the Tercero Cathecismo's 1585 sermons in Quechua/Aymara were published "a iniciativa del Tercer Concilio Limense"; they cover basic Christian concepts and already refute Andean beliefs directly; Avendano's segunda parte (1649) reproduces some of these sermons

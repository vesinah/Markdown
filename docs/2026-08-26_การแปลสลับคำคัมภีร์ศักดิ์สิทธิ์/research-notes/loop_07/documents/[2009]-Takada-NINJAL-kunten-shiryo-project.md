# Source Extract: Takada / NINJAL - Structured Description of Kunten-Shiryo Project

- Author: TAKADA Tomokazu (Project Leader; NINJAL, Department of Linguistic Theory and Structure)
- Title: "Structured Description of Kunten-Shiryo (Documents Written in Chinese with Marks for Rendering into Japanese)"
- Year: 2009-2012 (project period October 2009 - September 2012)
- Type: Funded research project page (NINJAL Collaborative Research, Incubation/Discovery-Type)
- Publisher/Journal: National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics (NINJAL)
- DOI/ISBN: none
- URL: https://www.ninjal.ac.jp/english/research/cr-project/project/c/kunten/
- Status: FULL-FETCHED

## Key extracts seen this session

- Keywords listed by the project: Kanbun-Kundoku, Kunten-Shiryo, Digital archive. Research field: History of Japanese Language.
- States kunten-shiryo has been used to grasp aspects of the history of Japanese "especially characters, pronunciation, vocabulary, and usage," but the field "lags behind other fields as regards the use of digital technologies such as electronic interpretation texts and digital imaging of originals."
- Pilot object: Kongocho Issai nyorai Shinjitsu sho Dai jogensho Daikyo Okyo Vol. 1 owned by NINJAL (an esoteric Buddhist sutra text).
- Aim: develop a method of structured description of interpretations (readings based on originals) to create a prototype of digital interpretations shareable among researchers, with a display system allowing verification of interpretations against digital images of originals.

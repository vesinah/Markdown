## Key extracts seen this session

- Database publishes gloss information (kunten information) assigned to kunten materials (classical Chinese textbooks); digitized material is NINJAL's copy of the Shangshu (Shōsho) old movable-type edition version 3, volumes 1-9.
- Searchable elements: wokototen ヲコト点 (including morphosyntactic gloss 音合符/訓合符, vernacular reading gloss, Sinoxenic reading gloss, tone gloss 声点) and inverted/order marks 語順点 (re-ten レ点, one-two marks 一二点); results show volume and folio where glosses occur.
- Simple search filters wokototen by appearance (color/shape); detailed search combines position, appearance, shape with AND conditions. A kunten mapping table (okototenzu PDF) is provided.
- Underlying projects per footer: NIHU multi-field project "Comprehensive Bibliothecology (総合書物学)" unit on refining historical Japanese corpora with orthographic and bibliographic information (2016-present), and JSPS KAKENHI 17K18506 "International document structure description of kanbun kunten materials for sharing and automatic generation of kakikudashi-bun" (2017-present).
- EAJRS 2023 note: first 2019 version required specialist knowledge; improved search accepts kanji+kana strings such as 明ニシ (akiraka ni shi), showing co-occurrence of nouns/particles and verb inflection forms; IIIF-based cutout character images auto-detected from page images using reprinted text; indexes of major words added; plan to extend to other texts.
- Companion dataset noted elsewhere at NINJAL: "Saidaiji-bon konkōmyō saishōō-kyō heian shoki-ten (Volume 1)" 西大寺本金光明最勝王経平安初期点 under the Kunten Shiryō section of Text Data Sets for Research on the History of Japanese (https://www2.ninjal.ac.jp/textdb_dataset/en/).

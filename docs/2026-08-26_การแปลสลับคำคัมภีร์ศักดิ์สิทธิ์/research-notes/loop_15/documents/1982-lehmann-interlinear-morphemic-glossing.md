## Notes

1. The Leipzig Glossing Rules page fetched this session names Lehmann's 1982 Folia Linguistica article first among its "Important references", and uses Lehmann's Hittite examples (n=an apedani mehuni essandu 'CONN=him that:DAT;SG time:DAT;SG eat:they:shall') to illustrate colon/period conventions - direct evidence that the modern standard descends from Lehmann's codification line.
2. Chain established from the author's own records: 1980 working-paper Guidelines (Koln Arbeitspapier 37) -> 1982 journal article in Folia Linguistica vol. 16 pp. 193-224 -> 2004 De Gruyter HSK 17.2 handbook chapter "Interlinear morphemic glossing" pp. 1834-1857. This is a 25-year continuous authorship track for the codification of interlinear glossing conventions.

## Notes

1. This is the classic first major extension of Ferguson's diglossia framework: the four-fold conceptual decoupling implied by the title (bilingualism without diglossia; diglossia without bilingualism; both; neither). Title verified verbatim via Crossref.
2. Its relevance to sacred-text gloss research: it legitimizes analyzing communities where the H variety (e.g., liturgical/sacred language) is nobody's mother tongue - i.e., "diglossia without bilingualism" configurations that medieval glossing practices often presuppose.

- Entry: 두시언해 杜詩諺解 (E0017018; new URL scheme https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0017018)

- Fetched: 2026-08-25 (full text)

## Key facts
- Original title 분류두공부시언해 分類杜工部詩諺解; 25 vols. 17 books; movable type 을해자.
- 초간본: translated by officials 유윤겸 (Yu Yun-gyeom) et al. and monk 의침 (Uichim) under royal order spanning Sejong-Seongjong reigns; printed 1481 (Seongjong year 12).
- Stated publication purpose per Cho Wi (조위) preface in vol. 1: 세교 (世敎, instruction/moral education of the age) - i.e., a lay-facing educational project, NOT a monastic curriculum item.
- 중간본: 1632 woodblock, prepared by Gyeongsang governor Oh Suk from a copied+corrected single set; a CORRECTED edition, not a facsimile (복각 아님) - shows 17th-century language, hence its value for Middle-to-Early Modern Korean comparison.
- Content: all 1,647 Du Fu poems plus 16 others classified into 52 categories; first vernacular-rendered Chinese poetry collection (최초의 국역 한시집).
- First edition survives incomplete: vols. 1, 2, 4, 5, 12 missing (matches Loop 08 record).
- Reference list anchors the specialist literature: 이병주 『두시언해비주』 (1958); 전재호 『두시언해논석』 (1969), 『두시언해의 국어학적 연구』 (1975); 안병희 「중세어의 한글 자료에 대한 종합적인 고찰」 (규장각 3, 1979).

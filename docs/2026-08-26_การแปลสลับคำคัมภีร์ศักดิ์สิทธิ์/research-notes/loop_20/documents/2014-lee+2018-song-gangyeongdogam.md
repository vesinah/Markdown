# Extracts: Gangyeongdogam (target D) scholarly articles

## 1. Lee Jeon-gyeong (이전경) 2014
- Title (KR): 간경도감 불경언해 사업의 또 다른 함의
- Title (EN): Gangyeong-Dogam's Buddhist "Eonhae" Editions And Its Implication
- Journal: Korean Language Research (국어연구), vol. 34, pp. 265-286
- Date: 2014-06
- DOI: 10.16876/klrc.2014..34.265
- KCI artiId: ART001886742
- URL: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001886742

### Abstract highlights (verbatim from KCI page)
- "an interpretation method of high authority is introduced regarding the 'eonhae' of Gangyeon-Dogam. Due to the authority of this method of interpretation, other interpretation method has been ceased and only this method remains."
- Evidence: after the emergence of Gangyeong-Dogam, various attachment forms became unified in interpretation of Buddhist texts; paper focuses on differences in Buddhist text attachments BEFORE Gangyeong-Dogam.
- Examines Beophwa-gyeong and Neungeom-gyeong attachment forms in the 15th and 16th centuries.
- "In the method of translation, right attachment follows 'EumdokGugyeol' and the left attachment follows 'SukdokGugyeol' styles. Therefore, the reader first reads aloud and then interprets the meanings." (oral lecture/test system completed)
- Three conclusions: (1) unified ways of interpreting Buddhist texts; (2) spread/enlightenment of Hunminjeongeum literacy; (3) influenced re-arrangement of empty spaces in Hanmun texts.
- Keywords: Gangyeong-Dogam's Buddhist eonhae project, interpretation of Buddhist text, kugyeol, History of Korean Writing.

## 2. Song Il-gie (송일기) 2018
- Title (KR): 간경도감 중수본에 대한 오해 (刊經都監 重修本에 대한 誤解)
- Title (EN): The Misunderstanding about Repairing Edition Printed in Kangyung-dogam
- Journal: Journal of the Institute of Bibliography (서지학연구), no. 73, pp. 83-98
- Date: 2018-03
- DOI: 10.17258/jib.2018..73.83
- KCI artiId: ART002329667
- URL: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002329667

### Abstract highlights (verbatim from KCI page)
- Examines whether Gangyeongdogam 중수본 (repairing editions) are 번각본 (re-engraved) or 후인본 (reprinted from original blocks); concludes they are REPRINTS from repaired original woodblocks, not new engravings.
- View first raised by a Japanese scholar during the colonial period; Dr. Cho Myung-gi (조명기) detailed that repairs replaced individual characters or whole blocks; Chon Hye-bong (천혜봉) rejected this and proposed a re-engraving theory which became bibliographic orthodoxy - hence the current misunderstanding.
- Keywords: Kangyung-dogam(刊經都監), repairing edition(重修本), reprinting edition(後印本), Cho Myung Gi, Chon Hye Bong.

## Relevance
Lee 2014 documents Gangyeongdogam as standardizing authority for sutra eonhae WITH kugyol attachment layers (right=eumdok, left=seokdok styles); Song 2018 is a solid bibliography-source on Gangyeongdogam print history (woodblock repair/reprint chronology). Both citable for target D alongside the AKS entry.

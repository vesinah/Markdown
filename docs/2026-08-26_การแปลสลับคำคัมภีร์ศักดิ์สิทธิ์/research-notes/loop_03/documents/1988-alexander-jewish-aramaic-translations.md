## Notes (facts seen this session)
- Alexander's chapter is the standard encyclopedic survey of Jewish Aramaic bible translation inside the Mikra project, deliberately placed next to Tal's chapter on the Samaritan targum — i.e., rabbinic and Samaritan Aramaic traditions treated as parallel comprehensibility strategies.

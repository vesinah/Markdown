## Extracts

- Neungeom-gyeong eonhae 楞嚴經諺解 glosses and translates the Song commentator Wenling Jiehuan(溫陵戒環)'s exposition of the Dabuljeong Yerae Milin Sujeung Uijeui Je Bosal Manhaeng Su Neungeom-gyeong; King Sejo added hangul kugyol to the Chinese commentary and led the translation.
- Two editions: movable-type edition printed 1461 (Sejo yr. 7) at the Gyoseogwan with Eulhaeja type, 400 copies; woodblock edition 1462 by the Gangyeongdogam, reprinted from the same blocks in 1472 (Seongjong yr. 3) and 1495 (Yeonsan-gun yr. 1).
- Project history per royal prefaces: begun 1449 (Sejong yr. 31) under Prince Suyang (later Sejo) on Sejong's orders but left unfinished; resumed 1461 after relic manifestations and a request by Prince Hyoryeong. The translation was collaborative despite being labeled roayl translation (어역 御譯): Sejo supplied kugyol; monk Hyeogak-jonja Sinmi adjudicated correctness and fixed sentence parsing (구두); Han Gyeohui and Kim Su-on drafted the Korean; eminent monks corrected it; Sejo finalized.
- Translation style: "thoroughly direct/literal translation" (철저한 직역); format shows the source passage with hangul kugyol in large characters first, followed by the translation set in paired lines (쌍행).
- Orthographic split between kugyol layer and translation layer: kugyol notation lacks bangjeom (tone dots), the letter 'ㅭ' and each-character-doubled clusters (각자병서); the translation text always carries bangjeom and uses 'ㅭ'/각자병서 in adnominal endings etc.; otherwise identical conventions.
- Every hanja in the translation carries a reading according to Dongguk Jeongeun; prefaces attribute these phonetic annotations to specialists Jo Byeong-an and Jo Ji - i.e., standardized Sino-Korean pronunciation guides embedded line-by-line in the eonhae.
- This book became the norm-setting model for later Gangyeongdogam eonhae (Beophwa-gyeong eonhae, Geumgang-gyeong eonhae etc.) in format, translation style, and orthography.

## Notes

- Related item seen this session (KCI ART002612831, SEARCH-VERIFIED): BnF-held volumes 1-2 of the same sutra carry Goryeo-era character-borrowing (借字表記) kugyol plus hangul seokdok-kugyol notes, letting scholars compare pre-Hangul kugyol practice directly against the 1461/1462 eonhae - presented there as evidence of continuity from Goryeo seokdok-kugyol to Joseon eonhae within royal translation projects.
- Related item seen this session (Journal of Korean Buddhist Research Institute, 2006, https://journal.kabs.re.kr/articles/article/1noO/, SEARCH-VERIFIED): Yukjobeopbodan-gyeong eonhae 六祖法寶壇經諺解 - the Platform Sutra rendered into Korean by monk Hakjo under King Yeonsan-gun, publication funded by Queen Insu, issued in 3 vols with a special cut wooden type; study extracts philological and linguistic features of the 15th-c. language from it.

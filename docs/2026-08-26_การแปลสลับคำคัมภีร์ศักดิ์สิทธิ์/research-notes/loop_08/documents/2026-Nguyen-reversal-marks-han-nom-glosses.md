## Extracts

- Abstract: "Mirroring the glossing traditions of literary Sinitic in Japanese kanbun kundoku and Korean seokdok kugyeol, Vietnam has also been theorized to have a similar system for vernacular interpretation of classical Chinese texts. While Japanese and Korean glossing practices have been extensively studied, evidence for Vietnamese equivalents has remained understudied."
- The article examines REVERSAL MARKS in Vietnamese Han Nom manuscripts from the Canh Phuoc Collection currently held at the University of Kyoto.
- Several of these manuscripts carry extensive glossing in the Nom script; the reversal marks "provide evidence of gloss-based reading practices, indicating a method for reordering classical Chinese syntax to match Vietnamese word order."
- Keywords: Chu Nom; classical Chinese; exegesis; glossing; Vietnamese.

# Extract: Paul Demieville, Le Concile de Lhasa (1952; corr. reprint 1987)

- Full title: Le Concile de Lhasa : une controverse sur le quietisme entre bouddhistes de l'Inde et de la Chine au VIIIe siecle de l'ere chretienne
- Publication: Paris, PUF, 1952; Bibliotheque de l'Institut des hautes etudes chinoises VII; corrected reprint College de France/IHEC-Boccard 1987; Chinese translation by Geng Sheng, Lanzhou: Gansu renmin chubanshe 1984 (Tufan seng zheng ji 吐蕃僧諍記)
- Status: SEARCH-VERIFIED (author preface fetched from terebess.hu PDF; table of contents via buddhanature.tsadra.org; Persée review by Francoise Aubin, Archives de sciences sociales des religions 66.285, 1988; e-aoi.uzh.ch bibliography entries). NOT Wikipedia.
- Type: monograph

## Key facts seen this session

- Structure (tsadra TOC): Part 1 annotated French translation of the Chinese dossier of the council (Dunhuang ms Pelliot 4646 = Dunwu dasheng zhengli jue 頓悟大乘正理訣); Part 2 historical commentary; APPENDIX = "Fragments du dossier indien de la controverse" (pp. 333-353): (I) analysis of Kamalasila's FIRST Bhavanakrama based on the Chinese version, (II) translation of the THIRD Bhavanakrama from Tibetan by Etienne Lamotte; followed by Addenda et Corrigenda, index, plates I-XXXII.
- Preface states the volume contains the annotated translation of the Chinese dossier, a historical commentary, and "la traduction, en appendice, de deux fragments du dossier indien"; a second volume was projected for doctrinal commentary but never appeared (Aubin review).
- Council dated last quarter of 8th c., between 792-794 (or 780-782), at bSam yas/Lhasa, Mo-ho-yen (Hva-shang Mahayana, Chinese Chan side) vs Kamalasila (Indian gradualist side), presided by the Tibetan king; Chinese party defeated, some suicides; Kamalasila later murdered.
- Aubin review: Demieville's conclusion was that misunderstanding between the parties was inevitable, arising less from language diversity than from fundamental conceptual opposition between subitism and gradualism; notes are "veritable petites monographies"; ~300 additional manuscript notes deposited at Societe Asiatique.
- TLS bibliography (hxwd.org) confirms Dunwu dasheng zhengli jue preserved on Pelliot 4646 (basis of Demieville's edition), Stein 2627, Beijing 5306.

## UNVERIFIED this session

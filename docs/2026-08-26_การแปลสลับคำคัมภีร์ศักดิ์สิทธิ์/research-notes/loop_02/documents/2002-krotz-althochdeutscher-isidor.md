## Relevance

The Paris Isidor is a column-layout bilingual (Latin left, OHG right) rather than a true between-the-lines version; its OHG translation breaks off early (fol. 22r). The Monsee fragments and the Junius 25 glossaries (Jc glossary re-edited by Krotz) connect the Isidor group directly to the same Bodleian booklet collection that contains the Murbach Hymns.

## Relevance

Cod. 916 is the paradigm case of a Latin rule-text carrying a word-for-word OHG interlinear translation for monastic use; Masser's trilogy (1997 edition, 2000 correction-copy study of Cod. 915, 2002 commentary) is the modern reference apparatus.

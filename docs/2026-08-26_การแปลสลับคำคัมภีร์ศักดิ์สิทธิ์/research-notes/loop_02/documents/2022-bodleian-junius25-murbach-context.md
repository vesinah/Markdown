# Bodleian MS Junius 25 - Murbach Hymns manuscript context and provenance

- **Object:** Oxford, Bodleian Library, MS. Junius 25 (booklet collection, 8th-9th centuries; contains the Murbach Hymns = Latin hymnal with OHG interlinear glosses, plus three OHG glossaries)
- **URLs:** https://hab.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/en/collections ; https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/collections/manuscripts-from-german-speaking-lands ; https://medieval.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/catalog/manuscript_6321 ; https://medieval.ox.ac.uk/workshop-report-the-murbach-hymns/ ; https://archive.org/details/diemurbacherhym02sievgoog
- **Access date:** 2026-08-25
- **Status:** SEARCH-VERIFIED (Bodleian HAB collections page and Oxford Medieval workshop reports retrieved in extended excerpts; the Bodleian medieval catalogue page for Junius 25 was blocked by a bot-protection challenge - only its search snippet seen)

## Excerpts / observed facts

- HAB/Bodleian digitization project collections page: "the Library's most celebrated manuscript from the German lands, MS. Junius 25, a set of distinct fascicles from the 8th and 9th centuries, including three Old High German glossaries and the Latin hymns with interlinear German glosses known as the Murbach Hymns."
- Provenance (same page): "From Reichenau on Lake Constance the earliest parts of the manuscript were taken to (and augmented at) the abbey of Murbach in Alsace, whence by the 17th century the codex had come to the Netherlands and into the hands of the philologist Marcus Zuerius Boxhorn, and from Boxhorn to the humanist scholar Isaac Vossius, who passed it on to his kinsman Junius."
- Polonsky project page: "MS. Junius 25 ..., a volume of booklets from the 8th and 9th centuries that includes three Old High German glossaries and the glossed Murbach Hymns."
- Workshop report (Oxford Medieval Studies, 15/04/2022, by Luise Morawetz): workshop 'The Murbach Hymns (MS. Junius 25) - Vernacular Glossing in the Early Middle Ages', 17-18 February 2022; describes "the Murbach hymns, a Latin hymnal with Old High German interlinear glosses ... one of the oldest sources of Old High German"; speakers included Daniela Mairhofer (Princeton), Michael Stolz (Bern), Elke Krotz (Vienna), Matthias Standke (Berlin), Alderik Blom (Marburg), Helen Gittos (Oxford), Elvira Glaser (Zurich), Stephan Mueller (Vienna); included a Latin-OHG compline with the glosses set to music; further Bodleian glossed manuscripts consulted: MS Auct. F. 1. 16, MS Rawl. C. 697, MS Canon. Pat. Lat. 57.
- Search snippet from medieval.bodleian.ox.ac.uk catalogue (manuscript_6321): "Includes isolated Old High German glosses, both marginal and interlinear, on fols. 137r, 147v and 148v. The glosses are edited in Steinmeyer, Elias, and Eduard Sievers (eds.), Die althochdeutschen Glossen, 5 vols (Berlin 1879-1922), no. 493." (snippet refers to part of the composite volume; full record not accessible this session).
- Standard edition located on archive.org: "Die Murbacher Hymnen" ed. Eduard Sievers (Bodleian Library Manuscript Junius 25) - scanned volumes available.

## Relevance

Establishes the physical and institutional frame of the Murbach Hymns (Reichenau-Murbacian transmission, Junius booklet collection shared with Isidor-group glossaries Ja/Jb/Jc studied by Krotz) and identifies the current research network around the manuscript.

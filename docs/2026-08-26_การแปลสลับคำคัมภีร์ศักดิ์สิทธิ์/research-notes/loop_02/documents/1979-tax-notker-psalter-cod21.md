## Relevance

Notker's Psalter preserves the Latin psalm verse with OHG translation plus running commentary; the surviving codex is a 12th-century Einsiedeln copy with interlinear and marginal glosses, demonstrating continuation of the bilingual sacred-text format into the 12th century. Tax's "Notker latinus" volumes model how to document the Latin sources of a vernacular sacred translation.

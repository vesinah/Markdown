## Key extracts
- Urai as cultural phenomenon presupposes: "return to classicism", "unquestioned authority of the original text", "initiatory structure of learning", "urge toward ratiocination ... and learned classification", "positive, appreciative criticism", and "the concept of the division of the totality of recorded literature into underlying texts (mulam) and comments upon them (urai)".
- Periodization: these conditions prevailed "in Tamilnad between the 12th-16th centuries, but especially in the 13th-15th centuries, the 'golden age' of the commentators."
- Authority structure: "the commentator paraphrased, analysed, explained the meaning of the original text ..., questioning or even refuting the views of other commentators, but never the views of the original author; for the entire recorded literature was divided into the mulam, the original texts, 'revealed' by sage-poets or by poets revered ... and urai, the prosaic commentaries where disagreement and polemics were quite welcome."
- Typology: "It seems that from the earliest times (i.e., from the age of the earliest extant recorded commentary, Nakkirar on Iraiyanar Kalaviyal, 8th Cent. A.D.), four types of commentaries were distinguished"; later elaborate forms termed virivurai/viritturai ("detailed commentaries, dissertations") and vilakkurai/vilakkavurai ("exemplifying commentary").
- Developmental chain also reported elsewhere this session (Wikipedia summary of same tradition, corroborated by rupkatha article): arumpadha urai (glossary commentary) -> kurippurai (notes commentary) -> vilakka urai (explanatory commentary); example: Tiruvaimoli commentary growth 6,000 -> 9,000 -> 24,000 -> 36,000 lines (the Edu).
- Commentators' prose is "a powerful accumulator" feeding modern written Tamil; medieval commentators named include Parimelazhakar (14th c.) among Ilampooranar, Senavaraiyar, Perasiriyar, Naccinarkkiniyar (11th-14th c.).

## Relevance to loop topic
Classic account of the mulam/urai division as ideology: the root text is unquestionable while polemics live entirely in commentary - precisely the authority/access split the loop investigates; also supplies the Tamil genre taxonomy (arumpadham/kurippuram/vilakkam).

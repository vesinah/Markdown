- Pannabhoga Herngseng 2023 (Manusya 26:1, p. 7, full text read this session) summarizes: "According to John Okell (1965, 186-227) nissaya is a linguistic analysis with special emphasis on Pali grammar features in the Burmese language. Since its early development in the late Pagan period, Burmese has been heavily influenced by Pali grammatical syntax... Burmese was greatly influenced by nissaya literature on the one hand and that of Pali grammar on the other."

## Relevance to loop topic
The foundational linguistic description of nissaya as a register of Burmese reshaped by word-for-word rendering of Pali grammar; establishes the "systematic adaptation" framing later authors build on or dispute. Note: an item sometimes attributed elsewhere is actually THIS article - the SOAS digital scan titled "Nissaya Burmese: A case of systematic adaptation..." is Okell's piece, not Handley's.

DEFINITION (2004 ch., sec 1.1): An interlinear morphemic gloss (IMG) is a representation of a text in L1 by a string of elements from L2 where ideally each morph of L1 is rendered by an L2 morpheme/config of symbols, sequence-preserving; primary aim = make reader understand grammatical structure by identifying aspects of free translation with meaningful elements of the L1 text. IMG characterized as hybrid: both "a kind of translation that accompanies the original" (cf. synoptic editions) AND "a kind of linguistic analysis" competing with grammar fragments. Standardization analogy with IPA.

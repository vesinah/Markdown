## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

The anthology's very architecture documents that the word-for-word vs sense-for-sense debate originates in classical rhetorical imitation theory (Cicero, Horace) and is immediately entangled with scripture: Philo's account of the Septuagint's miraculous inspired translation and Paul's "five words with the mind" utterance (1 Corinthians 14) are included as founding documents. These two entries show the anthology treats sacred-text translation questions (inspired literalism vs intelligible speech) as constitutive of Western translation theory from its beginnings.

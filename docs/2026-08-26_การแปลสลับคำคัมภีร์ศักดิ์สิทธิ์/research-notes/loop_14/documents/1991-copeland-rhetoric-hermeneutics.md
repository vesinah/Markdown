## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

The subtitle's pairing of "academic traditions" with "vernacular texts" encodes the book's documented scope: learned Latin hermeneutic/rhetorical traditions versus vernacular translation practice. The chapter title seen this session ("the place of translation and the value of hermeneutics") shows the book treats translation as an interpretive (hermeneutic) act from antiquity into the Middle Ages - the historical matrix in which Jerome's word-for-word/sense-for-sense debate was transmitted to medieval vernacular translators. The standard citation of this book for "translatio studii/imperii" (transfer of learning/empire as a frame for medieval translation) could not be verified verbatim online this session.

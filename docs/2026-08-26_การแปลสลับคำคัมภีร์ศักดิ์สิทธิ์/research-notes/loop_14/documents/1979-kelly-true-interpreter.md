## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

Kelly's book is the standard single-volume history of Western translation theory spanning the classical, biblical, and modern periods; its subtitle ("Theory and Practice in the West") places the biblical word-for-word/sense-for-sense tradition (Septuagint, Jerome, Reformation Bibles) inside a general history rather than a purely theological frame. It is routinely cited alongside George Steiner's After Babel (1975) and Nida (1964) as one of the three historical syntheses that re-founded translation historiography in the 1970s.

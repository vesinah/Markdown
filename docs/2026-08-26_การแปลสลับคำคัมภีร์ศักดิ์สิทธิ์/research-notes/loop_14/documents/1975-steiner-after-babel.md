## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

Steiner frames all translation as a hermeneutic of trust, aggression, incorporation, and restitution rather than as a choice between literal and free methods; for sacred-text debates this reframes literalism vs comprehension as psychological/ethical interpretation rather than mere technique. The Babel title motif itself invokes the biblical origin myth of linguistic plurality that underlies scripture-translation anxieties.

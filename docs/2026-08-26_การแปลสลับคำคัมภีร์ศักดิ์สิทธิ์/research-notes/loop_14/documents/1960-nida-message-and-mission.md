## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

Message and Mission is cited as the work in which Nida framed Bible translating as communication to receptors - the missiological precursor to dynamic equivalence. Its subtitle ("The Communication of the Christian Faith") locates it within communication theory rather than philology, four years before the formal/dynamic terminology appeared in Toward a Science of Translating.

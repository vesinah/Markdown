## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

The book's title thesis - that mission translation had a decisive cultural impact precisely by rendering the Christian message into vernacular languages - is standardly cited as the argument that Bible translation vernacularized and thereby empowered receiving cultures rather than merely exporting European forms. Within this loop's frame it supplies the historical claim that the comprehension-oriented (vernacular, sense-oriented) impulse of missionary Bible work transformed societies.

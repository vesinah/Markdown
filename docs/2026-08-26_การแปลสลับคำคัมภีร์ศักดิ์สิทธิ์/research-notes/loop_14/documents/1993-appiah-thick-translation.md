## Relevance to sacred-text literalism vs comprehension

Thick translation names translation accompanied by scholarly contextualization (annotations, ethnographic framing) so that receivers access the source text's meanings rather than either bare literalism or fluent assimilation. For sacred texts, it offers a third way beyond the literalism/comprehension binary: keep the foreignness but make it intelligible through explicit context.

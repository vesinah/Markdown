## Relevance to early Malay Qur'an translation/tafsir

Per Annabel Teh Gallop (RAS blog 25 Nov 2022): Riddell traces gradual chronological development of Malay Qur'anic exegesis from the Erpenius-era CUL manuscript commentary on Surat al-Kahf ("by the middle of the 17th century, the Malay world was actively producing Qur'an manuscripts, translations of certain verses, and at the very least exegesis at the level of the Surah"), to Tarjuman al-Mustafid by Abd al-Ra'uf (2nd half 17th c., Aceh), then a ~250-year gap until Tafsir Nur al-Ihsan by Muhammad Said bin Umar of Kedah (1925-1927).

From Majid Daneshgar (Cambridge UL blog, 14 Aug 2023): Dd.5.37 (copied Aceh 1604 by Pieter Willemsz van Elbinck/Peter Floris) is now argued to be the earliest known dated MALAY COMMENTARY on the Qur'an (on Q12), based on Persian prototypes (Tafsir-e Hada'iq al-Haqa'iq by Mu'in al-Din Farahi Heravi d. ca. 1501); this post-dates and nuances Riddell's framework.

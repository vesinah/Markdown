## Assessment

- No article by Witkam devoted specifically to MALAY interlinear Qur'an translations was found on his own publications list or via web search. His Malay-related output is catalogue/editing work; his Qur'an-manuscript articles concern Ottoman copying rules, phonography, and individual mss.
- The hypothesis that "Witkam's inventory of Leiden oriental manuscripts" contains interlinear-Qur'an descriptions is supported (the inventories describe e.g. Acehnese ms Or. 4976 with Arabic prayer with interlinear Malay translation, per Vol. 5 excerpt on docslib.org / bibliography.mk.iq mirror).
- A book title "The Islamic Manuscript Tradition" was NOT matched to Witkam in any retrieved source (open question).

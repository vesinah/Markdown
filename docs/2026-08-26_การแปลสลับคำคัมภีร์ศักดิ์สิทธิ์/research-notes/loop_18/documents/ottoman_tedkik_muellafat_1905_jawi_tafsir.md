# Document: Ottoman Tedkik-i Muellefat committee and the 1905 Jawi tafsir approval (item F)

Primary citable page: Osman Nuri Solak, "The Discovery of a Manuscript of Tarjuman Al-Mustafid, the first complete Quran interpretation in Malay", Southeast Asia Library Group (SEALG) blog, 30 June 2023. URL: southeastasianlibrarygroup.wordpress.com/2023/06/30/the-discovery-of-a-manuscript-of-tarjuman-al-mustafid-the-first-complete-quran-interpretation-in-malay/

## The 1905 documents (as reported by Solak)

1. Application/report document to print the Tarjuman al-Mustafid, dated 4 Cemaziyelahir 1323 (= 6 August 1905). Meşihat Archive, Istanbul: IST_MFT_MSH_DFT02_01785 S.133, NR. 31. Solak's translation: "Upon the request for the review of Qazi Beyzavi Tafsir, which was translated into Jawi and printed with the approval of the editorial board of the Makkah printing house, since there was no officer who knew the Jawi language, the Makkah scholars who knew this language gave the approval for the printed edition of the tafsir. It has been reported that it is appropriate to reprint it." (The identification of the work as Beyzavi's tafsir was erroneous - it is Abd al-Ra'uf's Tarjuman al-Mustafid; error per Riddell 1990.)
2. Official Sheikhul-Islam approval for printing, dated 18 Cemaziyelahir 1323 (= 20 August 1905). Ottoman Archives, Istanbul: BOA, MF.MKT. 865/52 (cited as MF.MKT./865-52-3). Text: "The petition for the reprint of Kadi Beyzavi's Tafsir and the Jawi books of the Mecca printing house were checked by the inspection committee. Deciding that the translation is true to the original, it was reported that there was no obstacle for printing."

## Print history table (Solak 2023)

Matba'a-i Osmaniyye, Istanbul, 1884 | Matba'atul-Miriyye, Bulaq, 1885 | Matba'atul-Miriyye, Mecca, 1905 | Matba'a-i Osmaniyye, Istanbul, 1906 | Mohammed al-Marbawi, Cairo, 1951 | Sulayman Maraghi, Singapore, 1951.

The Bursa manuscript (Uludag University Faculty of Theology, donated 1974, 866 folios, Italian paper watermarked Andrea Galvani Pordenone) is identified as the copy brought to Istanbul by Ahmad al-Fatani (d. 1908), presented to Abdulhamid II, used for the 1884 edition.

## Committee scholarship

- Filiz Digiroglu, article quoted verbatim by Solak: "It is interesting that the only application made to the Tedkik-i Muellefat Committee from the province of Hijaz is related to the translation of Kadi Beyzavi's Tafsir into the Jawi language. The request relates to the approval of the reprint of the translation of the tafsir published in Meccah." Source given as: Filiz Digiroglu, 'Religious Publishing in Abdulhamid II Era: Who Produces the Islamic Texts?', Journal of Marmara University Faculty of Theology, Issue 54, June 2018.
- Filiz Digiroglu, "TEDKIK-I MESAHiF-I SERiFE VE MUELLEFAT-I SER'IYYE MECLiSi KUTUPHANESi'NDEKi MUSHAFLAR" (2018), ResearchGate/Academia.edu - on the Meclis formed at the Sheikhulislamlik; its core established under II. Abdulhamid.
- History Studies (historystudies.net) article on Abdullah Hasib Efendi / Encumen-i Teftis ve Muayene: records that in 1892 the Tedkik-i Muellefat Commission was created within the Maarif Nezareti for second inspection of Turkish/Arabic/Persian books; from 1900 censorship bureaucracy increased; 1902 Kutub-i Diniye ve Ser'iye Tedkik Heyeti founded for religious books; later the Tedkik-i Muellefat Encumeni was merged with the Meşihat mushaf-inspection body, continuing as "Tedkik-i Mesahif ve Muellefat-i Seriyye".
- Dergipark SAMER article: "Tedkîk-i Mesâhif ve Müellefât-ı Şer'iyye Meclisi'nin Siyer ve ..." (published Dec 2025) - council examined religious works and Mushafs before printing at the Sheikhulislamlik.
- ISAM library archive page: "Tedkik-i Muellefat Azasindan" (Grand Mufti's Office personnel registry book) - arsiv.isam.org.tr.
- Filiz Digiroglu, "Malay Publications Printed in the Ottoman Empire and Mecca Provincial Printing House" (2024), in Studies on the Relations Between the Ottoman Empire and Southeast Asia (Academia.edu): Mecca Provincial Printing House operational from 1883, Jawi output, Ahmad al-Fatani supervisor role.

VERDICT item F: VERIFIED. Exact archival shelfmarks and Julian dates for the 1905 Jawi tafsir (Tarjuman al-Mustafid) print approval via the Tedkik-i Muellefat Committee are documented on a citable public page quoting Ottoman documents, corroborated by Digiroglu's articles.

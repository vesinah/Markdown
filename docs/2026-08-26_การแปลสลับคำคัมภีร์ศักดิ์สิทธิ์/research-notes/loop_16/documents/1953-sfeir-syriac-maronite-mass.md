## Notes (loop 16 relevance)
- Concrete attestation that in Maronite (West Syriac) practice the scripture readings themselves (Epistle, Gospel) are customarily said in Arabic within a Syriac rite - a living case of sacred-language hierarchy with vernacular rendering of readings.

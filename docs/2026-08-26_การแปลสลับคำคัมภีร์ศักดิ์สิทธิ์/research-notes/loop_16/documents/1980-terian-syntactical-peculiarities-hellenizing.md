## Extracted facts (from archive.org item description, read 2026-08-25)
- "The Hunaban /Hellenophile movement in Armenian literature began in the 5th century, when Armenian translators, unable to find Armenian words for certain Greek philosophical terms and concepts, deliberately created a 'translationese' version of Classical Armenian." (uploader annotation by Robert Bedrosian)
- "Sometimes translating literally from Greek (including word order) the resulting works are often difficult to read and understand without the Greek text."
- "However, they served their intended purpose excellently, and also greatly enriched the vocabulary of Classical Armenian through the creation of new compound words, based on Greek models."

## Extracted facts (from search excerpts seen 2026-08-25)
- "The document that we conventionally call the Lectionary of Jerusalem is titled as follows: 'Regulation and order of the holy hierarchs, as observed in Jerusalem'."
- "It is universally acknowledged to be a version in Georgian of a Greek original (now lost), with only a few specifically Georgian commemorations added."
- "There is scholarly consensus that the Lectionary reflects the liturgical situation of the Church of Jerusalem in around 600."
- Dating formula in title: Greek composition 7th century; Georgian translation 8th century.

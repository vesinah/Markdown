## Extracted facts (seen 2026-08-25)
- Gorgias description: "a basic introduction to the various Syriac translations of the Bible and the ways in which they were used in the Syriac tradition... biblical manuscripts, lectionaries, printed editions, and translations. A reception history... its use in preaching, in liturgy, and in spirituality."
- Visible text fragment (dokumen.pub search excerpt): "Before the invention of printing the main context in which the Bible was read was during church services..." and, on liturgical use: "Syriac liturgical prayers and hymns are so soaked in the phraseology of the Syriac Bible, we can accordingly see the importance of having translations based on the Peshitta for the purposes of liturgical readings from the Bible." Sanctus wording ("heaven and earth are full of his praises") shown to follow Peshitta Isaiah 6:3.
- Brock's article "The use of the Syriac versions in the Liturgy," in R. B. ter Haar Romeny (ed.), The Peshitta: Its Use in Literature and Liturgy (Leiden, 2006), 3-25 (listed in his Oxford profile/CV) covers the liturgical-use question in article form.
- Reviewer P. J. Williams (TC: A Journal of Biblical Textual Criticism 13, 2008): "Written by the world's leading Syriac scholar."

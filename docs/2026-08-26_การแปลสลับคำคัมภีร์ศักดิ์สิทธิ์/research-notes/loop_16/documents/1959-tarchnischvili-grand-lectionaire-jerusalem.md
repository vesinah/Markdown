## Extracted facts (from the Oxford page, read 2026-08-25)
- Edition reconstructed from four manuscripts:
  1. P = Paris, BnF georgian 3, discovered 1898, 387 ff., dated 10th/11th c.
  2. S = Sinai georg. 37, hand probably that of John Zosimus; two sections (lectionary for divine service; hymns); 294 ff., dated 982.
  3. L = Lathal manuscript (church of St George, Lathal), 10th c., also attributed to John Zosimus, 322 ff.
  4. KA = Kala manuscript, 174 ff., 10th c., "with a large number of errors".
- Fragments: GR = 7th-century fragment inside the University of Graz lectionary; H = 8th-century fragment with Pentecost lections and November.
- Partial earlier edition: K. Kekelidze 1912, based on Kala and Lathal.
- Separate "Georgian index to the Gospel readings for the ancient liturgy of Jerusalem. Published by G. Garitte in 1972," based on Sinai mss georgian 30 & 38, copied by two scribes in 979 (Ezra: Matthew-Mark; John Zosimus: Luke-John). Its eight sections include fixed feasts, Lent/Holy Week/Easter, Sunday office cycles, commons.

## Notes (loop 16 relevance)
- Core evidence for item (C): an Old Georgian Grand Lectionary transmitting the Jerusalem (Hagiopolite) rite of roughly the 5th-8th centuries, i.e., Georgian as the vehicle of a translated Greek sacred order.

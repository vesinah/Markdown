## Extracted facts

- E.I. Kychanov "created a solid academic foundation for the research on history, society, law, and religion of Xixia" and contributed significantly to Tangut language and script studies.
- Kychanov's Tangut Dictionary: Slovar' tangutskogo (Si Sia) iazyka = Tangut Dictionary. Tangut-Russian-English-Chinese Dictionary, ed. E.I. Kychanov, co-editor S. Arakawa, Kyoto: Faculty of Letters, Kyoto University, 2006.
- Arakawa as co-editor typed Tangut characters in a special font, checked phonetic reconstruction forms, and worked on character-index arrangement; the dictionary's unique character-index lookup method is argued to be useful for studying the shapes of Tangut script.

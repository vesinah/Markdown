## Extracted facts

- The Maharatnakuta collection exists in Chinese Tripitaka, Tibetan Kanjur, and Mongolian Kanjur versions; "almost nothing is known" of the Mongolian versions prior to this study.
- Study object: the Ratnakuta inside the Mongolian manuscript Kanjur compiled 1628-1629 under Ligdan Khan.
- Findings per abstract: possibly the major part or the whole Ratnakuta was translated into Mongolian en bloc in 1628-1629; the Mongolian collection lacks eight sutras and places the forty-ninth work between texts thirty-five and thirty-six.
- Textual elements preserved from the Tibetan original(s) point to proximity and possible relation of the Mongolian Ratnakuta to the Them spangs ma and Western Tibetan Kanjurs - i.e., the Mongolian translation transmits traces of its specific Tibetan source family.

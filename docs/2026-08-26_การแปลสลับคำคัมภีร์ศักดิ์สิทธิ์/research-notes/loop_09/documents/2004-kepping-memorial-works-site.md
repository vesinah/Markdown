## Extracted facts

- Ksenia Borisovna Kepping (07.02.1937 - 13.12.2002): Russian name, Swedish family surname, born in Tianjin (China); described on the site as a world-famous specialist in Tangut linguistics who in her last years turned to culture and history of the Tangut state, writing a rapid series of articles on the new themes.
- The site is the electronic version of the book "Ksenia Kepping. Last Works and Documents" (St Petersburg 2003), containing previously published and unpublished articles in English and Russian, a bibliography of her works, documents and photographs from personal archive.
- Works list (last works) includes, among others: "Zhuge Liang's 'The General's Garden' in the Mi-nia Translation" (in collaboration with Gong Hwang-cherng); "Tangut Ritual Language" (paper presented at the 29th International Conference on Sino-Tibetan Languages and Linguistics, Leiden, Oct 10-13, 1996; PDF at http://kepping.net/pdfs/works/Tangut_Ritual_Language.pdf); "The Famous Liangzhou Bilingual Stele: a new study" (PDF at .../Liangzhou_Bilingual_Stele.pdf) - the Liangzhou stele being a celebrated Tangut-Chinese bilingual inscription; "The Verb in Tangut"; "Portraits of Tibetan and Indian Teachers in a Tangut Engraving"; "Mi-nia (Tangut) Self-appellation and Self-portraiture in Khara-Khoto Material"; "'The Autumn Wind' by Han Wu-di in the Mi-nia (Tangut) Translation"; plus Russian-language pieces on the name of the Tangut state and Chinggis Khan materials; also L.N. Menshikov's memorial essay.
- Relevance to topic D: Kepping's late work documents Tangut translation practice from Chinese literary sources into Tangut ("Mi-nia translations") and studies bilingual artifacts (Liangzhou stele); "Tangut Ritual Language" addresses a distinct ritual register of Tangut. Full texts are freely downloadable at kepping.net.

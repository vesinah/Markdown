## Key extracted content

- Context: "In the study of the language history of Irish, the so-called Old Irish glosses (edited in W. Stokes & J. Strachan, *Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus*, Cambridge 1901-3) take up a special position. They are almost the only testimonies of Old Irish from contemporaneous manuscripts."
- Corpus definition: "The largest corpus within the glosses is formed by the Milan glosses (Thes. i 7-483), interlinear and marginal notes to and translations of a Latin commentary on the psalms in the manuscript Ambrosianus C301 inf. kept at the Biblioteca Ambrosiana in Milan."
- Aim: to compile all Old Irish material of the Milan glosses in a special dictionary giving full phonological/morphological/syntactic information; modelled on Kavanagh's Würzburg lexicon (Séamus Kavanagh, *A Lexicon of the Old Irish Glosses in the Würzburg Manuscript of the Epistles of St. Paul*, ed. Dagmar S. Wodtko, Wien: Österreichische Akademie der Wissenschaften 2001).
- Text basis: entire Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus text typed in by Griffith, incorporating TP corrections/supplement; revisions checked against R.I. Best's collotype facsimile (*The Commentary on the Psalms with glosses in Old-Irish preserved in the Ambrosian Library (MS. C 301 inf.)*, Dublin: Royal Irish Academy 1936) and against the manuscript itself during a visit to Milan in December 2010; also Ascoli, *Il Codice irlandese dell'Ambrosiana* (Rome: Loescher 1878-9).
- Outputs downloadable: complete text in ten PDFs covering Ml. 2a1-146d1 (Thes. Pal. i 7-483); FileMaker databases for Mac (73 MB) and Windows (56 MB), last changed 12.09.2013; series of correction files to Thes. Pal.
- Planned publication stated on page: printed + digital lexicon in the Prähistorische Kommission series of the Österreichische Akademie der Wissenschaften, planned for 2012 or 2013.

## Related items seen this session

- Notre Dame Medieval Studies Research Blog post cites Milan-gloss infinitive solutions: e.g. *inficere* glossed *fris-n-orr* (f. 15a10), *opinari* glossed *du-menammar* (f. 78b24), *supplicare* glossed *nges* (f. 39b3) - nasalising-relative constructions compensating for Irish lacking an infinitive.

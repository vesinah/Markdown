## Key excerpts (from publisher description)

- "This work is composed of two parts. The first or introductory part contains a palaeographical discussion about Bodleian Library, MS Auctarium D.2.19, that is to say, the MacRegol Gospels or the Rushworth Gospels ... The provenience of the MS, the Latin text, the use of the MS, and the Old English gloss are discussed in detail in the introductory part." Aims include surveying preceding editions "such as Stevenson & Waring (1856-65) and W.W. Skeat (1871-87)" and publishing the complete edition with the whole Latin text interlineally glossed in Old English.
- Blurb (Marcelle Cole, Leiden): "For the first time, the Latin text of the MacRegol Gospels and its Old English interlinear gloss have been brought together in a single complete edition."

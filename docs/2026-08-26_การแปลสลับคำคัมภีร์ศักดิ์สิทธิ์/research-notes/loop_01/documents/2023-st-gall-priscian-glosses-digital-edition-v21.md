## Bibliographic metadata observed

- Site citation: Rijcklof Hofman, Padraic Moran, Bernhard Bauer, *St Gall Priscian Glosses*, version 2.1 (2023) <http://www.stgallpriscian.ie/>
- Original transcription of glosses: Rijcklof Hofman (early 1990s); digital edition created by Padraic Moran (2010), maintained by Moran; Bernhard Bauer contributed Old Irish linguistic material (his 2015 Vienna project "A Dictionary of the Old Irish Priscian Glosses").

## Key extracted content

- Manuscript: St Gall, Stiftsbibliothek, MS 904 (e-codices link http://www.e-codices.unifr.ch/en/list/one/csg/0904).
- Contents: *Ars grammatica* (also known as *Institutiones grammaticae*) by Priscian, "a Latin grammarian based at Constantinople in the sixth century AD"; treatise "(c. 1,000 pages in the modern edition), completed c. 526/7"; edited by Martin Hertz in Keil, *Grammatici Latini* (Leipzig, 1855-80), vols 2-3.
- Dating/origin: "The manuscript was written in AD 850-1, probably in Ireland". Homepage adds: "It was brought to the Continent between 855 and 863, reaching St Gall sometime after 888."
- Gloss corpus: "over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses, in addition to c. 4,000 construe marks (symbols to aid reading). More than one-third of these glosses were written in Old Irish, and as such constitute one of our most important corpora for that phase of the Irish language." Homepage variant wording: "a commentary of over 9,400 interlinear and marginal glosses, as well as c. 3,000 symbol glosses. About one-third of the verbal glosses are written in Old Irish".
- Prior editions: Old Irish glosses published in Stokes & Strachan, *Thesaurus Palaeohibernicus* (London, 1901-1910), vol. 2, pp. 49-224; Hofman published roughly the first half of all glosses (Irish, Latin and symbols) in *The Sankt Gall Priscian Commentary. Part 1* (2 vols, Münster, 1996). The digital edition "presents the first complete transcription of all of the glosses".
- Structure note: manuscript breaks off in book 17; book 18 missing. Browse interface covers Books 1-17 with Hertz page references.
- Supplementary bibliography 1996-2013 downloadable from the site (StGall904_bibliography.pdf).

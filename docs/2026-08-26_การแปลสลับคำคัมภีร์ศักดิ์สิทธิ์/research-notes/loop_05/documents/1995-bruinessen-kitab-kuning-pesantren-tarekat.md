# Source extract: Kitab Kuning, Pesantren dan Tarekat (Martin van Bruinessen)

- **Author:** Martin van Bruinessen
- **Title:** *Kitab kuning, pesantren dan tarekat: tradisi-tradisi Islam di Indonesia*
- **Year:** 1995 (first edition, Mizan, Cet. 1); reprinted Gading Publishing (2012; 2nd printing May 2015 per gusdur.net)
- **Type:** Scholarly monograph (Indonesian language)
- **Publisher:** Mizan / Gading Publishing, Yogyakarta
- **Preface:** Abdurrahman Wahid ("Martin van Bruinessen dan Pencariannya"), dated 1994
- **URLs:** https://archive.org/details/martin-van-bruinessen-1994-2012-kitab-kuning-pesantren-tarekat ; https://openlibrary.org/books/OL955893M/Kitab_kuning_pesantren_dan_tarekat ; HathiTrust record 002965329
- **Status:** SEARCH-VERIFIED (publisher/catalogue pages, table of contents, and preface excerpts retrieved this session)

## Key facts extracted

1. Table of contents (via gusdur.net) shows the book's three parts: (1) Jaringan Ulama dan Dampak Transnasionalisme [ulama networks and transnationalism]; (2) Pendidikan Islam Tradisional di Indonesia, containing chapters "Pesantren dan Kitab Kuning: Kesinambungan dan Perkembangan Tradisi Keilmuan Islam di Indonesia", "Kitab Fiqh di Pesantren Indonesia dan Malaysia", "Kitab Kuning: Buku-buku Berhuruf Arab yang Dipergunakan di Lingkungan Pesantren", "Kitab Kuning dan Perempuan, Perempuan dan Kitab Kuning"; (3) Tarekat-tarekat dan Perkembangannya di Indonesia.
2. The chapter title defines kitab kuning functionally as "books in Arabic letters used in pesantren circles" - i.e., the Arabic-script (including pegon) textbook corpus.
3. Origin: the book grew out of van Bruinessen's research consultancy at LIPI Jakarta (1986-1990) on attitudes of Indonesian ulama; Abdurrahman Wahid introduced him to leading kiai. Gus Dur's preface states that van Bruinessen's bibliographic work assembled hundreds of titles by 'ulama Jawi' into a library of kitab kuning.
4. Google Scholar profile lists this as his most-cited item (c. 2682 citations shown on the retrieved page).
5. A 2024 SIWAYANG journal article (Alfinur) cites "van Bruinessen ... 'Kitab Kuning: Pesantren dan Tarekat' (1999)" for the point that kitab kuning covers both sharia and non-sharia Islamic disciplines as main teaching material in pesantren - indicating varying edition dates in circulation (1994/1995 Mizan; 1999 printing; 2012 Gading).

## Key facts extracted

1. Scope: kitab kuning manuscripts preserved in Javanese-Penginyongan pesantren (Banyumas, Banjarnegara, Tegal, Kebumen), late 19th to mid-20th century; two dominant textual formations: localized adaptations of authoritative Sunni works, and original compositions by local ulama for pedagogical needs.
2. Corpus spans tafsir, fiqh, kalam/theology, tasawuf/Sufism, ethics, khutbah - i.e., the full pesantren curriculum carried by interlinear-gloss manuscripts.
3. Physical description of the translation practice: "Arabic text often in larger or bolder script, and explanatory Pegon prose or glosses arranged interlinearly or in the margins", a "layered architecture" that "reflects classical pedagogical practices in which teachers recite the Arabic text and immediately unpack it in a vernacular language."
4. Script ideology: "The deployment of Pegon, rather than Romanised Javanese or Indonesian, situates these manuscripts within a distinctly pesantren-based literacy tradition"; citing Johns and Azra, Arabic-script adoption for Malay/Javanese "facilitated a mode of Islamisation in which transregional canons were naturalised within local linguistic ecologies."
5. Cites Riddell's *Transferring a Tradition: 'Abd al-Ra'uf al-Singkili's Rendering into Malay of the Jalalayn Commentary* (with Peter G. Riddell et al., University of California, 1990) as comparandum.
6. Notes prior work on Dawuhan (Banyumas): thirteen manuscripts / thirty-five texts written in Arabic and in Pegon/Javanese with interlinear glosses, covering Qur'anic exegesis through history.
7. Conclusion: Penginyongan pesantren manuscripts are "active educational tools, using Pegon script, marginal annotations, and interlinear glosses to effectively transmit Arabic-Islamic knowledge to Javanese-speaking students"; also documents vulnerability of pesantren manuscript culture to colonial disruptions.
8. Bibliography seen includes Bruinessen, *Kitab Kuning, Pesantren, Dan Tarekat* (Gading Publishing, 2012) and Rosidin et al., ISLAGE 2021 paper on kitab kuning history from a philology perspective.

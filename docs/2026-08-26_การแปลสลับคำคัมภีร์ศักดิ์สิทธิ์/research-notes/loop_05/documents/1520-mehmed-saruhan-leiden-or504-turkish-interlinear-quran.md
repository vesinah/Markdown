## Catalogue facts extracted

1. The copy contains "a word by word interlinear Turkish translation, with occasional commentary, in Old Anatolian Turkish of the entire Arabic text".
2. Script hierarchy: bold script for the Arabic text, smaller script for the translation; the volume has illumination and a full-leather Islamic binding with flap and gold-tooled insets.
3. The dated colophon (1520, Gallipoli) makes this a securely datable exemplar of the Ottoman word-for-word interlinear mushaf tradition referenced by Wilson (dozens of extant interlinear translations, 15th-19th centuries).
4. The referenced editions (Inan 1961; Topaloglu 1976 attributing a version to Muhammed bin Hamza, early 15th c.; Karabacak's edition of the Manisa manuscript) show an established philological literature on Old Anatolian Turkish satir-arasi Qur'an translations.

## Abstract key points (as deposited)
- Studies persuasive and pedagogical strategies in "Sermones de los misterios de nuestra santa fé católica", published in Lima in 1649 by the criollo priest Fernando de Avendaño.
- The sermonario was devised for evangelization of the Andean area ("el área andina").
- Two strategies analyzed under the names "cambios de ritmo discursivo" (discursive rhythm shifts) and "licencias semántico-referenciales" (semantic-referential licences).

## Publisher abstract (as deposited, Crossref)
"When the first European missionaries arrived on other continents, it was decided that the indigenous languages would be used as the means of christianization. There emerged the need to produce grammars and dictionaries of those languages..." The volume is described as "the first published collection of papers on missionary linguistics world-wide", with an introduction by the editors plus papers on methodology, meta-historiography, historical/cultural background; studies of early-modern works written in Spanish, Portuguese, English, and French describing languages including North American languages, Australian languages, Maya, Quechua, Xhosa, Japanese, Kapampangan, Visaya; topics include missionary innovations in lexicography, grammatical analysis, phonology, morphology, syntax; descriptive creativity; cross-continental comparison; Catholic vs Protestant backgrounds.

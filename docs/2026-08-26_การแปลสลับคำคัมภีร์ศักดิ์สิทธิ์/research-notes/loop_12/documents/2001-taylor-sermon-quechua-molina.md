## Abstract key points (as deposited)
- Diego de Molina, cura of Huánuco, completed his "Sermones de la Cuaresma en Lengua quechua" in February 1649.
- Until this article only the preliminary text had been published (Romero 1928, Revista Histórica IX(1): 51-87).
- Taylor presents a paleographic version of the Castilian original of one sermon fragment together with its contemporary Quechua translation -- the task complicated because the manuscript was partially damaged by the 1943 fire of the Biblioteca Nacional del Peru.
- A normalized Quechua version with Spanish translation is also given.

## Notes
- Concrete evidence for topic (D): parish priests composing sermon cycles directly in Quechua (with Spanish originals), outside the officially mandated Lima catechism corpus; shows bilingual composition practice mid-17th century in Huánuco.

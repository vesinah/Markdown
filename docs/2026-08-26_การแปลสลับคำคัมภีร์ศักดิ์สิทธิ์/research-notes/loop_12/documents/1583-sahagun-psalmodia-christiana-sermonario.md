## Notes
- Title explicitly couples "Psalmodia Christiana" with "Sermonario" -- a concrete Mexican example of topic (D): the sermonario in an indigenous language, here arranged "en cantares o Psalmos" so that Indians may sing them "in the areytos they perform in the churches", i.e., adaptation to indigenous song performance practice.
- Printed by Pedro Ocharte, one of the earliest printers of New Spain; predates the Lima Doctrina Christiana (1584) by one year.
- OCR derivatives exist on archive.org (DjVu TXT ~366KB) enabling future text mining of the Nahuatl sermon texts.

## Notes
- The volume's programmatic chapter is Hovdhaugen, "Missionary grammars. An attempt at defining a field of research", in the same volume, pp. 9-22 -- confirmed via the reference list of Zwartjes's Oxford Research Encyclopedia entry "Missionary Grammars" (DOI 10.1093/acrefore/9780199384655.013.384), where it is cited as: "Hovdhaugen, E. (1996). Missionary grammars. An attempt at defining a field of research. In E. Hovdhaugen (Ed.), ... and the word was God: Missionary linguistics and missionary grammar (pp. 9-22). Munster: Nodus."
- This is the publication that launched "missionary linguistics" as a named historiographic subfield; the John Benjamins Missionary Linguistics conference series that followed (2003 Oslo onward) traces to it.
- Review existence in Language (the LSA journal) and Historiographia Linguistica confirms wide scholarly reception within two years of publication.

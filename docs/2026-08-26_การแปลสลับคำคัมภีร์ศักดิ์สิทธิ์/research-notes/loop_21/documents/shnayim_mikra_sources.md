# Document: Shnayim Mikra Ve-Echad Targum - authoritative descriptions

## Core halakhic chain (verified from fetched pages)

1. Talmud Bavli Berakhot 8a-b (Rav Huna bar Yehuda in the name of Rabbi Ammi):
   "לְעוֹלָם יַשְׁלִים אָדָם פָּרָשִׁיּוֹתָיו עִם הַצִּבּוּר. שְׁנַיִם מִקְרָא וְאֶחָד תַּרְגּוּם" - "A person should always complete his portions with the congregation - twice Scripture and once translation." Reward stated: long life ("מֵאֲרִיכִין לוֹ יָמָיו וּשְׁנוֹתָיו").
   Source text visible at Sefaria: https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot.8a.30-8b.5

2. Codification: Rambam, Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot Tefillah 13:25; Tur, Orach Chayim 285; Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 285:1-7.
   - SA OC 285:1: obligation to read each weekly parasha twice in Hebrew and once in Aramaic targum even though one hears the public reading (per search-result rendering of Sefaria text).
   - Rema (Isserles) OC 285: Rashi's commentary shares the status of Targum; God-fearing people read both Rashi and targum. Also Rema OC 285:7: haftarah reading customary but not obligatory.

3. Key secondary rulings (from Yeshivat Har Etzion article + OU Ohr Olam, both fetched; underlying rulings are primary halakhic texts - Rambam Tefillah 13:25, Mechilta Bo via Bet Yosef OC 285:5, Magen Avraham 285:1, Chazon Ish practice - verifiable on Sefaria):
   - Ra'avan, siman 88: mitzva as "personal keriyat ha-Torah" for those absent from public reading; structure of two readings corresponds to sirsur (two readers standing at the bima), targum reading mirrors the synagogue meturgeman practice.
   - Tosafot on Berakhot: debate whether a translation into another language can substitute for Onkelos; rejected on technical grounds (Onkelos preserves precise "meanings").
   - Rosh: may substitute Rashi or other commentary; Semag, positive commandment 19: geonim insist on Onkelos because it was "delivered at Sinai" (cf. Megillah 3a).
   - Shulchan Arukh OC 554:4: shnayim mikra permitted even on Tisha Be-Av when Torah study is forbidden - evidence it functions as an adjunct to keriyat ha-Torah rather than ordinary Torah study (Terumat Ha-Deshen 1:23 discussion).
   - Mechilta, Parshat Bo (cited by Bet Yosef OC 285:5): Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi commanded his sons to complete their portions before the Shabbat meal.
   - Timing rules (SA OC 285): ideally Friday afternoon; at latest until Mincha of Shabbat, or Tuesday night; Mishnah Berurah 285:5-6, 12; Aruch HaShulchan 285.
   - Verse-by-verse order (pasuk-pasuk-targum) vs whole-sedra methods: Magen Avraham 285:1; Arizal (Shaarei Kavanot); Chazon Ish practice; MB 285:14 permits whole-sedra method.
   - Chafetz Chaim (Michtavei Chafetz Chayim, letter 18): nowadays substitute Rashi since we cannot fulfill via Targum (minority view).

## Layout relevance for the project

The practice prescribes verse-level alternation of original (Hebrew, twice) and translation (Aramaic Onqelos once). Printed chumashim engineered for this exist: e.g., "Chumash Haavarat HaSidra" and "Chumash Shnayim Mikra Ve'Echad Targum" print the Hebrew text twice followed by the Aramaic so the sequence enforces the ritual repetition; Chok l'Yisrael prints Hebrew once with daily aliyah divisions. This is a printed-layout embodiment of an oral alternation rule - directly comparable to interleaved Yiddish/Ladino formats (target E).

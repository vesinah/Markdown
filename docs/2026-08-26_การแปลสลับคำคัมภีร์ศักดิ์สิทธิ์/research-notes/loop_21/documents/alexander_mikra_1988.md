## Contextual detail from Kuśmirek article quoting Alexander's chapter area

The article (which draws on the Mikra chapter and related literature) notes the synagogue practice rule: when reading aloud, the Hebrew text is read by one person and the translation by another; the Hebrew reader may not look away from his scroll while the meturgeman must recite from memory (this is the Mishnah Megillah 4:4 rule discussed in targum scholarship). This links targets A/B/C: oral alternation of Hebrew verse-by-verse with Aramaic translation.

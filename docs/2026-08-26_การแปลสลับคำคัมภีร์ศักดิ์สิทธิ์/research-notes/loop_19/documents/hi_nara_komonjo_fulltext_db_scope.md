## Interpretation for target (A)

This is a full-text database of transcribed Nara-period documents (mostly Shosoin
monastery/archive documents) as printed in Dai-Nihon Komonjo, henshen monjso volumes,
covering 702-780 CE. It indexes document metadata and transcription text; it does not
encode kunten glosses (wokototen positions/shapes/readings) as searchable data.

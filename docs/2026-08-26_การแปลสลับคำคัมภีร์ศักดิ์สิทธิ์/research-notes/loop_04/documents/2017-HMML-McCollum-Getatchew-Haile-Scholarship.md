## Key facts (session-verified)

- HMML: Getatchew Haile was "Regents Professor Emeritus of Medieval Studies at Saint John's University and Curator Emeritus of the Ethiopia Study Center at HMML." His "most significant scholastic legacy pertains to the Ethiopian Manuscript Microfilm Library (EMML)... the most important archive of manuscript material in Ethiopian Studies."
- He joined the EMML project in 1973 while teaching at Haile Sellassie I University; after exile he became EMML's principal cataloger. "By 1993 he had published detailed descriptions of several thousand manuscripts, singlehandedly exceeding the historical efforts of the entire field in this area, a contribution for which he was awarded the prestigious MacArthur Fellowship."
- MacArthur page (Class of 1988): "philologist and linguist of modern and ancient Ethiopian languages and literature"; catalogued more than 5,000 manuscripts, published ten catalogues; among them "are the oldest texts of virtually every book of the Ethiopic Old Testament, including important intertestamental books such as the Book of Enoch, which survived in its entirety only in Ethiopia."
- Special focus: "theological controversies in mid-fifteenth century Ethiopia, particularly the disputes between Emperor Zar'a Ya'eqob and the Esṭifānosite sect."
- Festschrift: Studies in Ethiopian Languages, Literature, and History: Festschrift for Getatchew Haile, ed. Adam Carter McCollum, Aethiopistische Forschungen 83, Wiesbaden: Harrassowitz, 2017 (ISBN 978-3-447-10848-5); twenty-six articles in three sections (history and culture; texts and manuscripts; languages) plus "A selected bibliography of Getatchew Haile's writings" (by Bausi and Rave, pp. 609-619).
- Posthumous article seen this session: Getatchew Haile (†), "A Fragment of the Late Aksumite or Early Zagʷe Period on the Commentary on the Gospel of Matthew," Aethiopica 24 (2021), pp. 223-232 — transcription/translation of a commentary fragment found in EMML 7229 fols 122r-123v, "likely to be a translation of a commentary by an early church scholar," Vorlage unknown.
- AfricaBib listing confirms journal articles across Journal of Ethiopian Studies, Paideuma, Northeast African Studies, Rassegna di studi etiopici.

## Relevance to loop topic

Haile is the central modern scholar for Ge'ez sacred-text culture and the manuscript base (EMML) through which Ge'ez originals and their Amharic commentary traditions are preserved and edited; his editions make the hieroglossic corpus itself accessible.

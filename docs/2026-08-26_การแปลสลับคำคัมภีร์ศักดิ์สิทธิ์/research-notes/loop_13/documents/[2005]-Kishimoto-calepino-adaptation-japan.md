- Title establishes the analytical frame: the Amakusa Dictionarium Latino-Lusitanicum ac Iaponicum (1595) is treated as an adaptation of Ambrogio Calepino's European polyglot dictionary for the Japan mission - the same derivation stated on the 1870 Petitjean reprint title page ("ex Ambrosii Calepini volumine depromptum") verified independently this session via NDL records.

## Notes

- This chapter is the standard scholarly reference linking the Japan Jesuit dictionaries to the European polyglot lexicographic tradition; combined with the NDL title evidence gathered this session it anchors the claim that Kirishitan-ban language tools were deliberate transpositions of a European print genre into a Japanese setting.
- Full text was not accessible this session (paywalled); concrete internal findings (which Calepino edition served as base, how Japanese entries were inserted, layout decisions) remain open questions.

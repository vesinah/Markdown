2. **ซีเรียค:** Syriac interlinear traditions (Peshitta, East Syriac exegetical schools)
3. **อาหรับ:** Arabic interlinear Qur'an translations (Persian tarjuma), Judeo-Arabic
4. **ทิเบต:** interlinear/dbu-med annotations, sa bcad, manuscript notes tradition
5. **มลายู-จาวี:** interlinear Qur'an translations (tarjamah), Pegon script texts
6. **ล้านนา/ไทย:** nissaya glossing tradition, glosses in palm-leaf manuscripts
7. **เกาหลี:** gugyeol 구결 口訣 reading glosses on Chinese texts
8. **Old High German:** Reichenau glosses, Abrogans, Tatian interlinear
9. **Reichenau Psalter** Latin-OHG interlinear glosses
10. **Egypt/Coptic:** Sahidic interlinear traditions (ถ้ามี)
11. **Maya/Aztec:** colonial-era interlinear alphabetic glosses on pictorial codices (ถ้ามี)
12. Digital humanities projects: e-ktobe? GlossIT outputs, kunten databases

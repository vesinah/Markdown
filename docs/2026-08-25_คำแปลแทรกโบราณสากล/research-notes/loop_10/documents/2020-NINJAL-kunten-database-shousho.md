Content (bilingual page, JP/EN):
- The page publishes a database of gloss information (Kunten information) assigned to Kunten materials (Classical Chinese textbooks). Digitized material at present: Shangshu (尚書, old movable-type third edition) volumes 1-9 owned by NINJAL (National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics).
- Allows searching of wokototen ヲコト点 ("including morphosyntactic gloss, vernacular reading gloss, Sinoxenic reading gloss, tone gloss, etc.") and inverted glosses / word-order marks (語順点: re-point レ点, one-two points 一二点), showing volumes and folios where glosses exist.
- Simple search page searches wokototen by appearance (color) and shape with frequency counts; detailed search page supports AND queries on point position, appearance (color), shape etc.
- Okototenzu (kunten mapping table) for the material is available as a PDF (mappingtable.pdf).
- Material images: NINJAL digital library "日本語史研究資料[国立国語研究所蔵] 尚書（古活字版第三種本）", https://dglb01.ninjal.ac.jp/ninjaldl/bunken.php?title=syousyo
- Update history: test public release 2020-01-31.
- Developed under: NIHU wide-area collaboration project "総合書物学" (2016-present), NINJAL unit "表記情報と書誌形態情報を加えた日本語歴史コーパスの精緻化"; JSPS KAKENHI 17K18506 "漢文訓点資料の国際文書構造記述による共有化と書き下し文自動生成のための基礎研究" (2017-present).

Related NINJAL resource referenced in Tsutsumi et al. 2023:
- Wokototen charts Database (ヲコト点図集データベース): https://cid.ninjal.ac.jp/wokototendb/

Note on terminology: the database's English description explicitly includes "tone gloss" among wokototen categories, i.e., sando 声点 (Middle Chinese tone marks) are treated within the wokototen/kunten apparatus.

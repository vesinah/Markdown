Project metadata:
- Abbreviation: Structured Description of Kunten-Shiryo
- Project Leader: TAKADA Tomokazu, Associate Professor, Department of Linguistic Theory and Structure, NINJAL
- Project period: October 2009 - September 2012
- Research field: History of Japanese Language
- Keywords: Kanbun-Kundoku, Kunten-Shiryo, Digital archive

Summary (page text):
- Kunten-Shiryo = classical Chinese documents with marks added for rendering them into Japanese; used to grasp aspects of the history of Japanese (characters, pronunciation, vocabulary, usage).
- Study of Kunten-Shiryo lags behind other fields in use of digital technologies such as electronic interpretation texts and digital imaging of originals.
- Focusing on Kongocho Issai nyorai Shinjitsu sho Dai jogensho Daikyo Okyo Vol. 1 (owned by NINJAL), the project developed a method of structured description of interpretations based on the originals, creating a prototype of shared digital interpretations verifiable against digital images of the originals.

Notes:
- This is the NINJAL collaborative research project behind the later kunten databases (Shousho kunten DB at cid.ninjal.ac.jp/kunten-syousyo3/, Wokototen charts DB at cid.ninjal.ac.jp/wokototendb/) per those pages' funding statements.
- Takada Tomokazu is also co-author of Tsutsumi et al. 2023 (F1000Research 12:506) on wokototen measurements.

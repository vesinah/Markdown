Key points visible from page and search-result snippet of the same article (shesl.org):
- The article is an English-language introduction to kunten-glossed texts (documents written in classical Chinese annotated so they can be read in Japanese) and to their research history in Japan.
- From DDG snippet of the same URL: the dossier treats kanbun kundoku in relation to theories of reading and translation; Japanese scholars prefer NOT to construe kanbun kundoku as translation.
- The dossier n°7 was co-edited by John Whitman and Franck Cinato (2014), pairing Japanese kunten with other vernacular-reading traditions.

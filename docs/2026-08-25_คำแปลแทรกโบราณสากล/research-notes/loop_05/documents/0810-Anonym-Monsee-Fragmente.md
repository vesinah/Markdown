# Die Monseer Fragmente (Monsee-Wien Fragments)

- Project page fetched: https://www.germ.univie.ac.at/projekt/monseer-fragmente/
- Institut fuer Germanistik, Universitaet Wien; project leader Stephan Mueller; team Elke Krotz, Lisa Rethage, Dennis Wegener; runtime 2008-2012; funded by DFG (grant 68994184); cooperation with Oesterreichische Nationalbibliothek.

## Full text of project description (translation of key claims)

The Monsee Fragments are the remains of the only surviving Latin-German collective manuscript (Sammelhandschrift) of the Early Middle Ages. The astonishing vernacular virtuosity of these texts - which even led to their being understood as a concrete result of Charlemagne's efforts toward a German grammar - is nevertheless not reflected in their philological accessibility. The translations of the Gospel of Matthew, several sermons/homilies, and the treatise De fide catholica of Isidore of Seville do not exist in a uniform modern edition. Access to this singular translation project as a whole is currently impossible, so that neither the linguistic nor the contentual consistency of the collection can be assessed. This gap - made more painful by some new discoveries - was to be closed by the DFG project: for the first time the text ensemble was edited manuscript-near as a whole, with the Latin models given due space; through a translation, reading text, commentary, and dictionary, this central document of early Carolingian vernacular culture was made accessible beyond early medieval German studies.

## Manuscript facts (from Handschriftencensus https://handschriftencensus.de/11565)

- Sigle: Discissus; Wien, Oesterreichische Nationalbibl., Cod. 3093* [a] (remains of at least 47 leaves); Hannover, Landesbibl., Ms. I 20b [b] (2 leaves); Wien, ONB Cod. 2997 back cover pastedown [c].
- Traditional name: "Monsee-Wiener Fragmente" (VL), otherwise always "Monseer Fragmente".
- Parchment; leaf size 313 x 200 mm; writing space 250 x 140 mm; 1 column; 30 lines (partly 29).
- Scribal language: Bavarian (bair.).
- Writing place: Mondsee.
- Dating: um 810 (Menhardt); 9th century, first third (Bischoff catalog I.1502).
- Type: Latin-German bilingual, lat.-ahd. Sammelhandschrift.
- Script: Carolingian minuscule.
- Text arrangement note: "Textgestaltung verso der lat., recto der entsprechende ahd. Text" (Latin on the versos, corresponding Old High German text on the rectos).
- Contents (per project page): Gospel of Matthew (translation), several homilies, Isidore of Seville De fide catholica.
- Key editions/literature listed there: George A. Hench (ed.), The Monsee Fragments. Newly collated text with introduction, notes, grammatical treatise and exhaustive glossary and photo-lithographic fac-simile, Strassburg 1890; Hans Eggers (ed.), Der althochdeutsche Isidor. Nach der Pariser Handschrift und den Monseer Fragmenten (ATB 63), Tuebingen 1964; Klaus Matzel 1970; Elke Krotz, Auf den Spuren des althochdeutschen Isidor (Heidelberg 2002); Krotz/Kaska, Ein neues Monseer Fragment, ZfdA 145 (2016), 82-88.

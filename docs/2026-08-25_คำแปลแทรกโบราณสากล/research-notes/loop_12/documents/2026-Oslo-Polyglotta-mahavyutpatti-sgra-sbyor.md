Significance for interlinear-gloss project:
- The Mahavyutpatti is the standardized Sanskrit-Tibetan glossary promulgated under imperial decree (late 8th-early 9th c.), included in the Tengyur (Toh. 4346 per standard catalogs); the paired sGra sbyor bam po gnyis pa explains translation decisions and restricts translation of tantras/dharanis/mantras without royal permission.
- Standard critical edition for reference: Ishihama Ryozo & Fukuda Yoichi, A New Critical Edition of the Mahavyutpatti - Sanskrit-Tibetan-Mongolian Dictionary of Buddhist Terminology (Tokyo: Toyo Bunko, 1989).

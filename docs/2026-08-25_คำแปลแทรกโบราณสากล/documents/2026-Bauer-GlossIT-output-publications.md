## Project frame (from project pages)
- ERC Consolidator Grant "GlossIT - Celtic and Latin glossing traditions: uncovering early medieval language contact and knowledge transfer", Grant Agreement No 101123203 (Horizon Europe), PI Assoz. Prof. Bernhard Bauer, Institut fur Digitale Geisteswissenschaften, University of Graz.
- Core sources: early medieval copies of Bede's computistical works and Priscian's Ars grammatica transmitting Insular Celtic (Old Breton, Old Irish, Old Welsh) and Latin glosses, 9th-10th c.
- Methods: comparative philology, historical linguistics, digital humanities (handwritten text recognition / eScriptorium, network analysis, NLP), biological computation (DNA-sequence alignment applied to gloss corpora).

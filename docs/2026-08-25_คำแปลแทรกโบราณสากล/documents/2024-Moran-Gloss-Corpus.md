# Full-text notes: Gloss Corpus (glossing.org) - platform status

- Source: Moran, Padraic, "Gloss Corpus", version 1.0 (2024), https://www.glossing.org/glosscorpus/ ; staging instance https://staging.glossing.org/glosscorpus/
- Fetched: 2026-08-25 (via websearch full-page extraction)

## Platform description (verbatim extract)

"Gloss Corpus is a platform for publishing and analysis of glosses on medieval manuscripts, based on Open Access and open standards for data. Contributors receive full credit for their work, with each edition of glosses assigned a unique DOI and persistent URL. Contributors also have the option to avail of peer-review."

"Open-access publishing for medieval glosses... As well as a reading and browsing interface, the resource currently offers a simple search feature. Tools for more complex searches, visualisations and analytics are currently in development."

Citation format used on site: "Padraic Moran, Gloss Corpus, version 1.0 (2024) <http://www.glossing.org/glosscorpus/>".

## Collections currently available

Production site (v1.0, 2024): 66 collections on 4 primary texts = 24,872 glosses total:
- Isidore of Seville, Etymologiae book 1: 54 gloss collections, 4,279 glosses (mostly ed. E. Stein; MSS incl. BL Arundel 129, Bern 101, Bologna 797, Orleans 296 with 768 glosses, Leiden VLO 41 with 682, Paris lat. 7670 with 353...)
- Priscian, Ars grammatica: 1 collection (St Gall, Stiftsbibliothek 904, ed. R. Hofman rev. P. Moran), 13,395 glosses
- Paul, Epistles: 1 collection (Wurzburg, Universitatsbibliothek M.p.th.f. 12 [Wb.], ed. Stokes & Strachan rev. A. Doyle), 3,642 glosses
- (4th text group per header navigation: Glosses on Priscian / Isidore / Paul's Epistles)

Staging site (newer build, accessed same date): 73 collections on 7 primary texts = 33,648 glosses total, adding:
- Anon., Commentary on the Psalms: Milan Ambrosiana C 301 inf. [Ml.] ed. Stokes & Strachan rev. A. Griffith, 8,443 glosses (the "Milan glosses")
- Aelfric of Eynsham, Glossary: BL Cotton Faustina A X [F], ed. H. Pagan & A. Seiler, 239 glosses
- Oengus mac Oengobann, Felire Oenguso: Bodleian Rawlinson B505 [R1], ed. N. Stam, 1,519 glosses
- Priscian expanded to 9 collections / 15,432 glosses (adding BnF lat. 10290 [E] 1,435 glosses marked "(testing)", BnF lat. 7503/7504/7505..., Exeter College MS 25, Clm 11302, Montpellier H 21, BnF lat. 8959, Columbia Plimpton MS 43 - ed. A. Ellis & J. Mania)

## Institutional context
- PI: Padraic Moran, Classics, University of Galway; database registered as peer-reviewed research output (University of Galway Pure portal, published 1 Jan 2022).
- Embedded in GLOSSAM project "Global and Local Scholarship on Annotated Manuscripts", Research Ireland Laureate Awards 2022-2026 (https://glossam.ie/); GLOSSAM component G2 develops Gloss Corpus as "open-access, accessible, sustainable and citable publishing system"; component G1 comparative international survey of glossing traditions.
- Related resources by same PI: stgallpriscian.ie (v2.1, 2023), Early Irish Glossaries Database, Armarium Digital Editions platform; planned Handbook of Glossing (with Matthew Zisk), 27 chapters spanning Ireland to Japan (pmoran.ie).

## Assessment for chapter 12
Most mature open infrastructure dedicated specifically to interlinear/marginal gloss data; growth from v1.0 (24,872 glosses) to staging (33,648) visible within 2024-2026 window; DOI-per-edition model operational.

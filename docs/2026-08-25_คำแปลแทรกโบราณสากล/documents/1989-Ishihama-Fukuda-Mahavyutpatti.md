## Evidence on entry counts observed in the scan's OCR (this session)
- The edition includes a concordance appendix mapping its own sequential entry numbers to source witnesses: L = Leningrad manuscript folios, N = Narthang Tanjur, P = Peking Tanjur, MT = Mongolian Tanjur, DC = another witness; cross-references to Sarat Chandra Das's edition appear as "(S.xxxx)".
- Observed maxima in the OCR: own entry numbers run to about 9985; Das-edition reference numbers reach S.9565 near the end.
- Interpretation (cautious): the Mahavyutpatti entry stock in these editions is on the order of roughly 9,500-9,900 entries. A canonical published statement of the exact total was not accessible this session; ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยันสำหรับจำนวนบท (chapters/le'u) ที่แน่นอน.

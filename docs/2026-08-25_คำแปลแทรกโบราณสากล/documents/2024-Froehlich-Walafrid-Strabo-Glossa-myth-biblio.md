Note: this is the classic study arguing that the traditional attribution of the Glossa
ordinaria to Walafrid Strabo is a post-medieval myth. Its argument is summarized in detail
in Ekman 2019 (U Toronto dissertation, fetched full text in this workspace):
the attribution goes back to Johannes Trithemius (16th c.), entered the printed Gloss itself
in the 1590 Paris edition, was split into marginal (=Strabo) vs interlinear (=Anselm) in the
1617 Douay/Antwerp edition, repeated by Migne (PL), questioned by Samuel Berger (1893) because
no pre-twelfth-century manuscripts of the Gloss exist, and dismantled by Jean de Blic (1949).

# 《宗喀巴大師廣解事師五十頌》/《事師五十頌廣解》 — attribution evidence: 能海, NOT 法尊

Loop B04, 2026-08-26. RESEARCH ONLY. All quotes verbatim from pages actually accessed this loop.

## 1. The claim under test

Task brief assumed: "宗喀巴大師廣解事師五十頌 by 法尊". Loop A11 had already flagged this as unconfirmed (NTU DDBC credits 釋能海; 法尊法师全集 TOC has no such entry). This loop re-tested and now DISPROVES the 法尊 attribution with converging independent witnesses.

## 2. Evidence A — 《中國宗教歷史文獻集成》 catalog listing (bbs.gujige.com thread, accessed 2026-08-26)

The Republic-era holdings list contains BOTH works, each with explicit translator credit:

> |事师法五十颂释 |宗喀巴著. 汤芗铭等译 |民国铅印本 |
> |事师五十颂广解 |宗喀巴著. 能海译 |民国木刻本 |

⇒ 《事師五十頌廣解》 = 能海譯, surviving in a Republic-era WOODBLOCK print (民國木刻本) — matching the kongfz.com used-book listing already saved in loop A11 (线装本一册, 出版社/年代不详).

## 3. Evidence B — Shi Shiliang's essay on the two Gelug translators (xlmz.net thread 12579, text from 藏人文化网, accessed 2026-08-26)

In the section on 能海法師 (1928ff., 康薩喇嘛 lineage):

> 「还译有《事师五十颂》，并编撰《事师五十颂广解》。」

⇒ Nenghai TRANSLATED the root text AND COMPILED the Guangjie (expanded explanation). Same essay lists for 法尊 only unrelated titles (密宗道次第广论 etc.) — no 事師五十頌 item.

## 4. Evidence C — 《能海大师传》 译述著作 list (putixia.org/wenku/read_33632.html, accessed 2026-08-26)

Items 3–4 of the 译述著作 list:

> 「3、事师五十颂
> 4、事师五十颂广解」

## 5. Evidence D — NTU DDBC authority record + Bliss Wisdom republication note (saved in loop_A11)

> 「事师五十颂注疏：成满学者一切希愿 — 著者: 马鸣菩萨 ; 大宝贤藏 ; 释能海 ; 宗喀巴 — 出版年: 2001」
> 「书名原译《宗喀巴大师广解事师五十颂》，今依藏文原著校订。」

⇒ The modern Bliss Wisdom edition explicitly says its book was ORIGINALLY titled 《宗喀巴大師廣解事師五十頌》 — i.e., the title in question IS the 能海-lineage translation/commentary, re-edited against the Tibetan in 2001.

## 6. Evidence E — negative check: 《法尊法师全集》 (中国藏学出版社)

tibetology.ac.cn new-book page (accessed 2026-08-26) gives the complete 14-volume TOC (译著 显教类/因明戒律类/密教类/仪轨史传类, 影印本 入中论善显密意疏, 菩提道次第广论, 密集五次第, 宗喀巴大师传 …): NO 事师五十颂 item of any kind.
(Also: 索达吉's putixia edition is credited 「巴布拉尊者 造 索达吉堪布 译」; 湯薌銘's is 「馬鳴菩薩 造頌 宗喀巴大師 造釋 湯薌銘 等譯」 — neither is 法尊.)

## 8. Consequence for the comparative chapter

The Chinese commentarial tradition to cite is:
1. 能海《事師五十頌廣解》 (=《宗喀巴大師廣解事師五十頌》), 民國木刻本; modern recension 《事師五十頌注疏——成滿學者一切希願》 (2001, 福智/Bliss Wisdom lineage) — translator 能海 with Tibetan collaborator tradition crediting 大寶賢藏 (the Rinchen Zangpo of the Tibetan colophon).
2. 湯薌銘等譯《事師法五十頌釋》(民國鉛印本; e-text secured in loop_A05).
3. 索達吉堪布譯講《事師五十頌釋/略解》(甘肅民族出版社《宗喀巴大師文集》 basis; colophon 「公元二零零二年十一月一日 译毕于色达喇荣圣地」).
Any sentence attributing a Chinese 廣解 translation to 法尊 must be corrected.

## 2. TASK-BY-TASK FINDINGS

### Task 1 — Thai
Queries: คุรุปัญจาสิกา แปล พุทธตันตระ; "ศรัทธาคุรุ" ห้าสิบบท อาจารย์ แปลไทย dhammahome; "ปัญจาสิกา" ครูอาจารย์ "ห้าสิบ" บทสวด วัชรยาน ไทย.
Outcome: ยังไม่พบ Thai translation on dhammahome.com, kalyanamitra.org, or any Thai Vajrayana site indexed. The word พุทธตันตระ appears only in academic articles (Thaijo) discussing tantric Buddhism generally. Thai remains an open gap; candidate future channel: Thai translations of FPMT/Lama Yeshe materials.

### Task 2 — Spanish / Russian / Mongolian / Vietnamese
- Spanish: CONFIRMED. Nagarjuna Valencia PDF (translator dedication quoted verbatim in evidence file); identical text mirrored at archive.org and Scribd; Spanish audio reading on YouTube (2021).
- Russian: CONFIRMED, richest non-Western European tradition after English. Four independent venues: abhidharma.ru (full text + Dhargyey commentary), narthang.ru free e-book, buddhist-translations.ru book, academia.edu DOC. Russian Karmapa teaching video series also exists ("Кармапа. Пятьдесят строф о гуру").
- Vietnamese: CONFIRMED, three distinct renderings (Nguyễn Pram 2003; Hồng Như 2021; kimcuongthua.vn web). Strongest new discovery of Loop A15.
- Mongolian: ยังไม่พบ. Only generic results.

### Task 3 — Lotsawa House (live fetch)
https://lotsawahouse.org/indian-masters/ashvaghosha/ — Aśvaghoṣa Series holds exactly 3 English texts (+3 Tibetan): Extremely Concise Dedication (Toh 4390, 29 Nov 2021); Root Downfalls of the Vajra Vehicle (confession recension, 11 Jul 2018); Summary of the Root Downfalls of the Vajra Vehicle (Toh 2478, 10 Jul 2018). Page names author variants "Durdharṣakāla, Bhavideva (bha bi lha)".
Finding: Lotsawa House does NOT carry Gurupañcāśikā itself.

### Task 4 — 84000 (live fetch)
read.84000.co/search?q=Gurupancasika → "No matching results."
Finding: 84000 has not published Toh 3721; consistent with its Kangyur-first mandate (3721 is Tengyur).

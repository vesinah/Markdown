=== Interpretation (researcher note, Loop A10) ===
The canonical colophon of ALL Tanjur editions consulted (dPe bsdur ma, Cone, Derge,
Golden, Narthang, Peking) attributes the work to "slob dpon chen po rta dbyangs"
= great Ācārya Aśvaghoṣa, translated/revised by the Indian paṇḍita Padmākaravarman
and the chief editor-translator Rin chen bzang po. NO canonical edition names
Bha bi lha or Vāpilladatta in its colophon.

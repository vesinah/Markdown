## Synthesis: theoretical axes for the analytical chapters

1. **Authority bifurcation (Ruegg):** guru as *mchod gnas* — sacred authority structurally opposed to and symbiotic with temporal order. The *Gurupañcāśikā* radicalizes this: the Buddha is said to dwell in the guru; hence veneration of the guru outranks veneration of the Buddha (textual claim attributed to the text, to be cited from the Tibetan canon in Phase C).
2. **Lineage as institution (Kapstein; unverified item flagged):** transmission theory explains why the text demands total service/devotion — the guru is the sole gateway to abhiṣeka.
3. **Social history (Davidson):** the ācārya as medieval institutional entrepreneur; the text as ideology legitimating a new professional class.
4. **Vow framework (Dalton 2022):** guru-honoring as the first/root samaya; the text as proto-vow-manual.
5. **Critical hermeneutics (Wedemeyer; Lucia 2018):** semiology of esoteric authority and modern scrutiny of charismatic asymmetry — required for a balanced analytical chapter.

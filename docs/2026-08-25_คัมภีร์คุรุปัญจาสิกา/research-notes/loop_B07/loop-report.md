## 2. Findings

### 2.1 The thematic grouping (18 groups)

G1 v.1 maṅgala+commitment | G2 vv.2-3 universal worship protocol (abhiṣeka authority claim, thrice-daily cult) | G3 vv.4-5 ordained-disciple exception (lay/junior guru) | G4 vv.6-9 mutual examination, guru/disciple qualifications | G5 vv.10-15 consequences of despising guru (Avīci, calamity catalogue) | G6 vv.16-21 offering/dakṣiṇā/samaya | G7 vv.22-23 guru = Vajradhara; shadow/caitya | G8 vv.24-26 obedience; property & entourage | G9 vv.27-32 deportment in presence (sight/hearing) | G10 vv.33-34 bodily service; naming rules | G11 vv.35-37 communication script; requesting teaching | G12 vv.38-39 humility (navavadhū simile) | G13 vv.40-43 ritual agency by permission; reciprocity | G14 vv.44-45 co-disciple correction; sickness exemption | G15 vv.46-47 summary: pleasing guru → siddhi (Vajradhara quote) | G16 v.48 refuge+bodhicitta threshold for receiving text | G17 v.49 dharma-vessel, mantra-gift, fourteen root downfalls | G18 v.50 dedication.

### 2.2 Commentator structure confirmed

The long commentary's indigenous outline (lines 1485-1547) matches the grouping almost perfectly: Brief explanation (vv.2-9) / Extensive explanation split into (A) giving up irreverence (vv.10-15) and (B) eight reverences — offerings 16-21, perceiving-as-buddha 22-23, obeying word 24-25, goods&entourage 26, behavior purification 27-32, body/speech devotion 33-34, abandoning pride 35-39, not acting on own wishes 40-43 / Discussion 44 / Exception 45 / Summary 46-47 / Time 48 / Receptacle 49 / Conclusion 50. Short commentary headings agree on all major joints (its variants: merges 1-2, 43-44, 48-49). The tradition itself treats vv.48-49 as meta-instruction (who may receive the text and when), not further practice.

### 2.3 Key terms located

abhiṣeka v.2 (+ three/four initiation doctrines, comm.); samaya vv.6/17/19/44 (explicit Skt gloss at long comm. line 159; dam-tsig bla ma = samaya guru at lines 696-700); avīci v.14 (three supporting tantras cited); ḍākinī + vināyaka v.12 (vināyaka lexically fixed by Ch 頻那夜迦 per B04); siddhi vv.21/25/47/50; fourteen root downfalls confirmed at **v.49** not v.48 (Ch omits caturdaśa); tri-ratna refuge at **v.48** paired with pure motive/bodhicitta; bodhicitta cluster vv.18/20/21/22/48/50.

### 2.4 Most-emphasized verse (local proxy measurement)

By commentary exposition volume: single-verse maximum at **v.14** (~56 lines vs ~23 for v.25 — the task-hinted alternative loses decisively here), block maximum at **vv.7-9** (~175 lines). v.47 is the formal proof-text (Vajradhara quotation). Szántó confirms wide quotation in 10th-11th c. Indian commentaries but saved extracts do not isolate a verse there — gap flagged for Phase C.

## 3. Cross-tradition notes affecting the structure chapter

- bsa045 brackets vv.34-50 as re-Sanskritized from Tibetan (Lévi ms breaks mid-v.33; new ms NAK 5-135 covers ≤35b then resumes v.48) — cite this status for any Sanskrit phrasing in G10-G18.
- Chinese recension: Skt vv.11-12 → Ch vv.11-13 (calamity expansion, G5); Ch v41 is a Chinese-only insertion ("never seek faults in the guru"), thematically adjacent to G7/G12 — evidence that fault-seeking was felt under-regulated in the Indic count of 50.
- Metre: vv.1-49 anuṣṭubh in all witnesses; v.50 expanded in Sarnath Sanskrit, regular in new ms and Tibetan.
- Attribution caveat for framing: Aśvaghoṣa (old ms + translations) vs Vāpilladatta/Bha bi lha (new ms colophon).

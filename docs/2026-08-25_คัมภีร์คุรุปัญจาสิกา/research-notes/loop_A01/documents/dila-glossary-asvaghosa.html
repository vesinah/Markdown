    • Other texts in Tanjur attributed to him: Tôh. nos. 1753, 2478, 2479, 3721, 3911 (and 4519), 3912 (and 4518).

        Note that the Lha gcig Kun dga' 'bum mentioned here is definitely the woman by that name who was wife of Chos skyong lde, a Gung thang king (son of Khri Rin chen bzang po) whose dates were 1280-1352. There is something about her in Deb ther khag lnga, pp. 113-4. She was sister of the Sa skya Bdag nyid chen po Bzang po dpal. All this is discussed in David Jackson's article.
      • David Jackson, On the Date of the Tibetan Translation of Aśvaghoṣa's Buddhacarita, Studia Indologiczne, vol. 4 (1997), pp. 41-62. Jackson concludes that, for the dating of the translation, it most likely took place in the 1260's or '70's.
      • Did this translation take place in last half of 13th century? Or in the 16th? Gangs can rim byon, p. 140, says this Blo gros rgyal po was Khams pa Blo gros rgyal po, giving him a birth date in mid-13th century. However, it could be by Zur mkhar ba Blo gros rgyal po (b. 1509), the famous physician (as argued in the Tibetan preface of the Sarnath translation of the Saundarananda). It surely took place in the late 13th century, since the co-translator was Mahīndrabhadra (the Newar teacher of Shong ston).
      • The proof that this is a 13th century translation is found in the 1360 Kālacakra history by a student of Dol po pa Shes rab rgyal mtshan (1292-1361), Bcom ldan 'das dpal dus kyi 'khor lo'i chos 'byung ngo mtshar rtogs brjod, at folio 47r.3. Here it says that Shong ston and Mdo smad pa Blo gros rgyal po traveled together to Nepal. Rabsal, p. 147, says that Mdo smad pa Blo gros rgyal po, who traveled with Shong ston to Nepal, had been a personal servant (gzim g.yog pa) of 'Phags pa. This substantiates the conclusions reached by David Jackson in his article.

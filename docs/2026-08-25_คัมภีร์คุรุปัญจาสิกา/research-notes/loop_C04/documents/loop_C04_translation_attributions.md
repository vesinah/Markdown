- Author: Ашвагхоша. **Перевод: А.И. Бреславец**. Год издания (reissue): **2015**, ISBN **5-87761-008-2**, 40 pp., softcover.
- Source: buddhist-translations.ru book page ("Сайт переводчиков буддийских текстов", Фонд «Наланда»), posted 5 Feb 2015: https://buddhist-translations.ru/books/848-book.html
- Exact page fields: "Автор(ы): Ашвагхоша / Перевод: А.И. Бреславец / Год издания: 2015 / ISBN: 5-87761-008-2". Related authors listed on same edition: Цонкапа, Пабонка Ринпоче, Геше Нгаванг Даргье.
- Original edition per library bibliographic record: _Ашвагхоша. Пятьдесят строф благочестивого почитания Учителя (Гурупаньчашика) / Пер. с англ. А. И. Бреславца. — СПб.: Ясный Свет, 1995. — 48 с. — ISBN 5-87761-008-2._
  - KEY POINT: "Пер. с англ." = translated FROM ENGLISH. The English base is almost certainly the Library of Tibetan Works & Archives edition by Geshe Ngawang Dhargyey et al. (see 1.4), matching the "related authors" tags.
- Breslavets identity (beyond initials А.И., and the 1995 SPb "Ясный Свет" imprint): full bio NOT FOUND this loop (ยังไม่พบ). Do not conflate with other Breslavets names without further sourcing.

### 1.2 narthang.ru e-book (Podlesnov) — VERIFIES the A15 claim
- Product page: «Гурупаньчашика (электронная книга)»: https://narthang.ru/product/gurupanchashika-elektronnaya-kniga
- Listing text (exact): «Ашвагхоша. Гурупаньчашика (Пятьдесят строф преданного почитания Учителя). [Опубликовал Vladimir Podlesnov – видимо, слегка подредактированный перевод Бреславца]»
- Conclusion: **Vladimir Podlesnov = publisher/e-book editor** who released a lightly-edited version of **Breslavets's translation** as a free e-book on narthang.ru; he is NOT an independent translator. A15's "Podlesnov publ. (Breslavets-based rendering)" CONFIRMED. Blog post: https://narthang.ru/blogs/news/pyatdesyat-strof-pochitaniya-uchitelya (22 Aug 2022).
- Companion volume found: _Цонкапа Лосанг Драгпа. Объяснение текста Ашвагхоши «Пятьдесят строф почитания Гуру», называемое «Исполнение всех надежд учеников». Редактор Никитин М. Изд-во «Возрождение буддизма в России», 2020, 64 с._ (narthang.ru product page) — Russian translation of Tsongkhapa's commentary, editor M. Nikitin.

### 1.3 abhidharma.ru web text
- Page: http://abhidharma.ru/A/Tantra/Content/Gurupancasika.htm — «Пятьдесят строф преданности Гуру. Ашвагоса. "Gurupancasika", "Bla-ma lnga-bcu-pa". С устными комментариями Чеше Нгаванг Даргьей.»

- Translator credit on the page: NONE given → ยังไม่พบ (likely another anonymous Russian rendering from Dhargyey's English).

## 2. SPANISH

- PDF: "Ashvaghosa: Cincuenta Versos de Devoción al Gurú." — **Trad. UPASAKA LOSANG GYATSO**
  URL: https://nagarjunavalencia.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/Ashvaghosa-Cincuenta-Versos-de-Devoci%C3%B3n-al-Gur%C3%BA.pdf
  Landing page: https://nagarjunavalencia.com/document/ashvaghosa-cincuenta-versos-de-devocion-al-guru/ (pdf, 233 KB; posted by marilo Jiménez). Mirrors: archive.org/details/AshvaghosaCincuentaVersosDeDevocinAlGurr.; academia.edu/12064617.
- Colophon (exact): "Trad. por ignorante y falto de devoción upasaka Losang Gyatso."
- Dedication (exact): "Dedico esta traducción de los \"Cincuenta versos de devoción al Gurú\" del gran bodhisattva conocido como Ashvaghosa o Aryasura, al Venerable Lama Jigme Thubten Tendar, el Tercer Ahbay Rinpoche. ¡Que puedan ser beneficiosos para todos los hispano hablantes!"
- Content: root verses 1–50 in Spanish prose (verse 50 disclaims personal interpolation), no Tsongkhapa commentary.
- Date: PDF indexed/upload date 2014-11-13 (search metadata); file path suggests re-upload 2018-02. Internal translation date not stated → gap (ยังไม่พบ exact translation year).
- Context: dedication to Lama Jigme Thubten Tendar, 3rd Ahbay Rinpoche (Ahbay Tulku lineage, Ganden; active in Italy/Spain) — consistent with a Gelug-affiliated lay disciple (upasaka) translating for Spanish speakers.

Conclusion: the main modern vernacular root-text translations circulating online are Sodargye's 7-character verse rendering (from Tibetan) inside his 略解/讲解 series; 汤芗铭's older verse translation remains the common alternative root-text base; Song-era 日称 is classical canon. Independent non-Sodargye 白话 prose translations of the root: ยังไม่พบ within budget.

## 4. VIETNAMESE (verification of A15)

### 4.1 Hồng Như 2021 — CONFIRMED with details
- PDF: "Năm Mươi Hạnh Kính Thầy [Fifty Verses on Guru-Devotion]", hongnhu-archives, Online Publication {25/10/2021}: https://www.hongnhu.org/files/pdf/0-ALL/bi-ashvagosha_50-hanh-kinh-thay%20%5B50-verses-on-guru-devotion%5D_NEW_viet_20211025.pdf
- Credits (exact): "Tác giả: Cao tăng xứ Ấn Mã Minh – Ashvagosha / Việt ngữ: Hồng Như Thupten Munsel"; Sanskrit title "Guru Pañcasika", Tibetan "bla ma lnga bchu pa".
- Verse 50 colophon: composed by Bhavideva (Bha-bi-lha), autre nom of Mã Minh. License note: free use if content preserved, non-commercial.
- Translator profile (hongnhu.org "Tiểu Sử Người Dịch"): lay dharma name Hồng Như / Thupten Munsel; translates directly from TIBETAN since ~2000; interpreter for Dalai Lama events in Australia/Europe 2007–2014; students of Geshe Thupten Dawoe & Lab Kyabgon Rinpoche, Sera Jey.
- Earlier prose versions on same site: "Năm Mươi Câu Tụng Tôn Kính Đạo Sư" PDFs dated 2016-11-28 and 2017-10-04. Catalogue (hongnhu-archive.org/thu-muc) marks the 2021 one as "Bản văn vần dịch từ Tạng ngữ" (rhymed verse translated from Tibetan).

### 4.2 Nguyễn Pram 2003 — CONFIRMED with details
- "NĂM MƯƠI (50) BÀI KỆ VỀ BẬC ĐẠO SƯ (GURUPANCHASHIKA)": ohay.pro.vn/nam-muoi-50-bai-ke-ve-bac-dao-su
- Credits (exact): "Nguyên tác: Tổ sư Ashvaghosha (Mã Minh) – Còn được biết dưới bút hiệu là: Aryashura / Dịch ra Anh Ngữ: Geshe Ngawang Dhargyey / Việt dịch: Nguyễn Pram. (Pháp danh: Thiện Thọ, Pháp hiệu: Pháp Định)"
- Academia.edu entry (GURUPANCHASHIKA - Aśvaghoṣa, 50 BÀI KỆ VỀ BẬC ĐẠO SƯ ... Nguyen Pram): "translation and full explanations of 50 gathas of Gurupanchsashika in Vietnamese on August 12, 2003" → confirms 2003 date; it is Dhargyey-English-based WITH explanations (not root-only).

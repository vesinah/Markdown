## 4. JAPANESE TRANSLATION OF THE TEXT (日本語訳) — STATUS

- No dedicated scholarly Japanese translation (和訳・現代語訳) of Gurupañcāśikā (either Tibetan or Chinese recension) was found in INBUDS, CiNii (via INBUDS cross-links), or web search.
- Conclusion: 日本語単独訳は「ยังไม่พบ」 (not yet found). Existing access is via the Chinese text (Taishō/Kanripo/CBETA-type resources) and secondary descriptions (Yoritomi 1973 gives content summary; Sakai 1972 unexamined).
- Adjacent Japanese translations of guru-devotion literature DO exist and should not be confused with a translation of this text:
  - 大正大学綜合佛教研究所 チベット仏教研究会, ツォンカパ『善説心随』和訳 (serialized; INBUDS-indexed) — Legs bshad snying po ("Essence of Fine Explanation"), Tsongkhapa's guru-devotion treatise.
  - ポタラ・カレッジ チベット仏教叢書 publishes Gelukpa グルプージャ（上師供養）liturgical materials (http://www.potala.jp/) — devotional, non-academic.

Enhancing Emotional Intelligence Through the
Stories of Panchatantra
Dr. Tulna Sharma
Principal, Dr. H.R. Gajwani College of Education
Abstract: The Panchatantra, an ancient collection of Indian fables, has been a cornerstone of moral and ethical education for
centuries. Each story is rich with lessons on human behavior, relationships, and emotional management. Emotional intelligence (EI),
which involves the ability to understand, manage, and use emotions effectively, is crucial in fostering personal and social development.
This paper explores how the stories of the Panchatantra can be used as tools to enhance emotional intelligence in individuals, especially
children, by imparting essential life skills such as empathy, self-awareness, emotional regulation, and social harmony.
Keywords: Panchatantra, Emotional Intelligence, Empathy, Self-awareness, Emotional Regulation, Storytelling, Education

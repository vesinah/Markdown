CHAPTER I
GENERAL INTRODUCTION

The present study is a report on the "Salvage Excava-
tion Project in Cebu City" undertaken by a team from the
Department of Anthropology of the University of San Carlos,

Cebu City, during the period February through June 1967.
I. ARCHAEOLOGY IN THE PHILIPPINES

Archaelogy* is still a young science in the Philip-
pines. Alfred Marche, a French traveller, was the first
one to excavate in the Philippines in 1881. His work,
moreover, was unmethodical by present-day standards, and the
results of his excavations of jar- and coffin-burials in
Marinduque were taken back to France. The next noteworthy
effort was undertaken by Dr. Carl Guthe who led an exped i-~
tion of the University of Michigan in the Philippines from
1922={25. As will be noted below in more detail, this ex-=
pedition was also lacking in systematic methodology.
Furthermore, only a small part of the great amount of mate-
rial now in the Museum of the University of Michigan has

been published to date. A good number of anthropologists

English Summary
This article deals with a Javanese bronze sculpture held in th e Museum for Asian Art at Berlin, with
inventory number II 196. The sculpture shows a female figure with ten arms, who has in severa l
publications been identified as the Buddhist deity Cundå. The back of the sculpture be ars an inscription.
This turns out to comprise two mantras that do not pertain to the cult of Cundå, but to th at of another
Buddhist deity. This fact gives reason for a reappraisal of the ic onography of the female figure, and
leads to the hypothesis that the deity depicted in bronze is the deity o f the mantras, namely
Mahåpratisarå.

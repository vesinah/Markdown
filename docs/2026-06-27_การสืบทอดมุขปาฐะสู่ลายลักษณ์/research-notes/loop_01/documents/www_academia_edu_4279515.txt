Household, Family and Urban Life (1918-1939)Shkodra. Household, Family and Urban Life (1918-1939)2013E. Papa-PandelejmoniAbstractThe purpose of this thesis is to give an overview of the household pattern and family life in Shkodra in the interwar period, based mostly on population census such as the data of 1918. The aim is to investigate the urban milieu and urban life of Shkodra’s inhabitants and see if it changed or remained stable during the 1920s and 1930s when changes happened in Albanian political, economic and social life. The main research topics would be demography as population data, household and family structure, marriage pattern as well cityscape and spatial layout and urban life. Several questions will be raised as, was there a change in the urban household and family life from the so called ‘traditional structures’ of family form usually seen in scholarly literature? Did the household structure of Shkodra differ from the country’s general household structure? Is there a difference in Shkodra’s general urban marriage pattern from the rest of the country? Was the pattern developed under local factors such as social, cultural, economic and educational developments, or was it something else that influenced this pattern? The thesis will also shed light on the urban transformation of Shkodra in the interwar period. Attention will be paid to showing the urban pattern and whether a continuity of the Ottoman urban pattern which characterised the old city could be discerned or to what extent new trends in urban planning, urban infrastructure and public life of this city were introduced. In this view other questions are raised, as if there was a kind of modernisation or ‘de-Ottomanisation’ in the city layout? Did modernisation touch the urban milieu, the family life and everyday life of the people? What about public life, was there a change? This study is organised into six chapters each corresponding to specific topics and conceptualised from a historical, statistical, demographical, urban and anthropological view. The first chapter will deal with the historical background of Shkodra until the 20th century. There the main urban historical moments will be reflected. Importance will be given to the 19th century and Shkodra’s urban development and economic institutions. In order to understand the demographic developments and household and marriage pattern of Shkodra, the second chapter will have a statistical character and there the main focus will be dedicated to the analysis of the Population Census of 1918 and to population census data of 1923 and 1930. The third chapter will focus mostly on household and family structures of Albania based on the Population Census of 1918. In this chapter the main theories on household and family will be discussed and the analysis will be focused on household structures, differences in the rural and urban households and family typologies, household size and their composition. Further in this chapter I will analyse the Shkodra household and family pattern based on census of 1918. In the fourth chapter the analysis will deepen on the city quarter level and will shed light on the household structures for each quarter based on the demographical analysis of the population census of 1918, in order to track the similarities and differences among Shkodra’s quarters. In the fifth chapter the marriage pattern in Shkodra will be analysed, and I will try to reveal some deviances from marriage and household structures in the rest of the country. The sixth chapter will analyse the urban transformation and urban life of the city in the interwar period. Some questions which will get answers are those as to how the urban pattern differed from the Ottoman ones or whether in the city there was continuity of the old urban pattern and of the urban life. Also if there was a change in urban life how was it reflected in the everyday life of people? Was there a public life for both men and women?

# บรรณานุกรมรวม (Consolidated Bibliography)

### 1. แหล่งข้อมูลปฐมภูมิ (Primary Canonical & Epigraphical Sources)

#### 1.1 จารึกและโบราณคดี (Epigraphical & Archaeological Sources)
- Cunningham, Alexander. *The Stûpa of Bharhut: A Buddhist Monument Ornamented with Numerous Representations Illustrative of Buddhist Legend and History in the Third Century B.C.* London: W.H. Allen and Co., 1879.
- Hultzsch, Eugen. *Inscriptions of Asoka* (Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum, Vol. I). Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1925.
- Lüders, Heinrich. *Bharhut Inscriptions* (Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum, Vol. II, Part II). Revised by Ernst Waldschmidt and M.A. Mehendale. Ootacamund: Government Epigraphist for India, 1963.

#### 1.2 คัมภีร์ภาษาบาลี (Pāli Canonical & Commentarial Texts)
- *Aṅguttaranikāya*. Edited by Richard Morris and Edmund Hardy. 5 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1885–1900.
- *Atthasālinī: Buddhaghosa's Commentary on the Dhammasaṅgaṇī*. Edited by Edward Müller. London: Pali Text Society, 1897.
- *Buddhavaṃsa and Cariyāpiṭaka*. Edited by Richard Morris. London: Pali Text Society, 1882.
- *Dhammapadaṭṭhakathā*. Edited by Helmer Smith and H.C. Norman. 5 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1906–1915.
- *Dīghanikāya*. Edited by T.W. Rhys Davids and J. Estlin Carpenter. 3 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1890–1911.
- *Jātaka together with Its Commentary*. Edited by V. Fausbøll. 6 vols. London: Trübner & Co., 1877–1896.
- *Majjhimanikāya*. Edited by V. Trenckner and Robert Chalmers. 3 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1888–1899.
- *Saṃyuttanikāya*. Edited by Léon Feer. 5 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1884–1898.
- *The Vinaya Piṭakaṃ: One of the Principal Buddhist Holy Scriptures in the Pâli Language*. Edited by Hermann Oldenberg. 5 vols. London: Williams and Norgate, 1879–1883.

#### 1.3 คัมภีร์ภาษาสันสกฤต (Sanskrit Texts)
- *Abhidharmakośabhāṣya of Vasubandhu*. Edited by P. Pradhan. Patna: K.P. Jayaswal Research Institute, 1967.
- *Bodhisattvāvadānakalpalatā of Kṣemendra*. Edited by Sarat Chandra Das and Pandit Harimohan Vidyabhushana. Calcutta: Asiatic Society of Bengal, 1888.
- *Divyāvadāna: A Collection of Early Buddhist Legends*. Edited by E.B. Cowell and R.A. Neil. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1886.
- *Gilgit Manuscripts*. Edited by Nalinaksha Dutt. 4 vols. Srinagar and Calcutta: Calcutta Oriental Press, 1939–1959.
- *Mahāvastu-Avadāna*. Edited by Émile Senart. 3 vols. Paris: Imprimerie Nationale, 1882–1897.

#### 1.4 คัมภีร์ภาษาจีน (Classical Chinese Tripiṭaka - CBETA Taishō Editions)
- *Da Tang Xiyu Ji* 大唐西域記 (Records of the Western Regions by Xuanzang). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 51, No. 2087.
- *Da zhidu lun* 大智度論 (Mahāprajñāpāramitāśāstra, attributed to Nāgārjuna, translated by Kumārajīva). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 25, No. 1509.
- *Foshuo Shengmu jing* 佛說生經 (translated by Dharmarakṣa). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 3, No. 152.
- *Gaoseng Faxian Zhuan* 高僧法顯傳 (Account of the Eminent Monk Faxian). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 51, No. 2085.
- *Kṣitigarbha-bodhisattva-pūrvapraṇidhāna-sūtra* 地藏菩薩本願經 (translated by Śikṣānanda). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 13, No. 412.
- *Mūlasarvāstivāda-vinaya-kṣudrakavastu* 根本說一切有部毘奈耶雜事 (translated by Yijing). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 24, No. 1451.
- *Xianyu jing* 賢愚經 (Damamūka-nidāna-sūtra). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 4, No. 202.
- *Za baozang jing* 雜寶藏經 (Storehouse of Sundry Valuables). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 4, No. 203.
- *Zengyi Ahan jing* 增一阿含經 (Ekottarika Āgama, translated by Gautama Saṅghadeva). Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō, Vol. 2, No. 125.

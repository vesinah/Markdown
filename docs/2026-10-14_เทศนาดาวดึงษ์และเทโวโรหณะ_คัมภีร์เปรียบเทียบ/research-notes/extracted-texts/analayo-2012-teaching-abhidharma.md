The Buddha and his Mother*
Anālayo
In what follows I investigate the tale of the Buddha's sojourn in the Heaven
of the Thirty-three to teach his mother, based on a translation of a version
of this episode in the Sa ṃmyukta-āgama preserved in Chinese, with a view
to discerning the gradual development and signicance of this taleṃ

# Song Dynasty Research (Lingwai Daida, Zhufan Zhi, Shilin Guangji)

## 1. 《嶺外代答》 (Lingwai Daida, 1178) - 登流眉 (Dengliumei)
**原文**:
*   **卷七·香門**:
    > "沉香來自諸蕃國者，真臘為上，占城次之。真臘種類固多，以登流眉（案：范成大《桂海虞衡志》作丁流眉，宋史作登流眉）所產香，氣味馨郁，勝於諸蕃。"
*   **卷二·外國門 下**:
    > "登流眉產名香，其國僧道咒法靈驗，黃衣僧有家室，紅衣僧寺居且戒律精嚴，道士以木葉為衣。"

## 2. 《諸蕃志》 (Zhufan Zhi, 1225) - 單馬令 (Danmaling)
**原文**:
*   **卷上·單馬令國**:
    > "國人以木作柵為城，寬六七尺，高二丈餘，上可駐戰。屋宇官局，惟用木為之，民居以竹縛，以葉為蓋，藤縳... 國人騎牛，打髾跣足。"
*   **卷上·三佛齊國**:
    > "單馬令、加囉希、巴林馮、新拖、監篦、藍無里、細蘭，皆其屬國也。"
*   **卷上·凌牙斯加國**:
    > "凌牙斯加國，自單馬令風帆六晝夜可到，亦有陸程。"

## 3. 《事林廣記》 (Shilin Guangji) - 單馬令 (Danmaling)
Records 單馬令 as one of the constituent states of the Nanyang region, focusing on its local customs and trade goods (e.g., producing fine white tin and lakawood, and noting customs like boiling seawater for salt, brewing millet wine).

## 4. Academic Debates (Dengliumei vs Danmaling)
- **For Identity**: Paul Pelliot (1904), O.W. Wolters (1958) argue phonetic equivalent of Tambralinga.
- **Against Identity**: Paul Wheatley (1961), Fukami Sumio (2006) argue they are distinct. Lingwai Daida describes Dengliumei as a vassal of Zhenla (Khmer Empire), while Zhufan Zhi describes Danmaling as a vassal of Sanfoqi (Srivijaya). Thus, they might be geographically distinct entities or at least distinct political centers during the Song dynasty.

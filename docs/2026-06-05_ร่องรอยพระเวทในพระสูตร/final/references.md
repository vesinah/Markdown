## แหล่งปฐมภูมิ (Primary Sources)

*Aṅguttara Nikāya*. Edited by Richard Morris and Hardy. 5 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1885–1900. Translated by Bhikkhu Bodhi as *The Numerical Discourses of the Buddha*. Boston: Wisdom Publications, 2012.

*Baudhāyana Dharmasūtra*. Translated by Patrick Olivelle. In *Dharmasūtras: The Law Codes of Āpastamba, Gautama, Baudhāyana, and Vasiṣṭha*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1999.

*Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad*. Translated by Patrick Olivelle. In *The Upaniṣads*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1996.

*Chāndogya Upaniṣad*. Translated by Patrick Olivelle. In *The Upaniṣads*. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1996.

*Dīgha Nikāya*. Edited by T. W. Rhys Davids and J. Estlin Carpenter. 3 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1890–1911. Translated by Maurice Walshe as *The Long Discourses of the Buddha*. Boston: Wisdom Publications, 1995.

*Majjhima Nikāya*. Edited by V. Trenckner and R. Chalmers. 3 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1888–1899. Translated by Bhikkhu Bodhi as *The Middle Length Discourses of the Buddha*. Boston: Wisdom Publications, 1995.

*Pratiṣṭhālakṣaṇasamuccaya*. Edited by Vibhuti Bhushan Bhattacharya. Calcutta: Asiatic Society, 1985.

*Ṛgveda Saṃhitā*. In *The Rig-Veda Sanhita: The Sacred Hymns of the Brahmans*. Edited by F. Max Müller. 4 vols. London: Oxford University Press, 1890. Translated by Ralph T. H. Griffith as *The Hymns of the Rigveda*. Benares: E. J. Lazarus and Co., 1896.

*Saṃyutta Nikāya*. Edited by L. Feer. 5 vols. London: Pali Text Society, 1884–1898. Translated by Bhikkhu Bodhi as *The Connected Discourses of the Buddha*. Boston: Wisdom Publications, 2000.

*Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa*. Translated by Julius Eggeling. In *The Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa according to the Text of the Mādhyandina School*. Sacred Books of the East, vols. 12, 26, 41, 43, 44. 5 vols. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1882–1900.

*Sutta Nipāta*. Edited by Dines Andersen and Helmer Smith. London: Pali Text Society, 1913. Translated by K. R. Norman as *The Group of Discourses*. Oxford: Pali Text Society, 2001.

*Tāṇḍya Mahābrāhmaṇa*. Edited by A. Chinnaswami Sastri. Benares: Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series Office, 1935.

*The Schøyen Manuscripts*. Edited by Jens Braarvig. *Buddhist Manuscripts in the Schøyen Collection*. Oslo: Hermes Academic Publishing, 2000.

*The Senior Kharoṣṭhī Manuscripts*. Analyzed by Richard Salomon. *Ancient Buddhist Scrolls from Gandhāra*. Seattle: University of Washington Press, 1999.

*Udāna*. Edited by Paul Steinthal. London: Pali Text Society, 1885. Translated by John D. Ireland as *The Udāna and the Itivuttaka*. Kandy: Buddhist Publication Society, 1997.

*Udānavarga*. Edited by Franz Bernhard. Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1965.

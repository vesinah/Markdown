# **Ambangan**


The “Ambangan” exhibit at the Ethnology Gallery of National Museum Eastern
Northern Mindanao features the different indigenous communities such as
Butuanons, Manobos, Higaonons, and Mamanwa, through the contemporary
implements they use for survival like gold panning, fishing, farming, weaving,
crafting, food gathering, etc. and the food products yielded such as local starch
and wine. Also included are their musical instruments.


Ambangan is a small place, a sitio, a part of Barangay Libertad, considered a
Pre-Hispanic multi-site being the trading center, settlement site, wharf or
pier/port, workshop/place of fabrication of gold, metal and  glass, and burial
sites within 9th to 16th century.

## **National Museum of the Philippines**

A trust of the Government, is an educational, scientific and cultural institution that acquires,
documents, preserves, exhibits, and fosters scholarly study and public appreciation of works of
art, specimens, and cultural and historical artifacts representative of the unique cultural
heritage of the Filipino people and the natural history of the Philippines.

## NEW SOURCES IDENTIFIED

### 1. Zhang Yongquan (2024) - PRIMARY SOURCE ⭐⭐⭐
**Title**: "Alignment of double-line annotation" (双行注文齐整化)
**Publication**: *Manuscript Philology of Dunhuang* 敦煌写本文献学, Chapter 15, pp. 415-448
**Publisher**: Springer Singapore
**DOI**: 10.1007/978-981-99-8331-5_15
**Date**: Published October 6, 2024

**Key Content**:
- **Direct discussion of jiazzhu format**: Main body in large characters, annotation in "double-line small characters" (双行小字)
- **Layout calculations**: Scribes had to calculate character distribution between two annotation lines to maintain neat appearance
- **Specific technique**: First line and second line of annotation must have roughly equal numbers of characters
- **Transcription process**: Scribes must count characters before writing to avoid: (1) too many blanks in second line, or (2) insufficient space/compacted text in second line
- **Prior version**: Earlier published as article in *Dunhuang Turpan Research* 敦煌吐鲁番研究 vol. 12 (2011), pp. 279-302

**Significance**: 
- Most systematic treatment of jiazzhu layout mechanics in Dunhuang manuscripts
- Provides technical details about scribal practice
- Includes multiple manuscript examples with analysis
- Confirms jiazzhu was a deliberate, calculated practice requiring skill

**Coverage**: Direct evidence from Dunhuang manuscripts, addresses the "how" of jiazzhu layout

## TECHNICAL TERMINOLOGY CONFIRMED

Chinese terms for jiazzhu/interlinear annotation:
1. **夹注** jiāzhù = "inserted annotation" (primary term)
2. **双行小字** shuāngháng xiǎozì = "double-line small characters" (descriptive term)
3. **小字双行** xiǎozì shuāngháng = "small characters double-line" (alternative order, same meaning)
4. **细注** xìzhù = "fine annotation" (variant term)

Format characteristics:
- Two lines of smaller characters inserted between lines of main text
- Equal character distribution between two annotation lines (aesthetic principle)
- Requires pre-calculation by scribe before writing
- Used in Buddhist sutras, commentaries, and lexicographical works

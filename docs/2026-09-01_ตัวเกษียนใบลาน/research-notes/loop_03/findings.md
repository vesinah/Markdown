## S-2022-Nishanthi-01

- **Bibliography:** Nishanthi, M. and Wijayasundara, N. D. "Preservation and Conservation of Palm Leaf Manuscripts at the Library of University of Sri Jayewardenepura". *Vidyodaya Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences (VJHSS)* 7.2 (2022), 81–96. DOI: 10.31357/fhss/vjhss.v07i02.06 (ยืนยันผ่าน Crossref)
- **Language:** English (วัตถุศึกษา: หนังสือใบลานสิงหล/บาลี/สันสกฤต ศรีลังกา)
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.31357/fhss/vjhss.v07i02.06
- **Local Path:** `research-notes/loop_03/documents/2022-preservation-palm-leaf-manuscripts-sri-lanka.pdf`
- **Quote 1** (ภาษาของคลังใบลาน, p. 92): "Our collection of palm leaf manuscripts consists of various subjects such as Buddhism, Indigenous Medicine, Veterinary Science, Rituals (Shanthikarma), Astrology, History and Language. These are written in Sinhala, Pali and Sanskrit. In addition, some palm leaf manuscripts were written both in Sinhala and Sanskrit. These palm leaf manuscripts date back to the 18th and 19th centuries A.D."
- **Quote 2** (sannaya ในคลัง, p. 92): "Rare palm leaf manuscripts are available in our collection, such as Besajjamanjusawa, Besajjamanjusa Sannaya, and Saraswathi Niganduwa (drugs recipes) of indigenous medicine." + "Besajjamanjusa Sannaya contains the therapeutic prescription for monks in Sanskrit (19th century A.D.)."
- **Quote 3** (ช่วงเวลาของหนังสือใบลานส่วนใหญ่, p. 81): "it can be determined that most of the palm leaf manuscripts (95%) found in various institutions were written in the 18th and 19th centuries. There are also books written in the early 20th century. It is rare before the 18th century... Chulla Wagga, one of the oldest palm leaf manuscripts in Sri Lanka, was written in the 13th century A.D."
- **Quote 4** (ชนิดใบไม้, p. 82): "− Talipot palm – Corypha umbraculifera − Palmyra palm – Borassus Flabellifera − Lonta palm – Coryphautan"
- **Quote 5** (ความเสี่ยงสูญหาย + การ digitize, pp. 92, 81): "If we do not take immediate action to restore the damaged palm leaf manuscripts, the contents may not be readable. As a nation, we may face a situation to suffer due to the loss of our indigenous knowledge" + "Finally, we digitized the complete set of manuscripts where researchers are allowed to use them according to the established policy."
- **ข้อสังเกต:** เชิง codicology ล้วน — ไม่ได้พูดเลย์เอาต์ bitext โดยตรง แต่ยืนยันสถานะของ sannaya ในคลังหนังสือใบลานจริง + ชนิดใบ/อายุ/การอนุรักษ์ ที่จำเป็นสำหรับหัวข้อ "ใบลาน" ของโปรเจกต์

## S-2022-Deegalle-01

- **Bibliography:** Deegalle, Mahinda. "The First Sinhala Tripiṭakaya Translation: De Zoysa's 'Protestant' Buddhist Project for Mass Literacy in Twentieth Century Sri Lanka". *Vidyodaya Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences (VJHSS)* 7.2 (2022), 28–45. DOI: 10.31357/fhss/vjhss.v07i02.03 (ยืนยันผ่าน Crossref; หมายเหตุ: ชื่อไฟล์ระบุ "2023" แต่ตัวบทและ Crossref ยืนยันปี 2022)
- **Language:** English (วัตถุศึกษา: Pali + Sinhala)
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.31357/fhss/vjhss.v07i02.03
- **Local Path:** `research-notes/loop_03/documents/2023-first-sinhala-tripitakaya-translation-dezoysa.pdf`
- **Quote 1** (sannaya ในคลังใบลานใหญ่, p. 29): "The present colossal palm-leaf manuscript collections such as (i) the National Museum Library, Colombo, (ii) the University of Peradeniya Library and (iii) the Hañguranketa Potgul Vihāra Library... indicate that the Tripiṭaka texts included Sinhala sannayas among them."
- **Quote 2** (A.P. de Zoysa + Aṭuvā, pp. 35–36): "Devoting nearly twenty years (1950–1968), soliciting help from leading Buddhist monks and laypeople, de Zoysa accomplished a mammoth task: publication of a Sinhala translation of the Tripiṭaka containing Theravāda Buddhist canonical and post-canonical commentarial texts of forty-eight volumes for the first time." + "1950– Initiated translating and publishing the Tripiṭaka and Aṭuvās in Sinhala" (Table: "41–43 Dīgha Nikāya Aṭuvāva: Sumaṅgala Vilāsiniya (3 vols.) (1961)")
- **Quote 3** (คลังใบลานสมัย Turnour, p. 29): "In preparing The Mahāwanso in Roman Characters, with the translation subjoined (1837) for publication, Turnour looked for palm-leaf manuscripts of the Tripiṭaka... Turnour noted: 'Some of the above books [of the Tripiṭaka] are not to be obtained in Kandy, and others only in an incomplete form'."
- **Quote 4** (ช่องว่างการเข้าถึง — เหตุผลของการแปล, p. 29): "Until the printing of a complete set of the Tripiṭaka in English and Sinhala in the twentieth century, it is hard to substantiate that Theravāda Buddhists in mass-scale ever read the Tripiṭaka in Pāḷi in its entirety. Since a complete Sinhala translation of the Pāḷi Tripiṭaka was neither available earlier nor printed, it was not readily accessible or affordable for most."
- **ข้อสังเกต:** แสดงเส้นทางสิงหล: sannaya (ใบลาน, คำต่อคำ) → ยุคพิมพ์: Aṭuvā (คำแปล/อรรถกถาฉบับพิมพ์ของ de Zoysa) — การเปลี่ยนสื่อจากใบลานเป็นหนังสือพิมพ์ไม่ได้เปลี่ยนแนวคิด "คำแปลติดตามพระไตรปิฎก"

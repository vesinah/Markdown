## Codicological Classification

### NOT Interlinear Gloss System

**Interlinear gloss characteristics (absent in sannaya):**
1. ❌ Two text sizes (main text larger, gloss smaller)
2. ❌ Vertical layering (gloss above/below main line)
3. ❌ Main text readable independently as continuous passage
4. ❌ Gloss text as secondary annotation layer

**What interlinear gloss looks like (for comparison):**
- Example: Latin manuscripts with Old English glosses
  - Large Latin main text: "Pater noster qui es in caelis"
  - Small gloss above: "fæder ūre þū þe eart on heofonum"
  - Main text stands alone; gloss aids comprehension

### IS Continuous Bitext

**Bitext characteristics (present in sannaya):**
1. ✅ Two languages **inline** in same text flow
2. ✅ Single script size (no hierarchy)
3. ✅ Alternating pattern within lines
4. ✅ Neither language independently readable as continuous passage
5. ✅ Translation follows source phrase-by-phrase

**Analogy:** Similar to modern interlinear Bible editions (e.g., Greek-English interlinear), but in **single-line alternating format** rather than stacked lines.

**Technical term:** **Running bitext** or **inline alternating translation**

---

## Implications for "Interlinear" Terminology

### Why "Interlinear" May Appear in Literature

**Hypothesis 1: Translation method, not layout**
- "Interlinear" may refer to translation **technique** (phrase-by-phrase, word-for-word)
- NOT referring to physical layout (text between lines)
- Scholars may use "interlinear" to mean "closely literal translation"

**Hypothesis 2: Misapplication of Latin manuscript terminology**
- European scholars familiar with Latin-vernacular glosses
- Applied "interlinear" term to sannaya by analogy (translation aid function)
- Did not precisely describe physical codicology

**Hypothesis 3: Modern printed editions**
- Some printed sannaya editions (20th century) may use stacked format
- Print format ≠ original manuscript layout
- Modern editors may have reformatted continuous bitext into vertical interlinear for readability

### Proper Terminology

**Original manuscript layout:**
- ✅ **Continuous bitext**
- ✅ **Inline alternating translation**
- ✅ **Running bilingual text**
- ❌ NOT "interlinear gloss"

**Function:**
- ✅ Translation (Pali → Sinhala)
- ✅ Study aid (for understanding Pali texts)
- ✅ Pedagogical tool

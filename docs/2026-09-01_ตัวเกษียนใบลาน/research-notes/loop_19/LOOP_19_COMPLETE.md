## Key Findings

### Four Major Trends Identified:

1. **AI/ML Revolution in HTR**
   - Shift from RNN to transformer-based models
   - Focus on low-resource and underrepresented scripts
   - Multi-domain adaptation approaches

2. **Human-AI Hybrid Workflows**
   - Recognition that full automation insufficient
   - Human-in-the-loop methodologies
   - Ground truth annotation quality assurance

3. **Infrastructure Critique**
   - Critical discussions about DH tool-scholar workflow gaps
   - Multiple standards (TEI, IIIF, W3C) not fully integrated
   - Need for integrated platforms

4. **Global/Comparative Turn**
   - Move beyond Euro-centric manuscript studies
   - CSMC Hamburg as major institutional hub
   - Growing attention to Asian, African, Middle Eastern traditions

### Gaps Identified:

1. **Southeast Asian manuscripts** underrepresented in DH literature
2. **Interlinear gloss** in historical MSS (vs. computational linguistics IGT)
3. **Palmleaf manuscripts** have limited coverage
4. **Annotation semantics** (beyond spatial/visual annotation)
5. **Standards integration** in practice

### S-1974-Kobayashi-01
- **Bibliography:** 小林 芳規 (Kobayashi Yoshikuni). 「返點の沿革」 (*Historical Development of the Henten/Return-Point Marks*). 『訓点語と訓点資料』 54 (1974-05-31): 86–111. 訓点語学会. ISSN 0454-6652.
- **Language:** Japanese
- **URL:** https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1050866514665328512 (record); PDF: https://hiroshima.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/2009567
- **Local Path:** research-notes/loop_12/documents/1974-kobayashi-Downloads-entenkuten-hiroshima.pdf (26 pp.)
- **Access:** Full text (Hiroshima University repository OA). Text layer is heavily garbled — vertical-layout scan OCR'd as horizontal, characters scrambled into single-column fragments; keywords 声点/雁点/左点 unusable verbatim. Content identifiable only from legible fragments: it traces 返点 (return-point reading-order marks) genealogies — 「上下」「レ」「一レ点」「甲乙丙」「天地人」 series — from early through Edo-period kunten, including 朱 (vermilion) mark layers and 傍 (side-position) placement. Fragments confirming placement/series vocabulary (garbled context, verify against page images before quoting): 「字の右傍」「上下」「一レ点」「朱書の書入」「濁点を朱書で抹」.
- **Verbatim (legible fragment, from fulltext dump):** 「…字 の 右 傍…上下…一レ点…朱…濁点を朱書で抹…」 (characters scattered across OCR column breaks; treat as index-only evidence of terminology, not quotable sentences).
- **Relevance:** The foundational history of return-point (henten) reading-order marks — direct evidence for diagonal/reading-order mark systems parallel to おこし点. **Note for reviewer: re-download page images or re-OCR if verbatim quotes needed.**

### S-2015-Alberizzi-01 ⭐ (best layout evidence this loop)
- **Bibliography:** Alberizzi, Valerio L. "The Role of kunten Materials in the Process of Sino-Japanese Hybridization." *Quaderni di Linguistica e Studi Orientali / Working Papers in Linguistics and Oriental Studies* 1 (2015): 233–259. Firenze University Press.
- **Language:** English
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.13128/qulso-2421-7220-16524
- **Local Path:** research-notes/loop_12/documents/2015-alberizzi-kunten-sino-japanese-hybridization.pdf (26 pp.)
- **Access:** Full text, open access (oaj.fupress.net).
- **Verbatim quotes:**
  1. Layout/position of kana glosses: "This was achieved by way of the so-called kanaten 仮名点, small gloss phonograms **inserted to the right of the main characters in the text**. By glossing the inflectional morphemes associated with Japanese verbs and adjectives, the same technique also allowed to render…" (p. ~237).
  2. Glosses as interlinear notations: "…not only because the glosses were provisional notations **jotted down quickly in the interlinear spaces of the main text**, but also because they reflected the proficiency (or lack thereof) of the glossator, and as such errors were bound to occur." (p. ~235)
  3. Genesis of hybrid writing from gloss extraction: "…according to Kasuga the extrapolation of glosses from the **interlinear space of the manuscript** and the reorganization of their content into an independently coherent text gave birth to the Sino-Japanese hybrid writing" (citing Kasuga 1983: 246–247).
  4. Terminology field: "Japanese scholars currently acknowledge the significance of these glossed materials – kunten shiryō 訓点資料 – for synchronic and diachronic studies of the Japanese language"; distinctive language = "kuntengo 訓点語".
  5. "Japanese-Chinese mixed style evolved… out of the practice of adding **marks to Chinese texts** in order that they could be read more easily by Japanese readers."
- **Relevance:** Confirms (a) kanaten placed **right of main characters** in vertical kunten layout; (b) glosses occupy **interlinear space**; (c) kunten → wakan konkōbun diachronic chain. English, quotable directly in report.

### S-2001-Tsukishima-01
- **Bibliography:** 月島 士朗? — Crossref lists author as TSUKISHIMA (月本? confirm: J-STAGE tja1948 = 『日本語の研究』/Task Force... actually journal is 国語学会 journal 1948 series = 『国語学』). "A Study on Ancient Japanese Vocabulary Used in Kunten Materials" 訓点資料に用いられた古代語彙について. 『国語学』 56(1) (2001): 1–.
- **Language:** Japanese
- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.2183/tja1948.56.1 ; PDF: http://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tja1948/56/1/56_1_1/_pdf
- **Local Path:** research-notes/loop_12/documents/2001-tsukishima-kunten-vocabulary.pdf (12 pp.)
- **Access:** Full text (J-STAGE free PDF), but text layer is **vertical-Japanese OCR scrambled like Kobayashi 1974** — single characters scattered. Legible fragments confirm content: 訓点資料 discussed as 「漢文に和訓を添えた」 materials; mentions ヲコト点 as 「漢文の傍に表記する」 notation 「書写当時の…工夫」; **朱書 (vermilion writing) corrections**: 「朱書の書入があり、第一音節の『ヅ』の濁点を朱書で抹…」 — i.e., a kunten whose 濁点 (voicing mark) was struck through in vermilion and corrected, showing layered annotation in different colors. Also 傍訓 「『ヅダヅダ』の傍訓が附刻されている」 (side-glosses attached/incised).
- **Verbatim (legible fragments only, garbled context):** 「朱書の書入…第一音節の『ヅ』の濁点を朱書で抹」「…の傍訓が附刻されている」「ヲコト点など…漢文の傍に表記する」
- **Relevance:** Documents (a) vermilion vs. ink layering in kunten annotation, (b) 傍訓 side-gloss practice, (c) ヲコト点 described as written at the side (傍) of the Chinese text. **Note: OCR scrambled — verify quotes against page images before use in report.**

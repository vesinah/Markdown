--- PDF CONTENT NOTES (local extraction, garbled custom-font encoding; partial decode) ---
- p.443: "The Gurupancasika is a short work of fifty metrical stanzas on the Tantric guru... It is
  available only partially in Sanskrit, and completely in Tibetan and Chinese translations."
- The work "claims to be an anthology of tantras"; focuses on qualifications of masters to be
  followed/avoided and suitable behaviour of the disciple; "a social code for the subculture of
  ... gurus and their disciples".
- Previously known ms: incomplete, breaks off around verse 33, catalogued National Archives
  Kathmandu NA 045/3, SA 105 'ka'; codex edited in 1986 (editor name garbled - VERIFY).
- NEW manuscript discovered by Szanto: NA 040/10, SA 105 'kha' (duplicate microfilm A 39/144),
  preserving more text than hitherto known.
- Attribution point (via Rigpa Wiki citing this article): new manuscript colophon names the author
  Vapilladatta (= Tsongkhapa's "Bhabilha"), not Asvaghosa.

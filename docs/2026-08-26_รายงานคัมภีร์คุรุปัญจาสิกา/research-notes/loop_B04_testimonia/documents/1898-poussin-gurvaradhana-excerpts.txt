ADIKARMAPRADIPA citing GP under name "Gurvaradhana" — evidence from La Vallée Poussin 1898
Source: L. de La Vallée Poussin, Bouddhisme: études et matériaux. Adikarmapradipa,
Bodhicaryavataratika. London 1898 (archive.org identifier: bouddhismetudes00goog).
Full djvu text saved as: 1898-poussin-bouddhisme-etudes-materiaux-fulltext.txt
Poussin's own index: "Gurvaradhana, 189, 15; 195, 3" (two citation sites).

=== SITE 1: p.189 — the work is NAMED in a list of manuals taught to a novice ===

Sanskrit of Adikarmapradipa (Poussin's edition, OCR-normalized):
"evam dvir api trir api | aupayikaih sadhu iti | tadanu sisyopakaranaparikarana-
samskarah | sattvaradhana-Gurvaradhana-adibhih samskrtya sa-sekair anugraham kuryat |
evam labdhopasakanamadheyena ..."

French translation (verbatim):
"Le maitre continue l'education (samskara) du disciple en lui enseignant diverses
disciplines resumees dans des manuels connus : le Dasakugalapariyaga [Dasakusala-],
la Saigatikarika [Sadgati-karika], le SattvTiradhana [Sattvaradhana], le Gurvaradhana.
Abhiseka. - Enfin, au moyen des aspersions rituelles, il acheve l'initiation du novice."

=> Here AP only NAMES the Gurvaradhhana; no verses quoted at this site.

=== SITE 2: p.195 — AP QUOTES VERSES "from the Gurvaradhana" ===

Sanskrit (Poussin's edition, OCR-corrected reading):
"...anyad api triskandhakam paramaya bhaktya ityadinabhipretam Gurv-aradhane coktam |
'nityam ca gurave deyam nityam pujya(?) tathagatah' | asyayam arthah |
vidyamane vastuni srimad-gurubhattarakapadanam sva-manobhiprasitam vastu
upasthapayet ... 'yad yad isyataram kim cit visistataram eva va | tat tad dhi gurave
deyam' ... ity adina prahandhena uktam | avidyamane [tu] punar ... sa ca prakalpayet |"

French paraphrase by Poussin (verbatim):
"a propos de la propitiation (aradhana) du guru nous lisons que 'toujours il faut
donner au guru, toujours il faut venerer les Tathagatas': quand on la possede, il
faut dans une pensee joyeuse offrir aux pieds du venerable guru bhattaraka la chose
qu'il desire. 'Ce qui existe de plus desirable, de plus distingue, on doit le donner
au guru.' Faute d'offrande reelle, le disciple fait hommage au maitre d'une offrande
imaginee."

=== IDENTIFICATION WITH GURUPANCAASIKA ROOT VERSES ===

Quote "nityam ca gurave deyam / nityam pujyas tathagatah"
  = TD3721 v17 region: "RTAG TU RANG GI DAM TSIG BSRUNG, ,RTAG TU DE BZHIN GSHEGS
    PA MCHOD, ,RTAG TU BLA MA LA YANG 'BUL"

Quote "yad yad isyataram kim cid visistataram eva va / tat tad hi gurave deyam"
  = TD3721 v18: "MI ZAD DE NI 'DOD PA YIS, ,CUNG ZAD YID 'ONG GANG DANG GANG,
    ,SHIN TU KHYAD PAR GYUR PA NYID, ,DE DANG DE NI BLA MAR SBYIN"

=> AP quotes GP's giving-to-the-guru verses (our vv.17-18 zone). Poussin's printed
   excerpt shows approx. 2 full verses; Yoritomi (1973) states "about five verses"
   are quoted under the name Gurvaradhana — the discrepancy (whether further verses
   appear in the Tibetan AP beyond Poussin's excerpt) remains open.

NOTE on author/number: Yoritomi calls the AP author "Anupamavajra"; most catalogs say
Anupamaraksita. The task-prompt guess "Toh 3964?" is WRONG: our TENGYUR.zip shows
TD03964 = "RGYAL BA'I LAM LA 'JUG PA (Jina Margavatara)" (title line verified:
"RGYA GAR SKAD DU, DE NA M'A RGA AA BA nA RA"). The Adikarmapradipa's Derge file was
not located by title search ("dang po'i las kyi sgron me" pattern absent from all zip
entry names); its Tohoku number needs follow-up.

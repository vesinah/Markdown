=== WITNESS A: Vimalaprabha of Pundarika (TD01347) QUOTES GP vv.1-5 (variant recension) ===

File: Tengyur/.../VOL 14 (PHA)/TD01347-1/-2_BSDUS PA'I RGYUD KYI RGYAL PO DUS KYI
'KHOR LO'I 'GREL BSHAD (Vimala Prabha Nama Mula Tantranusrini ... Laghu Kalacakra
Tantra Raja Tika).txt  (total ~1.8 MB)

Verbatim passage (whitespace-normalized Wylie/ACIP):

"...DE LA YANG DE BZHIN GSHEGS, PHYOGS BCU'I 'JIG RTEN KHAMS BZHUGS PA RNAMS ...
SLOB MA DE RNAMS KYANG THUN MTSAMS GSUM DU 'ONGS NAS BLA MA LA PHYAG 'TSAL BAR BYED
DE ... ,'DIR DUR SMRIG 'DZIN PA'I DGE SLONG RNAMS KYIS DUS GSUM DU RDO RJE SLOB DPON
LA PHYAG 'TSAL BAR BYA STE, KHYIM PA DANG GSAR BU RNAMS LA NI DAM PA'I CHOS 'CHAD PA
MA GTOGS PAR MA YIN NO, ,'DI LTA STE, 'JIG RTEN SMOD PA SPANG BA'I PHYIR, ,DAM CHOS
LA SOGS MDUN BDAR TE, , KHYIM PA'AM NI GSAR BU LA, ,BRTUL ZHUGS 'DZIN PAS BLOS PHYAG
BYA, ,DE BZHIN DU, MCHOG MIN LAS DANG PHYAG SPANGS NAS, ,STAN BSTAB BA DANG LDANG BA
DANG, ,DON BYA LA SOGS BSNYEN BKUR 'DIR, ,THAMS CAD BRTUL ZHUGS CAN GYIS BYA ZHES PA
'DIR GAL TE KHYIM PA'AM GSAR BU DAG KYANG DGE SLONG GI RDO RJE SLOB DPON DU MTSUNGS
PAR GYUR NA DE'I TSE CI LTAR ZHE NA..."

Quoted material vs root TD3721:
  "'JIG RTEN SMOD PA SPANG BA'I PHYIR / DAM CHOS LA SOGS MDUN BDAR TE /
   KHYIM PA'AM NI GSAR BU LA / BRTUL ZHUGS 'DZIN PAS BLOS PHYAG BYA"
     = GP vv.3-4a, but with PADAS REORDERED (root: khyim pa'am ni gsar bu la comes first).
  "MCHOG MIN LAS DANG PHYAG SPANGS NAS / STAN BSTAB BA DANG LDANG BA DANG /
   DON BYA LA SOGS BSNYEN BKUR 'DIR / THAMS CAD BRTUL ZHUGS CAN GYIS BYA"
     = GP v5 variant (root: GDAN BSTABS PA DANG LDANG BA DANG / DON GYI BYA BA SOGS
       BSNYEN BKUR / BRTUL ZHUGS CAN GYIS KUN BYA STE / PHYAG BYA DAM PA MIN LAS SPANG);
       lexical variants: stan bstab~gdan bstabs, mchog min~dam pa min.
  Plus glossing of GP vv.1-2 phrase "de bzhin gshegs / phyogs bcu'i 'jig rten khams
  bzhugspas" (neyartha/nges-don discussion) and "dus gsum du" rendered as
  "thun mtsams gsum du 'ong nas bla ma la phyag 'tsal bar byed".

CONCLUSION: Szanto 2013 fn981's claim that the Vimalaprabha of Pundarika transmits
guru-devotion material matching GP is CONFIRMED by direct quotation of a variant
recension of GP vv.3-5 inside the VP's Kalacakra exegesis. No explicit title-name
("bla ma lnga bcu pa") is attached to the quotation in this passage.

Search note: distinctive GP phrases found in VP part 2 only:
  "'JIG RTEN SMOD PA SPANG" x2, "KHYIM PA'AM NI GSAR BU" x1;
  part 1: none. "BLA MA LNGA BCU": 0 hits in both parts.

=== WITNESS B: Sraddhakaravarman, Yoganuttaratantrarthavatarasamgraha (TD03713)
NAMES the GP ===

File: Tengyur/02 RGYUD.../VOL 77 (TZU)/TD03713_RNAL 'BYOR BLA NA MED PA'I RGYUD KYI
DON LA 'JUG PA BSDUS PA ZHES BYA BA (Yoganuttara Tantrarthavatara Samgraha (Nama)).txt

Verbatim passage:

"...BSAM YAS 'BRAS BU SMIN BYED PA, ,SANGS RGYAS 'BRAS BU 'DOD PA YIS, ,SEMS CAN DAD
PAS DE MCHOD CING, ,ZHES GSUNGS PA DANG, GZHAN YANG RGYUD THAMS CAD KYI DGONGS PA
BSDUS PA BLA MA LNGA BCU PA LA SOGS PAR GSUNGS PA YIN NO..."

= "...it is also taught in the Bla ma lnga bcu pa ('Fifty Verses on the Guru'), which
condenses the intent of ALL tantras..." — exactly the testimonium reported by
Yoritomi 1973 (Beijing ed. vol. 81, 158-5-2-3). CONFIRMED in Derge eText.

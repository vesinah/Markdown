=== 1. TITLE LINES (opening) ===

@12A #, ,RGYA GAR SKAD DU, GU RV'A R'A DHA NA
PANYTZI KA ,BOD SKAD DU, BLA MA'I BSNYEN BKUR GYI DKA' 'GREL,
'JAM PA'I RDO RJE LA PHYAG 'TSAL LO,

(Sanskrit: *Gurv-aradhana-pancika*; homage to Manjuvajra)

=== 2. TRANSLATOR COLOPHON (end of text, verbatim Wylie/ACIP) ===

@36A *, ,BAR SHOG ,BLA MA LNGA BCU PA'I DKA' 'GREL 'DI NI RDZOGS
SO,, ,,RGYA GAR SHAR PHYOGS KYI MKHAS PA CHEN PO DPAL NAGS KYI
RIN CHEN GYI BKA' DRIN LAS LEGS PAR SBYAR BA'I SKAD KYI CHA SHAS
RIG PA GZHON NU DPAL GYIS DPAL LDAN BLA MA DAM PA'I DBEN
GNAS LHA KHANG STENG DU BSGYUR BA'O,,

English: "Here ends the dka' 'grel of the bla ma lnga bcu pa. Through the kindness
of the great pandita dpal nags kyi rin chen (Sri Vanaratna) of Eastern India,
the language-portions properly composed/arranged were translated by rig pa
gzhon nu dpal at the upper temple (lha khang steng) of the hermitage of the
glorious supreme guru."

NOTE: The colophon gives NO author name for the commentary itself ("otherwise
anonymous" per Szanto 2013 fn982). It names only the pandita-instructor
(Vanaratna) and the translator (gzhon nu dpal). No clan/place epithet is given,
so identification with 'Gos gzhon nu dpal rests on external evidence.

NOTE 2: Szanto's quoted closing sentence "gzhung 'di la rgya gar pa'i 'grel pa
mi snang yang ..." does NOT occur anywhere in this Derge eText (checked:
"MI SNANG", "RGYA GAR PA", "GZHUNG 'DI" all absent). Per Szanto's own sentence
structure it comes from Tsong kha pa's Bla ma lnga bcu pa'i rnam bshad
(slob ma'i re ba kun skong), not from Toh 3722.

=== 3. STRUCTURE / VERSE-CITATION METHOD ===

Extent: folio markers @12A through @36A = ca. 24.5 folios (Derge vol. TSU).
The panjika proceeds sequentially through the namaskara + all ~50 root verses.
Citation style: it quotes ONLY THE OPENING WORDS of each root verse followed by
the formula "zhes bya ba la sogs pa gsungs te" ("...and so on"), then comments.

Formula counts in full text (whitespace-normalized):
  "ZHES BYA BA LA SOGS PA" : 44
  "CES BYA BA LA SOGS PA"  : 7   (= 51 verse-introductions, covering namo + 50 verses)
  "LA SOGS PA'O"           : 12
  "ZHE NA"                 : 40
  "GSUNGS TE"              : 75
  "ZHES BYA BA'I DON TO"   : 35

Verbatim cross-match against root text TD3721 (50 probe phrases, one per verse,
whitespace-normalized): only 2/50 matched:
  - v3 opening "KHYIM PA'AM NI GSAR BU LA" x1 (quoted with added 'ANG:
    "KHYIM PA'AM NI GSAR BU LA'ANG")
  - v48/closing dedication "BSAM PA DAG PA'I SLOB MA NI" x2
=> The panjikakara paraphrases rather than reproduces full root verses; only
   rare near-verbatim citations.

Sample fragment-quote introductions (verbatim):
  v1: "DPAL LDAN RDO RJE SEMS DPA'I ZHES BYA BA LA SOGS PA GSUNGS TE"
  v4: "DAM CHOS ZHES BYA BA LA SOGS PA GSUNGS TE"
  v14ff: "RJES MTHUN ZHES BYA BA LA SOGS PA GSUNGS TE"

External quotation found inside the panjika (Vinaya/Pratimoksa-style, on ochre robes):
  "DE LA NGUR SMRIG MI LDAN TE, ,BDEN DANG DUL DANG MI LDAN PA, ,DE NI MCHOD 'OS NGUR SMRIG MIN, ,ZHES SO"

Panjika's own closing dedication verse (before translator colophon):
  "BLA MA'I BSNYEN BKUR RNAM PAR 'GREL PA NI, ,BYAS LAS BDAG LA BSOD NAMS GANG SKYES PA, ,DES NI 'GRO BAS BLA MA'I ZHABS BSTEN CING, ,DE BZHIN GSHEGS PA NYID DU 'GYUR"

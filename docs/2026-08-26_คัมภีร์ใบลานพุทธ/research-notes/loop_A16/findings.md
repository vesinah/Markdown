## 1. Etymology and format of lontar (Borassus leaves)

- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "Deriving its Javanese name from the rontal leaf of the palmyra palm (Borassus flabellifer), the lontar is a tradition of highly preserved palm-leaf manuscripts etched with the history and legends of ancient Asia."
  URL: https://www.readhibiscus.com/balinese-lontar-the-making-of-sacred-manuscripts
- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "Lontar is derived from rontal, which is composed of two Old Javanese word, ron and tal. Ron means leaf and tal means rontal tree, member of the palm family (Borassus flabellifer) which is also widely called siwalan tree."
  URL: https://www.gustibali.com/lontar-balinese-palm-leaf-manuscripts/
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read, tool terminology): "The letters were carved onto the surface using a small knife called pangutik or pangrupak... To make it more visible, burnt candlenut (kemiri) was applied to the surface, darkening the grooves... Each sheet of lontar (lempiran) is tied together by a piece of cord... cakep, a protective cover similar to a book cover... Some manuscripts are kept in wooden boxes called kropak."
  URL: https://lontar.liacs.nl/
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read, production chain): "The stripped leaves are then boiled for up to eight hours in a brine infused with herbs like cloves, ginger, salt, cumin, pepper, lemongrass... piled under a heavy wooden pamlagbag press to flatten for six to twelve months." Also: "scribes etch memos, stories, or ancient epic comics into the lontar pages using a pengerupak - a specialized iron stylus with a sharp, triangular tip"; "edges painted with a vibrant red pigment (kincu)"; "With proper care, lontar manuscripts last an average of 300 to 600 years, at which point they become brittle from oil loss and require transcription onto a new set."
  URL: https://www.readhibiscus.com/balinese-lontar-the-making-of-sacred-manuscripts
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "A lontar manuscript is a palm-leaf text, usually made from prepared leaves of the lontar or palmyra palm, inscribed with a sharp tool and darkened so the writing can be read." Plus genre list: "religious rules and rituals (agama lontar)... medical information (usada lontar), astronomy (wariga lontar), epic poems (itahasa lontar), Balinese history (babad lontar)".
  URL: https://whatsnewindonesia.com/bali/feature/education/unveiling-mysteries-lontar-balinese-ancient-manuscript
- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "The Indonesian word lontar is often connected with the palmyra palm, known botanically as Borassus flabellifer."
  URL: https://indonesiamuseum.org/en/articles/the-history-of-lontar-manuscripts-in-bali-and-lombok

## 2. Bali as living lontar tradition

- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "The use of lontar as a writing medium in Bali has been actively employed in literature and daily writing by the Balinese people since the mid-15th century until today."
  URL: https://budayabali.com/lontar-palm-leaf-manuscripts-balis-globally-renowned-traditional-art-treasure (2023-09-28)
- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "Kabar baiknya, tradisi menulis di atas daun lontar masih ada sampai saat ini. Bali merupakan satu dari banyaknya daerah di Indonesia yang masih melestarikan tradisi ini." [Translation logged by agent: the palm-leaf writing tradition still survives; Bali is among regions preserving it.]
  URL: https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2021/07/19/digitalisasi-lontar-bali-sebagai-upaya-menjaga-warisan-leluhur
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "these lontars are now considered sacred pages by the Balinese, still kept safe to commune with Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge."
  URL: https://www.readhibiscus.com/balinese-lontar-the-making-of-sacred-manuscripts
- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "Every six months, the Kliwon Wuku Watugunung ceremony celebrated the wisdom encapsulated in Lontar-a tradition that resonated across the entire island."
  URL: https://whatsnewindonesia.com/bali/feature/education/unveiling-mysteries-lontar-balinese-ancient-manuscript
- QUOTE (PARTIAL): "Traditionally, scribes use a small knife called a pengrupak to carve letters into the palm leaves, then apply a mixture of charcoal and oil to make the characters visible... Lontar manuscripts are still preserved in temples and family compounds across Bali, often guarded by pemangku (temple priests)."
  URL: https://baliinstitute.com/blog/balinese-traditional-writing-preserving-the-ancient-script-of-aksara-bali/
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "In Bali, manuscripts may be ritually honored in connection with Saraswati, the goddess associated with learning and knowledge."

## 4. Old Javanese/Kawi Buddhist works transmitted on lontar

### Sang Hyang Kamahayanikan (SHK) - core Buddhist case
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full text via docslib mirror of JIABU PDF): "Sanghyang Kamahayanikan is an esoteric Buddhist text... It was probably written between 929-947 AD by Mpu Shri Sambhara Surya Warama from East Java."
  URL: https://docslib.org/doc/4177452/sang-hyang-kamah%C4%81y%C4%81nikan-translation-and-analytical-study ; canonical record: https://so06.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/Jiabu/article/view/219145 (Utomo, JIABU 11(3), 2018, pp. 253-267)
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, same source - lontar materiality and discovery): "Version A consists of 65 palm leaves as compared to the less complete Version B which has only 27 palm leaves... The old-Javanese manuscript written on palm leaves, now known as Codex Orientalis 5023 of the Legatum Warnerianum, Leyden University Library, was discovered on the 18th of November in 1894 by Dr JÑ Brandes in the palace compound of the Balinese King of Cakranĕgara, on the island of Lombok."
  Same URL as above.
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, same source - inventory of Buddhist Kawi works): "The Sutasoma, Vighnotsava, Kunjarakarna, Sang Hyang Kamahāyānikan, and Buddhapamutus are purely Buddhist works and the Tjantakaparva, Arjunavijaya, Nagarakretagama, Wariga and Bubukshah show striking traces of Buddhism."
  Same URL as above.
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, same source - structure): "The first section entitled Sang Hyang Kamahayanan Mantranaya, consists of 42 Sanskrit verses, each with a related commentary in elaborate old Javanese... The second section consists of instructions in 86 verses, written mainly in old Javanese".
  Same URL as above.
- Modern liturgical reuse (VERIFIED abstract + body excerpts): Buddhayana formulated its Godhead concept "Sang Hyang Adi Buddha" from SHK; Government Regulation No. 21/1975 Art. 4(5) oath "for the sake of Sang Hyang Adi Buddha".
  URL: https://hts.org.za/index.php/hts/article/view/7054/21526 ; mirror: http://www.scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S0259-94222022000400006&script=sci_arttext

### Kakawin Sutasoma
- QUOTE (PARTIAL, museum source): "The Sutasoma Book was written in ancient Javanese by Mpu Tantular at the end of the 14th century during the Majapahit Kingdom zenith. This book describes the Majapahit Kingdom's long-standing religious tolerance. Later, the Indonesian country adopted this attitude of tolerance as its motto."
  URL: https://www.museumnasional.or.id/4008/
- QUOTE (PARTIAL, university manuscript catalog): "Abstrak Lontar ini berisi teks Kakawin Sutasoma, karangan Mpu Tantular." [Translation: This lontar contains the text of the Kakawin Sutasoma, composed by Mpu Tantular.] Second entry: Sutasoma as "titisan Sang Hyang Buddha" (incarnation of Buddha) upholding dharma.
  URL: https://lontar.ui.ac.id/detail?id=20187062 ; https://lontar.ui.ac.id/detail?id=20187061
- QUOTE (PARTIAL, news): "Kitab-kitab karya pujangga Wilwatikta, seperti Negarakertagama dan Sutasoma yang ditulis ulang menggunakan media daun lontar." [Works such as Negarakertagama and Sutasoma rewritten using lontar-leaf media; photo credit Perpustakaan Nasional Indonesia.]
  URL: https://radarmajapahit.jawapos.com/maja-sains/2312280001/manuskrip-kitab-negarakertagama-dan-sutasoma-ditulis-ulang-menggunakan-daun-lontar
- Survival pattern (keyword lead, not cited): nearly all surviving Sutasoma copies originated in Bali, on lontar and paper, with one Merapi-Merbabu Java fragment - Wikipedia states this; non-Wikipedia confirmation still needed (Phase B).

## 7. Hindu-majority context: fragmentary survival of Buddhist material

- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "Śivaism was the dominant religion... Śivaism coexisted alongside Buddhism for more than a millennium, from circa the fifth century CE through the late fifteenth century in Java, and on Bali until today." [Cambridge chapter summary quoted via its own landing page excerpt]
  URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/buddhist-dynamics-in-premodern-and-early-modern-southeast-asia/revisiting-the-cult-of-sivabuddha-in-java-and-bali/29929FF1E28249EE47111C0ABA32C598 (Acri 2015)
- QUOTE (VERIFIED, full read): "Bali's long-standing Śiva-Buddha dual clergy system, in which both priestly lineages are recognized, but the Śiva Pedanda holds higher ritual authority... Astapaka... is remembered as the founder of the Buddhist priestly line, Pedanda Buddha... Buddhist priests are required participants in rituals like Ekadaśa Rudra, but their role is supportive and secondary". Also Crawfurd early-19th-c observation that Buddhists were "few in number" and viewed as "a fallen sect".
  URL: https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/religionglobalsociety/2025/11/hierarchies-of-buddhism-the-siva-buddha-cult-in-contemporary-bali/

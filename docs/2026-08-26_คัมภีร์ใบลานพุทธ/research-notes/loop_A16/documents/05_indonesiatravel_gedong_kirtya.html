UPTD Gedong Kirtya

UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja is a prominent cultural institution dedicated to preserving Bali’s traditional written heritage. Located in Singaraja, Buleleng Regency, Gedong Kirtya is widely recognized as a repository and research center for Balinese palm-leaf manuscripts (lontar), which contain invaluable knowledge on religion, law, medicine, history, and classical literature.

As one of the oldest manuscript preservation institutions in Indonesia, Gedong Kirtya serves not only as a museum, but also as a cultural archive and scholarly reference center.

What is UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja?

UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja is a Regional Technical Implementation Unit specializing in the collection, preservation, documentation, and utilization of Balinese palm-leaf manuscripts. The institution supports academic research, cultural studies, and public education related to traditional Balinese knowledge systems.

The name “Gedong Kirtya” reflects its noble mission—a dedicated place to safeguard intellectual heritage.

Type and Classification of the Museum

Gedong Kirtya is classified as a specialized museum, focusing on philology and manuscript archives. Its collections primarily consist of handwritten palm-leaf manuscripts, including:

  • Religious and philosophical texts

  • Customary law and governance manuscripts

  • Traditional medical texts (usada)

  • Classical Balinese and Old Javanese literature

  • Modern documentation and transliterations

This specialization makes Gedong Kirtya a key institution in Balinese manuscript studies.

History of UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja

Gedong Kirtya was established in 1928 during the Dutch colonial period. It was initiated by scholars and local leaders who recognized the urgent need to protect Balinese manuscripts from deterioration and loss.

Initially functioning as a manuscript collection and documentation center, Gedong Kirtya gradually expanded its holdings by gathering manuscripts from across Bali and neighboring regions. Over time, it became one of the most comprehensive lontar archives in Bali.

Today, Gedong Kirtya operates under the Bali Provincial Government as a UPTD under the Cultural Office.

Management of UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja

UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja is managed by the Bali Provincial Cultural Office. The institution is supported by experts in manuscript conservation, philology, and cultural studies, ensuring professional preservation and public accessibility of its collections.

Vision of UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja

The vision of Gedong Kirtya Singaraja is to become a leading center for the preservation and development of Balinese traditional literacy, serving as a national and international reference for palm-leaf manuscript studies.

Mission of UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja

Its mission includes:

  1. Preserving and safeguarding Balinese manuscripts from damage and extinction

  2. Advancing research, documentation, and digitization of lontar manuscripts

  3. Providing educational and scholarly access to traditional knowledge

  4. Raising public awareness, especially among younger generations, about Balinese literary heritage

UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja plays a vital role in protecting Bali’s intellectual and literary heritage. Through its extensive lontar collection and dedicated management, the institution ensures that traditional knowledge remains relevant, accessible, and preserved for future generations.

## Tier 1 - Fully read and archived
1. Lontar Project (Leiden LIACS / Universite de La Rochelle / Unpad consortium). https://lontar.liacs.nl/ - FULL, ARCHIVED (01_liacs_lontar_project.html). AI + paleography project for lontar; tool names pangutik/pangrupak, kemiri, lempiran, cakep, kropak; OCR accuracies May 2025.
2. Utomo, Budi (Bhikkhu Ditthisampanno), "Sang Hyang Kamahayanikan: Translation and Analytical Study", JIABU Vol. 11 No. 3 (2018), pp. 253-267. Landing: https://so06.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/Jiabu/article/view/219145 ; full text mirror read: https://docslib.org/doc/4177452/... - FULL (via mirror), ARCHIVED (02 landing, 03 fulltext). SHK dating 929-947 AD; Version A 65 palm leaves / B 27 leaves; Cod. Orient. 5023 Legatum Warnerianum Leiden; discovery 18 Nov 1894 Cakranegara, Lombok by Brandes; inventory of purely Buddhist Kawi works.
3. Bhattacharya, B. K., "Hierarchies of Buddhism and Hinduism? The Siva-Buddha Cult in Contemporary Bali", LSE Religion and Global Society blog, 2025-11-04. https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/religionglobalsociety/2025/11/hierarchies-of-buddhism-the-siva-buddha-cult-in-contemporary-bali/ - FULL, ARCHIVED (04). Dual clergy Pedanda Siwa/Pedanda Buddha; hierarchy not equal syncretism; Crawfurd quote.
4. Ministry of Tourism RI, "Getting to Know UPTD Gedong Kirtya Singaraja and Its History". https://www.indonesia.travel/us/en/destination/bali-nusa-tenggara/bali/uptd-gedong-kirtya - FULL, ARCHIVED (05). Founded 1928; specialized philology museum; collection classes incl. Old Javanese literature and usada; digitization mission.
5. ReadHibiscus, "Balinese Lontar: The Making of Sacred Manuscripts". https://www.readhibiscus.com/balinese-lontar-the-making-of-sacred-manuscripts - FULL, ARCHIVED (06). Production chain: harvest-soak-boil-press; cempurit punch; pengerupak stylus; candlenut soot ink; kincu red edges; keropak box; 300-600 year lifespan.

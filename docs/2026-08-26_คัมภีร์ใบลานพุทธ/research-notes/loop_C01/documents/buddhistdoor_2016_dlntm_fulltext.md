VERIFIED QUOTES (quantitative Lan Na material-support data):
1. "The DLNTM project, which began in 2013, brings together images of close to 5,000 manuscripts, most of which are written on palm leaf and have been stored in the libraries of Buddhist monasteries and in private collections."
2. McDaniel quote: "It's mostly Buddhist material, but also scientific material, historical material, botany, astrology, grammar, folk tales, philosophical tales, a massive corpus going back from 1410 to the 1950s when print became more popular." (via Phys.org)
3. PNTMP (CMU, Hundius, 1987-91): "120,625 images digitized from grey-tone microfilms of 2,768 manuscripts... contains possibly the oldest dated Pali manuscripts in Southeast Asia"
4. DLNTM direct digitisation: "15,212 color images of 334 manuscripts from eight temples"
5. DELMN (1971-74): "13,413 grey-tone images of 992 manuscripts" — first comprehensive survey of Lan Na language mss in eight northernmost provinces
6. HHHWC: "21,222 images of 181 manuscripts... copied into notebooks by local scribes—monks selected from temples in Thailand's Lamphun Province—in the early 1970s"
7. Wat Sung Men, Phrae: library "alone contains more than 1,700 manuscripts that were microfilmed as part of the PNTMP"; composed early 19th c. era of Khruba Kancana
8. Earliest dated: Timsati-nipata section of Jataka Atthakatha Vannana copied 1471
9. Wharton (DLNTM technical director): losses from "being borrowed but never returned, or damaged by termites or fires"
NOTE: figures here are 2016 snapshot; DLNTM About page (A12, later) gives 6,137 mss total.

## 1. Microfilm legacy: NGMPP / PNTMP / DELMN

**NGMPP (Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project)**
- "The Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project (NGMPP) was founded in 1970 under an agreement between His Majesty's Government of Nepal and the German Oriental Society, and was financed by the German Research Council (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft)."
  URL: https://www.aai.uni-hamburg.de/en/forschung/ngmcp/history/about-ngmpp.html [ARCHIVED 12_unihamburg_ngmpp_about.html]
- National Archives of Nepal holds "5,300,000 folios from 1,81,500 individual manuscripts and documents preserved on negative and positive microfilm reels microfilmed by the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project (NGMPP) during the period from 1970 to 2001."
  URL: http://narchives.gov.np/MicroFilm.aspx
- Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin Orientabteilung: "The collection currently comprises more than 42,000 volumes of original manuscripts as well as more than 180,000 films, mainly of manuscripts from Nepal, which have been filmed by the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project."
  URL: https://staatsbibliothek-berlin.de/en/about-the-library/departments/orient/translate-to-english-sammlungen/translate-to-english-handschriften [ARCHIVED 13_sbb_orient_manuscripts.html]
- Successor catalogue project: NGMCP "was launched in April 2002. Its main purpose is to prepare a comprehensive descriptive catalogue of the more than 180,000 Nepalese manuscripts microfilmed under the Nepal-German Manuscript Preservation Project." Catalogue portal: catalogue.ngmcp.uni-hamburg.de; DFG-funded metadata search + descriptive wiki 2002-2012.
  URLs: https://catalogue.ngmcp.uni-hamburg.de/content/index.xml ; https://www.openaccess.uni-hamburg.de/datenbanken/ngmcp.html
- CSMC Hamburg news (2024): NGMPP "recorded around 180,000 manuscripts, consisting of over 5 million folios, on microfilm"; digitised documents handed back to National Archives.
  URL: https://www.csmc.uni-hamburg.de/news/2024-03-19-x-series.html

**Thai/Lao microfilm legacy feeding DLNTM/DLLM (from DLNTM About page, fully read)**
- DELMN (Dokumentarische Erfassung literarischer Materialien in den Nordprovinzen Thailands): first comprehensive survey of Lan Na manuscripts, Harald Hundius 1971-1974, funded by DFG; ~25 rolls of microfilm from almost 100 locations.
- SRI Chiang Mai University project (from 1981) catalogued/microfilmed over 4,000 manuscripts.
- PNTMP (Preservation of Northern Thai Manuscripts Project), 1987-1991, implemented by CMU's Center for the Promotion of Arts and Culture, funded by German Federal Foreign Office Cultural Assistance Programme; produced over 400 rolls of microfilm (now at Chiang Mai University Library); entire Wat Sung Men library (>1,700 mss, largest in Northern Thailand) microfilmed; copies also at National Archives of Thailand and Akademie der Wissenschaften und der Literatur, Mainz.
- PNTMP microfilms digitised in Germany 2012-2013 by Herrmann und Kraemer GmbH using Zeutschel OM 1600 roll-film scanner; images edited/renamed by DLLM team at National Library of Laos; 300ppi TIFF masters + 72ppi JPEG derivatives; EXIF metadata incl. GPS and copyright; archived redundantly at Berlin State Library (server host), CMUL, University of Pennsylvania, National Library of Laos.
  URL: https://digital.crossasia.org/digital-library-of-northern-thai-manuscripts-about?lang=en [ARCHIVED 02_dlntm_about_crossasia.txt]

## 2. Current digital libraries — verified counts

**DLNTM (Digital Library of Northern Thai Manuscripts)** — total "6,137 manuscripts from Northern Thailand" (homepage, fully read).
Four collections with exact counts (About page + IIIF portal agree):
- PNTMP: 3,914 manuscripts / 159,564 images
- DLNTM direct digitisation (colour, 39 temples, Nikon D610, 2013-2017): 1,051 manuscripts / 39,547 images
- DELMN: 991 manuscripts / 13,419 images
- HHHWC (Harald Hundius Handwritten Collection): 181 manuscripts / 21,222 images
URLs: https://digital.crossasia.org/digital-library-of-northern-thai-manuscripts/?lang=en [01]; .../about?lang=en [02]; https://iiif.crossasia.org/s/lanna [09]
- Note: ANU LibGuide shows slightly older counts (PNTMP 3,935 mss / DLNTM 1,026 mss etc.) — counts drift as ingestion continues. URL: https://libguides.anu.edu.au/c.php?g=894949&p=6439226 [14]
- License: CC BY-NC 4.0 (exception: traditional-medicine texts).
- Funders: Henry Luce Foundation, German Federal Foreign Office (Auswaertiges Amt), Andrew W. Mellon Foundation, University of Pennsylvania Libraries + School of Arts and Sciences. Partners: UPenn, Chiang Mai University, National Library of Laos, Berlin State Library.
- Oldest dated ms: copy of Timsati nipata (Jataka-atthakatha-vannana), CS 833 = CE 1471, Wat Lai Hin, Lampang.

**DLLM (Digital Library of Lao Manuscripts)** — "makes images of over 12,000 texts from throughout Laos easily accessible"; "At present 12,168 texts are searchable." Launched September 2009; experimental in-image search tool added September 2011; direct colour digitisation at National Library of Laos from January 2012. Collaborators: National Library of Laos, University of Passau, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin. Funded by DFG and BMZ (German Federal Ministry of Economic Cooperation and Development).
URL: https://digital.crossasia.org/digital-library-of-lao-manuscripts?lang=en [03]
(Consistent with brief's "~12,000-13,000".)

**FPL scans in BUDA (BDRC)**
- BDRC About (fully read): founded as TBRC 1999 by E. Gene Smith; 2015 Board vote to expand beyond Tibetan; "In 2016 TBRC officially changes its name to the Buddhist Digital Resource Center (BDRC) and launches its first major digital preservation initiative in Southeast Asia at the Fragile Palm Leaves archive"; BUDA released 2021 as Linked Open Data platform; "BDRC has located, digitized, cataloged, and archived over 27 million pages" and site banner states "shares over 28 million pages"; current work includes "handwritten text recognition".
  URL: https://bdrc.io/about-us/ [04]
- Khyentse Foundation (fully read): between 1999 and 2014 the org "scanned more than 9.5 million pages in 18,000 volumes"; on expansion it "began preserving and making accessible texts in Pali, Sanskrit, Chinese, and other South Asian and Southeast Asian languages. Along with the new mission came a second major project, the Fragile Palm Leaves Digitization Project."
  URL: https://khyentsefoundation.org/kf-projects/buddhist-digital-resource-center-bdrc/ [05]
- BUDA hosts Tibetan plus "Sanskrit, Chinese, Pali, Burmese, and Khmer materials". URL: https://www.bdrc.io/buda-archive/
- Specific FPL scan-page count inside BUDA: ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน (not stated on pages fetched).

**MEAP UCLA Shan survey (fully read)**
- Project: "Documenting Colonial-Era Monastic Collections in Former Principalities of the Shan State, Myanmar" — Inya-Burma Institute, lead Francois Tainturier; MEAP planning grant; MEAP is "supported by Arcadia".
- Findings: surveyed 74 monasteries across Taunggyi, Kengtung, Mae Hong Son. Hamsi Monastery: 82 manuscripts, all palm-leaf. Four Pa-O village monasteries combined: 150 parabaik, 90 palm-leaf, 17 bound scrolls. Banyin Monastery: 48 mss (20 parabaik, 28 palm-leaf). Wat Tongsi: 14 large + 49 small mulberry-paper mss and 70 palm-leaf mss. Wat Papu: 94 mulberry-paper mss.
- Key finding: higher-than-expected linguistic diversity (Tai Long, Tai Khuen, Tai Yuan, Myanmar scripts, plus Pa-O, Tai Nuea); shared custodianship of mss (abbot + lay zare + donors); collections span mid-19th c. to early 1970s.
URL: https://meap.library.ucla.edu/projects/myanmar [06]

## 3. Metadata standards & IIIF

**IIIF at CrossAsia (fully read via proxy)**
- iiif.crossasia.org/s/lanna is a live IIIF Presentation implementation of DLNTM: faceted bilingual search ("either in transcription or in original script"), drill-down filters, gallery of "1600+ photographs taken by David Wharton".
- Research-data publication: "Digital Library of Northern Thai Manuscripts (Lanna)", Berlin State Library, author Andrey Buchmann, issued 15.03.2024, DOI 10.48796/20240306-000, URN urn:nbn:de:0308-20240306-000-0, license CC BY 4.0, GND keywords include Palmblatthandschrift, exports MODS/MARCXML/DataCite etc.
URLs: https://iiif.crossasia.org/s/lanna [09]; https://repository.crossasia.org/receive/crossasia_mods_00000347 [08]

**IFLA PLM scoping workshop (fully read) — the key standards document**
- Workshop 22 Nov 2017, 15 participants (incl. David Wharton/National Library of Laos, Rujaya Abhakorn, Lampang Manmart, Nisachol Chamnongsri).
- April 2017 survey found 16 PLM projects and NO dominant standard; five schemas in use: National Library of Thailand schema, National Library of Laos schema (also used by Lan Na projects at CMU and Isan project at Mahasarakham), NMM (National Mission for Manuscripts, India), KKUPLMMs (Khon Kaen Univ., FRBR-based, revised 2012/2015), MARC21 (Northern Illinois University only).
- "52 elements found from 16 projects": physical characteristics 20, bibliographic 19, administrative 13.
- Draft core framework agreed: "39 elements divided into 3 groups by functions; 15 elements for supporting PLMs access; 15 elements for management; and 9 elements for supporting collection administration."
- Problems recorded: item-level legacy catalogues unsuitable; one element multiple meanings; archaic scripts require scarce experts.
URL: https://www.ifla.org/wp-content/uploads/files/assets/hq/plm2017_scopingworkshopreport_0.pdf [10]
- TEI encoding specifically for DLNTM/DLLM records: ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน (no fetched page documents TEI usage; repository record offers MODS/MARCXML exports).

## 4. OCR / HTR research state (as evidenced this session)

- arXiv full-text API for exact phrase "palm leaf manuscript": only 3 papers — FCN binarization (Tensmeyer & Martinez, ICDAR 2017 oral, applied to palm leaves), Indiscapes layout parsing (ICDAR 2019 oral), Palmira deformable instance segmentation (ICDAR 2021) — all Indic-domain, none Tai Tham/Khom/Burmese-specific.
  URL: http://export.arxiv.org/api/query?search_query=all:%22palm+leaf+manuscript%22 [11]
- Burmese palm leaf: Sriatmaja & Nwe Nwe "Myanmar Palm Leaf Manuscript OCR" (1-D segmentation + 18 hand-crafted features; Scribd mirror only — pre-deep-learning era).
  URL: https://www.scribd.com/document/884100582/Sriatmaja-Nwe-Nwe-9001-AAP
- Benchmarking: MDPI Journal of Imaging 4(2):43 "Benchmarking of Document Image Analysis Tasks for Palm Leaf Manuscripts" — RNN-LSTM word recognition/transliteration baseline.
  URL: https://www.mdpi.com/2313-433X/4/2/43
- Multi-low-resource palm-leaf recognition (Balinese, Khmer, Sundanese): ScienceDirect S0167865525001734 (2025).
- Vision Transformer char detection under degradation: IEEE 11350246.
- Tamil line is most active: Nature s40494-026-02559-8; THPLMD dataset (Data in Brief S2352340924000738); DenseNet 97.8% claim (GitHub); Nature news piece d44151-026-00020-0 claims 99.8% Tamil handwriting accuracy.
- Dedicated peer-reviewed HTR for **Tai Tham (Lanna)** or **Khom**: ยังไม่พบข้อมูลยืนยัน in this session's searches. Only adjacent artifacts found: Unicode font repo (github.com/norto/taitham-lanna), ScriptSource profile (scriptsource.org, Lana script, ~6M speakers, low literacy), and one commercial blog claiming Lanna recognition (lekhak.app — not an academic source, low confidence).
- Generic LLM-era HTR: arxiv 2411.03340 "Unlocking the Archives" (LLMs achieve SOTA on archival HTR); Transkribus platform (300+ public models) — no confirmed Tai Tham model listed this session.
  URLs: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03340v1 ; https://www.transkribus.org/ai-text-recognition
- Isan Dhamma characters segmentation work exists (Index Copernicus file 660356) — image-processing era, relevant to Khom/Tham family scripts but not HTR-grade.

## 5. Sustainability / funding models (all fully read)

- **Arcadia EAP** (Arcadia grant page): hosted at British Library; "Total awarded $28,930,000", years 2004-2033; "Since it started in 2004 the programme has awarded 562 grants"; covers material up to early 20th century; parallel UCLA programme (MEAP) for later material; outputs deposited in country of origin + permanent free copy at BL; books "From Dust to Digital" and "Remote Capture"; call opens each September. Example project: Shankar Thapa Nepal digitisation of 478 Hindu/Buddhist mss.
  URL: https://arcadiafund.org.uk/grants/endangered-archives-programme-2/ [07]
  BL-side figure: ">450 projects in over 90 countries... over 100 languages and scripts" (https://bl.iro.bl.uk/collections/728aaf50-dab0-4613-a116-3221c34231cd)
- **MEAP** (UCLA): "Our grants enable digitization and access to at-risk cultural heritage collections"; "The UCLA Library Modern Endangered Archives Program is supported by Arcadia." URL: https://meap.library.ucla.edu/
- **Khyentse Foundation**: funds BDRC as flagship text-preservation grant ("one of the most visible success stories of Khyentse Foundation funding"); supports FPL Digitization Project. URL: khyentsefoundation.org/kf-projects/buddhist-digital-resource-center-bdrc/
- **DFG**: financed NGMPP microfilming (uni-hamburg), NGMCP catalogue 2002-2012 (openaccess.uni-hamburg.de), DELMN survey 1971-74, DLLM co-funding (CrossAsia DLLM page).
- **German Federal Foreign Office**: funded PNTMP 1987-1991, all DLNTM digitisation waves (microfilm, notebooks, direct temple capture). (DLNTM About.)
- **Henry Luce Foundation / Mellon Foundation / UPenn**: principal funders of DLNTM. (DLNTM home + About.)
- **BMZ**: co-funder of DLLM. (DLLM page.)

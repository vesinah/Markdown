## Snippet 1 - DuckDuckGo-indexed description of https://www.efeo.fr/base.php/base.php?code=736 ("EFEO - Digital resources - Inventory of Khmer manuscripts")
"The database of Khmer manuscripts is the result of the work conducted by the EFEO-FEMC research team since 1990, aiming to provide a comprehensive inventory and photographic collection of Cambodia's manuscripts. On 2023 the databse and the webiste were migrated to Heurist system hosted by Huma-Num."

## Snippet 2 - Biblioteca Nacional de España guide page https://guias.bne.es/az/khmer-manuscripts
"Khmer Manuscripts. Proyecto puesto en marcha por el equipo EFEO-FEMC (Ecole francaise d'Extreme-Orient - Fonds d'Edition des Manuscrits du Cambodge) en 1990, para establecer un inventario sistematico y una coleccion fotografica de manuscritos camboyanos."

## Snippet 3 - Southeast Asian Archaeology blog, 2024-05-06, https://www.southeastasianarchaeology.com/2024/05/06/khmer-manuscripts-resource/
"EFEO just announced the publication of Khmer Manuscripts, an online database of Khmer documentary heritage. Since 1990, under the responsibility of Olivier de Bernon (EFEO), the FEMC's five-person team has been methodically locating, physically restoring, identifying and microfilming the remaining manuscripts in Cambodia's monastic libraries."

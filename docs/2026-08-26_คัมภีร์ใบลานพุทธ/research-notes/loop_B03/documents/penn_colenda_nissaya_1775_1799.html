Nissaya manuscript between 1775 and 1799

Timespan:
Early works to 1800
Date:
1775
Description:
Pedagogical monastic work of Northern Thai commentary on a limited amount of Pali text. A complete text (two puks) but with some broken leaves (f. 16, f. 18, pieces stitched together)..
Language:
Thai; Pali
Provenance:
Written at the Wat Mansikam temple by Phra Gīnamgalajao or Phra Ginajao.; Gift of Mrs. W. H. Kneedler (Christina Butler Harris Kneedler, former Presbyterian medical missionary in Thailand with her husband, William Harding Kneedler), 1951.; Kneedler, Christina Butler Harris, 1904-1985, donor.
Subject:
Buddhist literature, Pali -- Early works to 1800; Buddhist literature, Thai -- Early works to 1800; Buddhist literature, Thai; Buddhist literature, Pali
Resource Type:
Text
Form/Genre:
manuscripts (documents); facsimiles (reproductions); commentaries; sermons
Physical Description:
1 online resource (46 folios) : color illustrations

Notes:
Title supplied by Justin McDaniel (viewed on December 22, 2015); Written in 5 lines per leaf.; Written on leaves of palm leaf with two central holes approximately 18 cm apart and red lacquered edges.; Written in Thailand in the late 18th century (Justin McDaniel).; Dimensions of original manuscript: 5.2 x 55 cm.; Reproduced from the original with permission of the University of Pennsylvania Museum of Archaeology and Anthropology under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0) license.; In Northern Thai and Pali (Lanna).

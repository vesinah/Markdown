# Inventory and Conservation of Palm Leaf Manuscripts
## Wat Buranaram, Khlang Sub-district, Mueang District, Na-khon Si Thammarat Province

Publisher: DIRI (New Zealand)
ISBN (e-book): 978-1-7385960-0-3
Length: 154 Pages
First Online Release: August 2026

Project Summary: "This project digitized, conserved, and created an academic index of ancient Buddhist palm leaf manuscripts preserved at Wat Buranaram in Thailand. The survey identified 92 bundles (574 items / 1,003 fascicles) containing texts primarily written in Khom script in Pali and Pali-Thai languages, dating from the Ayutthaya and early Rattanakosin periods."

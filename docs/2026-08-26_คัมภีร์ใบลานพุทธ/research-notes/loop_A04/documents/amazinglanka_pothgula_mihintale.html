Pothgula Ruins near Singha Pokuna of Mihintale (මිහින්තලේ සිංහ පොකුණ අසළ ඇති පොත්ගුල ණටබුන්)

57 years ago2 years ago01 mins

Sinha Pokuna is where one of the best animal carvings of the Anuradhapura period can be found in the country. On a higher terrace from Singha Pokuna ruins, about 50 meters away lies another group of buildings hidden from the traveller and unknown to many.

This site is called Pothgula (the library). When the education of both lay and clergy was centred around temples in the ancient past, only the temples had libraries and acted as the keepers of the ancient knowledge left written in Ola leaves. Very little is known about the buildings at Pothgula. However, the massive rock door frame of the main building is easily recognisable to someone exploring this site.

Driving Directions to Mihintale

Mihintale can be reached through many routes from Colombo. The two main routes are through Puttalam (Puttalama) and through Kurunegala. Traveling from Puttalam, you will pass the scenic Wilpattu area. From Kurunegala, there are two main routes to Mihintale. The most common route is through Dambulla. The other route is through Galgamuwa. Out of all the routes, the most commonly used is the Kurunegala–Dambulla route (Route 2).

## A. เขมร (Cambodia)

**A1. Trent Thomas Walker — "Unfolding Buddhism: Communal Scripts, Localized Translations, and the Work of the Dying in Cambodian Chanted Leporellos"**
- PhD dissertation, University of California, Berkeley, 2018; 1651 pp.; Chair: Alexander von Rospatt; Committee: Penny Edwards, Peter Jenks, Olivier de Bernon; ProQuest 10817352
- URLs: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5199w721 ; https://www.trentwalker.org/unfolding-buddhism (เว็บไซต์ประกอบ: manuscript images, catalogs)
- สาระสำคัญ:
  - catalogue 70 leporellos (สมุดข่องพับ) พร้อม provenance/materiality/content + transcription/edition/translation 195 texts (Pali, Khmer, Siamese) — 17th–19th c.
  - Ch.6 = "grammatical techniques for precise and cogent translations in bilingual Pali-Khmer and Pali-Tai prose texts" (หัวใจของ nissaya-style gloss แบบเขมร)
  - Ch.7: translators crafted Khmer poems from Pali and Tai sources
  - วางกรอบ bitext กับ Siamese/Lao/Lanna parallels → "broader Khmer-Tai Buddhist world"

**A3. Trent Walker — "Carved Chants and Sermons on Stone: Epigraphic Evidence for Buddhist Literature in Middle Cambodia"**
- Udaya, Journal of Khmer Studies 15 (2020 [ตีพิมพ์ 2021]): 57–93
- PDF: https://www.yosothor.org/uploads/images/Udaya/Udaya_pdf/Udaya-Yosothor/No-15-2020/ARTICLES/02-03_Trent_Walker_Udaya15_2021.pdf
- สาระสำคัญ (จาก snippets ยาว):
  - จารึก IMA 32 ปี ค.ศ. 1688 มี passage ปิดท้ายเป็น **Pali-Khmer interlinear bitext แท้** ("the earliest such datable example to surface in Cambodia"); ใช้ particle เขมร mark case: ri (nom), vocative pubitr heyº
  - K.484 (ปลาย 12c, Phimeanakas): ใช้ Khmer particles mark morphology ของ Sanskrit แบบ systematatic — "unprecedented... no other inscription attempts such a literal translation from Sanskrit to Khmer"
  - Quote: "The bitextual style dominates the Khmer manuscript tradition as well as related corpora surviving in contemporary Laos, Myanmar, and Thailand"
  - Quote: "the Pali portions are dispensable, the Khmer portions are not"; Pali phrases precede Khmer counterparts (regional pattern)
  - "interlinear Pali-Khmer compositions had already existed for several centuries before its first epigraphic appearance here in 1688"

### B. ศรีลังกา (Sri Lanka — sannaya/sanne)

**B1. William Pruitt — "The Study of Burmese by Westerns with Special Reference to Burmese Nissayas"**
- *International Journal of Lexicography* 5(4) (1992): 278–304
- [ref ยืนยันจาก Brill reference list] — quote (p.278): "**Sinhalese Sanne and Burmese Nissayas** have been important in Pali studies in the West."
- p.294ff: Shwe Zan Aung (1871–1932) + Pe Maung Tin (1888–1973) ใช้ nissaya method แปล Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha (1910) และ Atthasālini (1920) เป็นอังกฤษให้ PTS
- p.278: Sutta-nipāta-sannaya (12c) = earliest example ของ genre ใน Sri Lanka

**B2. Gnanaloka, Maho — "Textual Transmission of the Vajirabuddhiṭīkā: A Comparative Analysis of Nidānavaṇṇanā Sinhalese and Burmese Editions"**
- International Research Conference of the Open University of Sri Lanka (IRC-OUSL 2025); DOI: 10.65646/3rc20dmg5a0847; PDF 847–851
- URL: http://repository.ou.ac.lk/handle/94ousl/3721
- สาระ: Vajirabuddhiṭīkā (oldest Vinaya sub-commentary, Anurādhapura–Polonnaruwa transition); เทียบ 6 Sinhalese-script palm-leaf mss vs older word-by-word Burmese Nissaya vs Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana edition; **finding: Burmese Nissaya aligns closer with Sinhalese manuscript tradition → preserves earlier recension** — bridge ตรงระหว่าง sannaya-context manuscripts กับ nissaya พม่า

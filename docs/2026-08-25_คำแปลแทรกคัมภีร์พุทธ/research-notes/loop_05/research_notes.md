### A. งานวิชาการแกนกลาง (interlinear/phrase-by-phrase Hán→Nôm)
- **Taylor, Keith W. (2005). "Sino–Vietnamese Translation from Classical to Vernacular." In Eva Hung & Judy Wakabayashi (eds.), *Asian Translation Traditions*. London: St. Jerome, pp.169–194.**
  - URLs: https://www.routledge.com/Asian-Translation-Traditions/HungHung-Wakabayashi/p/book/9781900650786 ; https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9781315759876-11/sino%E2%80%93vietnamese-translation-classical-vernacular-keith-taylor ; ประวัติผู้แต่ง: https://asianstudies.cornell.edu/keith-weller-taylor
  - Case study = 17th-c. Buddhist text translated from Classical Chinese into vernacular Vietnamese, surviving in an 18th-c. reprinting (= Cổ Châu pháp vân Phật bản hạnh ngữ lục)
  - Quote (editor's abstract): "examples of the translator seeming to willfully change the sense of the original can be understood as arising from patterns of thought attributable to the vernacular culture"
  - จุดสำคัญ: source texts จำนวนมากแปลโดยชาวเวียดนามเขียน Hán เอง ("did not regard it as a foreign language" แต่ต้องแปลเพื่อคนหลัง); มีกรณี *absence* of translation จากนโยบายผู้ปกครองกลัวพลัง subversive ของภาษาแวernacular; Hán prose → Nôm poetry retelling เพื่อ oral circulation; scholars ใน Vietnam "biased toward Classical Chinese" ไม่ค่อย respect vernacular as autonomous entity
- **Taylor, Keith W. (2017). "What Lies Behind the Earliest Story of Buddhism in Ancient Vietnam?" *Journal of Asian Studies* (Cambridge Core).**
  - URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-asian-studies/article/what-lies-behind-the-earliest-story-of-buddhism-in-ancient-vietnam/DB39DCFD3FAD0971F056A896E3664C2F
  - **Cổ Châu pháp vân Phật bản hạnh ngữ lục**: printed with woodblocks 1752; earliest version = **Hán 漢 literary-Chinese prose with a phrase-by-phrase translation into Nôm 喃** — "the forms of Nôm used suggest that the translation was completed in the seventeenth century"
  - Taylor 2002/2005 framework: 7 editorial episodes; 4 divergence types ระหว่าง Hán↔Nôm: intentional shifts of meaning / interpretive additions / explanatory additions / errors ← ★ โมเดลวิเคราะห์ interlinear gloss ที่ตรงธีมโปรเจกต์
  - อ้าง Trần Văn Giáp (1932) งานบุกเบิก; Nguyễn Lang 1973/2008; Hà Văn Tấn 1993; Nguyễn Tài Thư 1988/1992/2008; Lê Mạnh Thát 1999; dictionary Chỉ nam ngọc âm giải nghĩa 指南玉音解義 (lục bát bilingual Hán-Việt)
- **Hoàng Thị Ngọ (1999; ฉบับใหม่ 2022). *Chữ Nôm và tiếng Việt qua bản giải âm "Phật thuyết đại báo phụ mẫu ân trọng kinh"*. NXB Khoa học xã hội, 231 pp.; 2022: NXB Văn học, 310 pp.**
  - URLs: https://books.google.com/books/about/?id=PmxkAAAAMAAJ ; https://nxbvanhoc.com.vn/chu-nom-va-tieng-viet-qua-ban-giai-am-phat-thuyet-dai-bao-phu-mau-an-trong-kinh ; full-text thesis record: http://luanan.nlv.gov.vn/luanan?a=d&d=TTkGGSfNPgPu1996
  - ผู้แต่ง: PGS Hoàng Thị Ngọ, researcher กอง Văn tự học, Viện nghiên cứu Hán Nôm
  - Bản giải âm ถูก Trịnh Quán khắc in lại ~1730 จาก bản in ก่อนหน้า (ผู้จัดพิมพ์บอก original late-15th c.; บทความ hannom.org.vn เสนอ giải âm อาจสมัย Trần)
  - **ปรากฏการณ์ "chữ Nôm hai mã chữ"** (two separate graphemes writing one Vietnamese syllable) — เก็บ pre-syllabic elements / initial clusters bl-, kl-, ml- → หลักฐาน historical phonology
  - Quote (hannom.org.vn): "Do sự thiêng liêng của một bản kinh Phật…mỗi khi tái bản, không ai dám tự ý sửa chữa 'hiệu đính'" — sacredness ⇒ textual fidelity across reprints ★ ตรงธีมศักดิ์สิทธิ์ของโปรเจกต์
- **Viện nghiên cứu Hán Nôm articles (www.hannom.org.vn)**:
  - https://www.hannom.org.vn/detail.asp?param=1081&Catid=591 (two-mã chữ phenomenon in Phật thuyết; Haudricourt/Ferlus/Nguyễn Ngọc San/Nguyễn Tài Cẩn chain on preglottalized initials)
  - https://www.hannom.org.vn/detail.asp?param=1676&Catid=344 (Thiền tông bản hạnh 1745 vs 1932 printings; chữ Nôm thời Trần "dọn" cleaned by Chân Nguyên; เทียบ Khóa hư lục giải âm của Tuệ Tĩnh, Nam dược quốc ngữ phú, Chỉ nam ngọc âm giải nghĩa)

### B. ต้นฉบับ Hán-Nôm ที่ chùa + การแปล interlinear จริง
- **"Khảo sát 'Pháp bảo đàn kinh giải' ở chùa Linh Quang Điều Hạ, Hải Phòng" — *Tạp chí Nghiên cứu Phật học*, ISSN 2734-9195, 14/09/2024**
  - URL: https://beta.tapchinghiencuuphathoc.vn/khao-sat-phap-bao-dan-kinh-giai-o-chua-linh-quang-dieu-ha-hai-phong.html (mirror: tapchinghiencuuphathoc.vn/...)
  - Pháp bảo đàn kinh 解 = Platform Sutra (法寶壇經) แปล Hán→Nôm โดย Thiền sư **Minh Châu Hương Hải 明珠香海** (法號 Huyền Cơ Thiện Giác 玄機善覺)
  - **Layout interlinear จริง:** "cứ một câu chữ Hán thì tác giả dùng một câu chữ Nôm để giải. Nguyên văn chữ Hán thì khắc chữ lớn, phần giải thì khắc chữ nhỏ" (big Hán characters + small Nôm gloss per sentence) ★ เทียบตรงกับ eonhae/kunten layouts
  - Lời tựa states: "Trước sau truyền đến nước Nam ta, chưa có phiên dịch ra quốc ngữ…" — self-conscious first-translation rhetoric
- **"Bước đầu khảo sát văn bản Diệu Pháp Liên Hoa Kinh Thích Giải Phương Ngữ của Hương Hải thiền sư" — same journal**
  - URL: https://www.tapchinghiencuuphathoc.vn/dieu-phap-lien-hoa-kinh-huong-hai.html
  - MS **AB.488** (Viện nghiên cứu Hán Nôm), ~1000 tờ; Hương Hải ทิ้งไว้ ~20 works แปลเป็น Nôm (Giải Pháp Hoa Kinh, Giải Kim Cương Kinh, Giải Sa Di Giới Luật, Giải Phật Tổ Tam Kinh…)
  - ลำดับเวลาที่ article สรุป: Kinh Báo phụ mẫu trọng ân prose translation by thiền sư Viên Thái in ~1442 → Hương Hải 17th c. → Chân Nguyên (Nam Hải Quan Âm), Như Trừng (Sa di thập giới quốc âm) late 17th–18th → Pháp Liên, Pháp hoa quốc ngữ kinh lục bát (late 19th)
  - Hương Hải ใช้ Diệu Pháp Liên Hoa kinh yếu giải ของ Giới Hoàn (戒環) เป็น reference; แปล faithful-to-Hán prose + แทรกคำอธิบายศัพท์
- **"Giới thiệu văn bản Pháp Bảo Đàn Kinh tại chùa Đồng Đắc tỉnh Ninh Bình" — same journal, 14/11/2025**
  - URL: https://www.tapchinghiencuuphathoc.vn/gioi-thieu-van-ban-phap-bao-dan-kinh-tai-chua-dong-dac-tinh-ninh-binh.html — copy ที่สองของ Hương Hải's Pháp bảo đàn kinh giải; GS Lê Mạnh Thát เคยแนะนำผลงาน Hương Hai แต่文本學 study ยังน้อย
- **CiNii record (2022-07-31): "Dialectal Features of Nôm Characters in the Sino-Nom Version of Phật Thuyết Thiên Địa Bát Dương Kinh" 漢喃版『佛説天地八陽經』に見る字喃の方言性**
  - URL: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390574411905458944 — ⚠️ author romanization カンナンバン [UNVERIFIED], journal name ไม่ครบใน snippet

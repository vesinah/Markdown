## 2. รายการแหล่งที่พบ (raw data)

### 2.1 Tibetan–Chinese bilingual manuscripts

**[08-S01] van Schaik, Sam. "A Chinese-Tibetan bilingual Buddhist manuscript." IDP Blog (British Library).**
- URL: https://idp.bl.uk/blog/a-chinese-tibetan-bilingual-buddhist-manuscript/ (HTTP 403 เมื่อ fetch โดยตรง — เนื้อหาได้จาก search excerpt)
- Manuscript: **Or.8210/S.5603** (Stein Collection, British Library)
- สาระ:
  - Concertina-format bilingual Laṅkāvatāra manuscript
  - **Chinese commentary (black ink, vertical top-to-bottom) + Tibetan sūtra translation (red ink, horizontal between the lines of Chinese)**
  - ต้องหมุน manuscript 90° เพื่ออ่านทิเบต → ดูเหมือน pothī; มี pothī string-hole design element
  - Text marked up to show where Tibetan corresponds to Chinese
  - Function hypotheses: language-learning aid; OR Ueyama Daishun's theory = used by translator **Chodrup (= Chos grub / Facheng)**, 9th-c. Dunhuang, preparing new translation from Chinese (Tibetan text on MS is a *different* translation made from Sanskrit)
- Reference given: Ueyama Daishun. 1990. *Tonkō bukkyō no kenkyū*. Kyōto: Hōzōkan.

**[08-S02] Li, Channa (2022). "By no means doodles or scraps: reading manuscripts IOL Tib J 3 and 218 as bilingual Dunhuang Vinaya works." Bulletin of SOAS 85(2): 265–305.**
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0041977X22000623 | Cambridge Core page fetched (abstract + refs)
- Affiliation: Austrian Academy of Sciences – IKGA, Vienna; ORCID 0000-0001-7187-5478
- Deep Reading: abstract + footnote + reference list สมบูรณ์
- สาระ:
  - ต่อต้าน "waste theory" ของ Dunhuang MSS: IOL Tib J 3 & 218 = coherent Vinaya compositions
  - **สามชั้นลายมือ**: large Tibetan script (โครงการเดิม — Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya commentaries); smaller Tibetan script + Chinese script = **same bilingual project** providing different textual sources on Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya
  - "proceeding from the high-level bilingualism of Dunhuang Buddhist communities... robust thematic affinity"
  - Uses Derge Kanjur main + Stog Kanjur variants (Yao 2012 on Stog value for Vinaya)
- Quote (abstract): *"the smaller Tibetan script and the Chinese script were created as parts of the same bilingual project to provide different textual sources on the Mūlasarvāstivāda Vinaya texts."*

**[08-S03] Pei, Changchun (2024). "A Re-Examination of Pelliot Tibétain 1257: A Workbook for Chinese Learning Tibetan?" Religions 15(6):748. MDPI.** [CORRECTED by Unbiased Agent 2026-08-25: Crossref DOI 10.3390/rel15060748 ยืนยันผู้แต่ง = Changchun Pei, School of History and Culture, Shandong Normal University; published online 2024-06-19; abstract ตรงสาระที่บันทึกไว้ทุกจุด]
- URL: https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1444/15/6/748 | DOI: 10.3390/rel15060748
- สาระ:
  - PT 1257 = **Tibetan-Chinese bilingual glossary ~570 entries**, Buddhist terms incl. excerpts from Saṃdhi-nirmocana-sūtra; word-family organization (headword + derivatives, e.g., 四部洲/gIIng bzhI...)
  - Tibetan terms written first by one person; Chinese filled in later by **three different hands** → teacher-student workbook model ("shun zhu" 順朱 vermilion-following practice analogy with P.4900V)
  - Dating hypothesis 779–814 CE (cited: Apple and Apple 2017:109)
  - Context: imperial sutra-copying project under Tibetan king; economic incentive for Chinese scribes
  - Digital images: Gallica (bnf.fr) + idp.bl.uk

**[08-S04] IDP "Collection items" learning page (bilingual items list).**
- URL: https://idp.bl.uk/discover/learning/dunhuang/collection-items/
- สาระ:
  - Chinese-Tibetan concertina (S.5603) described again
  - **Khotanese-Chinese bilingual scroll** — report by diplomat Ca Kīma-Saṃa (Chin. Zhang Jinshan), Mogao visit; Chinese section written left-to-right (unconventional, possibly Khotanese-script influence)
  - **Sino-Tibetan phrasebook** (on verso of Lotus Sūtra copy; cf. Or.8210/S.1000 & S.2736 per phrasebook blog) — Tibetan word followed by Chinese equivalent, in Tibetan script; merchant vocabulary (goods, haggling "last price", illness/robbery phrases, invoking bon po)

### 2.2 Old Tibetan canonical/pre-canonical translations at Dunhuang

**[08-S05] Apple, James B. (2016). "The Old Tibetan Version of the Kāśyapaparivarta preserved in Fragments from Dunhuang (1)." Annual Report of the IRiAB Soka University (ARIRIAB) 20: 205–230.**
- URL (mirror w/ summary): https://bodhicitta.tsadra.org/index.php/Articles/The_Old_Tibetan_Version_of_the_K%C4%81%C5%9Byapaparivarta_preserved_in_Fragments_from_Dunhuang_-_Part_1
- สาระ: first identification of Old Tibetan Kāśyapaparivarta; **seven Stein/Pelliot fragments** + eighth = PT 676 (identified by Jonathan Silk); collation vs Staël-Holstein (1926) Sanskrit ed.; versions known: 2 Central Asian Skt MSS, 5 Chinese, Khotanese frags., 9th-c. Tibetan Kanjur version.

**[08-S06] Apple, James B. (2021). An Old Tibetan Dunhuang Manuscript of the Avaivartikacakrasūtra (monograph, Academia.edu full text).**
- URL: https://www.academia.edu/49443368/
- สาระ:
  - Collation of Old Tibetan fragments (incl. **IOL Tib J 53**) vs Kanjurs
  - **Terminological comparison**: Old Tibetan Dunhuang terms closer to PT 1257 glossary than to finalized Mahāvyutpatti (ca. 814) or vulgate Kanjur → terminology coined/knew/utilized before imperially sanctioned register
  - Figure/table apparatus incl. Index of Old Tibetan Terminology

**[08-S07] Hu, Enbo. "From sūtra to practice: discovering the liturgy of the seven Tathāgatas including Bhaiṣajyaguru in Old Tibetan manuscripts." BSOAS (Cambridge Core).**
- URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-school-of-oriental-and-african-studies/article/from-sutra-to-practice-discovering-the-liturgy-of-the-seven-tathagatas-including-bhaisajyaguru-in-old-tibetan-manuscripts/FBF98988DC25501AA18F898AB1DF2F2C
- สาระ: ITJ 433/434 + PT 179/247/248 reunified; corrects Lalou 1939 & Schopen 1978 identifications; ITJ 433 ≈ Old Tibetan version of D 3132 (*Personal Instructions on the Sūtra of the Detailed Account of the Previous Aspirations of the Seven Tathāgatas*); liturgicalization process pre-canonical → canonical; Dalton & van Schaik 2006 catalogue corrections.

**[08-S08] Dotson, Brandon. "Misspelling 'Buddha': The officially commissioned Tibetan Aparimitāyur-nāma mahāyāna-sūtras from Dunhuang and the study of Old Tibetan orthography." BSOAS.**
- URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-school-of-oriental-and-african-studies/article/abs/misspelling-buddha-the-officially-commissioned-tibetan-aparimitayurnama-mahayanasutras-from-dunhuang-and-the-study-of-old-tibetan-orthography/49ECA266913E7841B94B92F0026A6A4A
- สาระ: corpus of **>1,600 copies** of Tshe dpag du myed pa'i mdo produced Dunhuang 820s–840s (BL); editors' corrections + ~200 explicits → Middle Old Tibetan orthographic norms; dating markers ('i(s) genitive/ergative as separate syllable increases 10th c.; tu terminative absence); related refs: Matko & van Schaik 2013 scribal colophons database (IDP); Iwao 2013 roll-type Śatasāhasrikā PP sūtra; Takeuchi 2013 writing boards of Chinese scribes; Huang Wenhuan 1982 colophon catalogue of Hexi sutras.

**[08-S09] Acta Asiatica Varsoviensia 34 (2021). "A Preliminary Study of the Dunhuang Tibetan Fragments of the Mūlasarvāstivāda-Ekottarakarmaśataka (I): Tarjanīyakarman."**
- URL: http://aav.iksiopan.pl/index.php/en/archive-issues/259-acta-asiatica-varsoviensia-34-2021-2/870-a-preliminary-study-of-the-dunhuang-tibetan-fragments-2
- สาระ: lost Old Tibetan version of EK preserved in **PT 945 + IOL Tib J 596**; compared vs Chinese Genbenshuoyiqieyoubu baiyijiemo (T 1453, Yijing); tarjanīyakarman passage derived mutatis mutandis from Pāṇḍulohitakavastu.

**[08-S10] Russian IOM article (journals.eco-vector.com 2410-0145): Дх. Тиб. 217 pothi book of 'Phags pa ting nge 'dzin mchog dam pa (Ārya-samādhyagrottama).**
- URL: https://journals.eco-vector.com/2410-0145/article/download/35146/23572
- สาระ: St. Petersburg small pothi book; different from later canon readings; parallel folios = PT 720 & PT 900 (same scribe); suggests older version existed; IDP transliteration search used for identification; RKTS (Wien "Resources for Kanjur&Tanjur Studies") colophons comparison.

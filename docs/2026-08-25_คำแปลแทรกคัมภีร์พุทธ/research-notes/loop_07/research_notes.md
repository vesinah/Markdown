### 2.3 Mahāvyutpatti / sGra sbyor bam po gnyis pa / bkas bcad

**[07-S07] Scherrer-Schaub, Christina A. (2002). "Enacting Words. A Diplomatic Analysis of the Imperial Decrees (bkas bcad) and their Application in the sGra sbyor bam po gñis pa Tradition." JIABS 25(1–2): 263–340.**
- URL: https://journals.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/index.php/jiabs/article/view/8927 (landing blocked by Anubis bot-protection; PDF link: .../article/download/8927/2820/8721 — เนื้อหาได้จาก search full-text excerpts)
- สาระ:
  - อ่าน sGra sbyor ในฐานะ **public act/chancery document** (protocol–dispositio–eschatocol; Fig. A §1–§3)
  - **สาม bkas bcad**: (1) "time of the Father/Yab myes" (relative dating only); (2) pig-year = **783 (Tabo version)** at Zun kar court; (3) horse-year **814** at 'On can rdo court, reign Khri lDe sron btsan (Sad na legs)
  - Uray 1979 traced "deviating tradition" attributing edict to Ral pa can wrongly
  - skad gsar bcad = "new lexical entries sanctioned by Imperial decision"; three vyutpattis (*Alpa-, Madhya-, Mahā-vyutpatti*)
  - Tabo fragment = fourth old incomplete manuscript discovered recently; Panglung 1994 published new fragments (East and West 44)
- Related verified items: Ishikawa, Mie (1990). *A Critical Edition of the sGra sbyor bam po gñis pa*, Studia Tibetica 18, Toyo Bunko (+ tr. Studia Tibetica 28, 1993); Panglung, Jampa L. (1994). "New fragments of the sGra sbyor bam po gñis pa," East and West 44(1): 161–172.

  - narrative-TAM findings: perfective -tsu/-nu foreground transformation; resultative -(a)ri/-tari background; past -ki frames discourse; conclusion: kunten-glossed sutra tales = "earliest examples of fully formed narratives written in Japanese" (vs Taketori in kana)

### C. Research-history & systematics of kunten
- **Cinato, Franck [& Alberizzi] (2014). Article PDF (shesl.org, file alberizzi2014.pdf).**
  - URL: https://shesl.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/alberizzi2014.pdf — ⚠️ exact title/page attribution incomplete from search (search engine tagged Author: Franck Cinato); Alberizzi 2014 cited by Osaka paper as source of glossing-scheme figure → treat as Cinato/Alberizzi 2014 [UNVERIFIED full citation]
  - Oldest extant glossed manuscript in Japan = **続華厳経略疏刊定記 Zokukegonkyō ryakuso kanjōki** (Avatamsaka commentary), transcribed by layman Chikyō 智鏡, collated twice at Tōdaiji in Enryaku 2 & 7 (783, 788); glosses in white & vermillion
  - okototen once considered uniquely Japanese invention — challenged by **July 2000 Korean-Japanese joint discovery of similar organized gloss sets in some 11th-c. Korean documents** ★ cross-loop link to Loop 04 gakpil jeomto kugyŏl
  - >200 schemes in 9th–10th c.; 8 main groups classified by position of -te/ni/wo/wa; "unitary theory of the origin of okototen"; groups 1–4 appear in 9th c., 5–8 in 10th; by 11th c. selection process left few
  - Complex systems: "[西墓点?]" showcases 31 boxes/230 morphosyntactic glosses [sic — OCR garble, verify original]
  - Sacred-text systems mostly tied to Kegon school / Six Nara Schools at Tōdaiji; hypothesis of birth among Kegon literati then spread to lay world via Tendai etc.; esoteric-kundoku thesis rejected (public lectures); Tendai↔Shingon exchanges centered Ishiyamadera (Enryakuji↔Ninnaji axis); Emperor Uda as lay transmission figure
- **Tsukishima, Hiroshi 築島裕 (1990). 平安時代の訓点資料の伝存状況についての一考察――ヲコト点から見た諸寺経蔵本の性格. *国語学* 160:1–14.**

## แหล่งพบ

### 1. Smith, Lesley. *The Glossa ordinaria: The Making of a Medieval Bible Commentary* (Leiden: Brill, 2009)
- URL: https://archive.org/details/glossaordinariam0000smit (Internet Archive — borrowable, 543MB scan)
- Glossa = "ubiquitous text of the Middle Ages"; compiled 12th-c. France; Vulgate surrounded by patristic commentary marginal + interlinear; thousands of manuscripts; chapters on layout/production/use; Beryl Smalley prehistory

### 2. Andrée, Alexander. "Unlocking the sacra pagina: Editing the Biblical Gloss with the Help of its Medieval Users" (Brepols, Lectio series)
- URL: https://www.brepolsonline.net/content/books/10.1484/M.LECTIO-EB.5.118726
- Glossa ใน hundreds of manuscripts, several versions/recensions; Peter Lombard, Peter Comestor, Stephen Langton lectures

### 3. PIMS PDF: "Textual Format and the Development of the Early Glossa on Genesis"
- URL: https://pims.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/ms78-125.pdf
- Glossa primitiva vs reformata; textus intercisus format; mise-en-page ของ gloss format; Laon origins; BNF lat. 14398 & lat. 64
- **ประโยคหลัก:** "commentaries that made up the Glossa on the Bible compiled seven hundred years of Latin Christian exegesis in the marginal and interlinear spaces around the sacred text"

### 4. Larson, Atria A. "The Exegetical World That Paved the Way for the Glossa Ordinaria..." Traditio (Cambridge)
- URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/traditio/article/abs/exegetical-world-that-paved-the-way-for-the-glossa-ordinaria.../
- two versions of Matthew Gloss; Laon exegetical community; mise-en-page invites modification

### 5. Salomon, David A. *An Introduction to the 'Glossa Ordinaria' as Medieval Hypertext* (University of Wales Press, 2012)
- URL: https://www.perlego.com/book/573061/
- etymology gloss < Greek glōssa (tongue); glossing practice from antiquity; Gilbert de Poitiers cum textu format; editio princeps Adolf Rusch Strasbourg 1480/81; De Hamel *Glossed Books of the Bible and the Origins of the Paris Booktrade*

### 6. GlossaOrdinaria.net — Handlist of Mediaeval Manuscripts (Froehlich & Zier)
- URL: https://glossaordinaria.net/
- 2500+ manuscripts handlist; GLOSSEM/Gloss-e project (IRHT/CNRS, Martin Morard); labels parva/ordinaria/media/magna/prisca

### 7. Andrée, Alexander. "The Glossa ordinaria on the Gospel of John: A Preliminary Survey of the Manuscripts..." (Revue bénédictine)
- URL: https://www.brepolsonline.net/content/journals/10.1484/J.RB.5.100573

## ข้อสังเกตเชิงวิธีวิทยา
- Glossa ordinaria = interlinear+marginal commentary รอบตัวบทศักดิ์สิทธิ์ (Vulgate) — กรณีเทียบตะวันตกที่แข็งแรงที่สุด มี scholarship หนา
- ยังต้องค้นต่อใน Phase A:
  - Old English interlinear glosses (Lindisfarne Gospels gloss by Aldred, Rushworth gloss) — vernacular gloss on Latin scripture
  - Wycliffite glossed gospels
  - Hebrew Masorah (marginal annotation system)
  - Qur'an Persian interlinear translations
  - เวท: padapatha / pada-pāṭha และ chāyā tradition

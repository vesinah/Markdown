## Sub-topic 3: Sheldon Pollock's vernacularization theory (VERIFIED)
- **Book**: Sheldon Pollock, *The Language of the Gods in the World of Men: Sanskrit, Culture, and Power in Premodern India*, UC Press 2006 (paperback 2009, ISBN 9780520260030, 706 pp.). Publisher TOC incl. - Precursor essay: "Cosmopolitan and Vernacular in History," *Public Culture* 12.3 (2000). https://culturalstudies.ucsc.edu/2001/02/08/february-8-2001-sheldon-pollock-cosmopolitanism-and-the-vernacular/

PREFACE 
This volume reproduces the Nepalese manuscript of the Kriyasangraha 
nama paiijika in the Prof. Raghu Vira Collection at the International Academy 
of Indian Culture. Its author is Kuladatta whence it is also known as Kuladatta­
pafijika. 
Its earliest palmleaf codex belonging to the 13-14th century is in the 
University Library at Cambridge,1 and it is entitled Kriya-pafijika:. Other 
manuscripts are found at London2, Paris3, University of Tokyo4, Calcutta5, 
Leningrad6, and Kathmandu7, 
This work was translated into Tibetan by Kirticandra and Yar-luns-pa 
Grags-pa-rgyal-msthan8 who was born in 1147, came to be the Abbot of the 
Sa-skya monastery in his 26th year (A. D. 1172) and attained nirvar:ia at the 
age of 70 in the year 1226.

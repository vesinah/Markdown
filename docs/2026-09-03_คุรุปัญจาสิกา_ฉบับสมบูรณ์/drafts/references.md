*   ———. *Gurupañcāśikā*. Romanized input (IAST) based on Sarnath edition. Digital Sanskrit Buddhist Canon (DSBC), text ID 221/914. Lalitpur: Nagarjuna Institute of Exact Methods, 2006. https://www.dsbcproject.org/canon-text/content/221/914.

*   ———. *bla ma lnga bcu pa*. In *sNar-thang bsTan-'gyur* (Narthang edition), rgyud, vol. 72, fols. 32a–34b.
*   ———. *bla ma lnga bcu pa*. In *Co-ne bsTan-'gyur* (Cone edition), rgyud, vol. 72, fols. 30b–33a.

In *gSung-'bum* (Collected Works), vol. 18 (tsa), fols. 1–45. sKu-'bum Monastery edition.
